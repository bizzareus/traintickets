- **`www.pmf.irctc.co.in/cn/TermsAndConditions.html`** (and similar static IRCTC T&C HTML) is
  **YEARS out of date** (checked 2026-07-10): it still states "6 tickets/calendar month",
  "**120 days** ARP", "Tatkal 10 AM, no AC/non-AC distinction", and **no** Aadhaar-OTP rule.
  All four are SUPERSEDED. **Do NOT use it to "correct" a blog backward.** For rules that
  changed, the authoritative current source is the relevant **PIB press release** (dated) — it
  overrides the static T&C. This page IS reliable for things that haven't changed, e.g. it
  cleanly confirms **max 6 berths/PNR (general) / 4 (Tatkal)**.
- General rule for the audit: prefer a **dated PIB notice** or a live IRCTC portal value over an
  undated static HTML T&C. If they conflict and PIB is newer, PIB wins.

  portal (`rr.irctc.co.in/termsCond`, cert-flaky). Retiring-room duration (3 vs 12 hr) still
  UNVERIFIED — needs that portal or a station-level tariff PDF.
