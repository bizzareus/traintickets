As of 2026-07-05, LastBerth's highest-impression blog pages (irctc-current-availability-explained ~13.3k imp, irctc-ticket-booking-limits-aadhaar-verification ~7k, wl-waiting-list-meaning-indian-railway ~5.7k, irctc-app-vs-website-tatkal-booking, retiring-room, auto-upgradation, vikalp) are already deeply comprehensive and rank on page 1 (avg pos ~6–9), yet nearly all convert **0.1–0.9% CTR** in Google Search Console. The daily blog task has been aggressively refreshing content, hitting diminishing returns.

**Why:** content depth is saturated and much of the low CTR is structural — definitional queries ("wl full form", "how many tickets per month") get absorbed by Google AI Overviews. Re-expanding these fresh pages daily is churn (scaled-content-abuse risk).

**How to apply:** in future daily-blog runs, before expanding a top page, check its git `updated` date and whether it was already refreshed in the last few days — if so, prefer the **CTR/title-meta lever** (sharper, decision-hook titles that promise more than the one-line AI answer) or **EXPAND genuinely stale page-2 pages** (pos 10–20). Reserve new content for real gaps. See [[blog-topics-written]] rows dated 2026-07-05 (EXPAND / EXPAND(CTR)).
