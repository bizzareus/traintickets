## Suggested caption

Waitlisted end to end? Try searching the journey differently. 🚆

In these screenshots for North East Express (12506), 30 September 2026:

• ConfirmTkt shows 3A WL 44 for Anand Vihar → Patliputra.
• LastBerth finds available-seat options across three legs of the same train:
  1. ANVT → PRYJ: 3A, AVL 17, ₹1,035
  2. PRYJ → DDU: 3A, AVL 12, ₹565
  3. DDU → PPTA: 2A, AVL 1, ₹770

Displayed total: ₹2,370.

That means separate tickets and a class/berth change, rather than one confirmed berth for the whole route. These are availability results, not issued tickets. Recheck all legs before booking; seats and fares can change.
