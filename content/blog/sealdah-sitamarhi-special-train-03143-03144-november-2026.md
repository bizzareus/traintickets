## Can You Travel Unreserved on This Special Train?

**Yes. The Eastern Railway announcement confirms the special carries General unreserved coaches alongside Sleeper and AC classes, so passengers can board the GS coaches with a regular unreserved ticket purchased through UTS or station counters without any IRCTC reservation.**

Unreserved travel on a festival special is the pragmatic choice for two groups: passengers traveling with bulky Chhath offerings who cannot risk a waitlisted reserved ticket, and standby travelers who missed the reservation window entirely. Keep three rules in mind:

- **Unreserved means General coaches only.** Sitting or sleeping in reserved Sleeper or AC coaches with a general ticket draws a flat **₹500 penalty plus fare difference** under Section 138 of the Railways Act, and enforcement is intensive during the ongoing October-November checking drive.
- **General coach seats fill fast.** Reach Sealdah platform early on November 11 or 18, because GS queues for Bihar-bound specials form hours before the 11:50 PM departure.
- **Counter waitlisted tickets behave differently.** A physical PRS counter ticket that remains waitlisted does not auto-cancel, and the TTE may accommodate you in Sleeper subject to availability, but an online waitlisted e-ticket is cancelled and refunded automatically. Our [waitlist travel rules guide](/blog/can-waiting-list-ticket-passenger-travel-in-train-rules-fines) covers the full comparison.

## What If Your Waitlisted Ticket on 03143 or 03144 Doesn't Confirm?

**Fully waitlisted online e-tickets that remain unconfirmed after the final chart are automatically cancelled by IRCTC and refunded to the original payment method, and passengers cannot board reserved coaches with them. Counter-booked waitlisted tickets stay valid for General coach travel but not for reserved berths.**

Plan your fallback before the chart drops rather than after:

- **Track the refund:** If your e-ticket auto-cancels, the refund initiates in the nightly reconciliation cycle. Track it with our [refund status guide](/blog/irctc-refund-status-check-track-ticket-refund); card refunds typically land in 3 to 5 business days.
- **Re-book Current Availability instead:** A cancelled WL e-ticket frees your budget for a CURR_AVBL berth at a 10% discount on the same special, which is a better outcome than boarding unreserved for a 15-hour journey.
- **Do not risk ticketless travel:** Traveling reserved classes without a valid ticket draws a minimum **₹250 penalty** up to the full fare plus fine under Section 138. During Chhath week, TTEs check every reserved coach with handheld terminals.
