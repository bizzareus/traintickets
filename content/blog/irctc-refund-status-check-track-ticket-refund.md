## How Do You Check IRCTC Refund Status Online in 2026?

**To check your IRCTC refund status online, log in to the official IRCTC website or Rail Connect app, navigate to My Transactions, and select Ticket Refund History. The portal displays your refund status, cancellation date, refund amount, and the official Bank Reference or Acquirer Reference Number (ARN) needed to verify bank settlement.**

Tracking your refund online gives you complete visibility into the settlement cycle between the Centre for Railway Information Systems (CRIS), the payment aggregator, and your issuing bank. Whether you cancelled a confirmed berth manually, had a failed booking transaction, or held an unconfirmed waitlisted e-ticket that was dropped at charting, every rupee is accounted for with a unique tracking ID.

Follow these direct steps to inspect your refund status:

1. **Log in to IRCTC:** Open `irctc.co.in` or the official IRCTC Rail Connect mobile application using your credentials.
2. **Access Transactions:** Tap **My Account** and open the **My Transactions** menu.
3. **Select Refund History:** Click on **Ticket Refund History** (or **Refund Status** for failed booking transactions).
4. **Locate Your PNR:** Find the relevant 10-digit PNR number or booking transaction date from the list.
5. **Verify Payment Details:** Review the status tag (**Refunded**, **Initiated**, or **Failed**), the net refund amount credited, and copy the **Bank Reference ID**.

If you booked your railway ticket through authorized third-party platforms such as MakeMyTrip, Ixigo, or ConfirmTkt, you can still check your PNR status on IRCTC. However, the actual monetary credit passes from IRCTC to the OTA gateway before reaching your bank, adding 24 to 48 hours to the timeline.

## How Many Days Does an IRCTC Refund Take for UPI, Net Banking, and Cards?

**An IRCTC ticket refund takes 1 to 3 working days for UPI, 3 to 5 working days for net banking, and 3 to 7 working days for debit or credit cards. IRCTC e-Wallet and iPay Autopay mandates process instantaneously or within 24 hours, while TDR claims require 15 to 45 working days for commercial audit approval.**

The processing duration depends entirely on your original payment mode rather than the railway reservation system. Once you hit cancel, IRCTC's backend system releases the funds during the nightly settlement batch. The money then moves through payment gateways (such as Razorpay, PayU, or IRCTC iPay) and the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) before reaching your bank branch.

The table below details the official refund timelines across all authorized payment channels in 2026:

| Payment Method | IRCTC Processing Window | Banking Settlement Timeline | Expected Total Turnaround | Tracking Identifier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **IRCTC e-Wallet** | Immediate (< 1 hour) | Instantaneous | Same day | Wallet Transaction ID |
| **IRCTC iPay (UPI Mandate / Single-Block)** | Same day | Instant to 24 hours | 1 business day | UPI UTR Number |
| **Standard BHIM UPI / GPay / PhonePe** | 24 hours | 1 to 3 working days | 2 to 4 business days | 12-digit UTR Number |
| **Net Banking (All Scheduled Banks)** | 24 to 48 hours | 3 to 5 working days | 3 to 6 business days | Bank Ref / Core Banking ID |
| **Debit Cards (RuPay / Visa / Mastercard)** | 24 to 48 hours | 3 to 7 working days | 4 to 8 business days | 23-digit ARN Number |
| **Credit Cards (Domestic & International)** | 24 to 48 hours | 3 to 7 working days | 1 to 2 billing cycles | 23-digit ARN Number |
| **Ticket Deposit Receipt (TDR) Claims** | 15 to 30 days | 5 to 15 working days | 15 to 45 working days | TDR Registration Number |

Weekends and public bank holidays do not count as working days for clearing houses. If you cancel a ticket on a Friday evening, the interbank clearing window only begins on Monday morning.

## What Does IRCTC Refund Status ₹0 or Initiated Mean?

**An IRCTC refund status showing Refund Amount ₹0 or Initiated means that your cancellation request is registered in the database, but the automated batch settlement engine has not yet finalized the net clerkage calculation and gateway payout. The zero figure updates to your exact refund amount during nightly CRIS reconciliation.**

Seeing a "Refund Amount: ₹0" message on your transaction screen can be alarming, but it is almost always a temporary database placeholder rather than a forfeiture of your ticket money. IRCTC handles millions of passenger queries simultaneously. To prevent payment gateway deadlocks, the frontend marks the ticket cancelled immediately and queues the financial ledger for batch execution.

Here is what the standard IRCTC refund status codes signify:

- **Initiated:** IRCTC has accepted your cancellation and forwarded the refund instruction to the acquiring payment gateway. The money is in transit between CRIS and the banking switch.
- **Refund Status ₹0:** The cancellation is recorded, but the commercial rules engine has not yet calculated the deduction slab (such as clerkage or percentage cancellation fee). This status updates between 11:30 PM and 01:00 AM IST during regular maintenance.
- **Refunded:** IRCTC and the payment gateway have successfully transferred the funds back to your card-issuing bank or UPI VPA. Your bank now holds the money.
- **Failed / Rejected:** The return transaction failed because the destination account is closed, the credit card expired, or the acquiring bank rejected the reversal packet.
- **Manual Refund:** The automated banking API failed repeatedly. IRCTC has assigned the case to an accounts officer for manual NEFT or RTGS processing.

If your status continues to display "₹0" for more than 48 hours after cancellation, verify your ticket type. Confirmed tickets cancelled less than 4 hours before scheduled train departure or after chart preparation are legally non-refundable under Indian Railways rules, which results in a genuine ₹0 refund.

## Why Did Your IRCTC Refund Fail and How Do You Fix It?

**An IRCTC refund fails when the receiving bank account is dormant, a credit card has expired, or temporary network timeouts occur between IRCTC and the banking switch. To fix a failed refund, submit a grievance with your PNR, Bank Ref ID, and updated account details to care@irctc.co.in or file a ticket on RailMadad 139.**

When an automated refund packet bounces back to IRCTC, the funds sit in a railway suspense account. CRIS never keeps failed refund money, but manual intervention is required to re-route the credit.

The table below breaks down the common refund failure triggers and their direct fixes:

| Failure Scenario | Root Cause | Primary Remedy | Resolution Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Expired or Blocked Card** | Issuing bank rejected credit to a deactivated card number. | Contact card issuer to transfer credit to your new card or bank account. | 3 to 5 business days |
| **Closed Bank Account** | The bank account used for net banking or debit card booking is closed. | Email IRCTC accounts department with cancelled cheque and NOC from old bank. | 7 to 14 business days |
| **UPI VPA Deactivated** | The UPI ID or mobile handle linked to the payment was deleted. | Bank routes credit to underlying account number via central NPCI mapper. | 3 to 5 business days |
| **Gateway Timeout (Status Failed)** | Network break during nightly batch settlement between bank and CRIS. | IRCTC re-attempts the refund automatically during the next settlement cycle. | 48 to 72 hours |
| **System Glitch During Tatkal Rush** | Severe server congestion leading to unmapped reversal response. | Log complaint on RailMadad portal under Refund of Unutilized Ticket category. | 2 to 4 business days |

Always check your bank statement directly rather than waiting for an SMS notification. Many private and public sector banks credit refund entries silently under generic merchant identifiers such as `IRCTC-PG`, `CRIS-DELHI`, or `ATOM-TECH` without triggering an automated text alert.

## How Do You Track an IRCTC Refund Using Bank Ref and ARN Number?

**To track an IRCTC refund with your bank, retrieve the 23-digit Acquirer Reference Number (ARN) or 12-digit UPI UTR number from the Ticket Refund History page, contact your bank support or relationship manager, and request a lookup on the merchant settlement desk.**

When an IRCTC representative tells you that a refund has been issued from their end, but your bank account balance shows no change, the Acquirer Reference Number (ARN) is your single most important proof. The ARN is an internationally recognized unique identifier assigned to a card transaction as it moves from the merchant's bank to the cardholder's bank.

Here is how you track and resolve stuck funds using reference numbers:

1. **Extract Reference Codes:** Open **Ticket Refund History** on the IRCTC portal and write down the **Bank Ref No** and **Refund Date**. For UPI transactions, note the 12-digit **UTR number**. For card payments, note the 23-digit **ARN**.
2. **Contact Bank Card Desk:** Call your bank customer care helpline. Ask specifically for the **Chargeback or Merchant Refund Desk**, not regular retail banking.
3. **Provide the ARN/UTR:** Quote the ARN or UTR number and the exact transaction amount. The bank executive can track whether the reversal packet is parked in the bank's internal nodal clearing pool.
4. **Escalate Uncredited Batches:** If the bank claims no record exists, raise a written dispute through your net banking portal under **Disputed Transactions - Refund Not Credited**. Attach a screenshot of your IRCTC refund confirmation screen.

Under Reserve Bank of India (RBI) mandates, banks must process inward refund files within definite turnarounds. Having an ARN eliminates finger-pointing between IRCTC and your bank.

## When Does IRCTC Refund Automatically Cancelled Waitlist (WL) Tickets?

**IRCTC automatically cancels fully waitlisted e-tickets upon final chart preparation (~4 hours before train departure) and initiates a refund within 24 hours. The railway refunds the entire fare back to the original payment source after deducting a flat clerkage fee of ₹60 plus GST per passenger.**

In Indian Railways reservation terminology, **WL full form is Waiting List**. The booking queue moves mathematically from **WL (Waiting List) → RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) → Confirmed**. When final charts are prepared, any passenger whose online e-ticket remains on the waiting list is automatically dropped from the passenger manifest. 

Here are the exact rules governing waitlist cancellations and refunds:

- **Zero Boarding Rights:** A dropped waitlisted e-ticket is completely invalid for travel. Boarding any reserved Sleeper or AC coach on a dropped e-ticket is treated as ticketless travel under Section 138 of the Railways Act, punishable by a minimum ₹500 fine plus single journey fare.
- **Automated Processing:** You do not need to cancel a fully waitlisted e-ticket manually or file a TDR. The central reservation system triggers the cancellation automatically.
- **Clerkage Deduction:** For unconfirmed waitlist e-tickets, Indian Railways deducts a flat administrative clerkage fee of **₹60 + GST per passenger** for Sleeper and Second Sitting, and **₹60 + GST** for AC classes. The remaining balance is refunded in full.
- **Train Cancellation Exception:** If the train itself is cancelled by Indian Railways due to floods, accidents, or operational issues, **100% of the fare is refunded with zero clerkage fee**.
- **Partially Confirmed PNR Exception:** If some passengers on your PNR get confirmed or RAC berths while others remain on WL, the ticket does not cancel automatically. To claim a refund for the waitlisted passengers, you must cancel them or file an online TDR at least 30 minutes before scheduled train departure.

If your waitlisted ticket just dropped at charting, do not panic about travel. Use [Chart Vacancy](/chart-vacancy) to view live unsold seats released on running coaches or look up historical charting trends on [Chart Times](/chart-times).

## How Long Does an IRCTC TDR Refund Take and How Is It Audited?

**An IRCTC Ticket Deposit Receipt (TDR) refund takes 15 to 45 working days to settle. TDR claims are not automated because they require manual physical verification and auditing by the Chief Commercial Manager (CCM - Refunds) of the destination Zonal Railway to verify passenger non-travel.**

A TDR is an official refund application filed when unusual circumstances prevent you from completing your scheduled rail journey. Common reasons include trains running late by more than 3 hours, coach AC failure, passenger illness, or railway diversions where you chose not to board.

The table below outlines the major TDR filing scenarios and their approval windows:

| TDR Filing Reason | Mandatory Filing Cutoff | Zonal Verification Authority | Settlement Window | Refund Eligibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Train Delayed > 3 Hours** | Before actual train departure | Station Superintendent / NTES Logs | 15 to 30 days | Full fare refunded (₹0 clerkage) |
| **AC Failure in 2A / 3A Coach** | Within 72 hours of arrival | TTE Certificate / Handheld Terminal Log | 30 to 45 days | Difference between AC and SL fare |
| **Party Partially Confirmed** | Up to 30 mins before train departure | CRIS Reservation Chart Audit | 15 to 30 days | Full fare for waitlisted passengers |
| **Improper Berth Allocation** | Within 72 hours of departure | Commercial Controller | 30 to 45 days | Subject to Zonal audit review |
| **Train Diverted / Short Terminated** | Within 72 hours of departure | Operating Department Circular | 15 to 30 days | Pro-rata fare for untraveled distance |

Unlike regular ticket cancellations, filing a false TDR or submitting a claim after the train departs can lead to outright rejection. Once approved by the Zonal Railway office, CRIS transmits the electronic payout advice to your original bank account within 3 to 5 business days.

## What Are Your Rights Under RBI Compensation Rules for Delayed Refunds?

**Under the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Harmonisation of Turnaround Time (TAT) circular, banks and payment aggregators must process failed transaction reversals within T+1 working days. If a failed railway booking refund is delayed beyond this timeline, the customer is entitled to compensation of ₹100 per calendar day of delay.**

When an IRCTC booking transaction debits money from your bank account or wallet but fails to generate a valid PNR, the transaction is governed by the RBI DPSS guidelines on failed electronic transactions. This compensation framework exists to ensure financial institutions resolve stuck payments promptly.

Key consumer protections established by the RBI include:

1. **Auto-Reversal Mandate (T+1 Rule):** For point-of-sale and e-commerce transactions where money is debited but the merchant system fails to confirm, the acquiring bank must auto-reverse the funds to the customer account within one working day (T+1).
2. **₹100 Per Day Compensation:** If the bank fails to credit the reversal within the prescribed turnaround time without a valid technical justification, it must pay compensation of **₹100 for each day of delay** directly into the customer's account without requiring a formal lawsuit.
3. **IRCTC vs Banking Scope:** This statutory compensation applies strictly to **failed booking transactions** where no ticket was created. For voluntary passenger cancellations or approved TDRs, standard railway refund processing timelines apply.
4. **How to Claim Compensation:** If your failed ticket money remains stuck for over a week, submit an escalation to your bank's Principal Nodal Officer quoting the RBI DPSS TAT circular. If unresolved within 30 days, file a complaint on the RBI Banking Ombudsman portal (`cms.rbi.org.in`).

Knowing these statutory timelines empowers passengers to hold financial intermediaries accountable whenever payment gateways freeze travel funds.

## How Do You Book a Confirmed Seat Immediately After Ticket Cancellation?

**When an IRCTC ticket is cancelled or drops at charting, you can immediately secure confirmed travel by searching Current Availability (CURR_AVBL) berths, splitting your journey into contiguous segments using Smart Seats, or booking unreserved coaches on the RailOne app.**

Losing a confirmed or waitlisted ticket does not mean your trip is cancelled. Indian Railways reserves dozens of berths across various quotas that are released to the public at different milestones before departure.

Use these four proven strategies to secure immediate confirmed travel:

1. **Book Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`):** After the first reservation chart is prepared (at least 4 hours before departure from origin, or 20:00 hrs the previous evening for morning departures), unallocated VIP, Defence, and Pooled Quotas convert into `CURR_AVBL`. These are **100% confirmed berths with coach and berth numbers**, sold at a **10% discount on base fare**, bookable on IRCTC or counters up to **30 minutes before departure**.
2. **Split-Ticket with Smart Seats:** When direct origin-to-destination tickets are sold out, [Finding Smart Seats](/) scans the train's entire station route to find confirmed contiguous berths on the same train (for instance, booking Seat 24 in Coach B2 from Station A to B, and Seat 42 in Coach B4 from Station B to C).
3. **Inspect Live Vacancies on Chart Vacancy:** Open the [Chart Vacancy Map](/chart-vacancy) to see physical empty berths across every coach on your train after chart preparation. You can spot berths that become vacant midway and request them directly from the onboard TTE.
4. **Set Preparation Alerts on Chart Times:** Check [Chart Preparation Times](/chart-times) to know the exact historical minute when your train's chart is finalized so you can log into IRCTC the moment `CURR_AVBL` seats drop.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### How can I check my railway refund status online?
You can check your railway refund status online by logging into the official IRCTC portal or Rail Connect mobile application, clicking on **My Account**, opening **My Transactions**, and selecting **Ticket Refund History**. The page displays your refund amount, cancellation date, and the unique Bank Reference ID or Acquirer Reference Number (ARN) for banking verification.

### Why is my IRCTC refund status showing as Refund Amount ₹0?
An IRCTC refund showing ₹0 is a temporary database placeholder indicating that your cancellation is accepted, but the backend CRIS rules engine has not yet finished calculating the clerkage fee and net payout. The zero amount automatically updates to your actual refund figure during nightly batch reconciliation between 11:30 PM and 01:00 AM IST.

### How long does an IRCTC refund take for Google Pay or PhonePe UPI?
An IRCTC refund for UPI transactions made via Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM takes between **1 and 3 working days** to reflect in your bank account. Once IRCTC releases the payment during its daily settlement, NPCI clears the funds directly to the underlying bank account linked to your Virtual Payment Address (VPA).

### What happens if an IRCTC refund is marked as Refunded but not received in bank?
If IRCTC marks your refund as Refunded but your bank statement does not reflect the credit within 7 business days, copy the 23-digit ARN or 12-digit UPI UTR number from your IRCTC refund history and contact your bank's merchant refund desk. The bank uses this unique reference code to track and release payments held in internal nodal clearing accounts.

### How much clerkage fee is deducted on auto-cancelled waitlist e-tickets?
Indian Railways deducts a flat administrative clerkage fee of **₹60 plus GST per passenger** on fully waitlisted e-tickets that fail to confirm and are automatically cancelled at chart preparation. The remaining fare balance is refunded automatically to your original payment source without requiring any manual cancellation.

### How many days does an IRCTC TDR refund take to get approved?
An IRCTC Ticket Deposit Receipt (TDR) refund takes between **15 and 45 working days** to process and credit to your account. Unlike automated ticket cancellations, TDR claims undergo manual verification and auditing by the Chief Commercial Manager (CCM - Refunds) of the destination Zonal Railway to confirm non-travel before releasing funds.

### What is the full form of WL in Indian Railways ticketing?
WL full form is **Waiting List**. It represents passengers who have booked a ticket on a fully occupied train and are placed in an electronic queue. As confirmed passengers cancel their journeys, waitlisted tickets progress mathematically through the queue: **WL (Waiting List) → RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) → Confirmed**.

### Can I travel on a train if my waitlist e-ticket is auto-cancelled?
No, you cannot travel on any train coach with an auto-cancelled waitlist e-ticket. Once chart preparation is complete, dropped waitlisted e-tickets are completely purged from the passenger manifest. Boarding a Sleeper or AC coach on a dropped ticket constitutes ticketless travel under Section 138 of the Railways Act, punishable by a minimum ₹500 fine plus the single journey fare.

### Do I get a full refund if a train is cancelled by Indian Railways?
Yes, if a train is cancelled by Indian Railways due to natural disasters, operational accidents, or weather disruptions, you receive a **100% full refund of the total ticket fare with zero clerkage fee**. For e-tickets, the system issues this refund automatically without requiring you to file a TDR or cancel manually.

### What should I do if my IRCTC refund status shows Failed?
If your refund status displays Failed, it usually indicates that your bank account is closed, your card has expired, or a transmission timeout occurred between payment switches. Email your 10-digit PNR, transaction ID, and bank account details to `care@irctc.co.in`, or log a formal service grievance on the official RailMadad portal at `railmadad.indianrailways.gov.in`.
