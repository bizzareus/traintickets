## What Are the Bangalore to Mumbai Vande Bharat Sleeper Train Timings?

**Train 27652 departs SMVT Bengaluru (SMVB) at 19:45 IST and reaches Mumbai CSMT at 11:55 IST the next morning, completing the 1,200 km run in 16 hours 10 minutes. In reverse, Train 27651 leaves Mumbai CSMT at 20:10 IST and arrives at SMVT Bengaluru at 12:00 IST afternoon in 15 hours 50 minutes.**

The introduction of the Vande Bharat Sleeper on this high-density southern trunk corridor marks a historic leap in overnight intercity transit. Historically, passengers commuting between Bengaluru and Mumbai depended on conventional mail/express rakes such as the Udyan Express (11301/11302) or the Chalukya Express, which require between 23 and 24 hours to cover the distance due to heavy freight saturation, single-line bottlenecks in central Karnataka, and banking operations on the Western Ghats. The semi-high-speed Vande Bharat Sleeper operates with dual-end aerodynamic cabs, rapid distributed traction acceleration, and 130 km/h operational capability, cutting travel time down to an effortless overnight trip.

Both train pairs operate on a dedicated 6-day weekly timetable to accommodate mandatory primary maintenance at their respective base depots. Train 27652 skips operations on Mondays from Bengaluru, while Train 27651 skips operations on Tuesdays from Mumbai CSMT.

## How Does Tatkal Booking and Chart Preparation Work for Train 27651 / 27652?

**Tatkal booking opens daily at 10:00 AM IST exactly 1 day prior to train origin departure for AC classes (3A and 2A). First reservation charts are prepared roughly 8 hours prior to train departure (between 11:30 AM and 13:00 IST for Train 27652; between 12:00 PM and 14:00 IST for Train 27651).**

Understanding the specific charting and Tatkal mechanics on this corridor is critical for business travelers seeking last-minute berths.

### Tatkal Rules & Surcharges
1. **Booking Window:** Because the train is composed entirely of air-conditioned sleeper coaches, Tatkal booking opens at **10:00 AM IST** one day prior to departure from the originating station. There is no 11:00 AM non-AC booking window for this train.
2. **Class Exclusions:** Tatkal quota is strictly available for **AC 3-Tier (3A)** and **AC 2-Tier (2A)**. Under Indian Railways commercial policies, **no Tatkal quota is allocated in First AC (1A)**.

4. **Mandatory Aadhaar Verification:** To combat automated booking scripts, IRCTC mandates dynamic Aadhaar OTP authentication during the opening 10:00 AM rush. Ensure your passenger Master List is pre-verified on your IRCTC profile before 09:55 AM.

## What Are the Baggage Allowances and Strict Boarding Rules?

**Passengers in 1A can carry up to 70 kg of personal luggage, 2A passengers can carry 50 kg, and 3A passengers can carry 40 kg. Boarding with a waitlisted e-ticket is strictly prohibited; under Section 138 of the Railways Act, violators face a mandatory ₹500 fine and removal at the next station.**

### Strict Boarding Rule: Confirmed Tickets Only

A critical regulatory standard governs all Vande Bharat Sleeper services:
1. **Fully Waitlisted E-Tickets Are Automatically Cancelled:** If your e-ticket remains on the waiting list (WL) after the preparation of the final reservation chart, the PNR is automatically cancelled by CRIS, and the ticket amount is refunded back to your bank account within 3 to 5 working days.
2. **No Boarding on Waitlisted E-Tickets:** Under Railway Board circulars and the Jan Vishwas Act, a passenger holding an auto-cancelled waitlist e-ticket has no legal right to board the train.
3. **Section 138 Penalty:** If found onboard any reserved coach with an unconfirmed e-ticket, the TTE cannot regularize your ticket. You will be penalized with a **flat fine of ₹500 plus the single fare difference from the boarding station**, and you will be deboarded at the next scheduled halt.
4. **Partially Confirmed Tickets:** On a group PNR where at least one passenger is confirmed or RAC while others remain on the waiting list, the waitlisted passengers may travel only if the TTE has vacant berths to allocate.

### Baggage & Permitted Dimensions

### 1. Smart Seats Split-Ticketing on the Same Train

Indian Railways partitions coach berths into multiple station quotas. Often, through-tickets from Bengaluru to Mumbai are fully booked under the General Quota (GNWL), while intermediate legs have plenty of vacant berths on the exact same train.

You remain on the identical train throughout the night. If the berth numbers differ between the two legs, you simply shift to your second assigned seat after Pune Junction at 08:40 AM.

### 2. Intermediate Quota Booking & Boarding Point Modification

If direct berths from intermediate halts show a waiting list or if booking downstream:
- If boarding at intermediate stops like **Anantapur (ATP)** or **Guntakal (GTL)** where local remote quotas are full, book from the originating terminal **SMVT Bengaluru (SMVB)** under the larger General Quota (GNWL).
- Change your official boarding point online on IRCTC up to the preparation of the second reservation chart (~30 to 45 minutes prior to departure) without paying any cancellation penalty.

### 3. Post-Chart Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`)

Between 11:30 AM and 13:00 IST on the day of departure, CRIS prepares the first reservation chart for Train 27652. Unutilized VIP, emergency, and quota berths are instantly released to the general public under **Current Availability**.
