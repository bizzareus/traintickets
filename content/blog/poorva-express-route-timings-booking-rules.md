Together, they offer daily Superfast connectivity covering up to 1,531 kilometers in 21 to 23 hours.**

Named after the eastern region of India ("Poorva" meaning East in Sanskrit and Hindi), Poorva Express was introduced as a premier broad-gauge link connecting eastern state capitals and river valleys to New Delhi. Operated under Eastern Railway (ER), Howrah Division, the train provides vital daily mobility for students, government personnel, business travelers, and pilgrims traveling between Kolkata, Asansol, Patna, Gaya, Varanasi, Prayagraj, Kanpur, and Delhi.

## When Is Poorva Express Chart Preparation Time?

**For morning departures from Howrah (12303 at 08:00 IST and 12381 at 08:15 IST), the first reservation chart is prepared the previous evening between 20:00 and 21:00 IST. For evening departures from New Delhi (12304 and 12382 at 17:40 IST), the first chart is prepared between 09:30 and 10:30 IST.**

Understanding reservation charting on Poorva Express is essential for travelers hoping to clear waitlisted tickets or grab last-minute vacant berths:

1. **Howrah Morning Departures (Trains 12303 and 12381):**
   - Under standard Indian Railways operating rules, passenger trains scheduled to depart before 14:00 (2:00 PM) have their first reservation charts prepared the preceding evening.
   - For 12303 (08:00 departure) and 12381 (08:15 departure), Howrah PRS finalizes the primary chart between **20:00 and 21:00 IST (8:00 PM to 9:00 PM) on the night prior to travel**.
   - As soon as the first chart completes, unallotted VIP, defense, and emergency quotas convert into **Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`)**. Passengers can book these seats online via IRCTC with a **statutory 10% base fare discount** right through the night.
   - The Second and Final Chart is printed at Howrah Junction **30 to 45 minutes prior to departure**, typically between 07:15 and 07:30 IST.

2. **New Delhi Evening Departures (Trains 12304 and 12382):**
   - For 17:40 IST departures from New Delhi, Northern Railway prepares the First Reservation Chart roughly **7 to 8 hours before departure**, generally between **09:30 AM and 10:30 AM IST** on the departure date.
   - The Second and Final Chart finalizes at New Delhi station approximately **30 to 45 minutes before departure** (around 16:55 to 17:10 IST).

3. **Remote Location Charting at Intermediate Stations:**
   - Important intermediate junctions like Patna (PNBE), Gaya (GAYA), Dhanbad (DHN), and Kanpur (CNB) hold Remote Location Waitlist (RLWL) quotas.
   - Local reservation charts for these intermediate stations are finalized separately roughly 4 hours prior to the train's scheduled arrival at that station. If you hold an RLWL ticket from Patna or Gaya, your seat may confirm hours after the train has already departed Howrah.

## What Are the Tatkal Booking Rules for Poorva Express?

**Tatkal bookings on Poorva Express open at 10:00 AM IST for AC classes (1A excluded, 2A, 3A, 3E) and at 11:00 AM IST for Sleeper class (SL) exactly one day prior to train departure from the originating station. Bookings are capped at two PNRs per user daily.**

Because Poorva Express serves busy business and festive corridors across Bengal, Bihar, and Delhi, Tatkal quotas exhaust within 60 to 180 seconds of opening. To improve your chances during booking rushes, follow these operational guidelines:

1. **The Origin Departure Rule:**
   - Tatkal ticket windows open based on the departure date from the train's originating station (Howrah or New Delhi).
   - If you plan to board at an intermediate station (like Patna, Gaya, Varanasi, or Kanpur), your Tatkal booking still opens one day prior to the train's origin departure. For example, because train 12303 departs Howrah on Friday morning and reaches Kanpur after midnight on Saturday, Tatkal for the Kanpur-to-Delhi leg opens on Thursday at 10:00 AM, not Friday.

2. **Tatkal Fares and Charges:**
   - Tatkal incurs a supplementary charge over the regular fare:
     * Executive / 2A: 30% of base fare (minimum ₹400, maximum ₹500)
     * 3A and 3E: 30% of base fare (minimum ₹300, maximum ₹400)
     * Sleeper Class (SL): 10% of base fare (minimum ₹100, maximum ₹200)

3. **No Refund on Confirmed Tatkal:**
   - Confirmed Tatkal cancellations yield **0% refund** under Railway Board rules.
   - If your Tatkal ticket remains in Tatkal Waiting List (TQWL) after final charting, it automatically cancels, and the full fare minus standard clerkage (₹60 + GST) is refunded to your bank account.

4. **Booking Speed Best Practices:**
   - Pre-save your travelers in the IRCTC Master List with verified Aadhaar credentials.
   - Synchronize your device clock with Indian Standard Time (IST).
   - Use UPI Intent payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) to bypass lengthy payment gateway OTP delays.

## How Does Food and Catering Work on Poorva Express?

**Poorva Express operates a dedicated onboard pantry car serving vegetarian and non-vegetarian breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks, and Rail Neer water at standard statutory IRCTC rates. Station e-Catering delivery is also available at 12 major transit junctions along the route.**

Unlike Rajdhani and Shatabdi trains where catering charges can be bundled into ticket fares, Poorva Express tickets do not include meals. Passengers pay for food as they order onboard:

1. **Standard Pantry Tariffs:**
   - **Packaged Drinking Water (Rail Neer):** Mandatory ₹15 for 1-liter sealed bottle. Never pay more than the printed MRP.
   - **Standard Morning Tea:** ₹10 per cup with dip bag.
   - **Vegetarian Breakfast (Bread & Cutlet / Upma / Idli-Vada):** ₹40.
   - **Non-Vegetarian Breakfast (Bread & 2-Egg Omelette):** ₹50.
   - **Standard Vegetarian Thali Meal:** ₹80 (Rice, Dal, Seasonal Vegetable Subzi, 4 Puris or 2 Parathas, Pickle).
   - **Standard Non-Vegetarian Meal (Egg Curry):** ₹90.

2. **IRCTC e-Catering at Transit Halts:**
   - If you prefer restaurant food from brands like Haldiram's, Domino's, or local specialties, you can place orders through the official IRCTC e-Catering app (Food on Track) using your 10-digit PNR.
   - Excellent e-Catering delivery stations for Poorva Express include:
     * **Asansol Junction (ASN):** Bengali meals, snacks, and sweets.
     * **Dhanbad Junction (DHN):** North Indian thalis and snacks.

     * **Kanpur Central (CNB):** Biryanis, chaat, and North Indian curries.

Always demand a printed electronic bill from the pantry vendor. Overcharging on food or water can be reported instantly to the Railway Board via the RailMadad portal or by dialing 139.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### What is the difference between train 12303 and 12381 Poorva Express?
Train 12303 runs four days a week (Monday, Tuesday, Friday, Saturday) from Howrah to New Delhi via Patna Junction over a distance of 1,531 km. Train 12381 runs three days a week (Sunday, Wednesday, Thursday) over the Grand Chord route via Dhanbad, Gaya Junction, Varanasi, and Lucknow, covering 1,452 km. Both share identical coach layouts and terminate at New Delhi.

### When does the chart get prepared for Poorva Express from Howrah?
Because both 12303 (08:00 departure) and 12381 (08:15 departure) depart Howrah Junction in the morning, their first reservation charts are prepared the previous evening between 20:00 and 21:00 IST. The final second chart is prepared 30 to 45 minutes before departure on the morning of travel.

### When does the chart get prepared for Poorva Express from New Delhi?
For trains 12304 and 12382 departing New Delhi at 17:40 IST, the first reservation chart is finalized between 09:30 AM and 10:30 AM IST on the day of departure. The final chart is released approximately 30 to 45 minutes before departure around 17:00 IST.

### Can I travel on Poorva Express with an online waiting list ticket?
No. Passengers holding fully waitlisted online e-tickets cannot board reserved Sleeper or AC coaches. IRCTC automatically cancels fully waitlisted e-tickets during chart preparation and refunds the fare. Boarding with an unconfirmed e-ticket attracts a flat penalty of ₹500 under Section 138 plus full fare to the next station.

### Does Poorva Express have dynamic Flexi-Fare pricing?
No. Unlike Rajdhani, Shatabdi, and Duronto trains, Poorva Express does not use dynamic Flexi-Fare pricing on AC 2-Tier or AC 3-Tier. Fares remain fixed according to standard Indian Railways Superfast distance slabs, making it a cost-effective alternative between Kolkata and Delhi.

### Does Poorva Express have a pantry car attached?

### What is the Tatkal booking time for Poorva Express?
Tatkal booking opens at 10:00 AM IST for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E) and at 11:00 AM IST for Sleeper class (SL) exactly one day prior to the train's departure from its originating station (Howrah or New Delhi).

### How can I get a confirmed seat on Poorva Express if it is waitlisted?
When direct tickets are waitlisted, check for continuous split seats using LastBerth Smart Seats on the same train. You can also monitor 10% discounted Current Availability (CURR_AVBL) berths on IRCTC right after the first chart is prepared at 20:00 to 21:00 IST the previous evening.

### What are the major halts of Poorva Express in Bihar?
On the Patna route (12303/12304), major halts include Jhajha, Jamui, Kiul, Mokama, Barh, Bakhtiyarpur, Patna Junction, Danapur, Ara, and Buxar. On the Gaya route (12381/12382), key stops include Koderma, Gaya Junction, RafiGanj, Anugraha Narayan Road, Dehri On Sone, Sasaram, and Bhabua Road.

### How do I check vacant berths on Poorva Express after charting?
You can view real-time coach-by-coach vacant seats using the LastBerth Chart Vacancy map. It shows all unallotted berths released into the general pool post-charting so you can book them on IRCTC before the train departs.
