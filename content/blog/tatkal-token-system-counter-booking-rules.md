## What is the new Tatkal token system at railway counters?

**The new Tatkal token system is a queue management rule introduced by Indian Railways on August 1, 2026, for physical booking counters (PRS). Passengers lining up for Tatkal tickets must collect a physical time-stamped token between 8:30 AM and 9:30 AM. Tickets are then issued strictly according to token numbers when counters open.**

This physical token system prevents queue jumping, overnight counter crowding, and agent extortion at major railway reservation offices across India.

Here is how the counter token allocation operates:

1. **AC Class Tokens (8:30 AM – 9:00 AM):** Passengers seeking Tatkal berths in Executive Class, 2AC, 3AC, 3E, EC, or AC Chair Car must collect their token between 8:30 AM and 9:00 AM.
2. **Non-AC Class Tokens (9:00 AM – 9:30 AM):** Passengers booking Sleeper Class (SL) or Second Seating (2S) Tatkal tickets collect tokens between 9:00 AM and 9:30 AM.
3. **Strict Numbered Order:** When the official Tatkal window opens (10:00 AM for AC and 11:00 AM for Non-AC), counter officials service buyers in exact numerical order of their issued tokens.
4. **One Token Per Person:** Each individual in the physical queue receives only one token and can book up to a maximum of 4 passengers on a single Tatkal requisition form.

## What time are Tatkal tokens distributed at railway station counters?

**Tatkal tokens are distributed at physical PRS counters starting at 8:30 AM for AC classes and 9:00 AM for Non-AC classes, one day prior to train departure. Counter staff stop issuing tokens 30 minutes before booking opens to finalize the queue list.**

Below is the official timeline for counter token collection versus actual booking execution:

| Travel Class | Token Collection Window | Counter Booking Opens | Max Passengers / Form |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AC Classes (2A, 3A, 3E, EC, CC)** | **8:30 AM – 9:00 AM** | **10:00 AM** | 4 Passengers |
| **Non-AC Classes (SL, 2S)** | **9:00 AM – 9:30 AM** | **11:00 AM** | 4 Passengers |

If you arrive after the token distribution window closes (e.g. at 9:45 AM for AC Tatkal), you will only be served after all token-holding passengers ahead of you have completed their transactions.

## How does Tatkal Waiting List (TQWL) work in 2026?

**Tatkal Waiting List (TQWL) is a separate waiting queue for tickets booked under the Tatkal quota. TQWL status clears only when another passenger with a confirmed Tatkal ticket cancels their booking. Because confirmed Tatkal tickets carry zero refund upon cancellation, TQWL confirmation odds are extremely low.**

When navigating Tatkal waitlists, passengers should keep these core rules in mind:

- **WL full form is Waiting List.** The waitlist queue moves from **WL (Waiting List) → RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) → Confirmed**.
- **Automatic E-ticket Cancellation:** If an online Tatkal e-ticket remains in TQWL status after the final reservation chart is prepared (~8 hours before departure), the system automatically cancels the ticket.
- **Refund Policy:** The base ticket fare (full refund with no cancellation or clerkage fee deducted) is auto-refunded to your bank account within 3–5 working days. Tatkal premium charges are non-refundable.
- **No Train Boarding:** Boarding a train with an unconfirmed online TQWL e-ticket is illegal. TTEs will treat you as travelling without a ticket and impose standard railway penalties.

If your Tatkal ticket lands high on the waiting list, do not rely solely on confirmation. Search split-journey options on [Smart Seats](/) to find confirmed seats on adjacent legs of the same train.

## How to prepare for online Tatkal booking with Aadhaar OTP

**To book online Tatkal tickets successfully without facing session logouts, you must link your Aadhaar card to your IRCTC profile, add your passengers to the IRCTC Master List in advance, and keep your Aadhaar-registered phone handy for instant OTP entry.**

Follow this pre-booking preparation guide:

1. **Link Profile with Aadhaar:** Log into IRCTC, navigate to *My Account → Link Your Aadhaar*, and verify your 12-digit number via mobile OTP.
2. **Verify Master List Passengers:** Save your travel companions in *My Master List* under profile settings. Ensure a green "Verified" checkmark appears next to each name.
3. **Log in 5 Minutes Early:** Sign into the IRCTC app or portal at 9:55 AM for AC Tatkal or 10:55 AM for Non-AC Tatkal.
4. **Select Master List at Checkout:** During booking, fetch passenger details directly from the Master List instead of typing manually to bypass delays.
5. **Complete Aadhaar OTP:** Promptly enter the verification OTP sent to your registered mobile number during the payment flow.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### What is the new Tatkal token system at railway station counters?

The new Tatkal token system is a queue management process implemented at physical Indian Railways reservation counters (PRS). It requires buyers to collect a physical numbered token during specific morning hours (8:30–9:00 AM for AC; 9:00–9:30 AM for Non-AC) before booking begins. Counter staff then service passengers strictly in numerical token order when booking opens.

### Does the new Tatkal token system apply to online IRCTC bookings?

No, the token system applies exclusively to physical counter bookings at station PRS centers. Online bookings made via the IRCTC website or Rail Connect app do not use tokens; instead, online Tatkal bookings require mandatory Aadhaar OTP authentication during login and checkout.

### What time are AC Tatkal tokens given at station counters?

Tokens for AC classes (2AC, 3AC, 3E, EC, and AC Chair Car) are issued between 8:30 AM and 9:00 AM at railway reservation counters. Counter booking for AC Tatkal tickets officially opens at 10:00 AM sharp.

### What time are Sleeper class Tatkal tokens distributed?

Tokens for Non-AC classes, including Sleeper (SL) and Second Sitting (2S), are distributed between 9:00 AM and 9:30 AM at PRS counters. Counter booking for Sleeper Tatkal opens at 11:00 AM sharp.

### Can one person collect multiple Tatkal tokens at the counter?

No, counter staff issue only one token per person standing in the physical queue. A single token holder is permitted to submit one Tatkal requisition form booking up to a maximum of 4 passengers.

### What happens if your Tatkal ticket remains in TQWL waiting list?

If an online Tatkal e-ticket remains on the Tatkal Waiting List (TQWL) after final chart preparation (~8 hours before departure), it is automatically cancelled by the IRCTC system. The base fare (full refund of fare with no cancellation or clerkage fee deducted) is automatically refunded to your original payment account. You cannot board the train on a waitlisted e-ticket.

### Are Tatkal tickets refundable if cancelled by the passenger?

No refund is granted on the cancellation of confirmed Tatkal tickets. If a waitlisted Tatkal ticket (TQWL) is cancelled prior to chart preparation, standard clerkage charges are deducted and the remaining balance is refunded.
