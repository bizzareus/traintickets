## TL;DR

- **Automatic Fare Difference Refund:** If you travel in the re-allotted 3E (AC 3-Tier Economy) coach, IRCTC automatically calculates and refunds the fare difference to your original payment mode within 3 to 5 business days. You do not need to file a TDR if you complete the journey.
- **100% Full Refund on Refusal:** If you refuse to travel in 3E due to cramped space or the Side Middle berth, you are entitled to a full refund with zero cancellation charges or clerkage fee deductions.
- **Cancellation Cutoff:** To claim a 100% full refund online, you must cancel your e-ticket before the preparation of the **second reservation chart** (roughly 30 to 45 minutes before train departure).
- **TDR Deadline (Post-Chart / Train Departure):** If you decide not to travel after the second chart prepares or after train departure, you must file a TDR online within **3 hours after the actual departure time** of the train, selecting the reason *"Passenger not travelled due to ticket class downgraded"*.
- **Counter / PRS Tickets:** If you hold a physical paper PRS counter ticket, you must obtain a Lower Class Certificate from the TTE during the journey or surrender the ticket at the station PRS refund counter within the stipulated timeframe.

## Why Did IRCTC Change Your 3AC Coach to 3E Economy?

**IRCTC re-allots passengers from standard 3AC (AC 3-Tier) to 3E (AC 3-Tier Economy) when Indian Railways replaces an ICF or regular LHB rake with a newly inducted 3E rake, or when an operational coach breakdown forces the railway zone to swap a damaged 72-berth B-series coach with an 83-berth M-series coach.**

Indian Railways has been systematically upgrading rolling stock across express and superfast routes. Regular 3A coaches feature 72 berths (in LHB rakes) with two bunks on the side aisle (Side Lower and Side Upper). In contrast, 3E coaches accommodate 83 berths by adding a third vertical bunk (Side Middle) and reducing the bay pitch. 

When a rake composition is modified due to rake availability or maintenance at the origin yard:
1. The Passenger Reservation System (PRS) automatically maps passengers from B coaches (such as B1, B2, B3) into M coaches (such as M1, M2, M3).
2. An automated SMS alert is dispatched to the primary passenger mobile number stating that coach and berth allocations have been adjusted.
3. Because 3E carries a lower tariff than 3A, the system flags the PNR for fare adjustment.

To inspect whether your coach code begins with B or M before reaching the platform, enter your train number in [Coach Journey Lookup](/seat-status).

## Do You Get an Automatic Refund If You Travel in 3E?

**Yes, IRCTC automatically processes the fare difference between 3AC and 3E directly back to your original booking payment method if you travel in the re-allotted coach. The refund is calculated based on the base fare difference and normally credits within 3 to 5 working days after chart preparation.**

When the second reservation chart is finalized and the Travelling Ticket Examiner (TTE) syncs the Handheld Terminal (HHT), IRCTC automated refund pipeline reconciles the ticket. 

The fare adjustment includes:
- **Base Fare Differential:** The difference between the 3AC base fare and the 3E base fare for the exact distance travelled.
- **Proportional GST Adjustment:** 5% Goods and Services Tax on the differential amount is refunded.
- **Retained Components:** Reservation fees, superfast charges, and IRCTC convenience charges remain identical and are not refunded.

Passengers who travel do not need to file an online TDR or lodge a complaint on RailMadad to receive this base difference. The transaction appears on your bank statement or card bill with the reference *"IRCTC REFUND"*.

## Can You Cancel for a 100% Full Refund If You Refuse to Travel in 3E?

**Yes, Indian Railways commercial rules explicitly grant passengers the right to a 100% full refund with zero cancellation fee or clerkage deduction if their booked class is downgraded and they decline to travel. You must cancel your ticket before the second chart is prepared.**

Many travellers choose not to travel in 3E because of restricted headroom, cramped bay width, or assignment to a claustrophobic Side Middle (SM) bunk. Under the official IRCTC alert and Railway Board directives:
- Normal cancellation penalty slabs (such as 25% or 50% deductions within 48 to 12 hours) do **not** apply to involuntary downgrades.
- Flat clerkage charges (normally ₹60 + GST per passenger on cancelled waitlists or RAC tickets) are completely waived.
- Full fare, including base fare, reservation fee, superfast charge, and GST, is refunded in full. Only the non-refundable IRCTC booking convenience fee (₹15 to ₹30) is retained.

The critical factor is timing: you must execute the cancellation before the **second reservation chart** is prepared. Once the second chart is frozen at the originating station or charting hub, the standard online cancellation button is disabled.

## How to File a TDR If You Decide Not to Travel After Second Charting?

**If you decide not to travel after the second reservation chart has been finalized, you must file an online Ticket Deposit Receipt (TDR) on IRCTC within 3 hours after the actual departure time of the train, selecting the reason "Passenger not travelled due to ticket class downgraded".**

Follow this step-by-step procedure to file your TDR correctly:

1. **Log in to IRCTC:** Open `irctc.co.in` or the IRCTC Rail Connect app on your smartphone.
2. **Navigate to Booked Tickets:** Go to **My Account** > **My Transactions** > **Booked Ticket History**.
3. **Select Your PNR:** Click on the journey where the coach was re-allotted from 3AC to 3E.
4. **Initiate TDR Filing:** Select **File TDR** from the transaction menu.
5. **Select the Mandatory Downgrade Reason:** From the dropdown menu, select:
   - *"Passenger not travelled due to ticket class downgraded"* (if you are cancelling the journey entirely).
   - Alternatively, select *"Difference of fare as passenger travelled in lower class"* (only if you travelled but the automated difference was not credited).
6. **Submit Confirmation:** Check the box next to affected passenger names and submit. IRCTC generates a unique TDR Reference Number.

> [!WARNING]
> The TDR filing window for an involuntary downgrade closes strictly **3 hours after the actual departure time** of the train from your boarding station. If you miss this 3-hour cutoff, your claim will be rejected by the zonal railway audit office.

## How Does the Refund Process Differ for Counter (PRS) Tickets?

**For physical PRS counter tickets, refunds cannot be processed automatically online. Passengers must approach the station reservation counter before departure or obtain a Lower Class Certificate from the onboard TTE and submit a physical TDR within the prescribed time limits.**

If you purchased a physical paper ticket from an Indian Railways booking counter:

### Option A: Refusing to Travel Before Train Departure
- Take your original counter ticket to any Computerized Reservation System (PRS) counter at the station.
- Inform the booking supervisor that your 3AC ticket was downgraded to 3E.
- The counter clerk will verify the coach swap in the PRS database and hand you a 100% cash refund with zero cancellation charges before chart preparation.

### Option B: Travelling in 3E with a Counter Ticket
- When the Travelling Ticket Examiner (TTE) inspects your ticket in the 3E coach, inform them that you were downgraded from 3AC.
- Request the TTE to issue a **Lower Class Certificate (LCC)** or an Excess Fare Ticket (EFT) receipt acknowledging the downgrade.
- Within 3 days (72 hours) of train arrival at your destination, present the original counter ticket and the TTE certificate to the PRS refund counter at the destination station to collect your cash fare difference.

## What Happens If You Are Allotted a Side Middle (SM) Berth?

**If your 3AC reservation is re-allotted to a Side Middle (SM) berth in a 3E coach, you have been assigned a 3-tier side bunk that does not exist in standard 3AC. You can travel and collect your automatic fare difference, or cancel for a full refund before second charting.**

In regular 3AC (B coaches), the side aisle has only two bunks:
- **Side Lower (SL)**
- **Side Upper (SU)**

In 3E Economy (M coaches), railway coaches have 83 berths, featuring:
- **Side Lower (SL)**
- **Side Middle (SM)**
- **Side Upper (SU)**

The Side Middle bunk folds against the partition wall during the day and provides sleeping room between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM. If you find this bunk unsuitable due to medical conditions, height restrictions, or mobility issues:
1. Speak to the onboard TTE immediately upon boarding. Ask if any regular 3AC berths are vacant due to passenger no-shows.
2. Check available vacant berths in real time using [Chart Vacancy](/chart-vacancy).
3. If no alternative berths are available and you decline to occupy the Side Middle bunk, deboard before the train departs and file an online TDR within 3 hours.

## What Should You Do If the Difference Refund Is Not Credited?

**If your automated fare difference refund is not credited within 5 business days after completing your journey in 3E, file an online TDR under the reason "Difference of fare as passenger travelled in lower class" or log a complaint with your PNR on RailMadad.**

While IRCTC automated reconciliation handles most coach swap refunds without manual intervention, glitches occasionally occur if the TTE fails to synchronize their Handheld Terminal properly. 

Follow these escalation steps:
1. **Check Refund Status on IRCTC:** Go to **My Account** > **My Transactions** > **Ticket Refund History** on the IRCTC portal to verify if a refund transaction was generated.
2. **File an Online TDR (Within 72 Hours):** If no refund is listed, file a TDR on the website under *"Difference of fare as passenger travelled in lower class"*.
3. **Lodge a RailMadad Grievance:** Visit `railmadad.indianrailways.gov.in` or dial **139**. Choose *Grievance > Ticketing & Refund > Refund of Fare Difference*. Provide your 10-digit PNR number, boarding date, and allocated 3E coach number.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### Can Indian Railways downgrade my seat without my consent?
Yes, Indian Railways reserves operational authority under Railway Passenger Tariff rules to alter coach compositions, substitute rake types, or re-allot berths due to mechanical faults, rake unavailability, or safety considerations. However, the railway must compensate you by either refunding the fare difference or providing a 100% refund if you refuse travel.

### Does the "Auto Upgradation" checkbox cause 3AC tickets to be downgraded to 3E?
No, the "Consider for Auto Upgradation" feature on IRCTC is strictly unidirectional upwards. It can only elevate your ticket to a superior class (such as 3AC to 2AC, or 3E to 3AC) without extra charges. It will never downgrade your booking. 3AC to 3E re-allotment happens exclusively due to physical rake substitutions by the railway administration.

### How much time do I have to file a TDR if I refuse to travel after chart preparation?
You have strictly **3 hours after the actual departure time of the train** from your scheduled boarding station to file an online TDR. If you file after the 3-hour window expires, the system will block the submission and your refund claim will be permanently forfeited.

### Will I get free food on Rajdhani or Duronto if downgraded to 3E?
Yes, if you booked a 3AC ticket on a train with mandatory catering (such as Rajdhani, Duronto, or August Kranti Express) and your coach is swapped to 3E, your complimentary prepaid meals will be served without disruption. The catering charges included in your ticket remain valid.

### Do I get free bedrolls and linen in 3E Economy coaches?
Yes, complimentary bedroll kits (two bedsheets, one pillow with cover, one blanket, and a face towel) are provided to all 3E passengers. While Indian Railways briefly unbundled bedrolls in 2021, current policy bundles linen directly into the 3E ticket tariff.

### Can the TTE force me to pay extra if I am shifted to another coach?
No, an involuntary downgrade or rake swap can never attract excess fare charges from the passenger. If a TTE demands money during an involuntary coach swap, note their name and badge number, request an official receipt, and report the incident immediately on RailMadad or by calling 139.

### What if one passenger in my group wants to travel in 3E and another wants to cancel?
On IRCTC e-tickets with multiple passengers, you can perform a partial cancellation before second chart preparation. Select only the passengers who decline to travel in 3E and proceed with cancellation to claim their 100% refund, while allowing the remaining travellers to continue on the journey.

### Can I upgrade back to 2AC on board if 3AC was downgraded to 3E?
Yes, if vacant berths exist in 2AC or 1AC, you can approach the onboard TTE. You can check live vacant berths after chart preparation on [Chart Vacancy](/chart-vacancy). The TTE will calculate the fare difference between your original 3AC payment and the higher class, issue an Excess Fare Ticket (EFT), and reallocate your seat.

### What is the difference between B coaches and M coaches?
B coaches (such as B1, B2, B3) designate standard AC 3-Tier coaches with 72 berths and 2 tiers on the side aisle. M coaches (such as M1, M2, M3) designate AC 3-Tier Economy coaches with 83 berths and 3 tiers on the side aisle, including a Side Middle bunk.

If the second chart has already been prepared, immediately file an online TDR within **3 hours of train departure** selecting *"Passenger not travelled due to ticket class downgraded"*.
