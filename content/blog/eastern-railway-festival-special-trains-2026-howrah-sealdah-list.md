## Which Festival Special Trains Has Eastern Railway Announced for 2026?

**Eastern Railway and South Eastern Railway have notified over 500 festival special train trips for Durga Puja, Diwali, and Chhath Puja 2026, originating from Howrah, Sealdah, Shalimar, Asansol, and Malda Town. These 0-series services connect West Bengal to North Bengal, Assam, Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, Delhi, and Odisha between October and November 2026.**

The festive period spanning Durga Puja, Kali Puja, Diwali, and Chhath Puja triggers intense rail travel demand across Eastern India. Millions of commuters, students, and holiday travelers travel between Kolkata and towns in Bihar, Jharkhand, Uttar Pradesh, and Assam, while thousands head to holiday destinations like Darjeeling, Dooars, and Puri.

To absorb this rush, Eastern Railway (ER) and South Eastern Railway (SER) deploy special rakes across multiple key corridors:

- **Northern Trunk Lines:** Express services connecting Kolkata Terminal and Asansol with Kanpur Central, Agra Cantt, VGL Jhansi, and Anand Vihar Terminal.
- **Odisha & Coastal Links:** Regular weekly and bi-weekly special runs between Sealdah/Shalimar and Puri.

These trains carry 0-series train numbers (such as 03005, 03043, and 03101) and include combinations of AC 2-Tier, AC 3-Tier, AC 3-Tier Economy (3E), Sleeper Class (SL), and unreserved General Second Class (GS) coaches.

## How Are Fares Calculated on Eastern Railway 0-Series Festival Specials?

**Fares on Eastern Railway 0-series festival special trains follow Trains on Special Fares (TOSF) regulations, adding a flat 10% surcharge on Second Sitting (2S) and a 30% surcharge on Sleeper Class and all AC tiers (3E, 3A, 2A, 1A) over regular Mail and Express base fares.**

Because running special trains requires empty rake positioning, additional crew rostering, and special path scheduling, Indian Railways levies a special fare surcharge. This surcharge mirrors Tatkal minimum and maximum limits, so a Sleeper special ticket costs approximately ₹100 to ₹200 more than a standard train, while a 3AC special costs ₹300 to ₹400 extra.

The table below details the fare structure and rules for 0-series special trains:

| Travel Class | Special Surcharge Rate | Minimum Surcharge Cap | Maximum Surcharge Cap | Concession Eligibility | Bedroll Inclusions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Second Sitting (2S)** | **10% of Base Fare** | ₹10 | ₹15 | No Concessions (₹0) | Not Provided |
| **Sleeper Class (SL)** | **30% of Base Fare** | ₹100 | ₹200 | No Concessions (₹0) | Not Provided |
| **AC 3-Tier Economy (3E)** | **30% of Base Fare** | ₹250 | ₹350 | No Concessions (₹0) | Full Bedroll Included |
| **AC 3-Tier (3A)** | **30% of Base Fare** | ₹300 | ₹400 | No Concessions (₹0) | Full Bedroll Included |
| **AC 2-Tier (2A)** | **30% of Base Fare** | ₹400 | ₹500 | No Concessions (₹0) | Full Bedroll Included |
| **AC First Class (1A)** | **30% of Base Fare** | ₹400 | ₹500 | No Concessions (₹0) | Full Bedroll Included |

Important commercial conditions for 0-series specials:
- **Zero Fare Concessions:** Concessions for senior citizens, students, or patients do not apply to special trains.
- **Fixed Pricing Structure:** Unlike Suvidha trains which use dynamic 20% slab increases up to 3x, standard 0-series TOSF specials use a fixed 10% to 30% surcharge.
- **Linen Inclusions:** All AC classes include complete bedrolls (two bedsheets, one pillow, one blanket, and one towel). Sleeper class does not provide linen.

## What Are the Tatkal and Cancellation Refund Rules for ER Special Trains?

**Tatkal quota on notified Eastern Railway special trains opens at 10:00 AM IST for AC coaches and 11:00 AM IST for Sleeper class one day before departure, while unconfirmed waitlisted e-tickets receive an automatic 100% full refund with zero clerkage fee deductions.**

### Tatkal Rules on Special Trains
- Tatkal quotas are enabled on specific 0-series trains based on Zonal Railway notifications.
- When active, Tatkal bookings open at **10:00 AM IST** for AC classes (3A, 2A, 3E) and **11:00 AM IST** for Sleeper class, exactly one day before the train departs from its originating station.
- **Aadhaar Verification:** Booking Tatkal and Premium Tatkal tickets on the IRCTC portal requires an Aadhaar-linked profile during peak morning hours.

### Cancellation & Refund Policy
Cancellation charges on confirmed special train tickets follow official Indian Railways guidelines:

| Cancellation Timing (Before Scheduled Train Departure) | Cancellation Deduction Policy |
|---|---|
| **More than 48 hours** | Flat cancellation fee: ₹240 (1A/EC), ₹200 (2A), ₹180 (3A/3E/CC), ₹120 (SL), ₹60 (2S) |
| **Between 48 hours and 12 hours** | **25% of total ticket fare** (subject to minimum flat fee per passenger) |
| **Between 12 hours and 4 hours** (or before 1st chart prep) | **50% of total ticket fare** (subject to minimum flat fee per passenger) |
| **Less than 4 hours / After chart preparation** | **Zero refund (100% fare forfeited)** |
| **Waitlisted E-Ticket Dropped Post-Charting** | **100% Full Refund** automatically credited with **zero clerkage fee** |
| **Train Cancelled by Railways** | **100% Full Refund** credited automatically without filing a TDR |
| **Train Delayed Over 3 Hours** | **100% Full Refund** by filing an online TDR before train leaves boarding station |

Under Section 138 of the Railways Act (as amended by the Jan Vishwas Act), passengers holding fully waitlisted online e-tickets cannot board reserved coaches. Doing so incurs a flat **₹500 penalty** plus single journey fare, followed by deboarding at the next station.

## How Can You Get Confirmed Tickets When Howrah and Sealdah Trains Show Regret or Waiting List?

**When Eastern Railway trains show Regret or deep waitlists, passengers can secure confirmed berths by booking split-segment journeys on LastBerth, using originating station booking with online boarding modification, or monitoring post-charting Current Availability.**

Waiting lists follow a structured progression: **WL (Waiting List full form) → RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) → Confirmed**. General Waiting List (`GNWL`) carries higher confirmation chances than Pooled Quota (`PQWL`) or Remote Location (`RLWL`). When quotas fill completely, the train enters `REGRET` status, locking further reservations.

Here are four proven tactics to find confirmed berths:

### 1. Split-Segment Smart Seats
Indian Railways allocates seats based on station quotas. Even if a direct ticket from Howrah to New Jalpaiguri is marked `REGRET`, seats may be fully available from Howrah to Malda Town and Malda Town to New Jalpaiguri on the exact same train.

Use [Finding Smart Seats](/) on LastBerth to search contiguous confirmed segments across your journey, letting you travel on a single train without changing rakes.

### 2. Origin Station Booking with 30-Minute Boarding Modification
Intermediate station quotas (`RLWL`) are limited and exhaust rapidly. Originating stations receive the largest `GNWL` pool. You can book from Howrah or Sealdah to your destination, and then change your boarding station online on IRCTC up to the preparation of the second reservation chart (~30 minutes before departure).

### 3. Track Chart Preparation Schedules & Alerts
First reservation charts are finalized at least **4 hours before departure** (or 8:00 PM the previous evening for trains departing before 2:00 PM). Use [Chart Times](/chart-times) to look up historical chart finalization schedules for your train and configure free alerts to know the moment charts release.

### 4. Check Vacant Berths on Chart Vacancy Map
Once charts are prepared, unallocated VIP quotas, cancelled tickets, and vacant berths are released into Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`) at a 10% discount. Check the [Chart Vacancy Map](/chart-vacancy) to inspect coach layouts and book confirmed berths online up to 30 minutes before train departure. To check vacant berths during a running journey, use [Seat Status](/seat-status).

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### When does booking open for Eastern Railway 0-series festival special trains?
Booking for regular trains opens daily at 8:00 AM IST 60 days before the journey date, while Eastern Railway 0-series festival special trains open on rolling schedules of 10 to 30 days before departure as soon as Zonal Railway notifications are issued.

### Are tickets on Eastern Railway festival specials more expensive than normal trains?
Yes, 0-series festival special trains operate under Trains on Special Fares (TOSF) rules, carrying a flat 10% surcharge on Second Sitting (2S) and a 30% surcharge on Sleeper and AC classes compared to regular express base fares.

### Does Tatkal quota exist on Howrah and Sealdah festival special trains?
Tatkal quota is available only on specific special trains notified by Eastern Railway. When enabled, it opens at 10:00 AM IST for AC classes and 11:00 AM IST for Sleeper class one day prior to departure from the originating station.

### What happens if my festival special train e-ticket remains waitlisted after charting?
If your e-ticket remains on the waiting list after final chart preparation, IRCTC cancels it automatically and issues a 100% full refund to your original payment account with zero clerkage fee deductions.

### Can I travel in Sleeper or 3AC on a waitlisted e-ticket during Durga Puja?
No, boarding a reserved Sleeper or AC coach on a waitlisted e-ticket is strictly prohibited. Passengers caught travelling without a confirmed seat face a ₹500 fine under Section 138 of the Railways Act plus single journey fare, and will be deboarded.

### How do I check vacant berths on Eastern Railway special trains after chart preparation?
You can view physical vacant berths across all coaches on the [Chart Vacancy Map](/chart-vacancy) or check IRCTC Current Reservation starting four hours before departure, where seats are bookable at a 10% discount until 30 minutes before departure.

### How many tickets can I book on IRCTC in one month for festival travel?
Standard IRCTC accounts can book up to 12 tickets per calendar month. Linking and verifying your Aadhaar on your IRCTC profile increases this limit to 24 tickets per month, provided at least one passenger on tickets 13 to 24 is Aadhaar-verified.

### Can I change my boarding station on a festival special train ticket?
Yes, Indian Railways allows passengers to change their boarding station online through the IRCTC portal up to the preparation of the second reservation chart, approximately 30 to 60 minutes before scheduled train departure.

### What is the difference between GNWL and PQWL on Eastern Railway trains?
GNWL (General Waiting List) applies to passengers boarding at the originating station and offers the highest confirmation probability. PQWL (Pooled Quota Waiting List) applies to intermediate journey segments and clears much slower because it draws from a smaller seat pool.

### How can I get a confirmed ticket if all Howrah to NJP trains show Regret?
When direct trains show Regret, use [Finding Smart Seats](/) on LastBerth to discover split-ticket options across intermediate stations like Malda Town or Kishanganj on the exact same train.
