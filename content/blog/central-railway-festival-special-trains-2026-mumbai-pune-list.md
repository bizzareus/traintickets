## When Does Booking Open for Central Railway 418 Festival Special Trains?

**Reservations for Central Railway 418 festival special trains open on September 27, 2026 at 08:00 AM IST across all computerized PRS counters and the IRCTC website/app. Bookings require mandatory Aadhaar OTP verification on opening day for general quota berths and follow Train on Special Fare (TOSF) rules with flat 10% to 30% surcharges.**

Understanding booking schedules and ticketing rules ensures you secure confirmed seats before quotas exhaust:

1. **Reservation Opening Schedule:** Bookings for the notified 418 services open progressively starting **September 27, 2026 at 08:00 AM IST**. Both the official IRCTC website/mobile app and railway PRS reservation counters at stations sync at 08:00 AM.
2. **Aadhaar OTP Authentication Mandate:** Under current Indian Railways ticketing directives, opening-day reservations for general quotas require Aadhaar-authenticated IRCTC user profiles. Completing KYC on your IRCTC account before the booking window opens avoids verification delays.
3. **Train on Special Fare (TOSF) Pricing:** 0-series festival specials run under TOSF guidelines. A flat surcharge applies over regular mail/express fares:
   - **Second Sitting (2S):** 10% surcharge on base fare.
   - **Sleeper Class (SL):** 30% surcharge on base fare.
   - **AC Classes (3E, 3A, 2A, 1A):** 30% surcharge on base fare.
4. **Concessions and Quota Rules:** Standard concession discounts (such as senior citizen, student, or institutional concessions) remain suspended on festival special trains. Quotas such as Ladies Quota (LD), Senior Citizen/Lower Berth Quota (SS), and Divyangjan Quota operate according to individual rake notifications.

## How to Book Confirmed Seats on Festival Special Trains When Tickets Are Waitlisted?

**When Central Railway festival specials enter waiting list or REGRET status, you can secure confirmed travel using LastBerth Smart Seats split-ticketing, origin-station booking with online boarding change, and post-charting Current Availability. The first reservation chart prepares roughly 8 hours before departure, releasing unallocated berths that passengers can book up to 30 minutes before train departure.**

Even on special trains, high demand can cause seats to sell out within hours. If your preferred train shows `WL` (Waiting List) or `REGRET`, use these proven booking strategies to secure your journey:

### 1. Split-Segment Booking via Smart Seats
Indian Railways partitions coach berths into separate intermediate station quotas (`GNWL`, `RLWL`, and `PQWL`). While a direct booking from Mumbai to Danapur may show `REGRET`, berths may remain available from Mumbai to Itarsi, and from Itarsi to Danapur on the exact same train.

Use [Smart Seats](/) to discover contiguous split-segment seats, enabling you to travel on a single train without changing coaches or rakes.

### 2. Origin Station Booking with Boarding Station Modification
Intermediate stations draw from smaller pooled quotas that exhaust quickly. Booking from the originating terminal station (such as Mumbai CSMT instead of Thane, or Pune instead of Daund) secures access to the larger General Quota (`GNWL`).

Under Indian Railways rules, you can modify your boarding station online on IRCTC up to the preparation of the second reservation chart (around 30 minutes before train departure).

### 3. Current Availability (CURR_AVBL) Post-Chart Discovery
When the first reservation chart is prepared (at least 4 to 8 hours before departure, or at 21:00 hrs (9:00 PM) the previous evening for morning departures), all unallocated quotas and last-minute cancellations are released as `CURR_AVBL` at a 10% discount.

You can inspect [Chart Vacancy](/chart-vacancy) and track charting timelines on [Chart Times](/chart-times) to claim released berths up to 30 minutes before departure.

### 4. Standby Festival Clone Trains
During peak festival days (October 16–21 for Durga Puja, November 6–10 for Diwali, and November 12–15 for Chhath Puja), Central Railway deploys unannounced duplicate/clone rakes running 30 to 60 minutes behind major express trains. These appear on IRCTC with 12 to 24 hours notice with fresh, unreserved general quotas.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### Are Central Railway festival special train tickets more expensive than regular trains?

Yes, 0-series festival special trains charge a flat Train on Special Fare (TOSF) surcharge of 10% on Second Sitting and 30% on Sleeper and AC classes over regular express base fares. Standard concession discounts for senior citizens and other categories do not apply on these special services.

### When does ticket booking open for Central Railway 418 special trains?

Booking for Central Railway 418 festival special trains begins on September 27, 2026 at 08:00 AM IST on the IRCTC website, Rail Connect app, and computerized PRS counters at railway stations. Subsequent supplementary rakes open 10 to 30 days before departure as schedules are finalized.

### Can you book Tatkal tickets on Central Railway festival specials?

Tatkal quota availability depends on the specific train notification. While select 0-series special trains allocate standard Tatkal quotas opening at 10:00 AM for AC classes and 11:00 AM for non-AC classes one day prior to departure, many festival specials operate with 100% general quota. Check quota availability on IRCTC for your train.

### Can you board a Central Railway special train with a waitlisted e-ticket?

No, passengers holding fully waitlisted e-tickets cannot board reserved coaches on special trains. Unconfirmed online tickets are automatically cancelled after chart preparation and refunded to the original payment source. Boarding with an invalid ticket attracts a flat ₹500 fine under the Jan Vishwas Act plus single fare under Section 138 of the Railways Act.

### Can you change your boarding station on a festival special train?

Yes, you can change your boarding station online through the IRCTC portal up to the preparation of the second reservation chart, which occurs approximately 30 minutes before train departure. Ensure the updated boarding station reflects on your PNR before boarding.

### Why do festival special trains take longer than regular superfast trains?

Festival special trains run on temporary timetabled paths and yield track precedence to scheduled priority trains such as Vande Bharat, Rajdhani, and Superfast express trains. Passengers should plan their itinerary with buffer time for potential operational delays.

### How can you check vacant seats on special trains after chart preparation?

You can check vacant seats on IRCTC or through [Chart Vacancy](/chart-vacancy) and [Smart Seats](/). After the first chart is prepared, unsold berths across all quotas are converted into Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`) and can be booked online or at station counters until 30 minutes before departure.

### Are concession fares available on Central Railway 418 festival special trains?

No, fare concessions for senior citizens, students, and other general concession categories are suspended on 0-series festival special trains under Ministry of Railways commercial rules. Only specific identity-based passes like Divyangjan identity cards are honoured where explicitly notified.
