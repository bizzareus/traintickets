## TL;DR
Sure, you can reserve an entire coach or even a whole train through Indian Railways' Full Tariff Rate (FTR) service. For a coach, you've got to put down ₹50,000 as a security deposit, and if you're eyeing a full train (which means at least 18 coaches), you'll need ₹9,00,000. Just remember, you have to apply 30 to 180 days beforehand.

---

## So, What Exactly is FTR Booking in Indian Railways?

**FTR booking, or Full Tariff Rate booking, lets you hire whole train coaches or even special trains for events like weddings or group travel. You can book this online through the IRCTC FTR portal or offline at major rail stations.**

When you're organizing something huge like a wedding or a pilgrimage, buying individual train tickets can be a headache. You know how it goes with Indian Railways: you start on a waiting list, hoping to get confirmed. The regular booking status is like a rollercoaster: **WL (Waiting List) → RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) → Confirmed**. 

If you book online and your ticket stays waitlisted, like it starts at `WL/10` and only moves to `WL/1` when the chart is finalized, your ticket gets canceled and refunded automatically. That's why people go for FTR bookings—they secure entire coaches or trains, avoiding the waiting game.

## Can You Book a Full Coach or Train Online? Here’s How

**To book a full coach or train online, you'll first need to set up an account on the IRCTC FTR portal (ftr.irctc.co.in). Enter your travel details and submit your request. If it gets the green light, pay the deposit within six days to lock in your FTR booking reference number.**

Here's a quick rundown of what you'll do:

1. **Get Started Online:** Hop over to the FTR portal, log in, and choose whether you're booking a coach or a whole train. Fill in all the details like travel date, class, and any stops along the way.
2. **Deposit Payment:** Once you submit, you'll get a reference number. Pay that security deposit within 6 days to keep things moving.
3. **Feasibility Check:** The Zonal Railways folks will review your request to see if it's doable. They’ll check if your coach can fit into the train's existing setup and if the station stops are long enough—at least 10 minutes.
4. **Prefer Offline?** If the web isn’t your thing, you can go the old-school route. Submit a written request to the Chief Booking Supervisor at the starting station. They'll check things out and guide you on the deposit.

Just a heads up: Premium trains like Vande Bharat, Rajdhani, Shatabdi, and Duronto Express usually aren’t up for FTR bookings.

## The Lowdown on Security Deposits and Costs for FTR Booking

**For an FTR coach, the security deposit is ₹50,000 per coach. If you're chartering a whole special train, you need ₹9,00,000 for at least 18 coaches. The total fare includes regular ticket prices with a 30% service charge and taxes on top.**

Here’s what goes into the cost:

* **Security Deposit:** It's ₹50,000 per coach, with a cap of 10 coaches in one go. For a full train, it’s ₹9,00,000 for 18 coaches minimum. More than 18? Add ₹50,000 per extra coach.
* **Service Charge:** A flat **30% service charge** is tacked onto the base fare.
* **Minimum Distance Charge:** You pay for at least **500 kilometers** even if your ride is shorter.
* **Detention Fees:** If you hold up the train at a station longer than planned, expect to pay around **₹900 to ₹1,000 per hour per coach**.
* **Empty Haulage Fees:** If the railways need to move empty coaches to your start point or back, those costs are on you.

## What’s the FTR Cancellation and Refund Policy?

**Cancel FTR bookings online or at the station before your trip. Cancellation fees depend on timing, from ₹5,000 per coach if canceled 30 days out, to a full charge if you cancel last minute.**

Here's the breakdown:

* **More than 30 days out:** You lose **₹5,000 per coach** or 10% of the deposit, whichever is higher.
* **29 to 15 days out:** **25%** of your deposit is kept.
* **14 to 7 days out:** **50%** is deducted.
* **Less than 7 days:** Kiss your whole deposit goodbye—no refunds here.

After your trip, to get that deposit back, show the original FTR receipt to the Chief Booking Supervisor within **30 days**. They’ll check for any damages or extra charges before refunding the rest.

## Your FTR Booking Questions, Answered

### Can we get Vande Bharat or Rajdhani coaches with FTR?
Nope. Premium trains like Vande Bharat, Rajdhani, Shatabdi, and Duronto aren't included in FTR bookings. You’re limited to regular Express, Mail, or Passenger trains, depending on feasibility and train length limits.

### How quickly do we get the FTR security deposit back?
Expect a 15 to 30-day wait post-trip to see that refund. Make sure to hand over the original FTR receipt to the station master at the journey's start point within 30 days to claim it.

### What’s the maximum number of coaches for FTR?
You can book up to 10 coaches per request. Need more? Go for a special charter train, which starts at 18 coaches and caps at 24.

### Do FTR bookings include food?
Nope, meals aren't part of the deal. You’ll need to sort out food separately. Consider IRCTC’s e-catering or use private delivery apps to get meals delivered to your train at specific stops.
