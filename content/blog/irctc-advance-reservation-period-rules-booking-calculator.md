## What Is the IRCTC Advance Reservation Period (ARP)?

**The IRCTC Advance Reservation Period (ARP) is the official booking window that dictates how many days before travel passengers can reserve seats on Indian Railways. The standard ARP is 60 days, excluding the journey date. General reservation opens daily at 8:00 AM Indian Standard Time (IST) across both IRCTC and physical PRS counters.**

For decades, Indian Railways oscillated between 60, 90, and 120-day reservation windows. In late 2024, the Ministry of Railways officially slashed the ARP from 120 days to 60 days to tackle widespread booking anomalies:

1. **High Cancellation Rates:** Under the old 120-day window, up to 21% of passengers cancelled their tickets before travel, while another 4% to 5% failed to turn up at all (No-Show). This tied up scarce berths for months and prevented genuine travellers from planning.
2. **Combating Touts and Hoarding:** Long advance horizons allowed unauthorized ticketing agents and tout syndicates to block blocks of berths under fictitious names and sell them off as journey dates approached.
3. **Realistic Travel Planning:** A 60-day window encourages passengers to book when their travel plans are reasonably concrete, reducing artificial waiting lists on high-demand corridors.

The 60-day limit applies uniformly across almost all regular train categories, including Vande Bharat Express, Rajdhani Express, Shatabdi Express, Tejas Express, Duronto Express, Superfast, and Mail/Express trains.

## How to Calculate the 60-Day Advance Booking Date (With Examples)

**To calculate your IRCTC advance booking date, subtract 60 days from your scheduled travel date without counting the day of travel itself. If you depart on November 15, do not count November 15; count back 60 calendar days to find your booking opening date on September 16 at 8:00 AM IST.**

The most frequent mistake travellers make when planning holiday trips is counting the journey day in their 60-day calculation, causing them to log in 24 hours too late after all confirmed berths have evaporated.

### The Official Calculation Formula

$$\text{Booking Opening Date} = \text{Journey Date} - 60 \text{ Days (Excluding Departure Day)}$$

Because calendar months have differing lengths (28, 29, 30, or 31 days), simply subtracting "two months" from your date of travel will lead to incorrect calculations. 

### Concrete Examples for 2026 Travel Planning

- **Example 1: Diwali Travel (Journey on November 8, 2026)**
  - Departure Date: November 8 (not counted).
  - Counting backward 60 days: 7 days in November + 31 days in October + 22 days in September = 60 days.
  - **Booking Opens:** September 9, 2026, at 8:00 AM IST.
- **Example 2: Chhath Puja Travel (Journey on November 16, 2026)**
  - Departure Date: November 16 (not counted).
  - Counting backward 60 days: 15 days in November + 31 days in October + 14 days in September = 60 days.
  - **Booking Opens:** September 17, 2026, at 8:00 AM IST.
- **Example 3: New Year Eve Travel (Journey on December 31, 2026)**
  - Departure Date: December 31 (not counted).
  - Counting backward 60 days: 30 days in December + 30 days in November = 60 days.
  - **Booking Opens:** November 1, 2026, at 8:00 AM IST.

## What Time Does Advance Train Ticket Booking Open on IRCTC?

**Advance train ticket booking opens promptly at 8:00 AM IST every morning for both online IRCTC bookings and offline Passenger Reservation System (PRS) station counters. For trains originating late at night or running across multiple days, the 60-day ARP opens based on departure from the train's starting station.**

Understanding the operational timing nuances prevents disappointment when trying to secure seats:

### 1. The 8:00 AM IST Synchronization
Online booking on `irctc.co.in` and the IRCTC Rail Connect app unlocks at exactly **08:00:00 AM IST**. Physical reservation counters at railway stations across India connect to the same central CRIS (Centre for Railway Information Systems) database and open at the exact same second.

### 2. The Multi-Day Train Origin Rule
The 60-day window is **calculated from the date the train starts from its originating station**, not when it reaches your intermediate boarding station:
- If a train departs Dibrugarh on Day 1 (Monday) and reaches your boarding stop in Patna on Day 2 (Tuesday), booking for your journey opens **60 days before Monday**, not 60 days before Tuesday.
- Failing to account for originating station departure dates means other passengers further up the route may book berths a full day before you log in.

### 3. Daily Server Maintenance Shutdown
IRCTC shuts down its reservation servers daily between **11:45 PM and 12:20 AM IST** for routine database maintenance. No ticket bookings, cancellations, or PNR enquiries can be executed during this 35-minute blackout window.

## The 2026 Aadhaar Mandate: Day-1 Booking Restrictions Explained

**Under the latest 2026 Indian Railways anti-fraud guidelines, only Aadhaar-authenticated IRCTC user accounts are permitted to book tickets online during the opening day (Day 60) of the Advance Reservation Period. Unverified accounts face booking restrictions until subsequent days to curb automated tout bots and illegal commercial hoarding.**

This critical rule was introduced to level the playing field for genuine citizens. In the past, automated scripts and commercial touts deployed dozens of dummy unverified IRCTC IDs to corner confirmed berths within seconds of 8:00 AM opening.

### What Happens on Opening Day (Day 60)?
- **Aadhaar-Verified Users:** Can log in and book general reservation tickets without restriction from 8:00 AM IST onwards.
- **Unverified Accounts:** Online booking for opening-day berths is restricted or delayed. If your profile is not linked to Aadhaar, the system will prevent you from completing payment during the high-demand opening window.
- **Master List Verification:** In addition to verifying your own profile, at least one passenger on the booking form must be pre-added and Aadhaar-verified in your IRCTC **Master List**.

### How to Verify Your Aadhaar on IRCTC in 3 Minutes
1. Log into your account on the official IRCTC website or mobile app.
2. Go to **My Account** > **Link Your Aadhaar**.
3. Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number and full name exactly as printed on your Aadhaar card.
4. Provide consent for OTP generation and enter the 6-digit OTP sent to your Aadhaar-registered mobile number.
5. Log out and log back in; your profile status will update to **Aadhaar Verified** with a green checkmark.
6. Beyond unlocking Day-1 booking, Aadhaar verification also doubles your monthly IRCTC booking limit from **12 tickets to 24 tickets per month**.

**Certain day-running intercity express trains, such as the Taj Express and Gomti Express, have shorter advance reservation periods of 15 to 30 days. ## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### Does the 60-day advance reservation period include the date of travel?

**No, the 60-day Advance Reservation Period strictly excludes the date of travel itself. To calculate your booking date, start from the day immediately before your travel date and count backward 60 calendar days. If travelling on November 15, booking opens on September 16 at 8:00 AM IST.**

### Can unverified IRCTC accounts book tickets on the 60th day opening?

**Under 2026 Indian Railways regulations, only Aadhaar-authenticated IRCTC user accounts are allowed to book general tickets online during the opening day rush. Unverified user accounts are restricted until subsequent days to prevent automated tout software and commercial ticket hoarding.**

### What is the exact opening time for 60-day advance train booking?

**Advance reservation opens daily at exactly 8:00 AM Indian Standard Time (IST) across both the online IRCTC web/app portals and offline railway station PRS reservation counters. Booking runs continuously until 11:45 PM IST, when the nightly 35-minute maintenance window begins.**

### Why did Indian Railways reduce the ARP from 120 days to 60 days?

**Indian Railways reduced the booking window from 120 days to 60 days because long horizons caused a 21% ticket cancellation rate and a 5% no-show rate. The shorter 60-day window curbs unauthorized commercial ticket hoarding and ensures genuine passengers get confirmed berths.**

### Can you book Tatkal tickets 60 days in advance?

**No, Tatkal and Premium Tatkal tickets cannot be booked 60 days in advance. Tatkal booking opens strictly one day prior to the train's scheduled departure from its originating station at 10:00 AM IST for AC classes and 11:00 AM IST for Non-AC classes.**

### How does the 60-day rule work for intermediate boarding stations?

**The 60-day advance booking window is calculated strictly from the date the train commences its journey from its originating station, not when it reaches your intermediate boarding station. For multi-day trains, booking opens 60 days prior to the train's Day 1 departure.**

### Can foreign tourists book train tickets beyond 60 days?

**Yes, international passport holders and Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) can book train tickets up to 365 days in advance under the Foreign Tourist Quota (FTQ) on IRCTC. Payment must be completed using an international credit or debit card.**

### What happens to waitlisted tickets booked under the 60-day quota?

**Waitlisted e-tickets booked under the 60-day quota move through GNWL, RLWL, or PQWL queues as cancellations occur. If an e-ticket remains fully waitlisted after final chart preparation (~30 minutes before departure), it is automatically cancelled and refunded to your source account.**
