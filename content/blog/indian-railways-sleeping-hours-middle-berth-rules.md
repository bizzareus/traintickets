## What Is the Official Train Sleeping Time in Indian Railways?

**Official train sleeping time in Indian Railways is strictly fixed from 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM (22:00 to 06:00 IST).** During this designated eight-hour window, passengers holding confirmed reserved berths in Sleeper (SL), Third AC (3A), AC 3 Economy (3E), and Second AC (2A) coaches are legally permitted to sleep with berths fully deployed.

Outside this statutory eight-hour nighttime slot (from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM), coaches switch to daytime sitting arrangements. Middle berths must be folded flat against the wall, allowing passengers assigned to upper, middle, and lower berths to sit comfortably upright on the lower seats.

The Ministry of Railways previously operated a 9:00 PM to 6:00 AM sleeping schedule. Railway Board commercial circulars revised the opening window to 10:00 PM, granting passengers an extra hour to complete dinner, socialize, and stow luggage without seating disputes.

| Schedule Period | Operating Status | Permitted Passenger Actions | Coach Lighting & Power Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **10:00 PM to 6:00 AM** | **Official Sleeping Hours** | Middle berths raised; lower berths used exclusively for sleeping; passengers rest. | Main lights switched off; night lamps on; charging ports cut (11 PM–5 AM). |
| **6:00 AM to 10:00 PM** | **Daytime Sitting Hours** | Middle berths folded flat; lower berths shared by lower, middle, and upper berth holders. | Full compartment lighting; charging ports fully active. |
| **10:00 PM to 6:00 AM** | **TTE Inspection Embargo** | Ticket checks prohibited for seated passengers who boarded before 10:00 PM. | Handheld Terminal (HHT) silent checks only; zero wake-up calls permitted. |

## What Are the Middle Berth Sleeping Rules in Sleeper and AC Coaches?

**Middle berth passengers are legally entitled to raise their berth only between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM.** Outside these hours, keeping the middle berth suspended is a direct violation of railway passenger regulations, because it prevents lower berth passengers from sitting upright and restricts aisle movement.

If you hold a middle berth ticket (marked `MB`), you cannot insist on raising your berth before 10:00 PM, even if you feel tired or wish to recline. Lower berth occupants have full authority to request that the berth remain hooked downward until the clock strikes 10:00 PM.

Similarly, once the morning clock reaches 6:00 AM, the middle berth passenger must fold the berth down and secure its safety chain. If a middle berth passenger refuses to fold the berth after 6:00 AM, co-passengers may immediately summon the onboard Travelling Ticket Examiner (TTE) or coach attendant to enforce compliance.

```
       [ Upper Berth (UB) ]  <── Remains fixed 24 hours; passenger can sleep anytime
               │
      [ Middle Berth (MB) ]  <── Can ONLY be raised 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM (Folded during day)
               │
       [ Lower Berth (LB) ]  <── Shared sitting space 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM; exclusive bed at night
```

Passengers occupying upper berths (`UB`) or side upper berths (`SU`) are not bound by the folding rule because their berths remain permanently fixed. However, upper berth passengers cannot demand that lower berth holders vacate space to let them lie down before 10:00 PM.

## What Are the Seating Rights for Lower Berth Passengers During Day Hours?

**Lower berth passengers do not possess exclusive whole-day rights over their berth, because it functions as a common seating bench from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM.** Middle berth and upper berth passengers in that bay share the lower berth for daytime sitting.

Lower berth holders cannot lie flat or demand that fellow bay passengers stand in the aisle during the day. If a lower berth passenger attempts to sleep across the entire bench at 2:00 PM, co-passengers have the legal right to wake them and claim sitting space.

However, this daytime sharing requirement applies strictly within the same six-berth bay:

1. **Main Bay Compartment:** Lower berth (`LB`) is shared exclusively by passengers holding `LB`, `MB`, and `UB` in that specific bay.
2. **Side Berths Separation:** Side lower (`SL`) and side upper (`SU`) passengers must sit on the side-lower berth. They cannot encroach on the main bay lower berth unless invited.
3. **Waitlist and RAC Boundary:** Passengers traveling on RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) are allotted shared side-lower seating. Waitlisted e-ticket holders have zero boarding validity and cannot occupy seats anywhere on the train.

When booking your journey, remember that **WL full form is Waiting List**. The booking progression queue follows a strict hierarchy:

$$\text{WL (Waiting List)} \longrightarrow \text{RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation)} \longrightarrow \text{CNF (Confirmed Berth)}$$

If an online e-ticket remains on the waitlist after chart preparation, IRCTC automatically cancels it and refunds the fare. You cannot board any reserved coach with an unconfirmed e-ticket.

## Can TTE Check Train Tickets Between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM?

**Travelling Ticket Examiners (TTEs) are strictly prohibited from checking tickets, verifying identity documents, or disturbing sleeping passengers between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM.** Railway Board guidelines instruct ticket checking staff to complete verification before 10:00 PM.

This rule protects passenger privacy and ensures undisturbed rest during overnight journeys. If you boarded the train at an originating station or intermediate halt before 10:00 PM and had your ticket verified, a TTE cannot wake you up at 1:30 AM to re-examine your PNR or ID card.

There is one critical operational exception:

* **Late Night Boarding Passengers:** If your scheduled boarding station falls between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM, the on-duty TTE is authorized to verify your ticket and assign vacant berths when you enter the coach.
* **Handheld Terminal (HHT) Digital Audits:** TTEs use digital HHT devices that sync silently with the CRIS central reservation servers. If your berth is already marked verified, no physical interaction is required.

If an onboard ticket examiner or railway staff member aggressively disturbs you for routine checking during nighttime hours without a boarding justification, you can report the incident directly through the digital RailMadad portal or by dialing railway helpline **139**.

## What Are the Night Travel Rules for Lights, Charging Sockets and Noise?

**Indian Railways enforces strict nighttime tranquility rules from 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM, including turning off main coach lights and banning loud music.** Passengers cannot play audio through mobile loudspeakers, conduct loud telephone conversations, or travel in noisy groups after 10:00 PM.

Violating nighttime noise regulations or causing nuisance can lead to penalties under Section 145 of the Railways Act, including compounding fines or removal from the train by Railway Protection Force (RPF) personnel.

| Amenity / Activity | Daytime Policy (6:00 AM to 10:00 PM) | Nighttime Policy (10:00 PM to 6:00 AM) | Regulatory Basis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Main Coach Illumination** | Fully illuminated for passenger convenience. | Main white lights switched OFF at 10:00 PM; blue night lamps remain on. | Railway Board Passenger Comfort Code. |
| **Mobile Charging Sockets** | Fully energized (110V DC / 230V AC output). | **Turned OFF from 11:00 PM to 5:00 AM** across Sleeper and AC coaches. | Fire Safety & Anti-Short-Circuit Circular. |
| **Mobile Audio & Phone Calls** | Permitted at moderate conversational volumes. | **Loudspeakers strictly banned**; earphones mandatory; whispering required. | Section 145 Railways Act (Nuisance Prevention). |
| **Pantry & Food Delivery** | Hot meals, snacks, and tea served regularly. | Food service stops by 10:00 PM; silent breakfast orders taken after 6:00 AM. | IRCTC Catering Operations Manual. |

The overnight charging port shutdown (11:00 PM to 5:00 AM) was implemented nationwide to prevent overnight battery overheating, short circuits, and coach fire hazards. Always charge your power banks and phones before 10:30 PM.

## How Do Sleeping Rules Apply to Side Lower and RAC Passengers?

**Side Lower (SL) berths serve as shared seating for both RAC passengers during daytime hours and nighttime until one passenger is upgraded to a confirmed berth.** Under standard RAC allocation, two passengers share a single side-lower berth for travel.

If both passengers on an RAC ticket remain unconfirmed after final charting, they must sit together on the side-lower berth overnight. They may choose to take turns sleeping or rest in an upright reclined position.

```
[ Side Upper (SU) Berth ]  <── 1 Passenger with Confirmed Berth (Sleeps anytime)
           │
[ Side Lower (SL) Berth ]  <── Allotted to 2 RAC Passengers (Shared seating day & night)
```

Here is how side berth mechanics operate:

* **Side Upper (`SU`) Occupant:** Holds exclusive ownership over the upper berth. They can climb up and sleep at any hour of the day or night.
* **Side Lower (`SL`) Confirmed Occupant:** If a single passenger holds a fully confirmed `SL` ticket (no RAC on the coach), they share the seat with the side-upper passenger during daytime hours (6:00 AM to 10:00 PM) and enjoy exclusive sleeping rights from 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM.
* **Mid-Journey Upgrades:** As confirmed passengers fail to show up (marked absent after two stops under the two-station rule), the TTE's Handheld Terminal automatically promotes the first RAC passenger to an empty confirmed berth elsewhere in the train. The remaining RAC passenger then gets full sleeping rights on the side lower berth.

## What Are the Exemptions for Sick, Elderly and Pregnant Passengers?

**Senior citizens, pregnant women, passengers with disabilities, and individuals suffering from acute illness are legally exempted from rigid middle berth daytime folding restrictions.** Co-passengers and railway staff are required to accommodate these travelers on compassionate and humanitarian grounds.

If a senior citizen holding a middle berth feels dizzy or cannot climb to their berth at night, co-passengers are encouraged to mutually swap seats. When voluntary seat exchanges fail, the TTE can intervene using their emergency allotment powers on the Handheld Terminal.

Official administrative courtesies provided by Indian Railways include:

* **Lower Berth Quota (SS Quota):** Automatically assigns lower berths to males aged 60+ and females aged 45+ traveling alone or in pairs, subject to availability.
* **Pregnant Women Facility:** Women in advanced stages of pregnancy who hold upper or middle berths can approach the onboard TTE for reallocation to vacant lower berths under emergency quotas.
* **Daytime Sleeping Permission:** Passengers with certified medical conditions or high fever may keep middle berths raised or sleep on lower berths during daytime hours, provided fellow bay travelers give mutual consent.

If a severe medical emergency occurs during sleeping hours, press the emergency alarm chain (ACP) or alert the coach attendant immediately to request a railway doctor at the next scheduled junction.

## What Penalties Apply for Berth Disputes and Nighttime Nuisance?

**Passengers who deliberately harass co-passengers, refuse to lower middle berths after 6:00 AM, or create drunken nuisance face compounding fines under Section 145 and Section 138 of the Railways Act.** Severe or repeated misconduct can lead to deboarding and arrest at the next railway station.

Under the Jan Vishwas Act provisions, routine procedural disputes are handled through compounding administrative penalties, while aggressive public nuisance retains strict enforcement:

| Offense Category | Applicable Legal Clause | Penalty / Consequence | Enforcement Agency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Refusal to Fold Middle Berth** | Railway Board Commercial Guidelines | Verbal warning by TTE; forced folding; seat forfeiture on repeat offense. | Onboard TTE & Train Conductor. |
| **Loud Audio / Nighttime Nuisance** | Section 145 Railways Act | Compounding fine up to ₹500; deboarding at next scheduled halt. | RPF / GRP Railway Police. |
| **Boarding Reserved Coach on General Ticket** | Section 138 Railways Act | Flat **₹500 penalty** plus fare difference from journey origin; deboarding. | Ticket Checking Staff (TTE). |
| **Drunkenness or Obscene Behavior** | Section 145(b) Railways Act | Immediate arrest, ticket cancellation, and removal from train without refund. | Government Railway Police (GRP). |
| **Unauthorized Chain Pulling (ACP)** | Section 141 Railways Act | Fine up to ₹1,000 or imprisonment up to one year for non-emergency stoppage. | Railway Protection Force (RPF). |

If an argument escalates over berth sharing or nighttime noise, do not resort to physical altercations. Open the **RailMadad app**, select your PNR, choose the category `Passenger Amenities > Seating / Berth Dispute`, and submit a live complaint. On-duty RPF officers or train staff typically attend to the coach within 15 to 30 minutes.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### What time can I sleep in train?

Official sleeping time in Indian Railways is from 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM across all reserved Sleeper and AC coaches. During this window, passengers can raise middle berths and occupy lower berths exclusively for sleeping. Between 6:00 AM and 10:00 PM, all passengers must switch to sitting arrangements.

### What is the middle berth rule in train?

The middle berth can only be kept raised for sleeping between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM. Outside these hours, from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM, the middle berth must remain folded down against the coach wall so lower berth passengers can sit comfortably.

### Can a lower berth passenger sleep during the day?

No, a lower berth passenger cannot sleep during daytime hours if it deprives middle and upper berth passengers of their rightful seating space. From 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM, the lower berth serves as a common sitting bench shared among all passengers in that bay.

### Can TTE check tickets after 10 PM?

No, TTEs are strictly prohibited from checking tickets between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM for passengers who boarded the train before 10:00 PM. Ticket checking during nighttime hours is permitted only for passengers whose scheduled boarding station falls between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM.

### Are mobile charging sockets turned off at night in trains?

Yes, Indian Railways switches off 110V/230V mobile charging points in all Sleeper and AC coaches from 11:00 PM to 5:00 AM. This nationwide policy prevents overnight device overheating, electrical sparks, and coach fire hazards. Charging points resume operation at 5:00 AM.

### Can RAC passengers sleep on the side lower berth?

RAC passengers share the side lower berth in a sitting position. Because one berth is allocated to two RAC ticket holders, neither passenger has an exclusive flat sleeping berth unless one is upgraded to a confirmed seat by the TTE when an absent passenger's berth is released.

### What happens if someone refuses to lower the middle berth in the morning?

If a passenger refuses to fold down the middle berth after 6:00 AM, politely inform them of the official 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM railway rule. If they persist, report the issue to the onboard TTE, coach attendant, or lodge a digital complaint on the RailMadad app or helpline 139.

### Can lights remain switched on in train coaches after 10 PM?

No, main white compartment lights must be turned off at 10:00 PM to allow passengers to rest. Only dim blue night lamps remain illuminated along the central aisle and near doorways for safety and emergency movement.

### What is the penalty for creating noise or playing loud music at night on trains?

Playing loud music on mobile loudspeakers or causing unruly disturbance after 10:00 PM is an offense under Section 145 of the Railways Act. Offenders face compounding fines up to ₹500 and potential deboarding by the Railway Protection Force (RPF) at the next station.

### How does Current Availability help get a sleeping berth after charts are prepared?

Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`) releases all unutilized emergency and VIP quota berths as 100% confirmed tickets right after chart finalization (~8 to 10 hours before departure). These seats carry a 10% base fare discount and remain bookable on IRCTC until 30 minutes before departure.
