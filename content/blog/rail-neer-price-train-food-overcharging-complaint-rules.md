## What Is the Official Price of Rail Neer in Trains and Railway Stations?
**The official MRP of a 1-litre bottle of Rail Neer is strictly ₹15, while a 500ml bottle costs ₹9 across all Indian Railways trains and station platforms.** Authorised pantry staff and static stalls cannot charge extra for chilling or refrigeration. If Rail Neer is out of stock, vendors may sell approved packaged drinking water brands, but only at the exact same ₹15 cap.

Pantry vendors frequently try to charge ₹20 for a 1-litre bottle by claiming cooling charges or refrigerator costs. Indian Railways rules explicitly forbid adding any refrigeration surcharge to packaged drinking water.

Whenever you purchase bottled water on board or at a platform stall, check the printed MRP on the neck or wrapper. If the vendor insists on charging ₹20, you are entitled to pay only the printed ₹15 and demand a machine bill.

## What Is the "No Bill, Food Is Free" Rule in Indian Railways?
**The "No Bill, Food Is Free" policy mandates that if a pantry vendor, catering attendant, or platform stall fails to provide a machine-generated POS bill, the passenger is legally entitled to refuse payment.** You do not have to pay for food, tea, or water without an official printed receipt showing the GST breakdown.

Indian Railways made Point of Sale (POS) handheld electronic billing machines mandatory for all pantry car attendants and station stalls. Vendors often claim that the billing machine is broken, out of battery, or that they will bring the bill later at the next station.

If an attendant refuses to produce a POS machine receipt upon delivery, you are under no obligation to hand over cash. State clearly that you will pay the moment the electronic receipt is printed.

## How Do You Complain Against Train Food Overcharging on RailMadad?
**To report catering overcharging, visit railmadad.indianrailways.gov.in or open the RailMadad mobile app, enter your PNR or train number, select the "Catering & Vending Services" grievance category, and upload a photo of the bill or item.** Complaints are geo-tagged and assigned to the on-board Train Captain or division commercial controller for immediate action.

RailMadad is the official unified grievance portal of Indian Railways and is monitored 24x7 by zonal railway control rooms.

Follow these steps to file an instant complaint while travelling:
1. Open the RailMadad app or go to `railmadad.indianrailways.gov.in`.
2. Select **Train Complaint**, then enter your 10-digit PNR number.
3. Choose **Catering & Vending Services** from the main grievance type drop-down.
4. Select the sub-type **Overcharging** (or **Non-issuance of Bill / Food Quality**).
5. Enter the incident date, coach number, and berth number.
6. Upload a photo of the printed menu, bottle MRP, or hand-written slip (if provided).
7. Submit the form. You will receive a unique Complaint Reference Number via SMS to track resolution status in real time.

## How to File a Train Food Overcharging Complaint via 139 or SMS?
**Call railway helpline 139 and select the option for catering complaints to register an immediate grievance with a live customer executive, or send an SMS formatted as "MADAD <Complaint details>" to 139.** Include your train number, coach, berth, and the overcharged amount to receive a real-time SMS tracking reference.

Helpline 139 operates 24 hours a day in 12 languages. The customer representative relays the message directly to the commercial inspector of the upcoming station or the on-board train superintendent.

When calling 139, state your PNR, the vendor's name or uniform badge number, the exact item purchased, the printed price, and the price demanded. The supervisor on board will visit your coach before the train reaches the next major junction to verify your bill and refund the excess amount collected.

## What Can You Do If a Pantry Vendor Misbehaves or Refuses to Give a Bill?
**If catering staff refuses a bill or acts aggressively, do not escalate physically; immediately locate your coach TTE or dial 139 to request on-board Train Captain intervention.** You can also post a photo with your PNR and coach number on X (formerly Twitter) tagging @RailMinIndia, @IRCTCofficial, and your respective Railway Division handle.

Social media and RailMadad alerts trigger immediate responses from the Railway Protection Force (RPF) and commercial officers on running trains.

Follow these practical safety steps:
- **Stay Calm:** Do not enter into physical altercations with pantry workers.
- **Record Evidence:** Note the vendor's name tag, take a clear picture of the bottle or food packet, and note the coach and time.
- **Alert the TTE:** Ask the Travelling Ticket Examiner (TTE) to call the catering manager to your seat.
- **Check Coach Berths:** If you need to switch berths away from disruptive pantry staff or seat disputes, consult the TTE or use **[Seat Status Coach Journey Lookup](/seat-status)** to check legitimate vacant seats along your train route.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### Can a pantry vendor charge ₹20 for a ₹15 Rail Neer bottle citing cooling charges?
No. Indian Railways rules strictly prohibit charging any additional cooling or refrigeration fees for packaged drinking water. The maximum price you can be charged for a 1-litre Rail Neer bottle is ₹15, regardless of temperature or whether it is chilled.

### Is tip or service charge mandatory for pantry car staff in train?
No. Tips are completely optional and voluntary. Pantry car attendants are paid staff, and IRCTC guidelines prohibit staff from demanding tips or adding arbitrary service charges to meal bills.

### Are food prices included in regular train ticket fares?
In standard Mail, Express, and Superfast trains, food is not included in the ticket fare; you purchase meals a-la-carte at standard rates. In premium trains like Rajdhani, Shatabdi, and Duronto Express, catering charges are automatically bundled with the ticket unless you specifically chose the meal opt-out option during booking.

### What should you do if an unauthorized vendor sells food on the train?
Avoid buying food or water from unauthorized vendors who lack IRCTC uniforms and valid identity badges. You can report unauthorized hawkers directly to the RPF or TTE via the RailMadad app or by dialing 139.

### Can you bring your own food and water on Indian Railways trains?
Yes. Passengers are fully permitted to carry their own homemade food, snacks, and sealed water bottles on all train classes without any restriction or fee.

### How fast does Indian Railways resolve RailMadad catering complaints?
Most RailMadad catering complaints filed during journey hours receive an initial response within 15 to 30 minutes, with on-board supervisors visiting your coach at or before the next scheduled stop.

### What penalty is imposed on pantry car contractors for overcharging passengers?
Indian Railways imposes heavy financial penalties on catering license holders, ranging from ₹25,000 to ₹1,00,000 per violation. Repeated complaints of overcharging or non-billing can lead to contract cancellation and blacklisting.

### How can you check vacant seats or switch berths if you need to move coaches?
If your ticket is partially confirmed or waitlisted (where WL full form is Waiting List), or you need an alternate seat during the journey, check vacant berths after chart prep via **[Chart Vacancy](/chart-vacancy)** or use **[Finding Smart Seats](/)** to find segment-based confirmed paths.
