## When Does Vande Bharat Booking Open and What Is the Advance Window?

**Vande Bharat ticket booking opens 60 days in advance at 08:00 AM IST daily on the official IRCTC portal and PRS station counters. Advance reservation applies across all Vande Bharat services nationwide, with Aadhaar OTP verification enforced for morning sessions to secure booking fairness.**

Passengers planning intercity travel on India's semi-high-speed express trains can reserve seats up to two months ahead. The 60-day Advance Reservation Period (ARP) calculation excludes the departure day itself, meaning seats for a December 30 journey unlock on October 31 at exactly 08:00 AM Indian Standard Time. High-density business corridors such as New Delhi to Varanasi, Mumbai Central to Gandhinagar, and KSR Bengaluru to MGR Chennai Central regularly exhaust general quotas within the first morning hour.

Unlike legacy mail or express services, Vande Bharat train rakes consist entirely of air-conditioned chair cars with zero unreserved general coaches. Every passenger boarding the train must hold a valid, confirmed reservation voucher. You can reserve up to 12 tickets per month on a standard IRCTC user profile, or up to 24 tickets per month if your profile is Aadhaar-authenticated.

To ensure your booking transaction clears before high-speed quotas lock out, add passenger names, age, and dietary preferences to your IRCTC Master List 24 hours in advance. This pre-saved list populates traveler information with a single click during the morning opening rush.

## How Does Tatkal Booking Work for Vande Bharat and What Are the Timings?

**Tatkal booking for Vande Bharat opens strictly at 10:00 AM IST one day before train departure from the originating station. Because Vande Bharat rakes contain air-conditioned classes only (CC and EC), no 11:00 AM non-AC Tatkal window applies to these train services.**

Tatkal reservation provides an emergency booking quota for travelers facing sudden travel requirements. The opening day calculation is determined by the date the train commences its journey from its original source station. If your Vande Bharat departs its origin on Friday, the Tatkal quota unlocks on Thursday at exactly 10:00 AM IST.

Under current railway anti-bot security protocols, individual IRCTC accounts can book a maximum of two Tatkal PNRs per day. Tatkal tickets permit a maximum of four passengers per booking transaction, compared to six passengers under general quota bookings. Mandatory dynamic Aadhaar OTP prompts are enforced during the first 30 minutes of the Tatkal booking window to block unauthorized touts.

Confirmed Tatkal tickets carry a strict zero-refund policy upon voluntary passenger cancellation. If your Tatkal booking lands on Tatkal Waiting List (TQWL), note that TQWL clears only when other confirmed Tatkal passengers forfeit their seats. If a TQWL e-ticket remains unconfirmed after chart preparation, IRCTC automatically cancels the ticket and refunds the full base fare minus standard clerkage fees.

## Does Vande Bharat Have RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation)?

**No, Vande Bharat Express trains do not have RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) status because all coaches are seated chair cars without sleeper berths. Waiting list tickets on Vande Bharat clear directly from Waiting List (WL) to Confirmed (CNF) status upon passenger cancellations.**

On traditional sleeper and multi-tier air-conditioned trains (such as Sleeper, 3A, and 2A), an RAC ticket permits two passengers to share a single Side Lower berth as day seating. Because Vande Bharat operates exclusively with AC Chair Car (CC) and Executive Class (EC) rakes, every passenger seat is an individual contoured chair. Splitting or sharing a single passenger chair between two travelers is physically impossible and prohibited by railway operating guidelines.

As a consequence of this rake architecture, the waitlist clearing sequence on Vande Bharat skips the intermediate RAC stage entirely:

$$\text{WL (Waiting List)} \longrightarrow \text{CNF (Confirmed Seat)}$$

When a confirmed ticket holder cancels their journey, the system automatically promotes the top waitlisted passenger directly into an assigned coach and seat number. If your booking shows WL 1 or WL 2 on normal weekday departures, your odds of direct confirmation remain reasonable as business travelers adjust travel plans. However, if your status sits at WL 25 or higher on holiday weekends, booking contiguous segmented seats on [Smart Seats](/) provides a far more reliable alternative.

## When Is Vande Bharat Reservation Chart Prepared?

**The first Vande Bharat reservation chart is prepared approximately 8 to 10 hours before scheduled train departure from the originating station. For early morning services departing before 02:00 PM (14:00), the first chart is finalized the previous evening between 08:00 PM and 09:00 PM IST.**

Early charting gives catering teams adequate lead time to prepare fresh meals, assemble sealed trays, and load pantry carts onto the train rake. The second and final reservation chart is locked down 30 to 15 minutes before the train pulls away from the platform. During this final window, Handheld Terminals (HHTs) synchronize with railway reservation servers to upload current passenger manifests.

```
Originating Station Departure
   │
   ├─► ~8 to 10 Hours Before Departure: First Chart Preparation
   │     • VIP and Emergency quotas released to general queue
   │     • High-priority GNWL tickets upgraded to confirmed berths
   │     • Unsold inventory released as 10% discounted Current Availability (CURR_AVBL)
   │
   └─► 30 to 15 Minutes Before Departure: Final Chart Preparation
         • Handheld Terminals (HHT) synced for onboard TTE verification
         • Final booking cutoff executed; no further online bookings permitted
```

For morning Vande Bharat trains departing at 06:00 AM (such as the New Delhi to Varanasi or KSR Bengaluru to Dharwad services), the initial chart preparation takes place around 08:30 PM the night before. Travelers holding waitlisted tickets can check their confirmed status before going to bed rather than waking up in suspense at dawn. You can verify the exact historical chart release pattern for your specific service on [Chart Times](/chart-times).

## How Does the 15-Minute Current Booking Rule Work on Vande Bharat?

**The 15-minute booking rule allows travelers to purchase vacant Vande Bharat seats under Current Availability (CURR_AVBL) up to 15 minutes before train departure. Any seat remaining unsold after first chart preparation is released to this public pool at a 10% discount on base fare.**

Current Availability operates across both the official IRCTC portal and physical railway station Passenger Reservation System (PRS) counters. Once the first chart is generated, railway database systems calculate all remaining unallocated berths from emergency quotas, foreign tourist quotas, and unbooked Tatkal slots. These seats immediately transition into confirmed inventory marked as `CURR_AVBL`.

Tickets booked under Current Availability are 100% confirmed tickets with immediate coach and seat assignments printed on the voucher. Unlike standard tickets, these last-minute seats come with a 10% basic fare concession, making them cheaper than general advance bookings. Furthermore, zero Tatkal surcharge fees are applied to Current Availability transactions.

The online booking gateway remains open until exactly 15 minutes before the train's scheduled departure time from that station. Once the 15-minute threshold passes, the reservation software closes, and any remaining seats pass to onboard TTEs for physical platform allotment via Handheld Terminals.

## How Much Does a Vande Bharat Ticket Cost: AC Chair Car vs Executive Class?

**Vande Bharat ticket fares range from ₹1,000 to ₹1,850 for AC Chair Car (CC) and ₹1,950 to ₹3,600 for Executive Chair Car (EC), depending on route distance. Total passenger fare includes base fare, reservation charges, superfast surcharges, GST, and route-specific catering fees.**

The train provides two distinct seating tiers designed for different passenger preferences and budgets. AC Chair Car features a 3x2 seat configuration with ergonomic recline, personal reading lamps, and bottle holders. Executive Chair Car features a spacious 2x2 layout with wider armrests, extended footrests, and 360-degree rotating seats that can be aligned toward large panoramic windows.

Dynamic pricing models do not apply to standard Vande Bharat services in the same manner as Suvidha trains. However, catering fees vary depending on the travel duration and meal cycle (morning breakfast, high tea, or executive dinner) falling within the journey timetable.

## Can You Opt Out of Food Catering on Vande Bharat to Save Money?

**Yes, passengers can opt out of onboard catering services while booking Vande Bharat tickets to save ₹250 to ₹450 per passenger. If you untick the meal option during reservation, catering charges are completely deducted from your final ticket fare.**

Indian Railways introduced optional catering on Vande Bharat and Shatabdi trains to accommodate passengers who prefer packing their own food or who travel across shorter segments. When booking on the IRCTC website or app, look for the "Catering Option" dropdown under Passenger Details and select "No Food".

However, keep these practical dining rules in mind before choosing to opt out:

- **Onboard Ordering Surcharge:** If you opt out during online booking but later decide to order a meal from the onboard pantry manager, Indian Railways levies a mandatory ₹50 service fee per meal on top of standard menu pricing.
- **Delay Protection:** Under statutory railway rules, if your Vande Bharat is delayed by more than two hours behind schedule, pantry staff must serve complimentary refreshments and drinking water to all passengers, regardless of whether you opted out of catering during booking.
- **Standard Quality Controls:** Meals served on Vande Bharat include pre-packaged hot breakfast trays, afternoon tea snacks, and multi-course executive dinners adhering to IRCTC food standards.

## Can You Board a Vande Bharat Train With a Waitlisted or Platform Ticket?

**No, passengers cannot board a Vande Bharat Express with a waitlisted ticket, unreserved ticket, or platform pass. Boarding without a confirmed seat is an offense under Section 138 of the Railways Act, resulting in a flat ₹500 fine plus single journey fare.**

Under commercial railway regulations reinforced by the Jan Vishwas Act 2026, traveling in premium reserved trains without confirmed accommodations attracts strict penalties. Because Vande Bharat operates with zero general unreserved (GS) coaches, there is no permissible standing or hallway space on the rake.

```
Passenger Arrives at Station
   │
   ├─► Holds Fully Confirmed E-Ticket ──► Board Assigned Coach & Seat (Permitted)
   │
   ├─► Holds Waitlisted E-Ticket ───────► Auto-Cancelled Post-Chart; Boarding Prohibited
   │                                      (Section 138 Penalty: ₹500 Fine + Deboarded)
   │
   └─► Holds Platform Ticket ────────────► Station Entry Only; Train Boarding Strictly Banned
                                          (Section 138 Penalty: ₹500 Fine + Full Fare)
```

Ticket checking staff use Handheld Terminals (HHTs) to detect unauthorized passengers immediately after platform departure. Any traveler found inside a Vande Bharat coach holding an unconfirmed or invalid pass is issued a digital penalty slip and deboarded at the very next scheduled commercial stop.
