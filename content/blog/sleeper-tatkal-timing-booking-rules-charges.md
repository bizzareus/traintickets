## What is the Exact Sleeper Tatkal Timing in IRCTC?

**Sleeper Tatkal timing in IRCTC begins daily at 11:00 AM Indian Standard Time (IST), exactly one day before the train's scheduled departure date from its originating station. All non-air-conditioned classes, including Sleeper (SL) and Second Sitting (2S), share this 11:00 AM booking slot, while AC classes open one hour earlier at 10:00 AM IST.**

Every day, millions of passengers rely on non-AC Sleeper coaches as the lifeline of Indian passenger transit. Because Sleeper class carries over 60% of all reserved long-distance rail volume, competition during the 11:00 AM window is intense. High-demand trunk routes connecting major metropolitan hubs to eastern and northern states often exhaust their entire quota within 90 to 120 seconds.

Understanding how the booking calendar operates is vital for securing a seat:

1. **Originating Station Departure Governs the Calendar:** The 24-hour advance booking window is calculated from the day the train departs its originating station, not your boarding station. If you board an intermediate stop on Day 2 of a long-distance train, booking opens at 11:00 AM on Day 0.
2. **Synchronized Nationwide Launch:** The 11:00 AM opening applies simultaneously across the official IRCTC Next Generation e-Ticketing (NGET) website, the IRCTC Rail Connect mobile application, and all computerized Passenger Reservation System (PRS) counters at railway stations.
3. **Segregation from AC Tatkal:** Air-conditioned classes (1A, 2A, 3A, 3E, CC, EC) open at 10:00 AM IST. This one-hour separation prevents server overload by distributing database write locks across two staggered traffic spikes.

## When Should You Log in to IRCTC for Sleeper Tatkal Booking?

**You should log in to your IRCTC account at exactly 10:50 AM IST, precisely 10 minutes before the 11:00 AM Sleeper Tatkal window opens. Logging in earlier than 10:45 AM risks premature session timeout, while logging in after 10:55 AM exposes you to gateway throttling and delayed login captchas.**

The Centre for Railway Information Systems (CRIS) enforces strict session concurrency rules during peak booking periods. Between 10:00 AM and 12:00 PM, IRCTC operates an anti-bot filtration protocol that throttles automated traffic and caps session lifetimes.

To navigate the 10-minute pre-booking window effectively, follow this sequence:

1. **10:50 AM (Initial Login):** Open a single browser tab and log in with your credentials. Verify that your registered mobile number is within reach to receive any mandatory login OTP.
2. **10:52 AM (Pre-Fill Route Search):** Select your origin station, destination station, journey date, and choose "TATKAL" from the quota dropdown. Do not leave the browser idle; click once on the search results to keep the session alive.
3. **10:58 AM (Time Synchronization):** Compare your device clock against the official Indian Standard Time displayed on the IRCTC header. Even a 5-second variance can mean clicking "Book Now" before the quota opens, triggering a "Booking not permitted" error.
4. **11:00:01 AM (Instant Execution):** As the clock strikes 11:00:00, click "Refresh" on the Sleeper class card. The status will immediately flip from "TATKAL BOOKING NOT STARTED" to the live count of available berths.

## How Much Are Sleeper Tatkal Charges on Indian Railways?

**Sleeper Tatkal charges are calculated at 10% of the basic train fare, subject to a statutory minimum of ₹100 and a maximum cap of ₹200 per passenger. These surcharges are added directly to the standard Sleeper fare, alongside the standard ₹20 reservation fee and applicable superfast supplementary charges.**

Unlike Premium Tatkal, which uses dynamic surge pricing that can double or triple ticket costs, standard Tatkal fares are strictly regulated by the Railway Board. For journeys up to 500 kilometers where 10% of the base fare is less than ₹100, the minimum flat fee of ₹100 applies. On journeys exceeding 1,000 kilometers where 10% of the base fare would exceed ₹200, the fee is capped at ₹200.

## What Are the Key Rules for Booking Sleeper Tatkal Tickets?

**Key rules for Sleeper Tatkal booking include a maximum limit of 4 passengers per PNR, a cap of 2 Tatkal tickets per day per user ID, and a complete restriction on authorized travel agents between 11:00 AM and 11:30 AM IST. Concession tickets and senior citizen discounts are strictly disallowed under the Tatkal quota.**

To guarantee fair public access during peak rush hours, Indian Railways enforces several technical and administrative guardrails:

1. **4 Passengers per Transaction:** While general quota bookings allow up to 6 passengers on a single ticket, Tatkal limits every booking to a maximum of 4 passengers per PNR, including children.
2. **Strict Agent Embargo:** IRCTC authorized agents (including web portals, corporate travel agents, and physical retail agents) cannot search or book Tatkal tickets during the first 30 minutes of opening. For Sleeper class, this embargo runs from **11:00 AM to 11:30 AM IST**.
3. **No Concessions or Subsidies:** No fare concessions (senior citizens, Divyangjan, students, accredited journalists, or medical patients) apply to Tatkal quota bookings. Every traveler must pay the full adult fare plus the Tatkal surcharge.
4. **Single Active User Session:** CRIS restricts each registered account to a single active login across browsers. Attempting to open multiple tabs or logging in simultaneously on both mobile app and desktop causes immediate session termination.
5. **Monthly Account Limits:** Standard unverified IRCTC accounts can book up to 12 tickets per month, while Aadhaar-verified user profiles can book up to 24 tickets per month, provided at least one traveler on the booking is verified.

**The IRCTC Master List speeds up Sleeper Tatkal booking by pre-saving passenger names, ages, gender, and berth preferences in your profile before the 11:00 AM rush. During checkout, you select all saved passengers with a single click, reducing detail entry time from 40 seconds to under 2 seconds.**

Manual typing during a Tatkal booking is the primary reason tickets slip from confirmed to waitlisted status. When competing against thousands of concurrent users, shaving 30 seconds off your checkout flow is the difference between getting a Lower Berth and being stuck in TQWL.

Follow these steps to set up and verify your Master List before booking day:

1. **Navigate to Master List Settings:** Log in to the IRCTC portal during non-peak hours, open "My Profile", and select "Add / Modify Master List".
2. **Enter Accurate Passenger Data:** Enter the exact full name, date of birth, gender, and berth preference (Lower, Middle, Upper, Side Lower, Side Upper) matching the passenger's government photo ID.
3. **Complete Aadhaar Authentication:** Click the Aadhaar verification link next to each passenger. Authenticating passengers against UIDAI databases turns their status green ("Verified"), which prevents booking delays during Tatkal OTP checks.
4. **Select During Checkout:** When the clock hits 11:00 AM and you click "Book Now", click the checkbox next to the pre-filled passenger names on the booking screen. The form populates instantly, letting you jump straight to the payment gateway.

## How Do Origin Departure Dates Affect Intermediate Station Tatkal Timings?

**Intermediate station Tatkal timings are governed strictly by the scheduled departure date and time from the train's originating station, not when the train reaches your boarding junction. If a train leaves its origin on Monday morning and arrives at your intermediate stop on Tuesday afternoon, Tatkal opens on Sunday at 11:00 AM IST.**

This rule frequently confuses travelers boarding long-distance cross-country trains like the Kerala Express, Himsagar Express, or Karnataka Express. Because these services travel for 40 to 70 hours across multiple states, their Tatkal quota opens 2 to 3 days before local passengers see the train on their platform.

If a passenger at Nagpur waits until Wednesday at 11:00 AM to book their Sleeper Tatkal ticket, the entire Tatkal quota will have been sold out 24 hours earlier. ## Can You Cancel a Confirmed Sleeper Tatkal Ticket for a Refund?

**Confirmed Sleeper Tatkal tickets are non-refundable, meaning you receive a flat ₹0 refund if you cancel your confirmed booking voluntarily. A 100% full refund is granted only under statutory exceptions, such as complete train cancellation by the railway, route diversion, or departure delays exceeding three hours.**

Indian Railways enforces a zero-refund policy on confirmed Tatkal seats to deter touts and commercial speculation. Once a Tatkal ticket is issued in confirmed status, the entire fare (including base fare, reservation charges, and Tatkal fee) is forfeited upon passenger cancellation.

However, Railway Board rules protect passengers under these specific operational circumstances:

| Cancellation Scenario                  | Passenger Entitlement                 | Required Action to Claim                                 |
| :------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Voluntary Passenger Cancellation**   | **₹0 Refund (100% Forfeited)**        | Standard cancellation via IRCTC website                  |
| **Train Cancelled by Railways**        | **100% Full Refund (₹0 Clerkage)**    | Automated refund credited to bank account                |
| **Train Delayed by Over 3 Hours**      | **100% Full Refund**                  | File Ticket Deposit Receipt (TDR) before train departure |
| **Coach Detached / Class Downgrade**   | **Difference of Fare or 100% Refund** | TTE certificate + online TDR filing                      |
| **Passenger Placed in Lower Class**    | **Fare Difference Refund**            | TTE certificate obtained onboard                         |
| **Tatkal Waitlist (TQWL) Unconfirmed** | **Full Refund Minus ₹60 Clerkage**    | Automatic refund for online e-tickets                    |

If you hold a waitlisted Tatkal ticket that fails to confirm after chart preparation, IRCTC automatically cancels the e-ticket and credits the money back to your bank account within 3 to 5 working days, deducting only a standard clerkage fee of ₹60 per passenger.

## What Are Sleeper Tatkal Waiting List (TQWL) Confirmation Chances?

**Sleeper Tatkal Waiting List (TQWL) confirmation chances are extraordinarily low, typically below 10%, because Tatkal tickets clear directly to Confirmed status without passing through RAC. Since confirmed Tatkal cancellations yield zero refund, very few passengers cancel their seats, leaving the waiting queue stagnant.**

When all confirmed Tatkal berths on a train are booked, IRCTC issues tickets marked as Tatkal Waiting List (TQWL). Understanding why TQWL behaves differently from regular waiting lists protects you from stranded journeys:

1. **No RAC Buffer for Tatkal:** General quota waitlists (GNWL) first progress to Reservation Against Cancellation (RAC), giving passengers a guaranteed sitting seat. TQWL has no RAC stage; passengers remain on waitlist until a confirmed Tatkal traveler cancels.
2. **Zero Financial Incentive to Cancel:** Because confirmed Tatkal cancellations provide ₹0 refund, passengers who miss their train rarely cancel their tickets. Without cancellations, the TQWL counter rarely advances.
3. **Queue Progression Order:** If empty berths arise from unallocated emergency or VIP quotas during chart preparation, CRIS algorithms clear General Waiting List (GNWL) and Remote Location Waiting List (RLWL) passengers before touching TQWL.
4. **Statistical Rule of Thumb:** If your Sleeper Tatkal ticket is at TQWL 1 to TQWL 3 on an off-peak weekday train, there is a minor possibility of confirmation. If your ticket is at TQWL 5 or higher, you should immediately book an alternative travel option.

## Can Passengers Travel in Sleeper Coach on a Waitlisted Tatkal Ticket?

**Passengers cannot travel in a reserved Sleeper coach on an unconfirmed Tatkal e-ticket under any circumstances. Online Tatkal e-tickets that remain waitlisted after final chart preparation are automatically cancelled by IRCTC, and boarding a train with an auto-cancelled ticket is legally classified as ticketless travel.**

Under Section 138 of the Railways Act, as amended by the Jan Vishwas Act 2026, traveling in a reserved Sleeper compartment without a valid confirmed or RAC berth carries a **flat penalty of ₹500 plus the single Sleeper fare** from the train's originating point to the next stopping junction. The Travelling Ticket Examiner (TTE) is legally mandated to deboard unauthorized passengers at the next scheduled halt.

The distinction between online e-tickets and physical railway counter tickets is crucial:

- **Online Tatkal E-Tickets:** If your PNR status remains in waitlist after chart preparation, the ticket becomes legally invalid. The passenger name does not appear on the conductor's Handheld Terminal (HHT) chart, and you cannot board reserved or unreserved coaches.
- **Physical PRS Counter Tickets:** If you stood in line at a railway station and purchased a physical paper Tatkal ticket that remains waitlisted, the ticket does not auto-cancel. You are legally permitted to travel in unreserved General (GS) coaches, or seek permission from the TTE to occupy an unallocated berth if someone fails to turn up.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### At what time does Sleeper Tatkal booking open online?

Sleeper Tatkal booking opens online daily at exactly 11:00 AM IST on the IRCTC website and Rail Connect app. This opening time applies to all non-AC classes, including Sleeper (SL) and Second Sitting (2S), exactly one day before the train's departure from its originating station.

### Is Aadhaar verification mandatory for Sleeper Tatkal booking?

Aadhaar verification is not mandatory for booking standard Tatkal tickets up to your account's basic monthly limit of 12 tickets. However, IRCTC requires dynamic OTP authentication during peak Tatkal hours, and Aadhaar-verified users enjoy an expanded monthly limit of 24 tickets and faster checkout verification.

### Can travel agents book Sleeper Tatkal tickets at 11:00 AM?

No, authorized IRCTC travel agents and third-party booking APIs are strictly barred from booking Tatkal tickets during the first 30 minutes of opening. For Sleeper class, the agent restriction window runs from 11:00 AM to 11:30 AM IST daily, reserving early seats exclusively for individual passengers.

### What is the maximum number of passengers allowed on a Sleeper Tatkal ticket?

A maximum of 4 passengers can be booked on a single Sleeper Tatkal PNR. This differs from general quota bookings, which allow up to 6 passengers per ticket. If your traveling group has 5 or more passengers, you must split the party across two separate booking transactions.

### Are concession fares available on Sleeper Tatkal tickets?

No concession fares are permitted on Sleeper Tatkal bookings. Subsidies for senior citizens, students, patients, Divyangjan, and journalists are completely disallowed under the Tatkal quota. Every passenger must pay the standard adult base fare plus the applicable Tatkal surcharge.

### What happens if my Sleeper Tatkal ticket is waitlisted (TQWL)?

If your Sleeper Tatkal ticket is waitlisted and does not confirm by the time the final reservation chart is prepared, IRCTC automatically cancels the e-ticket. The ticket fare is refunded directly to your original payment method within 3 to 5 banking days, deducting a flat clerkage fee of ₹60.

### Can I book a Sleeper Tatkal ticket from a railway station counter?

Yes, you can purchase physical Sleeper Tatkal tickets from computerized PRS counters at railway stations starting at 11:00 AM IST. Many busy junction stations issue queue tokens in the morning to organize counter queues and ensure fair access before the 11:00 AM window opens.

### Does IRCTC charge GST on Sleeper Tatkal tickets?

No, Indian Railways does not levy Goods and Services Tax (GST) on Sleeper class or Second Sitting tickets, including their Tatkal surcharges. The standard 5% railway GST applies exclusively to air-conditioned travel classes such as 1A, 2A, 3A, 3E, CC, and Executive Class.

### What is the difference between Sleeper Tatkal and Premium Tatkal?

Standard Sleeper Tatkal charges a flat surcharge capped at ₹200 and allows waitlisted tickets (TQWL). Premium Tatkal uses dynamic pricing where fares rise continuously as seats sell out, completely disallows waitlists, and is available exclusively through online web and mobile bookings.

### When is the reservation chart prepared for Sleeper Tatkal tickets?

The first reservation chart for trains carrying Sleeper Tatkal passengers is finalized approximately 8 to 10 hours prior to departure from the originating station. The second and final chart is finalized 30 to 45 minutes before departure, after which remaining vacant berths are sold as Current Availability.
