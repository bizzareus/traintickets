## Can You Change Your Boarding Station in IRCTC Online?

**Yes, you can change your boarding station online through the RailOne app, the official IRCTC portal, or the IRCTC mobile app. Under official IRCTC rules, this online change is permitted up to the preparation of the second reservation chart, approximately 30 minutes before the train departs from its originating station.**

This facility offers passengers complete flexibility when travel schedules shift unexpectedly. Instead of visiting a physical reservation counter or forfeiting your ticket, you can make adjustments directly on your smartphone. The entire process is handled digitally, updating the passenger manifest in the Passenger Reservation System (PRS) database before the final chart is printed.

## What Are the IRCTC Boarding Point Change Rules?

**IRCTC rules allow a boarding point change only once per booking for Confirmed and RAC tickets. Waitlisted tickets are not eligible for this change. Passengers lose original boarding rights, and no fare refund is granted if the new boarding station results in a shorter travel distance across your booked journey.**

Once the boarding point is updated, your berth is officially reserved from the newly selected station. While passengers are expected to board at their updated station, railway regulations provide emergency relief: if you arrive at the original station, you can board if the seat remains vacant by paying the fare difference without penalties.

Waitlisted tickets cannot use this feature. Remember, WL full form is Waiting List, and your booking must move along the queue from WL (Waiting List) to RAC to Confirmed before you request a change. If your waitlisted e-ticket does not confirm by chart preparation, it is automatically cancelled and refunded.

## How to Change Your Boarding Point Online or Offline?

**To change your boarding point online, log into the RailOne app or IRCTC website, open Booked Ticket History, select your ticket, click 'Change Boarding Point', and select your new station before second chart preparation. For offline counter tickets, submit a written request at a computerized PRS reservation counter before chart preparation.**

The online interface displays all eligible stations along your train route instantly. Simply select your preferred boarding stop from the drop-down menu and confirm. The system updates your electronic ticket immediately, and a confirmation SMS is dispatched to your registered mobile number.

If you hold a physical PRS counter ticket, visit any computerized railway reservation counter before chart preparation. Bring your original paper ticket and a government-issued photo ID. The reservation supervisor will update the central database and provide an official stamped confirmation slip.

## What Happens If You Board from the Original Station After Changing It?

**If you board from the original station after changing your boarding point, you may travel if your seat has not been reallocated to another passenger. You must notify the TTE and pay any fare difference between stations. If your berth is already reallocated, you can only travel unreserved.**

When you submit a boarding point change, the onboard Ticket Examiner (TTE) is notified via their Handheld Terminal (HHT) that your seat is vacant between the original origin and your new boarding point. In high-demand sectors, that vacant segment may be allotted to RAC passengers or short-distance travelers.

To avoid awkward complications on board, ensure you finalize your boarding arrangements before the second chart is finalized. If you must board at the original station unexpectedly, report to the TTE immediately on the platform before train departure.

## How Late Can You Change Your Boarding Station Before Chart Preparation?

**You can change your boarding station online on IRCTC up to the preparation of the second reservation chart, approximately 30 minutes before scheduled train departure. Indian Railways revised the earlier rigid 24-hour cutoff rule, granting passengers maximum flexibility to update boarding points directly from the RailOne app or IRCTC portal.**

Under the modernized PRS framework, the first reservation chart is typically generated approximately 8 hours prior to train departure (or the previous night for morning departures), while the second chart is finalized roughly 30 minutes before the train pulls out of the station.

Passengers can initiate a boarding station change at any point prior to that second chart release. Once the second chart is sealed, the online "Change Boarding Point" link is disabled, and no further modifications can be recorded.

## Will the TTE Allocate Your Seat to Another Passenger If You Board Late Without Changing?

**Yes, if you board at a later station without updating your boarding point on IRCTC, the TTE is authorized to treat you as a no-show and reallocate your berth. Under Indian Railways rules, the TTE holds the berth for 10 minutes or until the next scheduled halt before reallocating.**

When a passenger fails to board at their booked station, the TTE marks them absent on the Handheld Terminal (HHT). The vacant seat is then immediately pushed into the computerized reallocation pool:

- **Berth Reallocation:** The vacant berth is automatically offered to on-board RAC passengers in queue order, or made available for post-charting Current Reservation.
- **Loss of Berth Rights:** If you board at a later station after your seat has been reassigned, you forfeit all claim to that berth and must travel in general unreserved class subject to TTE authorization.
- **Penalty Avoidance:** Updating your boarding point online takes less than a minute and protects your confirmed berth completely.

**The smart boarding point hack involves booking a ticket from a major originating station under the larger general quota and changing the boarding point to an intermediate station. This bypasses lower intermediate pooled quotas, securing a confirmed seat on popular trains while ensuring you board legally at your preferred station.**

Intermediate stations frequently run out of seats because Indian Railways allocates the vast majority of coach inventory to the originating terminus. ## When Does Chart Preparation Happen and What Is Current Availability?

**The first reservation chart is prepared approximately 8 hours before train departure, while the final second chart is prepared roughly 30 minutes before departure. You can change your boarding station online up to the preparation of the second chart, giving you full control over your itinerary until final departure.**

Submitting your boarding point change before the second chart ensures your updated boarding station is accurately recorded on the TTE's Handheld Terminal. Once the second chart is published, the passenger manifest is locked.

If seats remain empty after the first chart is generated, Indian Railways opens Current Reservation (CURR_AVBL). These last-minute confirmed seats are sold at regular base fares with no Tatkal markup on the IRCTC portal, RailOne app, or station counters.

## Can You Change Boarding Point for Tatkal Train Tickets?

**Yes, you can change the boarding point for Tatkal train tickets online or offline, provided your ticket is Confirmed or RAC. Under IRCTC rules, Tatkal tickets follow the same boarding station change policy as General quota bookings, allowing modification up to second chart preparation.**

Emergency travel plans often shift quickly. Indian Railways allows a one-time boarding point modification for confirmed Tatkal e-tickets through the RailOne app or IRCTC website. Note that waitlisted Tatkal tickets cannot change boarding stations, and no refund of the Tatkal surcharge or base fare will be given if your new boarding station is closer to your destination.

## Can Boarding Point Be Changed to a Station After the Original Destination?

**No, you cannot change your boarding point to a station located after your ticket's original destination station. IRCTC boarding point changes are strictly restricted to valid intermediate stations along the booked route prior to your destination, as the destination defines the end boundary of your booked train journey.**

The boarding point feature allows passengers to alter where they board the train between the origin and destination recorded on the ticket. Attempting to select a station beyond your booked destination is invalid because your reservation and fare coverage terminate at the destination station. If you wish to travel beyond your original destination, you must purchase a separate extension ticket or book a new ticket for the additional journey segment.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### Can I change my boarding station after the first chart is prepared?
Yes, under current IRCTC regulations, you can change your boarding station online up to the preparation of the second reservation chart (~30 minutes before scheduled departure).

### Do I get a refund if the new boarding station is closer to my destination?
No, you do not receive any refund of the fare difference. The ticket fare remains the same as originally booked, even if your new boarding station reduces the total travel distance.

### Can RAC ticket holders change their boarding station?
Yes, both Confirmed and RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) ticket holders are fully eligible to change their boarding station online or offline. Tickets on the Waiting List (WL) cannot change boarding points.

### What is the WL full form in Indian Railways?
WL full form is Waiting List. When a ticket is waitlisted, it is not yet confirmed and progresses through the queue: WL (Waiting List) to RAC to Confirmed. You cannot change your boarding point if your ticket remains waitlisted.

### How late can I change my boarding point on the IRCTC app?
You can change your boarding point online on the IRCTC app or RailOne app up to the generation of the second reservation chart, which occurs approximately 30 minutes before train departure.

### Can you change your boarding point for counter tickets online?
No. Physical tickets booked at railway reservation counters (PRS) cannot be changed online via the IRCTC app. You must submit a written request along with the original counter ticket at a computerized PRS counter before chart preparation.

### Is there any fee charged by IRCTC for changing your boarding station?
No. IRCTC does not charge any fee for changing your boarding point. The service is free of cost for both e-tickets and counter tickets.

### Can I change my boarding point more than once?
No, you can only change your boarding point once per ticket booking. Once the change is submitted, the action is permanent and cannot be modified again online.

### What happens if I board at my original station after changing the boarding point?
If your berth has not been reassigned to another passenger, you can travel by informing the TTE and paying any fare difference without penalty. If your berth was reassigned, you must travel in general unreserved class.

### Does changing the boarding point cancel my original ticket?
No, changing the boarding point does not cancel your ticket. It updates your boarding station in the official passenger chart, ensuring the TTE holds your seat until your new boarding stop.
