## When Does Booking Open for Western Railway Festival Special Trains?

**Ticket booking for Western Railway festival special trains opens progressively starting September 28, 2026 at 08:00 AM IST on IRCTC and computerized PRS counters. Reservations require mandatory Aadhaar OTP authentication on opening day for general bookings and follow Train on Special Fare (TOSF) pricing, which includes flat 10% to 30% surcharges over regular fares.**

Navigating the booking schedule correctly is essential for securing confirmed berths before quotas are exhausted:

1. **Reservation Opening Windows:** Primary special train bookings opened on **September 28, 2026 at 08:00 AM IST**. Online bookings on the IRCTC web portal and Rail Connect mobile app launch simultaneously with physical PRS counter windows at stations.
2. **Aadhaar OTP Authentication Requirement:** Under Indian Railways anti-bot security directives, users booking on opening morning must have Aadhaar-verified IRCTC profiles. The system sends an OTP to your UIDAI-registered mobile number before finalizing checkout.
3. **Train on Special Fare (TOSF) Tariffs:** Western Railway festival specials operate under TOSF guidelines with the following fixed surcharges:
   - **Second Sitting (2S):** 10% surcharge on base fare.
   - **Sleeper Class (SL):** 30% surcharge on base fare.
   - **AC Classes (3E, 3A, 2A, 1A):** 30% surcharge on base fare.
4. **Concession Suspension:** Concessionary tickets (including senior citizen and student concessions) do not apply to 0-series festival special trains. Quotas such as Ladies (LD) and Divyangjan operate only where specifically allocated in the rake circular.

## How to Book Confirmed Seats on Festival Special Trains When Tickets Are Waitlisted?

**When Western Railway festival special trains enter waiting list or REGRET status, passengers can secure confirmed travel using LastBerth Smart Seats split-ticketing, terminal origin booking with online boarding modifications, and post-charting Current Availability. The first reservation chart prepares roughly 8 hours before departure, opening unallocated berths for booking until 30 minutes before train departure.**

Even supplementary special trains can exhaust their reservation quotas quickly. If your target train displays `WL` (Waiting List) or `REGRET`, use these proven booking strategies to secure your seat:

### 1. Contiguous Split-Ticketing via Smart Seats
Indian Railways allocates berths across separate quota pools for different origin-destination pairs on the same train (`GNWL`, `RLWL`, and `PQWL`). While a direct seat from Surat to Danapur may show `REGRET`, berths often remain open from Surat to Itarsi and from Itarsi to Danapur on the identical train rake.

Use [Smart Seats](/) to identify contiguous split-segment seats, allowing you to complete your journey on the same train without shifting coaches.

### 2. Booking from Terminus with Boarding Point Modification
Intermediate stations draw from smaller pooled quotas that fill up fast. Booking from the originating terminal (such as Bandra Terminus instead of Surat, or Udhna instead of Nandurbar) grants access to the larger General Quota (`GNWL`).

Under railway rules, you can change your boarding station online on IRCTC up to the preparation of the second reservation chart, approximately 30 minutes before train departure.

### 3. Current Availability (CURR_AVBL) After Chart Preparation
When the first reservation chart finalizes (around 8 hours before departure, or at 20:00 the prior night for morning departures), unallocated VIP, emergency, and foreign tourist quotas are released into Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`) at a **10% discount on base fare**.

You can monitor real-time berth vacancy on [Chart Vacancy](/chart-vacancy) and check charting schedules on [Chart Times](/chart-times) to reserve confirmed berths up to 30 minutes before departure.

### 4. General Second Class (GS) Bio-Metric Queue
For completely waitlisted travellers, select Western Railway festival specials include 4 to 6 unreserved General Second Class (GS) coaches. At major hubs like Surat and Udhna, railways administer a bio-metric token queue system to prevent platform overcrowding, ensuring orderly boarding for unreserved ticket holders.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### Are ticket fares higher on Western Railway festival special trains?

Yes, 0-series festival special trains operate under Train on Special Fare (TOSF) tariffs, which add a flat 10% surcharge on Second Sitting and a 30% surcharge on Sleeper and AC classes over regular express base fares. Standard concession discounts do not apply on these festival specials.

### When does booking open for Western Railway Diwali special trains?

Booking for Western Railway festival special trains opens on September 28, 2026 at 08:00 AM IST on IRCTC and railway station PRS counters. Additional supplementary rakes are notified and opened on rolling 10 to 30-day schedules leading up to Diwali and Chhath Puja.

### Can you book Tatkal tickets on Western Railway festival specials?

Tatkal quota allocation depends on individual train circulars. While certain 0-series special trains provide standard Tatkal quotas opening at 10:00 AM for AC and 11:00 AM for Sleeper one day prior to departure, many festival specials run entirely on General Quota. Check quota availability on IRCTC.

### Can you travel on a special train with a waitlisted e-ticket?

No, passengers with fully waitlisted online e-tickets cannot board reserved coaches on festival special trains. Waitlisted e-tickets are automatically cancelled and refunded after chart preparation. Boarding without a confirmed ticket attracts a flat ₹500 fine under the Jan Vishwas Act plus excess fare under Section 138.

### Can you change your boarding station on a festival special train?

Yes, passengers can modify their boarding station online through the IRCTC website or app up to the generation of the second reservation chart, roughly 30 minutes prior to departure. Always confirm the updated boarding point on your PNR before boarding the train.

### Why do special trains take longer to reach Bihar than regular trains?

Festival special trains run on provisional timetables and yield track precedence to regular priority services like Vande Bharat, Rajdhani, and Superfast express trains. Passengers should anticipate possible operational delays and schedule sufficient travel buffer time.

### How do you find vacant berths after chart preparation on Western Railway?

You can inspect released seats on [Chart Vacancy](/chart-vacancy) and [Smart Seats](/). Once the first reservation chart is generated, unsold berths across all quotas convert into Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`) and can be booked on IRCTC or at station PRS counters until 30 minutes before departure.

### Which stations in Gujarat offer festival special trains to Bihar?

Festival special trains to Bihar depart primarily from Surat, Udhna Junction, Ahmedabad Junction, Sabarmati, Vadodara Junction, and Chhayapuri. Major intermediate boarding points across Gujarat include Vapi, Valsad, Bharuch, Anand, Nadiad, and Godhra.
