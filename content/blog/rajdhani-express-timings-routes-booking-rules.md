## TL;DR

Rajdhani Express reservation charts are finalized in two distinct phases: the first chart prepares approximately 8 to 10 hours before departure (or by 8:00 PM to 9:00 PM the previous evening for morning departures before 2:00 PM), while the second and final chart closes strictly 30 to 45 minutes before departure. Daytime Rajdhanis follow the morning Day Chart schedule, locking in unassigned 1AC coupe allocations and quota rebalancing. If waitlisted after charting, online e-tickets auto-cancel with a full refund, while vacant berths become bookable as Current Availability at a 10% base fare discount.

## When Is Rajdhani Express Chart Prepared?

**Rajdhani Express first reservation chart is prepared approximately 8 to 10 hours before scheduled departure from the originating station, while the second final chart is prepared 30 to 45 minutes before departure. For morning departures before 2:00 PM, the first chart is finalized the preceding evening between 8:00 PM and 9:00 PM IST.**

This dual-charting framework was introduced by the Ministry of Railways to give long-distance passengers adequate travel notice and streamline onboard catering logistics. The Centre for Railway Information Systems (CRIS) locks bookings, processes waitlists, and dispatches automated SMS confirmation alerts the moment the first chart generates.

After the first chart is prepared, unallocated emergency and official quotas return to the general booking pool. These berths become instantly bookable online through IRCTC or at PRS counters as Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`). Current booking remains live until the second and final chart locks 30 to 45 minutes prior to departure.

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Morning Departures (05:00 to 13:59)** | Previous Evening (20:00 to 21:00 IST) | 30 to 45 minutes before departure | Open overnight until second chart |
| **Afternoon Departures (14:00 to 17:59)** | Morning of journey (06:00 to 08:00 IST) | 30 to 45 minutes before departure | Open ~8 hours until second chart |
| **Evening Departures (18:00 to 23:59)** | Morning/Midday (09:00 to 11:00 IST) | 30 to 45 minutes before departure | Open ~8 to 10 hours until second chart |
| **Night Departures (00:00 to 04:59)** | Previous Evening (14:00 to 16:00 IST) | 30 to 45 minutes before departure | Open ~8 hours until second chart |

> **Insider Timing Tip:** For early departures like the Bilaspur or Howrah morning specials, the chart is finalized the previous evening at 8:00 PM. Check your PNR status before the 11:45 PM to 12:20 AM IRCTC daily maintenance window to know your final coach assignment.

## What Is a Rajdhani Express Day Chart and When Is It Prepared?

**A Rajdhani Express day chart is the reservation chart prepared for morning and afternoon daytime departures scheduled before 2:00 PM, finalized by 8:00 PM to 9:00 PM the previous evening. It allows passengers to know their berth confirmation status overnight and enables IRCTC catering staff to load exact breakfast and lunch rations.**

Day charts are vital for premium trains where hot meal logistics demand exact passenger counts hours ahead of departure. Base kitchens at major railheads like New Delhi, Mumbai Central, and Howrah receive passenger manifests immediately after the day chart locks. This prevents catering shortages and avoids food wastage.

For intermediate stations where a Rajdhani arrives the following morning or afternoon, a Remote Location chart is prepared. Remote Location charts finalize roughly 4 to 8 hours before the train is scheduled to arrive at that specific junction. Passengers boarding at stations like Kanpur Central, Pt. Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Junction, or Vadodara receive separate chart notifications based on that station's operational window.

## How Does 1AC Coupe and Cabin Allocation Work at Chart Preparation?

**AC First Class (1AC) coupe and cabin berths on Rajdhani Express are allocated manually by railway charting officers during first chart preparation, not at booking. Two-berth coupes are prioritized for couples traveling together on a single PNR, while four-berth cabins are assigned to families, larger groups, or solo travelers based on Emergency Quota approvals.**

When you book a 1AC ticket on IRCTC, your ticket shows status as `Confirmed` (CNF), but the coach and berth columns remain blank. This often alarms first-time travelers. Indian Railways deliberately withholds 1AC seat numbers until charting to accommodate protocol requirements, passenger demographics, and VIP movements.

### 1AC Allocation Hierarchy:

1. **Two-Berth Coupes:** Strictly reserved for pairs of passengers traveling on the same PNR, particularly husband-and-wife pairs, mother-child travelers, or high-ranking government officials.
2. **Four-Berth Cabins:** Allocated to parties of three or four passengers booking together, followed by solo male and female travelers segregated by gender where possible.
3. **Emergency Quota (EQ) Approvals:** VIPs, members of parliament, and senior railway officials receive allotment precedence before commercial general passengers.
4. **Offline Written Requisitions:** If you require a 2-berth coupe for medical or privacy reasons, submit a written requisition letter addressed to the Chief Commercial Manager (CCM) or Station Director at the originating station before 12:00 PM on the day of chart preparation.

Your exact coach number (such as H1) and berth assignment (Coupe A or Cabin B) are generated when the chart locks and dispatched via SMS to your registered mobile number.

## What Are the Current Food Catering Rules and Menu on Rajdhani Express?

**Meals are optional on Rajdhani Express, allowing passengers to opt out during booking to save between ₹300 and ₹500 per passenger depending on class and route duration. Onboard catering includes morning tea, breakfast, lunch, evening snacks, and multi-course dinners, prepped in IRCTC base kitchens and served hot directly at passenger berths.**

Catering fees are bundled into your ticket fare by default. When booking on IRCTC, check the catering preference dropdown. If you prefer carrying home-cooked food, selecting "No Food" immediately deducts the mandatory catering charge from your total payable fare.

If you opt out but later decide you want food onboard, notify the pantry manager. Pantry staff will serve standard meals subject to onboard availability, but you will pay the base meal tariff plus a flat ₹50 service charge per meal.

## How Does Dynamic Pricing (Flexi-Fare System) Work on Rajdhani Express?

**The Flexi-Fare dynamic pricing system on Rajdhani Express increases base fares by 10% for every 10% block of seats sold, capped at a maximum 1.5 times the base fare. Dynamic pricing applies strictly to 2AC and 3AC classes, while 1AC fares remain fixed at standard tariff rates with zero dynamic escalation.**

Dynamic pricing means ticket costs climb steadily as booking demand surges. When reservations open at 8:00 AM under the 60-day Advance Reservation Period (ARP), the initial 10% of seats in 2AC and 3AC sell at base tariff. Once that first block exhausts, fares step up automatically.

### Flexi-Fare Slabs (2AC and 3AC):

* **Initial 10% of Seats:** 1.0x (Standard Base Fare)
* **Next 10% of Seats (11% to 20%):** 1.1x Base Fare (+10%)
* **Next 10% of Seats (21% to 30%):** 1.2x Base Fare (+20%)
* **Next 10% of Seats (31% to 40%):** 1.3x Base Fare (+30%)
* **Next 10% of Seats (41% to 50%):** 1.4x Base Fare (+40%)
* **Remaining 50% of Seats (51% to 100%):** 1.5x Base Fare (Maximum 50% Cap)

Tatkal bookings on Rajdhani open at 10:00 AM one day prior to journey date. Tatkal tickets do not carry dynamic pricing, but they incur a statutory Tatkal surcharge. Because 1AC is exempt from both flexi-fare and Tatkal quotas, buying a 1AC ticket during peak festival periods can sometimes cost only marginally more than an inflated high-slab 2AC ticket.

## How to Book Confirmed Seats on a Waitlisted Rajdhani Express?

**To secure confirmed seats on fully booked Rajdhani Express trains, travelers can use split-ticketing algorithms to find contiguous confirmed berths on the same train, monitor Current Availability post-charting, or search intermediate station quotas. Unassigned emergency quotas released 8 hours before departure frequently create instant confirmed vacancies across 2AC and 3AC coaches.**

When direct tickets show Waiting List (WL), RAC, or Regret, multiple operational avenues remain open to help you travel legally:

### 1. Smart Seats Contiguous Split Booking
Direct routes from end to end may have exhausted quotas, while intermediate segments have open seats. LastBerth analyzes real-time seat availability to find contiguous split bookings on the same train rake. You might sit in 3AC coach B2 for the first half of the journey and transfer to coach B5 for the remaining distance without leaving the train.

### 2. Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`) Post-Charting
Once the first reservation chart generates roughly 8 hours before departure, all unutilized VIP quotas, defense quotas, and unbooked Tatkal seats return to the open pool. These tickets sell at a **10% discount on the base fare**. Monitor IRCTC or LastBerth right after chart preparation to book these confirmed seats.

### 3. Live Coach Layout on Chart Vacancy
Visit [Chart Vacancy](/chart-vacancy) after first chart preparation to view a live, coach-by-coach visual map of vacant berths. If a seat is vacant between intermediate halts, book it directly online before the second chart closes.

### 4. Origin Station Booking Hack
On long-distance Rajdhani trains, intermediate station quotas (RLWL and PQWL) are small and confirm slowly. Booking from the train's originating station under General Quota (GNWL) and changing your boarding point online up to 30 minutes before departure gives you higher confirmation odds.

## What Happens If a Rajdhani Waitlisted Ticket Does Not Confirm?

**If an online IRCTC e-ticket remains on the waiting list after final chart preparation, the reservation system automatically cancels the booking and initiates a 100% refund without clerkage deduction. Boarding a Rajdhani Express with a waitlisted e-ticket is strictly prohibited and attracts a Section 138 penalty of ₹500 plus fare differences.**

Under Indian Railways commercial rules, waitlisted ticket holders face strict boarding restrictions based on ticket type:

* **Online E-Tickets:** Automatically cancelled the moment the second chart is printed. The full fare refunds to your booking bank account or credit card within 3 to 5 business days. Your name does not appear on the onboard passenger manifest.
* **Physical PRS Counter Tickets:** Counter waitlist tickets do not auto-cancel. However, because Rajdhani Express trains are exclusively air-conditioned with zero unreserved general coaches, counter waitlist passengers cannot board unless the TTE officially regularizes the ticket against an RAC or vacant berth.
* **Partial Waitlist Rule:** If one passenger on a group PNR is Confirmed or RAC while others remain on Waiting List, the waitlisted passengers may board. However, all passengers on that PNR must share the allotted confirmed or RAC berths.
* **Penalties Under Jan Vishwas Act 2026:** Boarding any reserved coach on an unconfirmed e-ticket is treated as traveling without a valid ticket. Under Section 138 of the Railways Act, violators face a flat **₹500 penalty** plus the single journey fare from the train origin station, followed by compulsory deboarding at the next halt.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### When is Rajdhani Express chart preparation time?
The first reservation chart for Rajdhani Express trains is prepared 8 to 10 hours prior to departure from the source station. For early morning trains departing before 2:00 PM, the first chart is prepared the previous evening at approximately 8:00 PM to 9:00 PM. The second and final chart closes 30 to 45 minutes before departure.

### What is a day chart in Rajdhani Express?
A day chart is the reservation chart prepared for daytime Rajdhani departures scheduled to leave in the morning or early afternoon. Because these trains depart early, the railway finalizes the chart on the preceding night so passengers receive their coach and berth allocations before sleeping.

### Why is berth not allotted in Rajdhani 1AC after booking?
Berths and coupes in AC First Class (1AC) are not assigned at booking because railway charting officers manually allocate them during chart preparation. Coupes with 2 berths are allocated to couples or pairs, while 4-berth cabins are allotted to groups and families based on protocol and seniority.

### Can I opt out of food catering on Rajdhani Express?
Yes, catering is optional on Rajdhani Express. You can select "No Food" during the booking process on IRCTC, which reduces your total ticket price by deducting the catering fee. If you change your mind onboard, you can purchase meals from the pantry manager by paying standard tariffs plus a ₹50 service fee.

### Can I travel on Rajdhani Express with a waitlisted ticket?
No, passengers holding fully waitlisted online e-tickets cannot board the Rajdhani Express. Waitlisted e-tickets are automatically cancelled and refunded after chart preparation. Boarding with an unconfirmed ticket attracts a flat ₹500 fine under Section 138 plus journey fare differences.

### What is Current Availability (CURR_AVBL) in Rajdhani Express?
Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`) refers to confirmed berths that remain vacant after the first chart is prepared. These tickets become bookable online and at counters with a 10% discount on the base fare until the second chart is finalized roughly 30 minutes before departure.

### When does Tatkal booking open for Rajdhani Express?
Because Rajdhani Express trains consist exclusively of air-conditioned coaches (1AC, 2AC, and 3AC), Tatkal booking opens daily at 10:00 AM IST sharp, exactly one day prior to the train's departure from its originating station.

### Are meals free on Rajdhani Express if the train is delayed?
Yes, under Railway Board circulars, if a Rajdhani Express runs more than 2 hours behind schedule due to operational or weather delays, passengers are entitled to complimentary breakfast, lunch, or dinner from the onboard pantry at no extra charge.

### What is the maximum dynamic fare on Rajdhani Express?
Under the Flexi-Fare dynamic pricing system, base fares for 2AC and 3AC classes increase by 10% for every 10% of seats booked, capped at a maximum 1.5 times the standard base fare (a 50% increase). 1AC class is completely exempt from dynamic pricing.

### What is the fine for boarding Rajdhani Express without a confirmed ticket?
Under Section 138 of the Railways Act updated under the Jan Vishwas Act 2026, traveling on a Rajdhani Express without a valid confirmed ticket or with a cancelled waitlisted e-ticket incurs a flat penalty of ₹500 plus the single fare from the train origin station, followed by mandatory deboarding.
