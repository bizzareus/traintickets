## TL;DR
Looking for the fastest, most reliable way to travel across India? The Rajdhani Express is Indian Railways' flagship premium sleeper train, connecting state capitals directly to New Delhi. Running at speeds of up to 130 km/h with the highest track priority, it offers premium AC-only travel (1AC, 2AC, 3AC), optional high-quality catering, and dynamic ticket pricing. Tatkal bookings open at 10:00 AM daily, and waitlisted tickets progress through a strict queue system.

## What is the Rajdhani Express and How Does It Work?

**The Rajdhani Express is a premium, fully air-conditioned long-distance train network operated by Indian Railways that connects New Delhi with major state capitals. Introduced in 1969, it operates with the highest track priority on the network, minimizing delays and maintaining speeds of up to 130–140 km/h.**

The word "Rajdhani" translates to "The Capital" in Hindi, which perfectly describes its primary mission: linking the capital city of India to the rest of the country. These trains are designed for long-distance overnight travel, meaning they consist entirely of sleeper coaches. They bypass smaller stations and only stop at major railway junctions, making them significantly faster than standard superfast express trains.

Every Rajdhani Express is equipped with modern LHB (Linke Hofmann Busch) coaches, which offer superior safety, smoother rides, and excellent sound insulation. These trains operate with three primary travel classes:
*   **AC First Class (1AC):** Lockable 2-berth coupes or 4-berth cabins with personal attendants, utmost privacy, and premium meals.
*   **AC 2 Tier (2AC):** Open bays with 4 berths (two upper, two lower) and 2 side berths, equipped with privacy curtains.
*   **AC 3 Tier (3AC):** High-density configuration with 6 berths per bay and 2 side berths, offering the most economical way to experience Rajdhani travel.

## What are the Food Catering Rules and Menu on Rajdhani Express?

**Catering charges are bundled by default into the Rajdhani Express ticket fare, but passengers can opt out of meals during booking to save on costs. The onboard pantry serves premium, multi-course meals including morning tea, breakfast, lunch, high tea, and dinner, customized for veg and non-veg preferences.**

The food on the Rajdhani Express is prepared in base kitchens monitored by the IRCTC and served hot at your seat. If you choose to opt out of the catering service during booking, your ticket price will be reduced by the catering charge amount. However, if you change your mind onboard, you can still purchase meals from the pantry staff, though they will charge a flat ₹50 service fee in addition to the standard meal cost.

## When Does Rajdhani Express Booking and Tatkal Timings Open?

**General ticket bookings for the Rajdhani Express open 120 days in advance (excluding the date of journey). Because Rajdhani consists entirely of air-conditioned classes, the Tatkal quota booking window opens daily at 10:00 AM sharp, exactly one day before the train starts from its originating station.**

Due to the extreme popularity of these trains, tickets sell out within seconds of the booking window opening. If you fail to secure a general ticket, the Tatkal window is your next best bet. However, keep in mind that Rajdhani fares are subject to **Dynamic Pricing (Flexi-Fare)**. Under this scheme, the base fare increases by 10% for every 10% of seats sold, capping at a maximum of 1.5 times the standard base fare. Tatkal bookings do not have dynamic pricing, but they charge a hefty flat premium instead.

### Key Booking Parameters to Keep in Mind:
1.  **AC Class Quotas Apply:** Tatkal booking starts at **10:00 AM** (not 11:00 AM, which is reserved for non-AC Sleeper classes).
2.  **No Concessions:** Senior citizen concessions and other general discounts do not apply to Rajdhani Tatkal tickets.
3.  **Fast Checkout is Mandatory:** Use fast payment methods like the IRCTC e-Wallet to complete transactions before the seats run out.

## How to Book Confirmed Rajdhani Seats When Waitlisted?

**When Rajdhani tickets show a waitlist, check your confirmation chances on LastBerth. If your online waitlisted ticket does not confirm before the train departs, IRCTC will cancel it and issue an automatic refund. You can secure a confirmed seat using LastBerth's segment booking or current availability options.**

Waitlists are a common hurdle for Rajdhani passengers. The WL full form is Waiting List. When you book a ticket and it is waitlisted, you are placed in a queue. Your ticket progresses through a standard cycle:

$$\text{WL (Waiting List)} \longrightarrow \text{RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation)} \longrightarrow \text{Confirmed}$$

During this progression, a status like `WL/10` means you are 10th in line. If your ticket only clears to RAC, you are guaranteed a seat to sit on, but not a full berth to sleep. If your online e-ticket remains fully waitlisted (e.g., `WL/2`) after the final chart is prepared, it is automatically cancelled by the system, and a refund is sent to your bank account. You cannot travel on a train with a waitlisted e-ticket.

A current available ticket is a **fully confirmed seat** that goes on sale right after chart preparation (usually 4 hours before departure). It remains open for booking online or at the station until 30 minutes before the train departs.

## Rajdhani Express Booking Questions (FAQ)

### What is the full form of WL in Indian Railways?
**WL full form is Waiting List**. It indicates that all confirmed and RAC seats on the train are fully booked, and you have been placed in a queue. Your ticket will only confirm if other passengers cancel their bookings.

### Is catering free in Rajdhani Express?
No, catering is not free. The catering charges are included in the ticket fare by default. However, you can opt out of meals during the booking process on the IRCTC website or app, which will reduce the ticket cost accordingly.

### What is dynamic pricing in Rajdhani Express?
Dynamic pricing (or Flexi-Fare) means the ticket price increases as seats are sold. For every 10% of seats booked in a class, the base fare increases by 10%, up to a maximum cap of 50% extra (1.5x of the base fare).

### When does Tatkal booking open for Rajdhani Express?
Since the Rajdhani Express only has AC classes (1AC, 2AC, 3AC), the Tatkal booking window opens daily at **10:00 AM sharp**, one day prior to the train's departure from its originating station.

### Can I travel on a waitlisted Rajdhani ticket?
If you booked an online e-ticket and it remains on the waiting list after chart preparation, you cannot travel with it; it is automatically cancelled and refunded. If you have a physical waitlisted ticket purchased from a station counter, you may board the train but must consult the TTE for any vacant berths.

### What is a current available ticket on Rajdhani?
A **current available ticket** is a fully confirmed ticket with assigned coach and berth numbers that becomes available for booking after the reservation chart is prepared. This opens 4 hours before the train's scheduled departure and closes 30 minutes before departure.

### What is the Howrah to New Delhi Rajdhani train number?
The Howrah to New Delhi Rajdhani runs as **12301** (Howrah to New Delhi) and **12302** (New Delhi to Howrah). See the [12301 route and chart times](/chart-times/12301-rajdhani-expres-chart-times). There is also a second Howrah Rajdhani, 12305/12306, via a different route.

### How many Rajdhani Express trains are there?
There are around 25 Rajdhani Express services connecting New Delhi with state capitals such as Kolkata, Mumbai, Chennai, Bengaluru, Guwahati, Thiruvananthapuram, Jammu and Ranchi. See the table above for the main routes and train numbers.

### Which is the fastest Rajdhani Express?
The Mumbai and Howrah Rajdhani trains are among the fastest, running at up to 130 km/h with very few stops. Actual journey time depends on the route distance and number of halts, which you can see on each train's schedule linked above.
