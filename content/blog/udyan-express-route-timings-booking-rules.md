## What Is Udyan Express and What Route Does It Run?

**The Udyan Express is an iconic daily express train connecting Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CSMT) in Mumbai with KSR Bengaluru (SBC), covering 1,134 kilometers across Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Andhra Pradesh in 23 to 24 hours. Named after Bengaluru nickname as the Garden City, Udyan represents one of South-Western Railways premier interstate links.**

Inaugurated to bridge the commercial capital of India with its southern technology hub, the Udyan Express operates daily in both directions. Train number 11301 runs from Mumbai to Bengaluru, while train number 11302 runs in the reverse direction from Bengaluru to Mumbai. The train negotiates the scenic Bhor Ghat incline between Karjat and Lonavala, traverses the Deccan plateau through Pune, Daund, Kurduvadi, and Solapur, and enters Karnataka via Kalaburagi (Gulbarga) and Wadi before cutting through Andhra Pradesh junctions like Guntakal and returning south into Bengaluru.

Because it connects major commercial centres, educational institutions, and pilgrimage shrines like Mantralayam Road and Ganagapur Road, the Udyan Express witnesses intense passenger traffic throughout the year. Regular quotas frequently enter high waiting lists weeks before departure.

## When Is Udyan Express Chart Prepared?

**Udyan Express first reservation chart preparation depends on departure time: for Mumbai departure 11301 at 08:10 IST, the first chart is finalized the preceding evening between 20:00 and 21:00 IST. For Bengaluru departure 11302 at 20:45 IST, the first chart is prepared between 11:30 AM and 13:00 IST on departure day.**

Under Indian Railways dual-charting framework, charts finalize in two distinct stages:

1. **First Chart Finalization:**
   - **Train 11301 (Morning Departure):** Because the train leaves Mumbai CSMT at 08:10 AM, railway rules mandate that morning departures before 14:00 finalize charts the previous night between 20:00 and 21:00 IST. Passengers know their confirmation status before sleeping.
   - **Train 11302 (Evening Departure):** Leaving KSR Bengaluru at 20:45 PM, the first chart generates approximately 8 to 10 hours before departure, between 11:30 AM and 13:00 IST.
2. **Second & Final Chart:** Closes strictly **30 to 45 minutes** before scheduled train departure from the originating station. For 11301, this occurs around 07:30 AM at CSMT; for 11302, it occurs around 20:00 PM at SBC.
3. **Remote Location Charting (RLWL):** Intermediate commercial hubs like Pune Junction, Solapur Junction, and Guntakal Junction possess dedicated Remote Location Waitlist (RLWL) quotas. Charts for these quotas generate approximately 4 to 6 hours before the train reaches the respective boarding station.
4. **Current Availability Booking Window:** The moment the first chart generates, all unassigned emergency, defense, and VIP quotas return to the general booking pool as Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`) at a **10% discount on base fare**. These seats remain bookable online and at counters until the second chart closes.

## How Does Tatkal Booking Work on Udyan Express?

**Tatkal booking for Udyan Express opens exactly one day prior to train origin departure at 10:00 AM IST for AC classes (2A and 3A) and at 11:00 AM IST for Sleeper class (SL). Tickets carry a maximum limit of 4 passengers per PNR and require mandatory Aadhaar OTP authentication.**

Because Udyan Express connects two major business metropolitan areas, Tatkal quotas sell out in 60 to 90 seconds. To secure tickets successfully, keep the following operational rules in mind:

- **Origin Station Departure Benchmark:** The 1-day Tatkal window opens based on when the train leaves its originating terminal (CSMT or SBC). If you board 11301 at Pune or Solapur on Day 1 afternoon, Tatkal booking still opens at 10:00 AM / 11:00 AM the day before CSMT departure.
- **Class-Wise Window Breakdown:**
  - **AC Classes (2A, 3A):** Opens at **10:00 AM IST** sharp. AC First Class (1A) is strictly excluded from Tatkal across all trains.
  - **Sleeper Class (SL):** Opens at **11:00 AM IST** sharp.
- **Passenger Allocation Limits:** Tatkal limits every PNR to **4 passengers**, unlike General Quota which permits 6 travelers.
- **Aadhaar Verification Rule:** Primary booking accounts must be Aadhaar-authenticated, and users must complete dynamic SMS OTP checks during checkout.
- **Cancellation Forfeiture:** Confirmed Tatkal tickets yield a **0% refund** upon voluntary cancellation. If your Tatkal ticket remains waitlisted after final charting, IRCTC automatically cancels it and refunds the fare with nominal clerkage deductions.

**To get confirmed seats on a fully booked Udyan Express, passengers can use split-ticketing to combine contiguous confirmed berths on the same train, monitor 10% discounted Current Availability berths post-charting, book from intermediate station quotas, or utilize the 4-hour boarding station modification rule.**

When direct tickets from Mumbai CSMT to KSR Bengaluru display Waiting List (WL 50+) or Regret, these four legal strategies help you secure travel:

1. **Smart Seats Contiguous Split Ticketing:** End-to-end quota on 11301 may be waitlisted, but intermediate portions frequently have empty berths. 4. **Intermediate Quota Booking:** Remote Location quotas (RLWL) from stations like Pune (PUNE) or Daund (DD) often clear faster than General Waiting List (GNWL) from CSMT. You can book from the remote station and adjust your boarding station up to 4 hours before train departure.

## What Are the Boarding Rules and Fines on Udyan Express?

**Passengers must hold a valid confirmed or RAC ticket to board reserved coaches on Udyan Express. Under Section 138 of the Railways Act updated by the Jan Vishwas Act 2026, traveling on an auto-cancelled waitlisted e-ticket attracts a flat penalty of ₹500 plus the single fare difference from the train origin.**

Indian Railways passenger regulations enforce strict onboard rules:

- **Waitlisted E-Tickets Auto-Cancellation:** If your online e-ticket does not confirm after final chart preparation, it cancels automatically. The reservation system initiates a 100% refund to your source payment method within 3 to 5 business days. Waitlisted e-ticket holders are barred from boarding reserved Sleeper or AC coaches.
- **Physical PRS Counter Waitlist Rules:** Passengers with waitlisted tickets purchased at physical railway station counters may board non-AC general unreserved coaches, but they cannot occupy reserved berths unless regularized by the onboard TTE.
- **Jan Vishwas Act 2026 Penalty:** Boarding reserved Sleeper or AC coaches on an unconfirmed e-ticket or general unreserved ticket is treated as ticketless travel. TTEs levy a flat **₹500 statutory fine** plus the single fare from Mumbai CSMT or KSR Bengaluru, followed by deboarding at the next scheduled halt.
- **Night Sleeping Hours (10:00 PM to 06:00 AM):** Middle berths may only be raised between 22:00 and 06:00 IST. During daytime hours (06:00 to 22:00), middle berths must be folded down so lower berth occupants can sit comfortably.
- **Two-Stop Rule:** The onboard TTE cannot forfeit or reassign your reserved berth until the train passes **two scheduled commercial halts** or 60 minutes after departure.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### What is Udyan Express train number and route?

The Udyan Express runs under train number 11301 from Mumbai CSMT to KSR Bengaluru, and train number 11302 in the return direction from KSR Bengaluru to Mumbai CSMT, operating daily across 1,134 kilometers.

### When is Udyan Express chart prepared for 11301 from Mumbai?

Because train 11301 departs Mumbai CSMT at 08:10 AM, its first reservation chart is prepared the previous evening between 20:00 and 21:00 IST (8:00 PM to 9:00 PM). The second and final chart is finalized 30 to 45 minutes before departure.

### When is Udyan Express chart prepared for 11302 from Bengaluru?

Train 11302 departs KSR Bengaluru at 20:45 PM, so its first reservation chart is prepared between 11:30 AM and 13:00 IST on the day of departure. The final chart closes 30 to 45 minutes before train departure.

### Does Udyan Express have dynamic pricing or flexi-fare?

No. The Udyan Express operates on standard Mail and Express telescopic fare slabs without dynamic flexi-fare price escalations. Base fares in 2A and 3A remain constant throughout the advance reservation period.

### Can I book Tatkal tickets on Udyan Express?

Yes. Tatkal booking opens daily at 10:00 AM IST for AC classes (2A and 3A) and at 11:00 AM IST for Sleeper class (SL), one day prior to the train departure from its originating station. AC First Class (1A) does not have a Tatkal quota.

### Is pantry food available on Udyan Express?

Yes. Udyan Express has an attached pantry car serving Rail Neer bottled water (₹15), tea/coffee (₹10), breakfast (₹40–₹50), and standard veg/non-veg thalis (₹80–₹130). Passengers can also order food through IRCTC e-Catering at major stops like Pune and Solapur.

### Can I travel on Udyan Express with a waitlisted e-ticket?

No. Fully waitlisted online e-tickets automatically cancel and refund after final chart preparation. Boarding reserved coaches on a waitlisted e-ticket is illegal and incurs a flat ₹500 penalty plus single journey fare differences under Section 138 of the Railways Act.

### What is Current Availability on Udyan Express?

Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`) refers to confirmed berths that remain unsold after first chart preparation. They become bookable online on IRCTC and at PRS counters at a 10% base fare discount until 30 minutes before train departure.

### What are the main intermediate halts between Mumbai and Bengaluru on Udyan Express?

Major intermediate junctions include Dadar, Kalyan, Lonavala, Pune, Daund, Kurduvadi, Solapur, Kalaburagi, Wadi, Raichur, Mantralayam Road, Adoni, Guntakal, Anantapur, Dharmavaram, Hindupur, and Yelahanka.

### What is the fine for traveling without a confirmed ticket on Udyan Express?

Under Section 138 of the Railways Act (Jan Vishwas Act 2026), traveling in reserved coaches without a valid confirmed ticket carries a flat penalty of ₹500 plus the single journey fare from the train origin station, followed by compulsory deboarding at the next halt.
