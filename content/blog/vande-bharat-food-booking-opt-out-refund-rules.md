## Is Catering Mandatory in Vande Bharat Express Trains?

**No, catering is not mandatory on Vande Bharat Express trains. Passengers can opt out of the onboard food service while booking their tickets on IRCTC. If you choose not to receive meals during your journey, the catering charges are deducted from your total ticket fare.**

When Indian Railways launched Vande Bharat services, catering charges were initially bundled automatically. However, based on passenger feedback, IRCTC updated the rules to make onboard dining completely optional. When booking online, the system displays a popup asking you to select your food preference: Veg, Non-Veg, or "No Food" (Opt-Out).

If you are travelling with a child or have specific dietary restrictions, opting out allows you to bring your own home-cooked food. This option is available across all AC Chair Car (CC) and Executive Class (EC) coaches on every Vande Bharat route.

## Can You Order Food Onboard Vande Bharat if You Opted Out?

**Yes, you can order meals onboard a Vande Bharat train even if you opted out during booking, provided food is available. However, IRCTC charges an extra ₹50 convenience penalty fee per meal on top of the standard catering rate for onboard orders.**

If you change your mind during the journey, you must inform the coach service staff or the Train Captain. Because the pantry staff only carries a limited number of extra meals, onboard requests are subject to availability. 

The ₹50 convenience charge is a penalty for last-minute ordering, designed to discourage passengers from opting out to save money and then demanding meals onboard. The service provider accepts payment via UPI or cash, and must issue a printed bill for the transaction.

## What is the Vande Bharat Food Charges Refund and Cancellation Policy?

**If you cancel your Vande Bharat ticket, the food charges are fully refunded along with the ticket fare, subject to standard IRCTC cancellation fees. If the train is delayed by two hours or more, passengers are entitled to free emergency meals under Railway Board catering policy (while a full ticket cancellation refund requires a 3+ hour delay).**

To get a refund for food charges when cancelling a partially confirmed or waitlisted ticket, you must file a Ticket Deposit Receipt (TDR) online before chart preparation. If a waitlisted online e-ticket does not confirm, the system processes a full refund automatically, including the catering portion, within 3 to 5 working days.

## How to Opt Out of Meals During Booking vs Post-Booking on Vande Bharat

**To opt out during booking on IRCTC, select "No Food" under catering preferences on the passenger details page to save ₹150 to ₹350 per passenger. Once the ticket is booked, you cannot change your preference online; post-booking meal additions must be requested onboard subject to pantry availability and a ₹50 surcharge.**

During checkout:
1. Enter your passenger name, age, and berth preference on IRCTC.
2. In the **Food Choice** dropdown, select **"No Food"** instead of Veg or Non-Veg.
3. The catering charge is immediately deducted from the final payable amount.
4. If you later decide you want meals, you must pay the onboard catering supervisor directly.

## What Food is Provided in Executive Class vs Chair Car on Vande Bharat Express?

**Executive Class (EC) offers premium meal options including welcome drinks, high-tea snacks, and multi-course meals served with superior cutlery, while AC Chair Car (CC) provides standard vegetarian or non-vegetarian boxed meals and tea or coffee.**

The meal inclusions vary based on journey timing:
- **Morning Departures (6:00 AM – 9:00 AM):** Welcome drink, morning tea or coffee with biscuits, followed by breakfast (upma, cutlets, idli-vada, or omelette).
- **Afternoon Departures (12:00 PM – 2:00 PM):** Full lunch thali with paneer/chicken, dal, rice, parathas, curd, and dessert.
- **Evening Departures (4:00 PM – 6:00 PM):** High tea including samosa/kachori, roasted nuts, sweet muffin, and tea/coffee.
- **Night Departures (7:30 PM – 10:00 PM):** Full dinner similar to lunch.

Full nutritional and itemized price details for all these meals are available on our **[IRCTC Train Food Menu](/irctc-train-food-menu)**.

## Is Food Free on Vande Bharat Trains if Delayed by 2 Hours or More?

**Yes, Indian Railways rules mandate that if a premium train like Vande Bharat, Rajdhani, or Shatabdi is delayed by 2 hours or more, free meals, refreshments, and bottled water must be provided to ALL passengers on board, regardless of whether they opted out of catering during booking.**

Key delay catering rules:
- **Delay Threshold:** Must exceed 2 hours from originating station or key junctions.
- **Meal Type:** Served based on timing (Breakfast between 7–10 AM, Lunch 12–2 PM, Tea/Snacks 4–6 PM, Dinner 8–10 PM).
- **No Opt-Out Penalty:** Even passengers who selected "No Food" receive emergency delay catering free of cost.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### Is food free in Vande Bharat?
No, food is not free. Catering charges are pre-included in the ticket price. If you do not want the food service, you must choose the "No Food" option during booking to get a discount.

### How much can I save by opting out of food in Vande Bharat?
You can save between ₹150 and ₹350 per ticket. The exact savings depend on the route, class of travel (Chair Car vs. Executive Class), and the number of meals scheduled for that journey.

### Where can I find official Vande Bharat meal prices online?
You can view the full official IRCTC meal rates, including breakfast, lunch, and dinner prices for Vande Bharat and other express trains, on the LastBerth [IRCTC Train Food Menu](/irctc-train-food-menu).

### Can I buy food from local stations instead of Vande Bharat catering?
Vande Bharat trains only stop for 2 to 5 minutes at intermediate stations, making it risky to deboard to buy food. If you opt out of catering, it is recommended to carry your own food or use IRCTC e-catering services to deliver food to your coach.

### Is catering optional in Vande Bharat Sleeper trains?
Yes, catering is optional on all Vande Bharat trains, including Sleeper services. The same opt-out rules and onboard surcharge guidelines apply to both sleeper and chair car coaches.

### What is the extra charge for ordering food onboard Vande Bharat?
If you opted out during booking but order food from the onboard staff, you will have to pay the standard meal cost plus an additional ₹50 convenience fee per meal.

### Can I cancel my food choice after booking the ticket?
No. Once a ticket is booked on IRCTC, you cannot change your catering choice from "No Food" to "Food" or vice-versa online. Any changes must be handled onboard with the pantry staff, subject to availability and the ₹50 surcharge.

### Do children get free food on Vande Bharat trains?
No. If you book a separate seat for a child (aged 5 to 11) and pay the applicable fare, catering charges are included in the ticket. If you do not buy a separate seat, no food is provided unless ordered separately onboard.

### What happens to catering charges if the train is delayed?
If a Vande Bharat train is delayed by two hours or more, Indian Railways provides free breakfast, lunch, or dinner (depending on the time of day) to all passengers, regardless of whether they opted out of catering during booking.

### How do I check if my Vande Bharat ticket is confirmed?
You can enter your 10-digit PNR number on LastBerth's [PNR Status Search & Direct Booking](/) to check if your booking status has moved from WL or RAC to Confirmed. Waitlisted online e-tickets are automatically cancelled at chart preparation.
