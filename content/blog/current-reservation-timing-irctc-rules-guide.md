## TL;DR

- **What is Current Reservation?** It is IRCTC's official system for booking unbooked or cancelled seats after the first reservation chart has been prepared.
- **When does it open?** Opens immediately after 1st chart preparation (around 4 to 8 hours before train departure) and closes 30 minutes before departure (2nd chart preparation).
- **Is it a confirmed ticket?** Yes! A `"current available ticket"` is a **fully confirmed seat** with allocated coach and berth numbers.
- **Are there discounts?** Yes, Indian Railways often offers a 10% to 50% discount on base fares for unbooked berths under current reservation.
- **Where to book?** Online via the IRCTC portal/app or at station PRS counters.

## What Is Current Reservation in IRCTC and How Does It Work?

Current Reservation in IRCTC allows passengers to book remaining vacant berths after chart preparation until 30 minutes before train departure. It operates on a first-come, first-served basis for fully confirmed seats left unbooked by original quota holders or cancelled after final charting.

When regular advance bookings close and the first chart is generated, unsold berths from various quotas (such as General, Ladies, Senior Citizen, or Tatkal) are released into the current reservation pool. Passengers looking for last-minute travel can book these berths directly with guaranteed seat numbers.

When booking regular tickets earlier, waitlisted tickets follow a confirmation progression. For reference, **WL full form is Waiting List**. The waitlist progression works as follows:

$$\text{WL (Waiting List)} \rightarrow \text{RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation)} \rightarrow \text{Confirmed}$$

If waitlisted e-tickets fail to reach RAC or confirmed status by chart preparation, they are automatically cancelled by the system, and a full refund minus clerkage is issued. Any vacant berths generated through these cancellations become available under Current Reservation.

## When Does Current Reservation Open and Close in IRCTC?

Current Reservation opens immediately after 1st chart preparation (typically 4 to 8 hours before departure from originating station) and closes 30 minutes prior to departure when the 2nd final chart is generated.

Here is the exact timeline for Current Reservation ticket availability:

| Stage / Milestone | Timing Window | Seat Status & Availability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1st Chart Preparation** | 4 to 8 hours before departure | Unsold quota berths released to Current Reservation pool |
| **Current Booking Window** | Post 1st chart to 30 mins pre-departure | Confirmed tickets open for booking on IRCTC app, web & PRS counters |
| **2nd Chart Preparation** | 30 minutes before departure | Current reservation closes; remaining vacant berths handed to TTE |

Passengers can track exact station chart preparation schedules using [Chart Times](/chart-times) to know precisely when current reservation berths will go live for their train.

## How to Book Current Reservation Tickets Online and at PRS Counters

You can book Current Reservation tickets online through the IRCTC website/app by searching for your train after chart preparation, selecting the train, and choosing berths marked as "CURR_AVBL" or "CURRENT AVAILABLE". Alternatively, you can buy them directly at railway station PRS booking counters.

To book online:
1. Log in to the IRCTC Rail Connect App or web portal after chart preparation.
2. Enter your source and destination stations.
3. Select your train. If chart preparation is complete and vacant seats exist, availability will show as **CURR_AVBL** (Current Available).
4. Enter passenger details and complete payment to instantly receive your confirmed coach and berth assignment.

If you are already at the station, you can also view vacant berths on visual coach maps using [Chart Vacancy](/chart-vacancy) or check live berth allocations via [Coach Journey Lookup](/seat-status) before approaching the PRS counter or onboard TTE.

## What Are the Discount Fares and Cancellation Rules for Current Tickets?

Current reservation tickets are offered at a discounted rate of up to 10% to 50% off base fares for specific trains, encouraging passengers to utilize empty capacity. However, cancellation rules are strict: confirmed current tickets cancelled before departure incur standard flat clerkage or cancellation fees.

Key financial and cancellation rules include:
- **Base Fare Discount:** Up to 10% base fare discount applied automatically by IRCTC on select express and superfast trains.
- **Cancellation Deadline:** Tickets can be cancelled online up to 30 minutes before train departure (before 2nd chart preparation).
- **Post 2nd Chart Rules:** No refund or online cancellation is allowed after 2nd chart preparation; TDR must be filed for valid exceptions (e.g., train cancellation or major delay).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### When does current reservation open in IRCTC?
Current reservation opens immediately after the 1st reservation chart is prepared, which is typically 4 to 8 hours before the train's scheduled departure from its originating station.

### Is a current reservation ticket guaranteed to be confirmed?
Yes, a current reservation ticket (marked as CURR_AVBL) is always a fully confirmed ticket with an allocated coach and berth number.

### Can you cancel a current reservation ticket for a refund?
Yes, you can cancel a current reservation ticket online up to 30 minutes before the train's scheduled departure. Standard cancellation charges will apply.

### What is the difference between Tatkal and Current Reservation?
Tatkal booking opens 1 day prior to travel at 10:00 AM (AC) or 11:00 AM (Non-AC) with extra premium charges. Current Reservation opens 4 hours before departure post-chart prep, offers confirmed unsold berths, and often includes base fare discounts.
