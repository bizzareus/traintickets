## What Are the Key Diwali 2026 Dates and Festival Travel Schedules?

**Diwali 2026 festivities span five days from November 6 to November 10, 2026, with the main Lakshmi Puja celebrated on Sunday, November 8. Outbound departures from metro hubs peak between November 5 and November 7, while return travel surges from November 9 to November 12, directly merging into Chhath Puja.**

The five primary days of Diwali determine nationwide passenger congestion across Indian Railways corridors:

- **Day 1: Dhanteras (Friday, November 6, 2026).** Heavy departures start on Thursday evening as working professionals, families, and students begin traveling home.
- **Day 2: Choti Diwali / Naraka Chaturdashi (Saturday, November 7, 2026).** Peak intercity and regional travel window before the main festival night.
- **Day 3: Diwali / Lakshmi Puja (Sunday, November 8, 2026).** The central festival day. Most passengers aim to reach their destinations by Sunday morning.
- **Day 4: Govardhan Puja / Annakut (Monday, November 9, 2026).** Outbound travel slows while early return journeys to industrial hubs commence.
- **Day 5: Bhai Dooj / Yama Dwitiya (Tuesday, November 10, 2026).** Concludes the core Diwali calendar, triggering massive return travel to metros and immediately initiating the four-day Chhath Puja rush.

Because millions of passengers travel simultaneously, trunk routes connecting Delhi, Mumbai, Pune, Gujarat, Bengaluru, and Chennai to Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, Odisha, and West Bengal face acute seat shortages.

## How Much Extra Fare Do 0-Series Diwali Special Trains Charge?

**0-series Diwali special trains charge Train on Special Fare (TOSF) rates, adding a flat 10% surcharge on Second Sitting (2S) and a 30% surcharge on Sleeper, AC 3-Tier Economy (3E), AC 3-Tier (3A), and AC 2-Tier (2A) base fares. Concession discounts are completely suspended, while 5% GST applies to all AC classes.**

Unlike Suvidha trains where dynamic pricing scales up to 3x base fare in 20% slabs, standard 0-series festival special fares remain fixed throughout the entire booking lifecycle from the first seat to the last.

Official pricing rules governing 0-series festival special trains:

- **Second Sitting (2S):** Carries a 10% surcharge (approximately ₹15 to ₹30 additional per ticket).
- **Sleeper Class (SL):** Subject to a 30% surcharge over standard Mail/Express base fares.
- **AC Tiers (3E, 3A, 2A, 1AC):** Carry a 30% surcharge subject to minimum and maximum Tatkal-equivalent caps, plus 5% GST.
- **Suspension of Concessions:** Senior citizen, student, patient, and postal concessions are not granted on festival special trains.
- **Catering Services:** Catering charges are not included in base ticket prices. Meals and refreshments from the onboard pantry car or e-catering are billed separately at standard IRCTC rates.
- **Bedroll Provision:** Complete bedrolls (two bedsheets, pillow, blanket, face towel) are provided free of additional charge across all AC coaches (1A, 2A, 3A, 3E). Sleeper coaches do not provide linen.

## Can You Travel on Diwali Special Trains with Unreserved or UTS Tickets?

**You can travel on Diwali special trains with an unreserved general ticket only in designated General Second Class (GS) coaches. Boarding reserved Sleeper or AC coaches with an unreserved counter ticket or UTS mobile pass is illegal and attracts a flat ₹500 penalty under the Jan Vishwas Act 2026 plus single fare under Section 138 of the Railways Act.**

To accommodate lower-income workers and rural families traveling home for Diwali, Indian Railways attaches between 4 and 8 unreserved General (GS) coaches to most 0-series rakes. In addition, dedicated all-unreserved **Jan Sadharan Specials** and **Amrit Bharat Specials** operate without any reserved coaches.

Key rules governing unreserved festive travel:

1. **UTS on Mobile App Booking:** Passengers can purchase unreserved paperless tickets using the official UTS mobile application within 20 km of the boarding station, avoiding hours in station booking counter queues.
2. **General Ticket Coach Restriction:** General tickets purchased at PRS counters or via UTS are exclusively valid for coaches marked **GS**, **GEN**, or **UR** on coach exteriors.
3. **No Corridor Standing in Reserved Coaches:** Passengers holding unreserved tickets are strictly prohibited from standing in Sleeper or AC coach corridors, sitting near washroom vestibules, or sharing berths with relatives holding confirmed reservations.
4. **Section 138 Penalties under Jan Vishwas Act 2026:** Under the Jan Vishwas Act 2026, traveling in a higher class than authorized incurs a **flat ₹500 administrative penalty** plus the exact difference between the unreserved fare and the Sleeper or AC fare to your destination.
5. **Deboarding Mandate:** If no vacant berth can be regularized on the TTE's Handheld Terminal (HHT), the railway administration is legally mandated to deboard unauthorized passengers at the next scheduled halt.

## What Are the Cancellation Charges and Refund Rules for Special Trains?

**Cancellation refunds on confirmed Diwali special train tickets follow standard Indian Railways graduated slabs: flat clerkage applies until 48 hours prior to departure, 25% is deducted between 48 and 12 hours, and 50% is deducted between 12 and 4 hours. No refunds are granted after chart preparation.**

Because the 30% special surcharge is embedded directly into the base ticket fare, percentage deductions result in proportionally higher rupee forfeitures compared to regular express trains.

The table below summarizes official cancellation slabs across confirmed, RAC, and waitlisted tickets:

| Ticket Status | Cancellation Timing | Refund Eligibility | Deduction Applied |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Confirmed Ticket** | More than 48 hours before scheduled departure | Full fare minus flat clerkage fee | ₹240 (1A), ₹200 (2A), ₹180 (3A/3E), ₹120 (SL), ₹60 (2S) |
| **Confirmed Ticket** | Between 48 hours and 12 hours before departure | 75% of total ticket fare | 25% of ticket fare (subject to flat clerkage minimum) |
| **Confirmed Ticket** | Between 12 hours and 4 hours (up to 1st chart) | 50% of total ticket fare | 50% of ticket fare (subject to flat clerkage minimum) |
| **Confirmed Ticket** | Less than 4 hours or after chart preparation | Zero refund | 100% forfeiture of ticket fare |
| **RAC / Counter Waitlist** | Up to 30 minutes before departure | Full fare minus nominal clerkage | ₹60 flat clerkage fee per passenger |
| **Online Waitlist (e-Ticket)** | Automatically cancelled after chart preparation | 100% automated refund to original source | Zero clerkage deducted (gateway fees retained) |

_For partially confirmed group bookings (where some passengers are confirmed and others remain waitlisted), an online Ticket Deposit Receipt (TDR) must be filed up to 30 minutes before scheduled train departure to obtain a refund for the unconfirmed passengers._

## How Do You Get Confirmed Seats When All Diwali Trains Show REGRET?

**When direct Diwali festival trains display REGRET or extreme waitlists, you can secure confirmed travel by discovering split-berth routes on LastBerth Smart Seats, booking from originating stations with boarding point modifications, or booking Current Availability seats released after chart finalization.**

When standard waiting lists exceed CRIS safety caps, IRCTC flags the train as `REGRET`, preventing further reservations. However, four legitimate methods allow passengers to bypass this bottleneck:

### 1. Split-Segment Contiguous Booking via Smart Seats
Indian Railways partitions train inventory into distinct intermediate quota pools (`GNWL`, `RLWL`, `PQWL`). While a direct booking from New Delhi to Patna may show `REGRET`, berths often remain open from New Delhi to Kanpur, and from Kanpur to Patna on the exact same train.

Use [Finding Smart Seats](/) on LastBerth to uncover contiguous split-segment seats, enabling you to travel on a single train rake without needing to deboard or change coaches.

### 2. Origin Station Booking with 30-Minute Boarding Change
Intermediate stations draw from smaller pooled quotas that saturate almost immediately. Booking from the originating terminal station grants access to the larger General Quota (`GNWL`).

Under Indian Railways rules, you can modify your boarding station online on IRCTC up to the preparation of the second reservation chart (~30 minutes before train departure) without forfeiting your confirmed berth.

### 3. Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`) Post-Chart Release
Approximately 8 hours before train departure (or at 20:00 IST the previous evening for morning departures), first reservation charts are finalized. CRIS pools all unutilized emergency, defense, foreign tourist, and VIP quotas into the public inventory.

These berths are released online under `CURR_AVBL` at a **statutory 10% discount on base fares**. Inspect the [Chart Vacancy Map](/chart-vacancy) and verify historical release windows on [Chart Times](/chart-times) to claim seats up to 30 minutes prior to departure.

### 4. Standby Clone & Emergency Festival Trains
During peak departure days (November 6 to November 8), zonal railway authorities deploy standby rakes running 15 to 45 minutes behind major express services. These appear on IRCTC with 12 to 24 hours of advance notice, opening with completely fresh, uncongested quotas.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### Are Diwali special train tickets more expensive than regular express trains?
Yes, 0-series festival special trains charge a Train on Special Fare (TOSF) surcharge of 10% on Second Sitting (2S) and 30% on Sleeper and AC classes above regular express base fares. Concession discounts for senior citizens and students are not applicable.

### Can you book Tatkal tickets on Diwali special trains?
Tatkal quota availability depends on specific zonal notifications. While select special rakes allocate a standard Tatkal quota opening at 10:00 AM for AC and 11:00 AM for non-AC, many 0-series specials operate with 100% general quota. Check the quota dropdown on IRCTC to verify Tatkal availability for your specific train.

### Does Indian Railways operate clone trains during Diwali 2026?
Yes, Indian Railways deploys standby clone trains on high-density corridors like Delhi to Patna and Mumbai to Gorakhpur when waitlists on regular services reach extreme levels. These duplicate rakes run shortly behind primary express trains and open for booking 12 to 48 hours before departure.

### Can you board a Diwali special train with a waitlisted e-ticket?
No, passengers holding fully waitlisted e-tickets cannot board reserved coaches, as unconfirmed online tickets are automatically cancelled after chart preparation. Boarding a reserved coach with an invalid ticket attracts a flat ₹500 fine under the Jan Vishwas Act 2026 plus single fare under Section 138 of the Railways Act.

### How many days before departure are supplementary festival schedules notified?
Supplementary 0-series festival specials are typically notified between 10 and 30 days before departure as zonal authorities finalize rake availability. Emergency clone and standby services may be announced 12 to 24 hours ahead during peak festival days.

### Can you change your boarding station on a Diwali special train?
Yes, you can change your boarding station online through the IRCTC portal up to the preparation of the second reservation chart (~30 minutes before train departure). Always verify the updated boarding point on your PNR before boarding.

### Why do festival special trains often experience track delays?
0-series festival specials operate on temporary timetabled paths and yield track precedence to premium regular services such as Vande Bharat, Rajdhani, Shatabdi, and Superfast express trains. Passengers are advised to allow adequate buffer time for time-sensitive connecting journeys.

### How do you check vacant seats on festival trains after chart preparation?
You can view remaining vacant berths on IRCTC or through the LastBerth [Chart Vacancy Map](/chart-vacancy), which maps unsold berths across coaches in real time. These berths are bookable online or at station counters under Current Availability at a 10% base fare discount until 30 minutes before departure.

### When does the first reservation chart get prepared for morning special trains?
For trains scheduled to depart before 14:00 (2:00 PM IST), the first reservation chart is finalized the previous evening between 20:00 and 21:00 IST. For afternoon and evening departures, first charts are prepared approximately 8 to 10 hours prior to departure.

### How does LastBerth Smart Seats find confirmed tickets when direct booking shows REGRET?
LastBerth Smart Seats analyzes segmented station inventory across intermediate stops on the exact same train. When direct end-to-end seats show REGRET, Smart Seats discovers contiguous available legs (such as Delhi to Kanpur and Kanpur to Patna), allowing you to travel on one train without paying tout markups.
