## Can You Book Vande Bharat Tickets 15 Minutes Before Departure?

**Yes, under the new Indian Railways rule, passengers can book vacant seats on the Vande Bharat Express and other reserved trains up to 15 minutes before the scheduled departure time, even after the reservation chart has been prepared.**

This new feature is a massive lifesaver for anyone planning an emergency trip. Previously, booking options got locked down once chart preparation was complete. Now, Indian Railways keeps any remaining vacant berths open until the absolute last minute, helping you secure a confirmed seat right before boarding.

However, keep in mind that this rule only applies if there are actual unoccupied seats left on the train. If the train is completely sold out, you won't see this option.

---

## How Does the Vande Bharat 15-Minute Booking Rule Work?

**The 15-minute booking rule allows you to purchase confirmed tickets for any seats that remain unoccupied after the first chart is prepared. These last-minute seats stay open for booking until exactly 15 minutes before the train starts.**

Usually, IRCTC prepares the first reservation chart about 4 hours before the train's scheduled departure. Under the old rules, booking after chart preparation was quite restrictive. With the revised system, any seat that remains vacant after the chart is generated is automatically put up for grabs.

These seats are sold as a `"current available ticket"`. They are fully confirmed berths with assigned coach and seat numbers. You can book them right up until 15 minutes before the train pulls out of the station.

## Where and How Can You Book Last-Minute Vande Bharat Tickets?

**You can book these last-minute tickets online through the official IRCTC website, the IRCTC Rail Connect mobile app, or physically at station Passenger Reservation System (PRS) counters.**

Securing one of these seats is very straightforward. Here's what you need to do:
1.  **Check Live Availability:** Open the IRCTC website or app within 4 hours of the train's departure.
2.  **Look for Current Booking:** If seats are vacant, the availability status will display the number of seats under the "Current Booking" or "Current Available" category.
3.  **Book Instantly:** Complete your payment immediately, as these seats are limited and highly sought after by last-minute travellers.

Always make sure you book through official channels like IRCTC or authorized counters to avoid scams.

## What Should You Do if Vande Bharat Seats are Waitlisted?

**When Vande Bharat seats are waitlisted, check your status carefully. Since the WL full form is Waiting List, tickets usually progress from WL (Waiting List) → RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) → Confirmed. However, because Vande Bharat trains only feature chair cars, there is no RAC category.**

Because Vande Bharat trains only consist of sitting coaches (CC and EC) and have no sleeper berths, the progression queue goes straight from:

**WL (Waiting List) → Confirmed**

If your ticket remains waitlisted after the final chart preparation, IRCTC will automatically cancel your e-ticket and process a refund to your account. You cannot travel on a Vande Bharat train with a waitlisted e-ticket.

## Last-Minute Vande Bharat Booking Questions (FAQ)

### Can we book tickets on the train after it departs?
No, you cannot book tickets once the train departs the station. The booking window closes exactly 15 minutes before the scheduled departure time.

### Is the 15-minute booking rule available on all trains?
Yes, this facility is available on Vande Bharat Express trains and other reserved passenger services, provided there are vacant seats remaining after the chart is prepared.

### What is a current available ticket on Vande Bharat?
A `"current available ticket"` is a confirmed seat that becomes available for booking after the reservation chart is prepared. It remains bookable online or at station counters until 15 minutes before the train leaves.

### What happens if my Vande Bharat ticket stays on the waiting list?
If your e-ticket is still on the waiting list after chart preparation, IRCTC will automatically cancel the ticket and refund your money. WL tickets cannot be used to board Vande Bharat trains.

### Can I travel on Vande Bharat with a waitlisted counter ticket?
No, waitlisted passengers are not permitted to board Vande Bharat Express trains. Doing so will lead to heavy penalties and you will be asked to deboard at the next station.
