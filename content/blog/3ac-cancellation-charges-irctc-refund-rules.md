## What Are the 3AC Ticket Cancellation Charges in Indian Railways?

**The cancellation charges for a confirmed 3AC (AC 3-Tier) ticket depend on when you cancel before train departure. Cancelling 48+ hours prior incurs a flat fee of ₹180 plus 5% GST (₹189 total) per passenger. Between 48 and 12 hours, IRCTC deducts 25% of the fare, while 12 to 4 hours deducts 50%.**

Under official commercial rules framed by the Railway Board and enforced by the Centre for Railway Information Systems (CRIS), third AC tickets are classified under the AC Tier structure. Because AC tickets attract statutory Goods and Services Tax (GST) at a flat 5% rate, the cancellation fee also includes the applicable GST component.

Here is the master breakdown of cancellation charges for a confirmed 3AC ticket across all standard time frames:

| Cancellation Timing Before Scheduled Train Departure | Deduction Percentage / Fee | Effective Charge per Passenger (3AC) | Net Refund Amount Returned to Bank |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **More than 48 Hours Prior** | Flat Clerkage Fee | **₹180 + 5% GST (₹189)** | Full Fare minus ₹189 |
| **48 Hours to 12 Hours Prior** | 25% of Total Fare | **25% of base fare + charges** (Min. ₹189) | 75% of Ticket Fare |
| **12 Hours to 4 Hours Prior** (or up to 1st Chart) | 50% of Total Fare | **50% of base fare + charges** (Min. ₹189) | 50% of Ticket Fare |
| **Less than 4 Hours Prior** (or after Chart Preparation) | 100% Penalty | **100% of Ticket Fare** | **₹0 Refund (Zero Refund)** |

The flat fee of ₹180 plus GST represents the statutory minimum deduction. For example, if 25% of your 3AC ticket fare equals ₹160 on a short intercity journey, IRCTC will still deduct the baseline minimum of ₹189 rather than ₹160.

Convenience fees charged by IRCTC during booking (₹30 + GST for AC classes on net banking or cards, or ₹20 + GST on UPI) are non-refundable under all cancellation circumstances.

## How Do Cancellation Charges for 3AC Scale with Departure Time?

**IRCTC cancellation charges for confirmed 3AC tickets follow four strict time windows: flat ₹180 plus GST more than 48 hours prior; 25% of the fare between 48 and 12 hours; 50% between 12 and 4 hours; and zero refund within 4 hours of departure or after chart preparation.**

The countdown begins from the scheduled departure time of the train from your specific boarding station, not the origin station (unless you booked without modifying your boarding point). Understanding these cutoffs helps avoid severe financial penalties:

### 1. The 48-Hour Threshold (Flat ₹189 Fee)
If your train departs at 6:00 PM on Friday, cancelling anytime before 6:00 PM on Wednesday incurs only the flat clerkage fee of ₹180 + 5% GST per passenger. If you booked tickets for a family of four, the total deduction is exactly ₹756, and the entire remaining balance credits back to your account.

### 2. The 48 to 12-Hour Window (25% Deduction)
Between 48 hours and 12 hours before departure, Indian Railways switches from a flat fee to a percentage deduction. On a 3AC ticket costing ₹1,600 (such as Delhi to Patna or Mumbai to Danapur), a 25% deduction equals ₹400 per passenger. You receive ₹1,200 back.

### 3. The 12 to 4-Hour Window (50% Deduction)
Between 12 hours and 4 hours prior to scheduled departure, the penalty jumps to 50% of the total fare. On that same ₹1,600 ticket, IRCTC keeps ₹800 and refunds ₹800. This window also marks the preparation of the first reservation chart (~8 to 10 hours prior for daytime trains).

### 4. The 4-Hour Point of No Return (100% Forfeiture)
Once the clock ticks inside 4 hours before departure (or once chart preparation is completed, whichever occurs earlier), online cancellation is completely disabled for confirmed e-tickets. You receive zero refund.

```
[ >48 Hours ] ────────► [ 48h to 12h ] ────────► [ 12h to 4h / Chart ] ────────► [ <4 Hours ]
  Flat ₹189 Fee           25% Deduction            50% Deduction                   ₹0 Refund
```

## What Are the Cancellation Charges for 3AC RAC and Waiting List Tickets?

**The cancellation charges for a waitlisted (WL) or RAC 3AC ticket are a flat clerkage fee of ₹60 plus 5% GST (₹63 total) per passenger. Online waitlisted e-tickets auto-cancel after chart preparation with a full refund, while RAC tickets must be cancelled at least 30 minutes before departure.**

Waitlisted and RAC passengers enjoy significantly lower cancellation penalties than confirmed travelers because their seats have not blocked inventory:

| Ticket Status | Cancellation Cutoff Window | Cancellation Deduction | Net Refund Eligibility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Online E-Ticket (Fully Waitlisted)** | After Chart Preparation (~8h to ~30m) | **₹0 (Zero deduction)** | 100% Automatic Refund to Bank |
| **Online E-Ticket (Manual WL Cancel)** | Up to 30 mins before departure | **₹60 + 5% GST (₹63)** | Full Fare minus ₹63 clerkage |
| **RAC 3AC E-Ticket** | Up to 30 mins before departure | **₹60 + 5% GST (₹63)** | Full Fare minus ₹63 clerkage |
| **RAC 3AC E-Ticket (After 30 mins)** | Less than 30 mins to departure | **100% Deduction** | ₹0 Refund (Zero Refund) |
| **Counter PRS Waitlist Ticket** | Up to 30 mins before departure | **₹60 (Cash at counter)** | Full Cash Refund at station counter |

Important nuances for waitlisted and RAC passengers:

1. **Never Manually Cancel Fully Waitlisted E-Tickets:** If your 3AC e-ticket remains on the waiting list after final chart preparation, the IRCTC system automatically drops the PNR and refunds 100% of the fare with zero clerkage fee. If you cancel manually before charting, IRCTC deducts ₹63 per passenger.
2. **RAC Tickets Do Not Auto-Cancel:** An RAC ticket provides legal travel rights on a shared Side-Lower berth. It will never auto-cancel. If you decide not to travel on an RAC ticket, you must cancel it online at least 30 minutes before train departure to claim your refund minus the ₹63 fee.
3. **The 30-Minute RAC Deadline:** If an RAC ticket is not cancelled 30 minutes prior to departure, no refund is granted online, and filing a TDR requires special railway authority verification.

## Can You Cancel a Confirmed 3AC Tatkal Ticket and Get a Refund?

**Under official IRCTC rules, confirmed 3AC Tatkal tickets are 100% non-refundable upon voluntary cancellation, resulting in zero refund. However, waitlisted Tatkal (TQWL) tickets can be cancelled up to 30 minutes before train departure for a flat clerkage fee of ₹60 plus GST, or auto-cancel after chart preparation with a full refund.**

The strict zero-refund policy on confirmed Tatkal bookings was introduced by Indian Railways to curb commercial ticket touting and prevent speculative bookings during peak travel periods.

Here is how Tatkal refund rules apply across scenarios:

- **Voluntary Cancellation of Confirmed Tatkal:** If you booked a 3AC Tatkal ticket and voluntary circumstances prevent you from travelling, cancelling the ticket online yields exactly ₹0. Neither the base fare nor the Tatkal surcharge is refunded.
- **Waitlisted Tatkal (TQWL):** If your Tatkal ticket remains waitlisted, it follows standard waiting list refund rules. Cancelled manually up to 30 minutes before departure, IRCTC deducts ₹60 + GST (₹63). If left untouched, the system auto-cancels the waitlisted PNR after charting with zero deduction.
- **Exceptions Where Confirmed Tatkal Gets 100% Refund:** You can receive a 100% full refund on a confirmed 3AC Tatkal ticket by filing an online Ticket Deposit Receipt (TDR) under these specific conditions:
  1. **Train Delayed by More Than 3 Hours:** If the train is delayed by 3+ hours at your journey originating station and you choose not to travel.
  2. **Train Route Diverted:** If the train is diverted via another route and your boarding or destination station is bypassed.
  3. **Train Annulled or Cancelled:** If Indian Railways cancels the train due to weather, accidents, or operational reasons (100% auto-refund without filing a TDR).
  4. **Coach Not Attached or AC Malfunction:** If the 3AC coach is not attached or the air conditioning equipment fails during the journey.

For tactics on last-minute travel without Tatkal penalties, consult our guide on [Tatkal vs Current Availability](/blog/tatkal-vs-current-availability-last-minute-train-ticket).

## How Much Money Is Refunded If You Cancel a 3AC Ticket After Chart Preparation?

**Voluntary cancellation of a confirmed 3AC ticket after chart preparation yields zero refund (₹0). Online cancellation is blocked once reservation charts are printed. To claim a refund after charting, you must file an online Ticket Deposit Receipt (TDR) under valid railway exceptions, such as a train delay exceeding 3 hours.**

First reservation charts are finalized approximately 8 to 10 hours prior to departure (or 8:00 PM to 9:00 PM the previous evening for morning departures before 2:00 PM). Once the charting engine completes berth allotments:

1. **Online Cancellation Button Disabled:** On the IRCTC website and Rail Connect app, the "Cancel Ticket" button becomes inactive for confirmed passengers on that train.
2. **Vacant Berths Transferred to Current Availability:** Your berth cannot be casually surrendered online because CRIS immediately places unallocated seats into the `CURR_AVBL` pool for last-minute passengers at a 10% base fare discount.
3. **The TDR Mechanism:** If you are unable to travel due to genuine operational failures by Indian Railways, you must file an electronic TDR before the train departs or within statutory filing limits:

| TDR Refund Reason | Filing Deadline | Supporting Evidence Required | Expected Refund Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Train Delayed 3+ Hours** | Before actual train departure | None (Automatic PRS verification) | **100% Fare Refund** |
| **Train Cancelled by Railways** | No TDR needed | System automated | **100% Automatic Refund** |
| **AC Failure in 3AC Coach** | Up to 20 hours after arrival | Certificate from TTE / Conductor | Difference between 3AC and Sleeper fare |
| **Coach Downgraded to 3E** | Up to 72 hours after arrival | TTE excess fare memo / PNR record | Difference between 3AC and 3E fare |
| **Party Partially Confirmed** | Up to 30 mins before departure | Online TDR filing | Full refund for non-travelling passengers |

| **3A** | AC 3-Tier | **₹180 + 5% GST (₹189)** | 25% (Min. ₹189) | 50% (Min. ₹189) | ₹0 (100% Loss) | ₹60 + GST (₹63) |
| **3E** | AC 3-Tier Economy | **₹180 + 5% GST (₹189)** | 25% (Min. ₹189) | 50% (Min. ₹189) | ₹0 (100% Loss) | ₹60 + GST (₹63) |

Key takeaways from this comparison:
- **3A and 3E Share the Same Cancellation Fee:** Even though 3E Economy tickets have a 6% to 8% cheaper base fare than standard 3AC, Indian Railways applies the same flat ₹180 + GST cancellation charge to both classes.
- **GST Exemption on Non-AC Classes:** GST is only levied on air-conditioned travel. Sleeper (`SL`) and Second Sitting (`2S`) cancellation fees do not include GST.

## How Does Partial Cancellation Work on a Group 3AC Ticket?

**IRCTC permits partial cancellation on group 3AC bookings, allowing you to cancel individual passengers while keeping remaining travelers confirmed. The cancellation charges apply solely to the cancelled passengers based on their ticket status (confirmed, RAC, or waitlist) and the exact time of cancellation, and a fresh electronic reservation slip is issued.**

If you booked a single PNR for four passengers in 3AC and two passengers cannot travel, you do not have to cancel the entire ticket.

To execute a partial cancellation:
1. Log into your IRCTC account and navigate to *My Transactions ➔ Booked Ticket History*.
2. Select your 3AC booking and click the *Cancel Ticket* button.
3. A checklist of all passengers on the PNR appears. Check the boxes next to the names of the passengers who wish to cancel.
4. Leave the remaining passengers unchecked.
5. Click *Confirm Cancellation*.

The system calculates deductions exclusively for the selected travelers:
- If Passenger 1 and 2 are cancelled 3 days before departure, IRCTC deducts ₹189 per passenger (₹378 total) and refunds the balance.
- Passenger 3 and 4 retain their confirmed 3AC berths undisturbed.
- You can immediately print or download a revised Electronic Reservation Slip (ERS) showing only the active travelling passengers.

### Handling Mixed Status PNRs (Confirmed + Waitlisted)
On a group ticket where some passengers were assigned confirmed 3AC berths while others remained in RAC or Waiting List:
- If the waitlisted passengers decide not to travel, you must file an online TDR for the waitlisted passengers at least 30 minutes before train departure.
- All passengers on the PNR can obtain a full refund minus clerkage fees if the confirmed passengers also choose not to travel because their companions were waitlisted. A joint certificate must be obtained from the TTE or an online TDR filed stating "Party Partially Confirmed."

## What Can You Do Instead of Cancelling a Confirmed 3AC Ticket?

**Instead of cancelling a confirmed 3AC ticket and losing up to 50% of your fare, you can change your boarding station online up to 30 minutes before departure, transfer the ticket to a blood relative 24 hours in advance, reschedule your travel date at a counter, or discover connecting seats on LastBerth.**

Before forfeiting hundreds of rupees in cancellation penalties, consider these four official Indian Railways flexibility options:

### 1. Change Your Boarding Point Online
If your travel delay is due to connecting transport, you do not need to cancel your entire journey. IRCTC allows you to change your boarding station online up to the preparation of the second reservation chart (~30 minutes before train departure). Your confirmed 3AC berth is retained, and the TTE cannot reallocate your seat to RAC passengers before your new boarding point.

### 2. Transfer Your Ticket to a Family Member
Under Chief Commercial Manager guidelines, a confirmed train ticket can be transferred to a family member (father, mother, brother, sister, spouse, son, or daughter). Submit a written request alongside original identity documents to the nearest railway station Chief Reservation Supervisor at least 24 hours prior to scheduled train departure. No cancellation fee is charged.

### 3. Pre-pone or Post-pone Your Journey Date
You can reschedule your journey date or change your travel class on a confirmed ticket by surrendering your physical ticket or e-ticket printout at a railway PRS station reservation counter at least 48 hours prior to train departure. A nominal clerkage charge applies rather than a full cancellation penalty.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### What is the cancellation charge for a 3AC confirmed ticket cancelled 2 days before departure?

Cancelling a confirmed 3AC ticket more than 48 hours (2 days) prior to scheduled train departure incurs a flat administrative deduction of ₹180 plus 5% GST, which equals exactly ₹189 per passenger. The remaining ticket fare is refunded in full to your source payment account within 3 to 5 banking days.

### What is the cancellation charge for 3AC RAC ticket?

The cancellation charge for an RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) ticket in 3AC is a flat clerkage fee of ₹60 plus 5% GST (₹63 total) per passenger, provided the cancellation is completed online at least 30 minutes before the train's scheduled departure from your station.

### How much money is deducted if I cancel a 3AC waiting list ticket?

If you manually cancel a waitlisted 3AC ticket online before chart preparation, IRCTC deducts a clerkage fee of ₹60 plus 5% GST (₹63 total). However, if you leave a fully waitlisted online e-ticket untouched, it automatically cancels after final chart preparation with zero deduction, granting you a 100% full fare refund.

### What is the cancellation charge for 3AC ticket between 12 hours and 4 hours before departure?

If you cancel a confirmed 3AC ticket between 12 hours and 4 hours prior to scheduled departure (or before first chart preparation), IRCTC deducts 50% of the total ticket fare, subject to a minimum deduction of ₹189 per passenger.

### Can I get a refund if I cancel my confirmed 3AC ticket 2 hours before departure?

No, voluntary online cancellation of a confirmed 3AC ticket within 4 hours of train departure (or after reservation chart preparation) yields zero refund (₹0). Online cancellations are blocked once charts are finalized, and tickets are treated as lapsed.

### Are Tatkal cancellation charges different for 3AC tickets?

Yes. Under Indian Railways commercial regulations, confirmed 3AC Tatkal tickets are completely non-refundable upon voluntary cancellation, resulting in a 100% fare loss. Only waitlisted Tatkal tickets qualify for a refund, subject to the standard ₹63 clerkage fee.

### How long does it take for a 3AC ticket cancellation refund to reach my bank account?

Refunds for online 3AC e-tickets cancelled via IRCTC are processed immediately by CRIS payment switches. UPI payments usually reflect in your bank account within 24 to 48 hours, while net banking, credit card, and debit card transactions take 3 to 5 business days depending on interbank clearing cycles.

### Is the IRCTC convenience fee refunded when a 3AC ticket is cancelled?

No. The IRCTC convenience fee charged at the time of ticket booking (₹30 + GST for card/net banking, or ₹20 + GST for UPI on AC tickets) is non-refundable under all cancellation circumstances, even if the train is cancelled by Indian Railways.

### What is the cancellation fee for AC 3-Tier Economy (3E) compared to 3AC?

Indian Railways charges the exact same flat cancellation fee of ₹180 plus 5% GST (₹189 total) for AC 3-Tier Economy (3E) as standard 3AC (3A) when cancelled 48+ hours before departure. Between 48 hours and 4 hours prior, deductions follow identical 25% and 50% tiers based on the 3E ticket fare.

### Can I travel in a 3AC coach on a waitlisted ticket if I pay the fine?

No. Under the Jan Vishwas Act 2026, passengers holding waitlisted e-tickets are strictly prohibited from boarding reserved Sleeper or AC coaches. Boarding a 3AC coach without a confirmed ticket attracts a flat ₹500 penalty under Section 138 of the Railways Act, plus the full fare difference to 3AC from your origin station, alongside compulsory deboarding at the next halt.
