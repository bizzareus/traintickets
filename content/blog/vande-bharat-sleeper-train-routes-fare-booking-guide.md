## What Is the Vande Bharat Sleeper Train and How Fast Does It Travel?

**The Vande Bharat Sleeper is Indian Railways' flagship electric multiple-unit (EMU) train manufactured by BEML and Kinet Railway Solutions for long-distance overnight travel in India. Operating at maximum speeds of 160 km/h, it features self-propelled distributed power, cutting journey times by 2 to 3 hours compared to legacy Rajdhani Express trains.**

Key technical specifications:
- **Maximum Operational Speed:** 160 km/h on upgraded tracks (Delhi-Mumbai and Delhi-Howrah).
- **Trainset Composition:** 16 coaches (1 AC First Class, 4 AC 2-Tier, 11 AC 3-Tier) accommodating 823 passengers.
- **Safety Features:** KAVACH Automatic Train Protection (ATP) system, anti-collision design, and fire-retardant interior materials.

**In 2026, Indian Railways operates Vande Bharat Sleeper trains across high-density overnight corridors connecting major economic hubs, including Bengaluru to Mumbai Central, New Delhi to Mumbai Central, and New Delhi to Howrah (Kolkata). ## What Is the Difference Between Vande Bharat Sleeper and Rajdhani Express?

**While Rajdhani Express uses locomotive-hauled LHB coaches, Vande Bharat Sleeper is a self-propelled EMU trainset featuring faster acceleration, smoother braking, automatic interconnecting plug doors, bio-vacuum toilets with touchless sensor fittings, and superior acoustic soundproofing that dramatically reduces cabin noise compared to conventional LHB trains.**

| Feature | Vande Bharat Sleeper (2026) | Rajdhani Express |
|---|---|---|
| **Propulsion** | Distributed Power EMU (No engine swap) | Locomotive-hauled (LHB) |
| **Top Speed** | 160 km/h | 130–140 km/h |
| **Door Operations** | Automatic centralized plug doors | Manual swing doors |
| **Berth Comfort** | Extra-cushioned boles with reading lights | Standard LHB foam berths |
| **Noise Level** | Ultra-quiet insulated acoustic shell | Standard LHB noise profile |
| **Waitlist Policy** | Strictly Zero-WL / Zero-RAC | Standard WL and RAC issued |

### Zero-WL & Zero-RAC Policy Rules
Unlike regular mail/express services, Vande Bharat Sleeper trains enforce a strict **zero-waitlist (Zero-WL) and zero-RAC policy**. All tickets issued are 100% confirmed berths. Once the berth capacity of 823 seats is reached, the IRCTC system immediately displays `REGRET` to prevent overcrowded corridors and preserve passenger comfort.

## How to Book Vande Bharat Sleeper Tickets on IRCTC App and Website?

**You can book Vande Bharat Sleeper tickets up to 60 days in advance via the IRCTC Rail Connect app, official IRCTC website, or station PRS counters starting at 8:00 AM IST daily. Selecting passengers from your pre-saved IRCTC Master List helps secure high-demand confirmed berths within seconds of booking opening.**

Follow these steps for fast booking:
1. Open [Smart Seats](/) or IRCTC app and select source and destination stations.
2. Filter search by class: **1A**, **2A**, or **3A**.
3. Select your pre-saved passengers from the **IRCTC Master List** to bypass manual typing.
4. Complete payment via UPI or net banking to confirm your PNR status instantly.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### Is food included in the Vande Bharat Sleeper ticket fare?

Yes, onboard catering is included in the fare by default, serving dinner, breakfast, and tea. Passengers have the option to opt out during booking to save on catering costs and receive a direct refund credit.

### What is the Advance Reservation Period (ARP) for Vande Bharat Sleeper trains?

The Advance Reservation Period for Vande Bharat Sleeper trains follows the standard 60-day rule opening at 8:00 AM IST daily.

### Can you book Tatkal tickets on Vande Bharat Sleeper trains?

Yes, Tatkal booking opens at 10:00 AM IST for 2A and 3A classes under standard Tatkal surge pricing rules (First AC / 1A is excluded from Tatkal).

### What is the zero-WL and zero-RAC policy on Vande Bharat Sleeper?

Vande Bharat Sleeper trains do not issue waitlist (WL) or RAC tickets. Once all physical berths are booked, the system shows `REGRET`, ensuring every passenger onboard has a guaranteed confirmed seat.

### How much refund do you get if you opt out of meals on Vande Bharat Sleeper?

Depending on route distance and class, opting out of catering yields a refund of ₹210 to ₹850 directly deducted from your ticket total at checkout.

### What is the top speed of Vande Bharat Sleeper trains on the Bengaluru-Mumbai route?

Vande Bharat Sleeper trains operate at a top commercial speed of 160 km/h on upgraded tracks, completing the Bengaluru-Mumbai journey in approximately 13 hours and 15 minutes.

### Are there 1AC private coupes available on Vande Bharat Sleeper trains?

Yes, AC First Class (1A) on Vande Bharat Sleeper features private 2-berth coupes and 4-berth cabins with automatic touchless sliding doors and lockable security controls.

### How does Vande Bharat Sleeper fare compare to flight tickets?

Vande Bharat Sleeper 3A and 2A fares are generally 30% to 50% cheaper than last-minute flight tickets, providing overnight sleep comfort without airport security hassles or luggage fees.
