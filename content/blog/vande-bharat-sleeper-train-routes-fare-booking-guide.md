The Vande Bharat Sleeper is Indian Railways flagship overnight high-speed train service. Designed to replace legacy Rajdhani Express rakes on key trunk routes, Vande Bharat Sleeper trains operate at speeds up to 160 km/h with premium aerodynamic design, automated plug doors, modular toilets, and superior passenger comfort.

## What Is the Vande Bharat Sleeper Train and How Fast Does It Travel?

**The Vande Bharat Sleeper is an electric multiple-unit (EMU) train manufactured by BEML and Kinet Railway Solutions for long-distance overnight travel in India. Operating at maximum speeds of 160 km/h, it reduces journey times by 2 to 3 hours compared to Rajdhani Express trains.**

Key technical specifications:
- **Maximum Operational Speed:** 160 km/h on upgraded tracks (Delhi-Mumbai and Delhi-Howrah).
- **Trainset Composition:** 16 coaches (1 AC First Class, 4 AC 2-Tier, 11 AC 3-Tier) accommodating 823 passengers.
- **Safety Features:** KAVACH Automatic Train Protection (ATP) system, anti-collision design, and fire-retardant interior materials.

## What Are the Vande Bharat Sleeper Routes and Schedules in 2026?

**In 2026, Indian Railways operates Vande Bharat Sleeper trains across high-density overnight corridors connecting major metros: Delhi to Mumbai Central, Delhi to Howrah (Kolkata), and Bengaluru to Chennai Central.**

Primary routes and expected transit times:
- **Delhi Anand Vihar to Mumbai Central:** ~12 hours 30 minutes (saving 3 hours vs Rajdhani).
- **Delhi New Delhi to Howrah Junction:** ~11 hours 45 minutes.
- **Bengaluru KSR to Chennai Central:** ~4 hours 30 minutes.

## What Is the Difference Between Vande Bharat Sleeper and Rajdhani Express?

**While Rajdhani Express uses locomotive-hauled LHB coaches, Vande Bharat Sleeper is a self-propelled EMU trainset featuring faster acceleration, smoother braking, automatic interconnecting doors, and bio-vacuum toilets with touchless sensor fittings.**

| Feature | Vande Bharat Sleeper (2026) | Rajdhani Express |
|---|---|---|
| **Propulsion** | Distributed Power EMU (No engine swap) | Locomotive-hauled (LHB) |
| **Top Speed** | 160 km/h | 130–140 km/h |
| **Door Operations** | Automatic centralized plug doors | Manual swing doors |
| **Berth Comfort** | Extra-cushioned boles with reading lights | Standard LHB foam berths |
| **Noise Level** | Ultra-quiet insulated acoustic shell | Standard LHB noise profile |

## What Are the Ticket Fares for AC 1st Class, 2nd Class, and 3rd Class on Vande Bharat Sleeper?

**Vande Bharat Sleeper ticket fares are structured at a 15% to 20% premium over Rajdhani Express fares, incorporating onboard catering charges, air conditioning fees, and dynamic pricing rules.**

Approximate fare ranges by class:
- **AC 3-Tier (3A):** ₹1,800 to ₹2,400 depending on route distance.
- **AC 2-Tier (2A):** ₹2,800 to ₹3,600 with extra legroom and personal reading lamps.
- **AC First Class (1A):** ₹4,200 to ₹5,500 featuring private lockable coupes and complimentary gourmet meals.

## How to Book Vande Bharat Sleeper Tickets on IRCTC App and Website?

**You can book Vande Bharat Sleeper tickets up to 60 days in advance via the IRCTC Rail Connect app, IRCTC website, or station PRS counters starting at 8:00 AM IST daily.**

Follow these steps for fast booking:
1. Open [Smart Seats](/) or IRCTC app and select source and destination stations.
2. Filter search by class: **1A**, **2A**, or **3A**.
3. Select your pre-saved passengers from the **IRCTC Master List** to bypass manual typing.
4. Complete payment via UPI or net banking to confirm your PNR status instantly.

## Vande Bharat Sleeper FAQ

### Is food included in the Vande Bharat Sleeper ticket fare?
Yes, onboard catering is included in the fare by default, serving dinner, breakfast, and tea. Passengers have the option to opt out during booking to save on catering costs.

### What is the Advance Reservation Period (ARP) for Vande Bharat Sleeper trains?
The Advance Reservation Period for Vande Bharat Sleeper trains follows the standard 60-day rule opening at 8:00 AM IST daily.

### Can you book Tatkal tickets on Vande Bharat Sleeper trains?
Yes, Tatkal booking opens at 10:00 AM IST for 2A and 3A classes under standard Tatkal surge pricing rules (First AC / 1A is excluded from Tatkal).
