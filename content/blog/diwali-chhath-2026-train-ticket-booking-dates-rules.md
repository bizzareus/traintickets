## How Does the 60-Day Advance Reservation Period (ARP) Work for Festival Booking?

**The Advance Reservation Period (ARP) allows you to book train tickets up to 60 days before the train departs from its originating station, excluding the date of the journey itself. Booking opens daily at 8:00 AM IST across all online and offline Passenger Reservation System counters.**

To calculate your exact booking day, count backwards 60 full days from your intended departure date. For instance, if your train leaves its origin station on November 8, you subtract 60 days to arrive at September 9.

Three critical ARP rules you need to know:
1. **Origin Station Departure Date:** The 60-day timer counts down from the date and time the train departs its originating station, not an intermediate boarding point. If you board on Day 2 of a long-distance route, booking opens on the day calculated for Day 1.
2. **Opening Window Timings:** General quota bookings open at 8:00 AM IST sharp. AC Tatkal opens at 10:00 AM IST, and non-AC Sleeper Tatkal opens at 11:00 AM IST one day before origin departure.
3. **Foreign Tourist Quota Exception:** Foreign passport holders can book designated berths up to 365 days in advance under the Foreign Tourist Quota (FTQ) for 1AC, 2AC, and Executive classes.

## Why Is Aadhaar Verification Mandatory for Peak Festival Bookings?

**Aadhaar verification is mandatory on IRCTC to unlock the higher monthly cap of 24 tickets and to pass the anti-bot security protocols enforced during the high-traffic 8:00 AM general and 10:00 AM Tatkal booking windows.**

Unverified IRCTC accounts are restricted to booking only 12 tickets per calendar month. Linking your 12-digit Aadhaar number with your IRCTC profile doubles this limit to 24 tickets per month, provided at least one traveler on tickets 13 through 24 is also Aadhaar-authenticated.

During festival rushes, IRCTC deploys strict OTP-based multi-factor authentication. An Aadhaar-verified profile bypasses secondary security flags, preventing captchas from timing out your payment gateway session during peak minutes.

## How to Use the IRCTC Master List to Book Festival Tickets in Under 60 Seconds?

**The IRCTC Master List feature lets you pre-save up to 20 passenger profiles in your IRCTC account before bookings open, allowing you to auto-fill traveler names, ages, gender, berth preferences, and ID proofs with a single click at 8:00 AM.**

Manually typing passenger details when the clock strikes 8:00 AM costs between 30 and 45 valuable seconds. In that window, hundreds of confirmed berths vanish into waitlists.

Follow these steps to set up your Master List beforehand:
1. Log into `irctc.co.in` or the Rail Connect app at least one day before your booking opens.
2. Navigate to **My Account > My Profile > Add / Modify Master List**.
3. Enter the full legal name, age, gender, berth preference (Lower, Middle, Upper, Side Lower), food preference, and Aadhaar or ID details for each family member.
4. Click **Submit** to store the profile.
5. Create a **Travel List** sub-group if you are booking for a family of 4 to 6 people.
6. When booking opens at 8:00 AM, select your train, click **Add Passenger from Master List**, tick the boxes, and proceed straight to payment.

## What Should You Do When Direct Diwali Trains Show Regret or High Waitlist?

**When direct trains display Regret status or severe waiting lists (WL/100+), you can secure a confirmed seat by using journey segmentation on LastBerth, booking from an earlier station on the route, or selecting alternate origin-destination quotas.**

The progression queue moves from **WL (Waiting List) → RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) → Confirmed**. However, once the waiting list queue hits its ceiling (typically 50 to 150 passengers depending on coach count), IRCTC flags the train as `REGRET` and blocks all further booking attempts.

When direct booking hits a wall, use these proven rescue strategies:

### 1. Finding Smart Seats (Split-Segment Journey)
Indian Railways reserves separate seat quotas for intermediate stations. While a direct seat from New Delhi to Patna may show `REGRET`, seats on the same train might be vacant from New Delhi to Kanpur, and from Kanpur to Patna. Using [Finding Smart Seats](/) on LastBerth helps you identify contiguous confirmed segments on the exact same rake without having to change trains.

### 2. Book from Origin and Change Boarding Point
If berths from your local station are waitlisted under Remote Location Waiting List (RLWL) or Pooled Quota (PQWL), search availability from the train's originating station under General Waiting List (GNWL). GNWL has the highest seat allocation and confirmation priority. Once booked, change your boarding point online at least 24 hours before train departure.

### 3. Track Waitlist Progression with PNR Status
If you hold an existing waitlisted e-ticket, monitor its confirmation odds with [PNR Status Search & Direct Booking](/). Fully waitlisted online e-tickets that remain unconfirmed after chart preparation are automatically cancelled with a full refund.

## When Do Festival Tatkal and Premium Tatkal Windows Open?

**Festival Tatkal and Premium Tatkal booking windows open exactly one day before the train's departure from its originating station at 10:00 AM IST for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC, EC) and 11:00 AM IST for non-AC Sleeper class.**

Tatkal tickets carry a surcharge ranging from ₹100 to ₹200 for Sleeper and ₹300 to ₹500 for AC classes. Premium Tatkal (PT) uses dynamic pricing where base fares surge up to double based on real-time demand.

| Tatkal Rule | AC Classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC, EC) | Non-AC Sleeper (SL, 2S) |
|---|---|---|
| **Opening Time** | 10:00 AM IST (1 day prior) | 11:00 AM IST (1 day prior) |
| **Max Passengers per PNR** | 4 Passengers | 4 Passengers |
| **Confirmed Ticket Refund** | 0% (Non-refundable) | 0% (Non-refundable) |
| **Authorized Agent Entry** | 10:30 AM IST (30-min block) | 11:30 AM IST (30-min block) |
| **Token System Counter Hours** | 8:30 AM – 9:00 AM IST | 9:00 AM – 9:30 AM IST |

Keep in mind that First AC (1A) and Executive Anubhuti coaches do not have Tatkal quotas. Premium Tatkal never issues RAC or waitlisted tickets; it only sells confirmed berths.

## How Do Special Festival Trains and Current Availability Work?

**Special festival trains are additional services announced by Indian Railways 15 to 30 days before peak holiday dates, while Current Availability releases unsold quota berths for confirmed booking after the first reservation chart is prepared ~8 hours prior to departure.**

Every year, Indian Railways operates thousands of festival special trips (numbered starting with 0, such as 04044 or 02394) to manage festive crowds on high-density routes. These trains usually carry special train fare structures (around 1.15 to 1.3 times regular fares) and have independent 60-day or 30-day booking windows.

If you miss both general and Tatkal bookings, check for Current Availability tickets:
- The first reservation chart is prepared **~8 hours before departure** from the originating station.
- Any unallocated berths from VIP, emergency, or remote location quotas convert into `CURR_AVBL` (Current Availability).
- A Current Available ticket is **100% fully confirmed** with assigned coach and berth numbers.
- You can book current availability online or at station PRS counters until **30 minutes before train departure**.
- You can see which berths fall vacant across specific station legs using the [Seat Status Coach Journey Lookup](/seat-status).

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### What time does Diwali 2026 train ticket booking open in the morning?

General quota booking for Diwali 2026 train tickets opens daily at 8:00 AM IST on the official IRCTC website, Rail Connect app, and PRS reservation counters.

### Can I travel on a waitlisted online e-ticket during Diwali rush?

No. A fully waitlisted e-ticket that remains unconfirmed after final chart preparation is automatically cancelled by the system, and you are not permitted to board reserved coaches.

### What is the difference between GNWL and RLWL for festival booking?

GNWL (General Waiting List) applies when booking from the origin station and has the largest quota and highest confirmation chances, whereas RLWL (Remote Location Waiting List) applies to intermediate stations with a smaller quota and lower confirmation probability.

### How many passengers can be booked on a single festival general ticket?

You can book a maximum of 6 passengers per PNR on a general quota e-ticket, while Tatkal quota limits bookings to a maximum of 4 passengers per PNR.

### What happens if money is deducted on IRCTC but no ticket is generated?

If money is deducted during a failed booking attempt, the amount is automatically refunded by your payment gateway or bank within 2 to 4 working days (or within 24 hours on IRCTC e-Wallet).

### Does Vande Bharat Sleeper have RAC or Tatkal quota for festival travel?

Vande Bharat Sleeper trains issue only confirmed tickets and do not have RAC or waitlist provisions. Tatkal quota is available for 2AC and 3AC at 10:00 AM IST one day prior.

### What is the penalty for boarding a Sleeper coach with a general or platform ticket?

Under Section 138 of the Railways Act and the Jan Vishwas Act, travelling in a reserved coach with an unreserved or platform ticket attracts a minimum ₹500 penalty plus the full fare difference.

### Can I book festival train tickets using the RailOne app?

Yes. You can book unreserved general and platform tickets on the RailOne app, while reserved festival tickets are booked via IRCTC Rail Connect and PRS counters.

### When will Indian Railways announce Diwali and Chhath Puja special trains for 2026?

Indian Railways typically notifies festival special trains starting in late September and throughout October 2026 as regular train waitlists reach peak capacity.
