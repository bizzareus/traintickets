## How Do You Book an Ayodhya Dormitory or Retiring Room Online?

**You can book an Ayodhya railway station dormitory or retiring room online by visiting the official IRCTC Tourism portal (rr.irctc.co.in) and entering your 10-digit PNR number. The system retrieves your journey details for Ayodhya Dham (AY) or Ayodhya Cantt (AYC), allowing you to choose dormitory beds or private rooms.**

With millions of pilgrims visiting Ayodhya following the grand inauguration of the Ram Mandir, station retiring rooms and dormitories at Ayodhya Dham Junction (`AY`) and Ayodhya Cantt (`AYC`) experience extremely high demand. Booking online in advance is essential to secure a clean, affordable stay right on station premises.

### Step-by-Step Online Booking Process:
1. Visit the official [IRCTC Retiring Room Portal](https://www.rr.irctc.co.in/).
2. Enter your 10-digit confirmed (CNF) or RAC PNR number on the homepage and click **Search**.
3. Select whether you require accommodation at **Ayodhya Dham Junction (AY)** or **Ayodhya Cantt (AYC)** (source or destination station on your ticket).
4. Choose your check-in time and check-out time (slots available in 3, 6, 12, or 24-hour durations).
5. Select **Dormitory** or **Executive Double Room** (AC / Non-AC).
6. Provide passenger details and complete the payment using UPI, Net Banking, or Credit/Debit Card.
7. Save the digital booking voucher on your phone to present at station check-in.

## What Are the Key Rules and PNR Requirements for Booking?

**To book an Ayodhya dormitory or retiring room, your train ticket PNR must be in Confirmed (CNF) or Reservation Against Cancellation (RAC) status. Passengers holding fully waitlisted (WL) tickets, general unreserved tickets, or platform tickets cannot book these facilities.**

Key rules to remember when booking Ayodhya station stays:
- **Maximum Stay Limit:** You can book a maximum continuous stay of up to 48 hours per PNR.
- **Check-in Document:** At check-in, you must show your IRCTC retiring room booking voucher, valid train ticket, and an original government photo ID (Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License).
- **Luggage Storage & Security:** AC dormitories provide individual lockers for luggage safety while visiting the Ram Janmabhoomi complex.
- **Check-in Slot Tolerance:** If your train arrives late by more than 3 hours, inform the retiring room manager at the station so your check-in slot is preserved.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### Can I book an Ayodhya dormitory with a waitlisted (WL) ticket?
No. IRCTC strictly requires a Confirmed (CNF) or RAC ticket. Fully waitlisted PNRs are rejected by the system.

### How far is Ayodhya Dham Railway Station from Ram Mandir?
Ayodhya Dham Junction (`AY`) is located approximately 1.5 km to 2 km from the Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir complex, easily reachable by e-rickshaw or walking.

### Is there a separate ladies' dormitory at Ayodhya Railway Station?
Yes, Ayodhya Dham Junction features dedicated ladies' dormitory halls equipped with private lockers and separate washing facilities.

### What happens if I miss my check-in time due to train delay?
If your train is delayed, present your delayed train PNR at the retiring room manager's desk. The check-in time will be adjusted based on actual train arrival.

### How many dormitory beds can I book on one PNR?
You can book as many dormitory beds as there are passengers listed on your confirmed/RAC PNR (up to 6 passengers per PNR).
