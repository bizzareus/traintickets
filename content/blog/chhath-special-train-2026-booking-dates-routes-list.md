## What Are the Key Chhath Puja 2026 Ritual Dates and Travel Windows?

**Chhath Puja 2026 spans four days from November 12 to November 15, with evening Sandhya Arghya falling on Saturday, November 14. Outbound festival rush peaks between November 9 and November 11 as devotees travel to Bihar and eastern Uttar Pradesh, while the return travel rush peaks between November 16 and November 22.**

Because families travel together carrying traditional bamboo baskets (*soop* and *daura*), reaching your home station at least 24 to 48 hours before rituals begin is essential. Missing a connection or arriving late disrupts holy offerings.

The four ritual days govern railway passenger volume and reservation demand across the nation:

- **Day 1: Nahay Khay (Thursday, November 12, 2026):** Devotees take a ceremonial bath in the Ganges or local water bodies and prepare a satvik meal of bottle gourd (*kaddu-bhat*) and Bengal gram.
- **Day 2: Kharna (Friday, November 13, 2026):** A daylong waterless fast concluding at sunset with offerings of jaggery rice kheer (*rasiao-kheer*) and ghee chapatis.
- **Day 3: Sandhya Arghya (Saturday, November 14, 2026):** The primary festival ritual where millions gather at riverbanks and village ponds to offer evening oblations to the setting Sun God.
- **Day 4: Usha Arghya and Paran (Sunday, November 15, 2026):** Dawn oblations offered to the rising Sun God at sunrise, completing the rigorous 36-hour nirjala fast.

## What Are the IRCTC Fares and Train on Special Fare (TOSF) Charges for Chhath Specials?

**0-series Chhath festival trains operate under Train on Special Fare (TOSF) tariffs, levying a flat 10% surcharge on Second Sitting (2S) and a 30% surcharge on Sleeper, 3E, 3A, 2A, and 1AC base fares. Fares remain fixed from opening to departure without dynamic surge pricing, but concessions are completely disallowed.**

Unlike Suvidha trains where ticket prices double or triple as berth inventory sells out, 0-series festival special pricing remains constant. The flat surcharge helps Indian Railways recover empty haulage costs when returning rakes to origin terminals.

Key fare rules governing 0-series Chhath specials include:

- **Second Sitting (2S):** Carries an approximate 10% premium (typically ₹15 to ₹25 above standard mail/express fares).
- **Sleeper Class (SL):** Incurs a flat 30% surcharge over regular express base fares.
- **Air-Conditioned Classes (3E, 3A, 2A, 1A):** Incur a flat 30% surcharge on the base fare, plus statutory 5% GST.
- **Zero Concessions:** Senior citizen, student, medical patient, or press concessions do not apply on special trains. All passengers pay full adult fares.
- **Catering Charges:** Catering is optional and not bundled into basic fares. Because many 0-series rakes lack a pantry car, food must be purchased from platform vendors or pre-ordered via IRCTC e-catering.

## Can You Travel on Chhath Special Trains with an Unreserved or UTS Ticket?

**You can travel on festival special trains with an unreserved ticket only if the train rake includes designated General Seating (GS) coaches or runs as an entirely unreserved special. Boarding reserved Sleeper or AC coaches on a general ticket incurs a flat ₹500 penalty under Section 138 of the Railways Act.**

During peak festival congestion, Indian Railways operates dedicated all-unreserved rakes (such as Sealdah–Jaynagar 03185 and Hazrat Nizamuddin–Jhansi specials) featuring 16 to 18 General Second Class coaches. On these trains, any passenger holding a valid general ticket can board.

To travel legally in unreserved coaches during Chhath rush, follow these official ticketing rules:

1. **UTS on Mobile App Booking:** Use the official UTS app to purchase unreserved journey tickets digitally. Bookings must be made outside station premises (beyond 20 meters and within 20 kilometers of the boarding station).
2. **Station ATVM and Counter Tokens:** Major terminals like Anand Vihar, New Delhi, Surat, and Mumbai LTT have installed temporary ticketing counters and Automatic Ticket Vending Machines (ATVMs) to issue physical cardboard tokens.
3. **Queue Holding Enclosures:** Railway Protection Force (RPF) personnel manage separate holding enclosures on platforms. Passengers board in organized queues on a first-come, first-served basis.
4. **Strict Ban on Reserved Coach Boarding:** Boarding reserved Sleeper or AC coaches with an unreserved ticket violates Section 138 of the Railways Act. The Travelling Ticket Examiner (TTE) will levy a mandatory **flat ₹500 fine** plus the single fare difference to the next train stop, and may deboard you immediately.

## Can You Book Tatkal Tickets on Chhath Special Trains?

**Tatkal quota is available only on select festival special trains, while many 0-series rakes operate entirely under 100% General Quota without any Tatkal window. When Tatkal is enabled, bookings open at 10:00 AM IST for AC classes and 11:00 AM IST for Sleeper, exactly one day prior to source departure.**

Because 0-series special trains already incorporate a flat 30% festival surcharge across all seats, railway zonal administrations often do not carve out a separate Tatkal quota pool.

To verify whether your specific special train offers Tatkal booking:

- Open the IRCTC app or website and search your origin, destination, and departure date.
- Locate the 0-series special train number.
- Tap the **Quota** dropdown menu. If **Tatkal** appears selectable and displays positive berth availability, a Tatkal quota exists.
- If Tatkal shows **NOT APPLICABLE** or remains greyed out, the train operates under 100% General Quota.

When Tatkal is unavailable or sells out within seconds, do not book Tatkal Waiting List (TQWL). Tatkal cancellations yield zero refund, meaning TQWL tickets rarely clear. Instead, rely on standard waiting list progression or explore contiguous split-seat routes on [Smart Seats](/).

## How Can You Get a Confirmed Ticket When Trains Show REGRET or Heavy Waitlist?

**When IRCTC displays REGRET or deep waiting lists, you can secure confirmed travel by booking contiguous split journeys on the same train, booking from origin stations with boarding modifications, checking post-charting vacancies under Current Availability, or monitoring standby specials. REGRET indicates that even waitlist quotas are completely exhausted.**

Follow these four proven strategies to secure travel during the peak Chhath rush:

### 1. Split your journey into contiguous segments on the same train
Direct tickets from New Delhi to Patna frequently show REGRET while intermediate stretches have empty berths. Using [Smart Seats](/), you can book New Delhi to Kanpur in one coach and Kanpur to Patna in another coach on the exact same train. You travel on the same rake without missing your connection, simply shifting seats at Kanpur.

### 2. Book from the originating terminal and adjust your boarding station
If tickets from intermediate stops (such as Ghaziabad, Aligarh, or Tundla) are waitlisted under restrictive Roadside (RLWL) or Pooled Quota (PQWL), search from the train's originating station (such as New Delhi or Anand Vihar). Originating terminals carry the largest General Waiting List (GNWL) quota. After booking, modify your boarding station online on IRCTC up to 24 hours before journey departure or before second chart finalization.

### 3. Check live berths under Current Availability (CURR_AVBL)
Between 8 and 10 hours before departure, reservation charting systems release unutilized emergency, defense, and VIP quotas into the general pool. These berths appear on IRCTC as `CURR_AVBL` at a **10% statutory discount on the base fare**. You can explore physical empty berths across coaches on the [Chart Vacancy Map](/chart-vacancy) or check exact charting times on [Chart Times](/chart-times).

### 4. Monitor unannounced standby specials and clone trains
During peak travel dates between November 10 and November 14, zonal railways park spare rakes at major terminals including Anand Vihar, New Delhi, Patna, and Danapur. When waiting lists exceed 500, authorities open emergency clone special trains with 12 to 24 hours of advance notice. Keep watch on seat availability daily to catch these newly opened trains before quotas fill up.

## What Are the Cancellation Charges and Refund Rules for Festival Special Trains?

**Cancellation charges on 0-series special trains follow standard Indian Railways percentage slabs, but deduct higher absolute rupee amounts because deductions are calculated against the higher special base fare. Fully waitlisted online e-tickets that fail to confirm at chart preparation are automatically cancelled with a full refund.**

The cancellation penalty depends strictly on when you cancel relative to scheduled train departure from its originating station:

| Cancellation Timing Relative to Train Departure | Refund Entitlement | Deduction / Penalty Applied |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **More than 48 hours prior** | Full fare minus flat clerkage fee | Flat clerkage: ₹240 (1AC), ₹200 (2AC), ₹180 (3A/3E), ₹120 (SL), ₹60 (2S) + GST on AC |
| **Between 48 hours and 12 hours prior** | 75% of total ticket fare | 25% of ticket fare (subject to minimum statutory clerkage) |
| **Between 12 hours and 4 hours (or chart preparation)** | 50% of total ticket fare | 50% of ticket fare (subject to minimum statutory clerkage) |
| **Within 4 hours or post-charting** | Zero refund | 100% fare forfeiture |
| **Unconfirmed online waitlisted e-ticket** | Full refund | ₹0 clerkage deducted (gateway convenience fee non-refundable) |
| **RAC ticket cancelled up to 30 mins prior** | Full fare minus clerkage | ₹60 flat clerkage fee |

For partially confirmed PNRs where some passengers are confirmed while others remain waitlisted, you must file an online Ticket Deposit Receipt (TDR) at least 30 minutes before train departure to obtain a refund for the waitlisted passengers.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### What is the full form of TOSF in IRCTC special trains?
TOSF stands for Train on Special Fare. Indian Railways designates 0-series seasonal festival trains under the TOSF tariff category, applying an extra flat surcharge of 10% on Second Sitting and 30% on Sleeper and AC classes to cover empty rake return operations.

### Can I travel on a Chhath special train with an unreserved general ticket?
You can only travel in designated unreserved General Seating (GS) coaches or on completely unreserved special trains with a general ticket. Boarding reserved Sleeper or AC coaches on an unreserved ticket violates Section 138 of the Railways Act, resulting in a flat ₹500 penalty plus single fare difference.

### Do 0-series festival special trains provide bedrolls in AC coaches?
Yes, all passengers traveling in AC classes (1AC, 2AC, 3A, and 3E) on special trains receive standard bedroll kits including two bedsheets, a pillow with cover, a blanket, and a face towel. The cost is included in your ticket fare, whereas Sleeper passengers must bring their own bedding.

### Are pantry car meals provided on Chhath special trains?
Most 0-series special trains do not feature an operational pantry car. Meals and refreshments are not included in the ticket fare. Passengers should carry homemade food and water or pre-order fresh meals online via IRCTC e-catering for station delivery.

### What happens if my Chhath special e-ticket remains on the waiting list after charting?
If an online e-ticket remains waitlisted after final reservation chart preparation, IRCTC automatically cancels the reservation and processes a full refund directly to your original payment method within 3 to 5 working days. You cannot board reserved coaches on a dropped e-ticket.

### When does the first reservation chart prepare for Chhath special trains?
The first reservation chart is finalized approximately 8 to 10 hours before the train departs from its originating station. For morning trains departing before 2:00 PM, charting is completed the previous evening between 8:00 PM and 9:00 PM IST.

### Can two passengers travel on one RAC ticket during Chhath Puja?
Yes, an RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) status legally allows two passengers to board the train and share one side-lower berth as seating accommodation during daytime hours (6:00 AM to 10:00 PM). If cancellations occur, one or both passengers are upgraded to full berths.

### What is the difference between GNWL and RLWL on Bihar-bound trains?
GNWL (General Waiting List) applies when you book from the originating station to the terminating station and carries the highest confirmation probability. RLWL (Remote Location Waiting List) applies to intermediate stations and has a much smaller quota, clearing only if passengers cancel along that specific stretch.

### Can I change my boarding station after booking a Chhath special ticket?
Yes, you can modify your boarding station online through your IRCTC booking history up to 24 hours before journey departure or before the second reservation chart is prepared (~30 minutes before departure), provided your ticket was booked online.

### Why do festival special trains experience longer travel delays?
Special trains operate on provisional timetables without fixed route priority. When track congestion occurs, regular superfast trains like the Rajdhani, Vande Bharat, and Sampoorna Kranti receive track precedence, causing special trains to be halted at crossings. Plan for 3 to 5 hours of buffer time.
