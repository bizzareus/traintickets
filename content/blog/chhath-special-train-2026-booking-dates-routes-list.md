## When Does Chhath Puja 2026 Special Train Booking Open in IRCTC?

**IRCTC bookings for Chhath Puja 2026 special trains open daily at 8:00 AM IST either under the standard 60-day Advance Reservation Period (ARP) starting September 13, 2026, or through staggered 10-to-30-day booking windows as Zonal Railways notify individual 0-series festival special trains across September and October 2026.**

During Chhath Puja, passenger volume to Bihar and eastern Uttar Pradesh reaches the highest single peak on the Indian railway network. Regular express trains like the Sampoorna Kranti Express, Magadh Express, Bihar Sampark Kranti, Vaishali Express, and Vikramshila Express exhaust their general quotas within seconds of the 8:00 AM booking bell.

To handle this surge, Indian Railways schedules festival special trains using two distinct booking timelines:

1. **Scheduled Festival Specials (60-Day ARP):** Services planned months in advance enter the Passenger Reservation System (PRS) under the standard 60-day Advance Reservation Period (ARP). Bookings open at 8:00 AM IST exactly 60 days before the train departs from its originating station (excluding the departure date itself).
2. **Notified 0-Series Specials (Staggered 10-to-30-Day Window):** To respond to live waiting list data, Zonal Railways (ECR, NR, WR, CR, SWR) announce supplementary 0-series rakes in rolling batches. These trains are uploaded to IRCTC with shorter reservation windows ranging from 10 to 30 days before departure.

## How Are Ticket Fares and Surcharges Calculated for 0-Series Special Trains?

**Fares on 0-series Chhath special trains follow the Train on Special Fare (TOSF) structure, adding a mandatory 10% surcharge on Second Sitting (2S) and up to 30% on Sleeper and AC classes over regular Mail or Express base fares. No senior citizen, student, or general quota fare concessions apply.**

Indian Railways classifies these seasonal operations as Trains on Special Fares (TOSF). Because special trains require dedicated rake repositioning, empty return runs, and extra crew shifts, ticket prices reflect a fixed surcharge over standard Mail/Express fares.

The pricing structure works as follows:

- **Second Sitting (2S):** 10% surcharge applied directly to the regular Mail/Express base fare.
- **Sleeper Class (SL):** 30% surcharge applied to the base fare, bounded by minimum and maximum caps that mirror Tatkal surcharge limits.
- **AC Classes (3E, 3A, 2A, 1AC, CC):** 30% surcharge applied to the base fare, subject to class-specific maximum Tatkal ceilings.
- **No Concessions:** Special trains do not permit senior citizen, student, medical patient, or press quota concessions. Full fare is payable across all passenger categories.
- **Dynamic Pricing vs TOSF:** Unlike Suvidha trains (which raise prices by 20% after every 20% slab of tickets sold, up to three times base fare), regular 0-series TOSF specials maintain a uniform 10% to 30% surcharge from the first ticket sold to the last.

| **Second Sitting (2S)** | ~₹245 | ~₹270 – ₹280 | +10% on base fare | ₹15 – ₹30 |
| **Sleeper Class (SL)** | ~₹475 | ~₹620 – ₹650 | +30% on base fare | ₹100 – ₹200 |

*Note: Fares exclude GST (applicable on AC classes) and IRCTC payment processing fees. Catering charges are separate.*

## Is Tatkal and Premium Tatkal Booking Available on Chhath Special Trains?

**Tatkal and Premium Tatkal quotas are available on select 0-series Chhath special trains, opening at 10:00 AM IST for AC classes and 11:00 AM IST for Sleeper one day prior to origin departure. However, many festival specials operate strictly under flat special fares without any Tatkal quota allocations.**

Whether a Chhath special train offers Tatkal depends entirely on how the originating Zonal Railway configured the train profile in the PRS system:

1. **Special Trains with Tatkal Quota:** When notified as regular seasonal expresses, roughly 15% to 30% of coach capacity is allocated to Tatkal. The booking window follows standard timings:
   - **AC Classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC):** Opens at 10:00 AM IST one day before the train departs from its originating station.
   - **Non-AC Sleeper (SL, 2S):** Opens at 11:00 AM IST one day before origin departure.
2. **Special Trains Without Tatkal Quota:** Because 0-series trains already charge a 30% special fare surcharge across the entire train, some zones release 100% of seats under the General Quota at the special rate. On these rakes, no separate Tatkal window exists.
3. **Premium Tatkal (PT):** Premium Tatkal is relatively rare on 0-series specials. When enabled, fares surge dynamically based on remaining demand, and no waitlisted or RAC tickets are issued.

To check if your target special train has Tatkal quota:
- Open IRCTC search, enter your origin and destination, and pick your travel date.
- Locate the 0-series train and click the **Quota** dropdown menu.
- If Tatkal is greyed out or displays `TATKAL QUOTA NOT APPLICABLE FOR THIS TRAIN`, the train runs exclusively on general special fares.

## What Are the Cancellation and Refund Rules for 0-Series Festival Specials?

**Standard Indian Railways cancellation slabs apply to 0-series Chhath special trains, but cancellation deductions and clerkage fees are calculated against the higher special fare base. Fully waitlisted online e-tickets auto-cancel after final chart preparation with a full refund, while confirmed tickets cancelled within 4 hours of departure receive zero refund.**

If your travel plans change or you manage to book a faster train, keep these cancellation rules and refund deductions in mind:

| Cancellation Timing | Confirmed Ticket Refund Rule | Deductions / Clerkage |
|---|---|---|
| **More than 48 hours before origin departure** | Full refund minus flat clerkage deduction | ₹240 (1AC/EC), ₹200 (2A), ₹180 (3A/3E/CC), ₹120 (SL), ₹60 (2S) (+ GST on AC) |
| **Between 48 hours and 12 hours before origin departure** | 75% refund of total fare paid | 25% of the total ticket fare (subject to flat minimums above) |
| **Between 12 hours and 4 hours before departure (or chart prep)** | 50% refund of total fare paid | 50% of the total ticket fare (subject to flat minimums above) |
| **Less than 4 hours before departure or after charting** | 0% refund | No refund on confirmed tickets |
| **Waitlisted e-ticket unconfirmed after final charting** | 100% automatic system refund | Nominal IRCTC service charge retained; balance credited to bank |
| **RAC ticket cancelled up to 30 minutes before departure** | Refund minus clerkage fee | ₹60 per passenger (+ GST for AC classes) |

Because the base fare for special trains includes the 30% TOSF surcharge, percentage-based deductions (25% or 50%) will result in higher cash deductions compared to standard express trains. 

For waitlisted online e-tickets, you do not need to file a Ticket Deposit Receipt (TDR). The system automatically cancels unconfirmed e-tickets and processes refunds to your original payment method within 2 to 4 working days.

## How to Get a Confirmed Seat When All Chhath Trains Show REGRET?

**When direct Chhath trains show REGRET, you can get confirmed seats by booking split-segment tickets on the same train using LastBerth Smart Seats, booking from the origin station with an updated boarding point, tracking vacant berths on Chart Vacancy, or monitoring emergency festival specials announced by Zonal Railways.**

During the peak Chhath rush, direct end-to-end waitlists often exceed 200 to 300 passengers, triggering the `REGRET` status where IRCTC halts all further bookings. When facing this bottleneck, use these practical techniques to secure confirmed travel:

### 1. Split-Journey Booking via Smart Seats
Indian Railways splits carriage inventory across different station quota pools. While a direct seat from New Delhi to Patna may show `REGRET`, seats on that exact same train may be vacant from New Delhi to Kanpur, and from Kanpur to Patna. 

Instead of sitting on an unconfirmed waitlist, use [Smart Seats](/) on LastBerth to search contiguous confirmed segments along your train route. You travel on the same rake without changing trains, simply moving between assigned berths at the intermediate junction.

### 2. Book from Origin Station and Update Boarding Point
Intermediate stations draw from smaller quota pools like Remote Location Waiting List (RLWL) or Pooled Quota (PQWL), which hit `REGRET` very quickly. Originating stations use General Waiting List (GNWL), which commands the largest berth allocation.

Search availability starting from the train's originating station (for instance, booking from Anand Vihar instead of Aligarh or Kanpur). If confirmed seats or low waitlists are available from the origin, book that ticket. Under Indian Railways guidelines, you can change your boarding point online via IRCTC up to 30 minutes before chart preparation.

### 3. Check Real-Time Vacancy on Chart Vacancy
Reservation charts are finalized roughly 4 to 8 hours before train departure. Berths reserved for VIP quotas, emergency allotments, and unutilized intermediate quotas get released into Current Reservation (`CURR_AVBL`). 

Use [Chart Vacancy](/chart-vacancy) and [Chart Times](/chart-times) to see which coaches have unsold berths after charting. Current Availability berths are 100% confirmed and can be booked on IRCTC at standard rates up to 30 minutes before departure.

### 4. Watch for Unannounced Clone and Standby Specials
Northern Railway and East Central Railway routinely maintain standby rakes at Delhi, Anand Vihar, Patna, and Danapur. When station crowd density surges, authorities notify unannounced special services on 12 to 24 hours notice. Monitoring zonal railway social bulletins and local PRS notices lets you book these extra trips before general quotas fill.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### Can you travel in Chhath special trains with a general unreserved ticket?

Yes, but only inside designated unreserved General Second Class (GS) coaches. You cannot board reserved Sleeper, 3AC, or 2AC coaches with an unreserved paper or UTS ticket. Passengers caught traveling in reserved compartments without a valid confirmed reservation are penalized under Section 138 of the Railways Act with a minimum fine of ₹500 plus full class fare difference.

### How many days in advance do special train bookings open on IRCTC?

While standard express trains adhere to the 60-day Advance Reservation Period (ARP), 0-series Chhath special trains have variable booking windows. Some scheduled specials follow the 60-day timeline opening at 8:00 AM IST, while supplementary festival rakes open 10 to 30 days prior to departure as soon as Zonal Railways issue notifications.

### Do Chhath special trains have bedrolls and linen in AC classes?

Yes. All air-conditioned coaches (1AC, 2AC, 3AC, and 3E Economy) on 0-series festival special trains provide standard bedroll kits, including two bedsheets, one pillow with cover, one blanket, and a hand towel. The linen charge is built into the AC ticket fare. Sleeper and 2S classes do not provide bedrolls.

### Are pantry meals available on 0-series special trains?

Most 0-series Chhath special trains do not carry an operational pantry car. Indian Railways facilitates onboard food arrangements through IRCTC e-catering, platform refreshment stalls, and Jan Aahaar centers. Meals are not included in the ticket fare, so carrying personal food and water for long journeys is strongly recommended.

### What happens if my Chhath special train ticket remains waitlisted after charting?

If you booked an online e-ticket and it remains on the waiting list after final chart preparation, it is automatically cancelled by the PRS system. The ticket amount is refunded back to your original payment account within 2 to 4 working days. You cannot board any reserved coach with a fully waitlisted e-ticket.

### Can I change the boarding point on a Chhath special train ticket?

Yes. You can modify your boarding station online through your IRCTC account up to 30 minutes before the train's scheduled departure from its originating station or before final chart preparation. Once updated, your original seat allocation remains reserved from the new boarding station.

### Why do 0-series special trains take longer than regular express trains?

Special trains operate on temporary timetables without fixed permanent path priority. Scheduled flagship trains like Rajdhani, Vande Bharat, and regular Superfast expresses take precedence over 0-series specials. As a result, special trains are often routed into station loops for overtakes, leading to longer running times.

### How to track live platform coach position for special trains?

Because 0-series special trains often use spare rakes from various zones, coach layouts can vary from standard configurations. You can look up the verified carriage layout on [Coach Journey Lookup](/seat-status) before reaching the station, and double-check overhead digital platform indicators once you arrive.
