## When does booking open? The two-track confusion nobody explains

I get this DM a lot — "bhaiya, Chhath special ka booking kab khulega?" Honest answer: two different clocks.

Some specials are pre-planned. Railway decided in August they'll run Anand Vihar-Patna extra daily. Those behave like normal trains — 60 days before, 8 AM sharp. Nov 12 travel = Sept 13 booking. Simple. Set alarm 7:50, keep IRCTC logged in, passenger list ready. You know the drill.

But bulk of Chhath specials are last-minute reactions. Railways watches waitlists touch REGRET in September, then scrambles — "Patna ke liye do aur rake lagao." Press note comes, IRCTC uploads in 24-48 hrs, booking window only 10-30 days. My friend calls them "panic trains." Haha, but accurate.

Why the panic? Because Chhath week is *the* peak. Not Diwali peak — bigger. Sampoorna Kranti, Magadh, Bihar Sampark Kranti, Vaishali, Vikramshila — all dead in seconds at 8 AM. I tried for my chacha last year, 8:00:38 — REGRET. 38 seconds!

Zones pushing extras: Northern (Delhi), East Central (Patna/Hajipur — they handle the real load), Western, Central, South Western. Spare coaches from everywhere.

Dates I wrote on paper stuck to my fridge:

Short-notice specials? They open within a day or two of the press note. I check ECR/NR handles every Sunday with morning chai. Boring habit, but got me 2 confirmed seats in 3 years. Worth it.

## Fares — yeah, specials cost more, let's be real

Railways tag: TOSF, Train on Special Fare. Translation: festival surcharge.

Why? Empty rake movement, extra crew, overtime. I grumble, but I pay — flight Delhi-Patna at ₹14k vs special 3A at ₹1650? No contest.

What I paid roughly last time:

- 2S sitting: 10% extra. Like ₹10-15 more. Who cares.
- Sleeper: 30% extra. Normal ~₹475, special ~₹620-650. Pinches a bit.
- 3E / 3A / 2A / 1AC: 30% extra with caps. 3A normal ~₹1265, special ~₹1600-1680. 2A ~₹1825 vs ~₹2280-2380. Yeah.

Zero concessions. Senior citizen, student, patient — nobody. My dad argued at the counter, counter guy just smiled. Rules are rules.

One relief vs Suvidha trains: Suvidha keeps hiking — 20% slabs up to 3x base! Specials stay flat first-to-last ticket. So late booking doesn't punish extra (if seats left, big if).

Delhi-Patna ~1000 km, my notebook comparison:

| Class | Normal express | Special | Extra hit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2S | ~₹245 | ~₹255-260 | +10%, barely anything |
| Sleeper | ~₹475 | ~₹620-650 | +30%, my usual |
| 3E | ~₹1180 | ~₹1480-1550 | +30% |
| 3A | ~₹1265 | ~₹1600-1680 | +30%, dad's favourite to crib about |
| 2A | ~₹1825 | ~₹2280-2380 | +30% |

Add 5% GST on AC, IRCTC fee. Food separate — no pantry in most specials, just e-catering + platform stalls. My mother packs puri-aloo, achar, thekua. Whole coach smells amazing by Gaya. Carry extra, share a little. Chhath spirit.

## Tatkal on specials? Confusing, I'll simplify

Depends on that specific rake. Seriously, each train different.

Some specials keep Tatkal — then usual drill, day before, 10 AM AC, 11 AM sleeper. 15-30% seats kept aside. I got Tatkal once on 04068, hands were shaking while paying. UPI failed first time, second time went through. Heart attack stuff.

Many specials though? 100% general at special fare, no Tatkal window at all. Because they already charge 30% extra on all seats, why keep Tatkal? Makes sense, still annoying.

Premium Tatkal? Rare on specials. When it exists, price jumps with demand, no waitlist, no RAC. Rich-or-desperate option.

How I check: IRCTC → enter route + date → find 0-number → click Quota dropdown. If Tatkal greyed or says NOT APPLICABLE, that's it, no Tatkal. I check this *before* setting my 10 AM alarm. Saves heartbreak.

## Cancel karna pade toh? My ₹600 lesson

2023 I cancelled a special, lost more than expected because % cuts apply on higher base. Note it:

- 48+ hrs before: flat clerkage only. ₹240 (1AC), ₹200 (2A), ₹180 (3A/3E/CC), ₹120 sleeper, ₹60 2S. +GST on AC. Rest back.
- 48-12 hrs: 25% gone. On specials that's bigger rupees. Ouch.
- 12-4 hrs / first chart: 50% gone. Half!
- Under 4 hrs / after chart: zero. Bye money.
- Online waitlist never confirmed? Auto-cancel, full back, no clerkage (gateway fee stays, sneaky). No TDR needed, 2-4 working days.
- RAC cancelled 30 mins before? ₹60 clerkage.

Table for screenshot:

| You cancel | You get | Cut |
|---|---|---|
| 48+ hrs before start | full minus clerkage | ₹240/200/180/120/60 by class |
| 48-12 hrs | 75% | 25% of fare |
| 12-4 hrs | 50% | half fare |
| under 4 hrs | nothing | zero |
| waitlist e-ticket, never confirmed | full auto-refund | no clerkage |
| RAC till 30 mins before | minus clerkage | ₹60 |

Partial PNR — 2 confirmed, 1 waiting? File online TDR 30 mins before for the waiting guy, else money stuck. I forgot once, lost ₹600. Still stings when I think about it.

## All Chhath trains REGRET? What I actually do, step by step

Direct waitlist 200-300, IRCTC flashes REGRET, means "bas, full." Don't stare at it. Here's my order:

### I hunt two-ticket splits first

Delhi-Patna direct dead, but Delhi-Kanpur free + Kanpur-Patna free, same train? Book both. Stay in same rake, shift berths at Kanpur.

[Smart Seats](/) on LastBerth does the hunting. 2 mins. My hit rate Chhath week? 1 in 3 searches. But that 1 = reaching home for Kharna kheer. Worth every refresh.

### I book from start station, shift boarding later

Small stations get tiny quotas — RLWL/PQWL types. They REGRET first. Starting stations get big GNWL pool.

Like Aligarh shows REGRET but Anand Vihar (origin) shows confirmed or low WL. I book from Anand Vihar, then change boarding to Aligarh on IRCTC. Allowed till second chart (~30 mins before). My uncle from Tundla does this yearly. Never failed yet.

### I pounce after chart

Charts 4-8 hrs before start. VIP/defence/empty quotas spill as CURR_AVBL — fully confirmed, normal price, bookable till 30 mins before.

I open [Chart Vacancy](/chart-vacancy) + [Chart Times](/chart-times) right after chart time. Last Chhath my sister got LTT-Patna sleeper at 10 PM for 5 AM train. She screamed, baby woke, whole house woke. But confirmed!

### I watch for clones / standby rakes

NR and ECR park spare rakes at Delhi, Anand Vihar, Patna, Danapur during Nov 10-15. Crowd explodes → unannounced special in 12-24 hrs. Appears on IRCTC with zero waitlist initially.

Follow zone bulletins, station news, keep LastBerth open. Bags packed? Book in 10 mins flat. I got Delhi-Lucknow clone in 2022 at midnight refresh. Still my favourite train story.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### Can you travel in Chhath special trains with a general unreserved ticket?

Only in the general dabba, please. GS coaches — yes with UTS paper ticket. Reserved sleeper/3AC/2AC with general ticket? No. TTE will catch, Section 138, ₹500+ fare difference. I've seen a guy argue "Chhath hai sir, adjust kar lo" — didn't work. TTE was polite but firm. Don't try. Either get confirmed or stick to unreserved coach.

### How many days in advance do special train bookings open on IRCTC?

Mixed. Planned ones — 60 days, 8 AM. Supplementary 0-series — 10-30 days after press note. Clones — 1-2 days! Basically Sept-Oct keep looking. My routine: Sunday morning, chai in hand, check press notes + IRCTC + LastBerth. 15 mins. Boring, effective. Got 2 trips in 3 years this way.

### Do Chhath special trains have bedrolls and linen in AC classes?

Yes, in AC they do. 1AC/2AC/3AC/3E — two sheets, pillow + cover, blanket, towel. Charge already inside fare. Sleeper/2S? Bring your own chadar. I carry a thin blanket even for 3A in specials — sometimes stock runs short when rake is mixed. And a lock-chain for bags, Chhath crowd = full train, safety first.

### Are pantry meals available on 0-series special trains?

Mostly no pantry car, yaar. IRCTC e-catering (order online, delivered at station), platform stalls, Jan Aahaar. Food not in ticket. My advice: pack for 24 hrs. Puri-sabzi, thekua, bananas, 2L water. E-catering biryani at Itarsi is decent backup, but don't depend fully. Specials get delayed in loops, hunger + delay = bad mood. Been there.

### What happens if my Chhath special train ticket remains waitlisted after charting?

Online ticket + still waiting after final chart = auto-cancel. Money back in 2-4 working days to same account. You can't board reserved coach with it. I know it hurts — my 2021 Patna ticket died exactly like this, I watched Chhath on video call. If anyone in PNR is confirmed and rest waiting (partial), file TDR for waiting ones. Else money stuck.

### Can I change the boarding point on a Chhath special train ticket?

Yes, same rule. IRCTC app/website, boarding change till second chart (~30 mins before start). Seat stays yours from new station. I do Anand Vihar booking + Ghaziabad boarding often — GNWL benefit + skip Anand Vihar madness. Just don't forget to actually change it! Friend forgot, seat got allotted to someone else, 5 AM fight at station. Update it the night before, screenshot confirmation.

### Why do 0-series special trains take longer than regular express trains?

No priority, simple. Rajdhani, Vande Bharat, superfasts zoom, specials wait in side loops. Temporary timetable, no fixed path. My special took 21 hrs Delhi-Patna vs 17 normal. I now keep a day buffer — reach by Nov 11 for Nov 14 Arghya. Download movies, carry power bank, make friends with co-passengers. Half the coach is going to same ghat anyway, time flies.

### How to track live platform coach position for special trains?

Spare rakes = weird coach order. I check [Coach Journey Lookup](/seat-status) morning of travel — screenshot layout. At station, match with display boards + ask porter/announcement. Patna/Danapur displays during Chhath are actually decent, staff deployed extra. Still reach 45 mins early. With family + thekua baskets + kids, last-minute running is nightmare. Been there, done that, no repeat.
