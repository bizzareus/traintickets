"Chhath special kab khulega?" Two answers, honestly:

## Fares — exact extra, no sugarcoat

Tag: TOSF (Train on Special Fare). Translation: festival surcharge for empty-rake + crew overtime. I grumble, then pay — Delhi–Patna flight ₹14k vs special 3A ₹1,650? No contest.

- **2S:** +10%. ~₹10–15. Who cares.
- **Sleeper:** +30%. ~₹475 → ~₹620–650. My usual.
- **3E/3A/2A/1AC:** +30% with caps. 3A ~₹1,265 → ~₹1,600–1,680 (papa's favourite to crib about). 2A ~₹1,825 → ~₹2,280–2,380.
- **Concessions:** zero. Senior citizen, student, patient — papa argued at the counter once, the babu just smiled. Rules are rules.
- **vs Suvidha:** Suvidha climbs in slabs to 3x; specials flat first-to-last ticket. Late booking doesn't punish extra (if seats left — big if).

Delhi–Patna ~1,000 km (excl. 5% GST on AC + IRCTC fee; food separate — mostly no pantry, just e-catering + stalls. Mummy packs puri-aloo, achar, thekua; whole coach smells amazing by Gaya. Carry extra, share a little — Chhath spirit):

## Tatkal on specials? Direct answer

Depends on that rake — each train differs. Some keep Tatkal (**10 AM AC / 11 AM sleeper**, day before, 15–30% seats). I got Tatkal once on 04068, hands shaking, UPI failed first, went second time. Heart-attack stuff.

Many specials? 100% general at special fare, **no Tatkal at all** — they already charge 30% on everything, so no separate window. Premium Tatkal rare; when on, dynamic price, no waitlist/RAC.

How I check *before* setting my 10 AM alarm: IRCTC → route + date → 0-number → Quota dropdown. Greyed/NOT APPLICABLE = no Tatkal. Saves heartbreak.

## Cancellation — what you lose, directly

Cancelled a special in 2023, lost more than expected — % cuts hit the higher base. Screenshot table:

| You cancel | You get | Cut |
|---|---|---|
| 48+ hrs before start | full minus clerkage | ₹240/200/180/120/60 (1AC/2A/3A/SL/2S) + GST on AC |
| 48–12 hrs | 75% | 25% of fare — bigger rupees on specials! |
| 12–4 hrs / first chart | 50% | half fare |
| Under 4 hrs / after chart | nothing | zero |
| Online waitlist never confirmed | full auto-refund, 2–4 working days | no clerkage (gateway fee kept, sneaky) |
| RAC till 30 mins before | minus clerkage | ₹60 |

Partial PNR (2 confirmed + 1 waiting)? File online TDR 30 mins before for the waiting one, else money stuck. Forgot once — ₹600 gone. Still stings.

Online waitlist? No TDR needed — system auto-cancels + refunds. Just don't board on it.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### Can you travel in Chhath special trains with a general unreserved ticket?

Only in the general dabba. Fact: GS coaches yes with UTS paper ticket; reserved sleeper/3AC/2AC with general ticket = no. Caught = Section 138, **₹500+ fare difference**. Saw a guy try "Chhath hai sir, adjust kar lo" — TTE polite but firm. Didn't work. Get confirmed or stick to unreserved coach.

### How many days in advance do special train bookings open on IRCTC?

Mixed. Fact: planned ones 60 days/8 AM; supplementary 0-series **10–30 days** after press notes; clones **1–2 days**. Sept–Oct routine: Sunday morning, chai in hand, press notes + IRCTC + LastBerth. 15 mins. Boring, effective — 2 trips in 3 years for me.

### Do Chhath special trains have bedrolls and linen in AC classes?

Yes in AC. Fact: 1AC/2AC/3AC/3E get two sheets + pillow/cover + blanket + towel, charge inside fare. Sleeper/2S: carry your own chadar. I carry a thin blanket even in 3A specials — mixed rakes run short sometimes. Plus lock-chain; full train, safety first.

### Are pantry meals available on 0-series special trains?

Mostly no pantry, yaar. Fact: e-catering (order online, station delivery) + platform stalls + Jan Aahaar; food not in ticket. Pack 24 hrs: puri-sabzi, thekua, bananas, 2L water. E-catering biryani at Itarsi decent backup. Specials loop-wait + delay + hunger = bad mood. Been there.

### What happens if my Chhath special train ticket remains waitlisted after charting?

Online + still waiting after final chart = auto-cancel. Fact: refund in 2–4 working days, can't board reserved coach. My 2021 Patna ticket died exactly so — watched Chhath on video call. Partial PNR (some confirmed)? File TDR for waiting ones, else stuck.

### Can I change the boarding point on a Chhath special train ticket?

Yes. Fact: IRCTC app/website till second chart (**~30 mins before** start), seat stays yours from new station. I book Anand Vihar + board Ghaziabad — GNWL benefit, skip the crowd. But actually change it! Friend forgot, seat allotted away, 5 AM fight. Update night before, screenshot confirmation.

### Why do 0-series special trains take longer than regular express trains?

No priority — fact. Rajdhani/Vande Bharat/superfasts zoom, specials loop-wait. Temporary timetable, no fixed path. Mine: 21 hrs Delhi–Patna vs 17 normal. Keep a day buffer — reach Nov 11 for Nov 14 Arghya. Movies downloaded, power bank full, co-passengers half headed to your ghat anyway.

### How to track live platform coach position for special trains?

Spare rakes = weird order. Fact: check [Coach Journey Lookup](/seat-status) morning of travel + screenshot; match display boards at station + ask staff. Patna/Danapur deploy extra staff Chhath week, displays decent. Still reach 45 mins early — family + baskets + kids + last-minute run = nightmare. Done that, no repeat.
