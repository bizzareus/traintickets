## What Time Is the First Reservation Chart Prepared in IRCTC?

**The first reservation chart is prepared approximately 8 hours before scheduled train departure from the originating station. For early morning trains departing before 14:00 (2:00 PM), Indian Railways prepares the first chart the previous evening at 21:00 (9:00 PM) per Railway Board rules. This initial chart finalizes passenger berths and releases unallocated emergency, VIP, and foreign tourist quotas.**

Before the first chart is prepared, passenger status remains dynamic as cancellations occur and waitlists shift. Once the initial chart is generated:

- All pooled quotas (such as General, Tatkal, Senior Citizen, and Emergency Quota) are merged into a single final allocation list.
- Unused quota berths are automatically transferred to clear General Waiting List (GNWL) and Remote Location Waiting List (RLWL) passengers.
- Passengers receive an automated SMS from IRCTC displaying their confirmed coach and berth numbers (e.g., `B3-42` or `S4-17`) or their updated RAC status.
- Charting data is transmitted to the computerized Passenger Reservation System (PRS) and loaded onto the Train Ticket Examiner's (TTE) Handheld Terminal (HHT).

If you are boarding at an intermediate station rather than the train's originating station, the primary chart is prepared at the train's origin. However, remote location quota (RLWL) charts are finalized at their designated remote charting hubs before the train arrives at your boarding point.

## When Is the Second and Final Train Chart Prepared?

**The second and final reservation chart is prepared 30 minutes to 15 minutes before scheduled train departure from the originating station. This final chart captures last-minute cancellations made after the first chart, seats allotted to RAC passengers, and new confirmed tickets booked under Current Availability (CURR_AVBL). Once printed, no further online bookings or cancellations are permitted.**

The second chart serves as the definitive legal passenger manifest for the journey:

1. **Current Reservation cutoff:** Online booking on IRCTC and PRS counter booking for the train officially closes when the second chart is prepared (typically 15 to 30 minutes before departure).
2. **Final RAC promotion:** Any berths vacated by passengers who cancelled between the first and second charts are allotted to RAC passengers in order of priority.
3. **Waitlist auto-cancellation:** Any online e-ticket that remains on the waiting list after second chart preparation is automatically dropped from the passenger manifest.

## When Is Vande Bharat Chart Prepared: Morning vs Evening Rules

**The first reservation chart for Vande Bharat Express is prepared 4 to 8 hours before departure, with early morning services leaving before 14:00 finalized the previous evening between 20:00 and 21:00 IST. The second and final chart is finalized 30 to 15 minutes before departure, opening Current Availability at a 10% base discount.**

Because the vast majority of Vande Bharat Express services are daytime intercity chair cars (operating with AC Chair Car CC and Executive Class EC), their charting dynamics have distinct operational features:

### 1. Morning Vande Bharat Services (Departing Before 14:00)

For flagship morning trains like the New Delhi to Varanasi Vande Bharat (22436, departing 06:00 AM), Mumbai CSMT to Solapur Vande Bharat (22225, departing 06:05 AM), or KSR Bengaluru to Dharwad Vande Bharat (20661, departing 05:45 AM):

- **First Chart Preparation:** Prepared the **previous evening between 20:00 and 21:00 IST**.
- **Quota Release:** Unused VIP, administrative, and parliamentary emergency quotas are merged into the general pool overnight.
- **Notification:** Passengers on the waiting list learn whether their ticket confirmed before going to sleep the night before.

### 2. Afternoon and Evening Vande Bharat Services (Departing After 14:00)

For afternoon and evening runs like the Mumbai CSMT to Jalna Vande Bharat (20705, departing 13:10 PM) or New Delhi to Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Katra Vande Bharat (22439, departing 15:00 PM):

- **First Chart Preparation:** Prepared **4 to 6 hours before departure**, typically between 09:30 AM and 11:30 AM IST on the day of travel.
- **Current Availability Window:** Unoccupied seats immediately open for online booking at a 10% discount on base fares.

### 3. Critical Vande Bharat Distinction: No RAC Category

Unlike conventional mail and express trains where waitlisted passengers advance to RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) and share a berth, **Vande Bharat chair car trains have no RAC class**. Every seat is an individual contoured chair. Therefore:

- The queue advances directly: **WL (Waiting List) ➔ CNF (Confirmed Seat)**.
- If your e-ticket remains on the waiting list after the second chart is prepared, it is automatically cancelled with a 100% refund. You cannot board the train.

### 4. Vande Bharat Sleeper Services (Overnight Rakes)

On new overnight Vande Bharat Sleeper routes (such as 27651/27652 Mumbai to Bengaluru, 27421/27422 Varanasi to Delhi, and 27575/27576 Howrah to Kamakhya), charting follows overnight express rules:

- Evening departures (e.g., 27652 departing SMVB at 19:45 IST) have their first chart prepared between **11:30 AM and 13:00 IST** on departure day.
- Boarding is strictly restricted to confirmed ticket holders. Under Section 138 of the Railways Act, passengers attempting to travel on waitlisted e-tickets face a flat ₹500 penalty.

## What Is Shatabdi Express and Jan Shatabdi Chart Preparation Time?

**Shatabdi Express first reservation charts are prepared the previous evening at 21:00 IST for morning departures leaving before 14:00, or 4 to 6 hours before departure for afternoon services. The second chart is finalized 30 minutes before departure. Jan Shatabdi follows identical charting timelines across both AC Chair Car and Second Seating coaches.**

Shatabdi Express trains connect metropolitan capitals with commercial hubs within a 4 to 8-hour radius. Because almost all Shatabdi trains commence their runs early in the morning, the **21:00 previous-evening charting rule** applies to the majority of routes:

### 1. Morning Shatabdi Express Departures

- **New Delhi to Rani Kamlapati (Bhopal) Shatabdi (12002, departing 06:00 AM):** First chart prepared at **21:00 IST the previous night**.
- **New Delhi to Lucknow Swarna Shatabdi (12004, departing 06:10 AM):** First chart prepared at **21:00 IST the previous night**.
- **New Delhi to Kalka Shatabdi (12005, departing 05:15 AM / 12011, departing 07:40 AM):** First chart prepared at **21:00 IST the previous night**.
- **New Delhi to Ajmer Shatabdi (12015, departing 06:10 AM):** First chart prepared at **21:00 IST the previous night**.
- **MGR Chennai Central to KSR Bengaluru Shatabdi (12007, departing 06:00 AM):** First chart prepared at **21:00 IST the previous night**.

### 2. Afternoon and Return Shatabdi Express Departures

For return legs departing in the late afternoon or evening:

- **Ajmer to New Delhi Shatabdi (12016, departing 15:55 PM):** First chart prepared between **11:30 AM and 12:30 PM IST** on departure day.
- **Lucknow to New Delhi Swarna Shatabdi (12003, departing 15:30 PM):** First chart prepared between **10:30 AM and 11:30 AM IST**.
- **Chandigarh to New Delhi Shatabdi (12046, departing 12:05 PM):** First chart prepared between **08:00 AM and 09:00 AM IST**.

### 3. Jan Shatabdi Express Charting Details

Jan Shatabdi trains (such as the 12023 Howrah to Patna Jan Shatabdi, 12076 Kozhikode to Thiruvananthapuram Jan Shatabdi, or 12092 Kathgodam to Dehradun Jan Shatabdi) feature a dual-class rake structure comprising AC Chair Car (CC) and non-AC Second Sitting (2S).

- Charting rules are identical to Shatabdi Express: 21:00 IST the previous evening for morning runs, and 4 hours prior for afternoon services.
- Unallocated VIP quotas in both CC and 2S are released to clear the general waitlist. Unsold 2S and CC seats can be booked under Current Reservation up to 15 minutes before departure.

## What Is the Difference Between First Chart and Second Chart?

**The first chart is prepared 8 hours before departure to finalize initial berth allocations and release quotas, while the second chart is prepared 30 minutes before departure to record last-minute changes. First charting opens Current Availability for unsold seats, whereas second charting permanently closes all booking and triggers automatic refunds for waitlisted e-tickets.**

Here is a side-by-side comparison of the two charting stages:

| Feature / Rule             | First Reservation Chart                                                   | Second (Final) Reservation Chart                                |
| :------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Preparation Timing**     | **8 hours before departure** (or 21:00 previous night for morning trains) | **30 to 15 minutes before departure**                           |
| **Primary Purpose**        | Finalizes initial seat allotments and merges all special quotas           | Captures late cancellations and Current Availability bookings   |
| **Quota Berth Release**    | Unused VIP, EQ, Defense, and Tourist quotas released to waitlist          | Berths vacated by late cancellations assigned to RAC passengers |
| **Current Availability**   | Opens immediately after first charting at base fare or 10% discount       | Closes permanently upon second chart preparation                |
| **Waitlist Resolution**    | WL passengers may advance to RAC or Confirmed status                      | Remaining waitlisted e-tickets are auto-cancelled               |
| **Booking & Cancellation** | Current booking opens; confirmed cancellation incurs 50% fee              | All online cancellations and bookings strictly closed           |
| **Passenger Notification** | SMS sent with initial coach and berth allocation                          | Final status updated on IRCTC app, website, and station charts  |

Simply enter your train number, journey date, and boarding station to view an interactive coach-by-coach layout displaying available vacant berths by class (1AC, 2AC, 3AC, Sleeper, Chair Car).**

To inspect post-chart vacancies step-by-step:

1. Navigate to the IRCTC Charts portal or use [LastBerth Chart Vacancy](/chart-vacancy) for a rapid visual breakdown.
2. Enter the 5-digit train number (e.g., `22436` for Vande Bharat or `12002` for Shatabdi) and select your exact boarding station.
3. Select your travel class to view the color-coded coach diagram.
4. Green indicators highlight open, unbooked berths along with the exact station-to-station stretch for which they are vacant.
5. If a seat is free for your journey, log in immediately to IRCTC and book it as a **Current Reservation** ticket (`CURR_AVBL`).

Because vacant berths are sold in real time on a first-come, first-served basis, checking immediately after the first chart window gives you the highest chance of securing a confirmed seat.

## What Happens If Your Waitlisted Ticket Does Not Confirm After Charting?

**If a fully waitlisted online e-ticket remains on the waiting list after second chart preparation, IRCTC automatically cancels the ticket and issues a 100% refund. The full fare is credited back to your original payment source within 3 to 5 working days with zero clerkage fee deducted. Fully waitlisted e-ticket holders are legally barred from boarding.**

Key rules governing post-chart waitlisted tickets include:

- **Boarding prohibition:** Travelling in a reserved coach (Sleeper, AC, or Chair Car) with a fully waitlisted e-ticket is strictly illegal. Under Section 138 of the Railways Act (as amended under the Jan Vishwas Act), passengers caught doing so are treated as ticketless, removed at the next station, and fined a minimum penalty of **₹500 plus the single fare**.
- **Counter (PRS) tickets vs e-tickets:** Unlike e-tickets, physical counter waitlisted tickets are not automatically cancelled. A passenger with a counter waitlisted ticket may cancel it manually at any computerized PRS counter up to 30 minutes before train departure to claim a refund, or travel in the unreserved General coach if attached.
- **Auto-refund tracking:** You do not need to file a TDR for a fully waitlisted e-ticket dropped by the system. The refund is processed automatically by IRCTC.

## What Are the Rules for Partially Confirmed Tickets After Chart Preparation?

**If a single PNR has both confirmed (or RAC) passengers and waitlisted passengers after charting, the entire group is permitted to board the train. The waitlisted passengers may travel in the reserved coach alongside their confirmed co-passengers. The on-duty TTE will adjust seating onboard if vacant berths become available during the journey.**

If a partially confirmed group decides not to travel because some members remain waitlisted, they must follow the refund rules:

1. **Online TDR filing:** An online Ticket Deposit Receipt (TDR) must be filed on the IRCTC website or app at least **30 minutes before scheduled train departure**.
2. **Refund entitlement:** All passengers on the PNR (both confirmed and waitlisted) are entitled to a full refund of fare, minus the standard clerkage fee of ₹60 per passenger.
3. **Late filing forfeiture:** If the TDR is not filed within the 30-minute pre-departure window, the entire fare for confirmed passengers is forfeited.

## How Does TTE Handheld Terminal (HHT) Berth Allotment Work on Moving Trains?

**On-duty TTEs carry Handheld Terminals (HHTs) that synchronize in real time with the central Passenger Reservation System via cellular data. When a confirmed passenger fails to board by the second scheduled halt (the "two-stop rule"), the TTE marks them as a no-show on the HHT tablet, instantly freeing their berth for RAC and waitlisted passengers on board.**

The HHT automated allotment sequence operates under strict priority rules:

- **Step 1: RAC Promotion:** The vacant berth is automatically offered to the highest-ranking RAC passenger in the coach.
- **Step 2: Onboard Waitlist Reallocation:** If all RAC passengers have full berths, the system prompts the TTE to allot the berth to partially confirmed waitlist passengers travelling on the same train.
- **Step 3: Station-to-Station Transfer:** If no onboard passenger claims the seat, the vacant leg is transmitted back to the central PRS to appear as Current Availability for passengers searching from upcoming stations.

To check which berths may become vacant at upcoming stops, you can use LastBerth's [Chart Vacancy](/chart-vacancy) tool to see station-by-station occupancy for any specific coach and seat.

**Segment booking lets you find confirmed seats on fully waitlisted trains by splitting your origin-to-destination journey into two contiguous legs on the same train. Because IRCTC manages berth availability per station pair rather than train-wide, a seat that is waitlisted end-to-end may be completely confirmed from Station A to B in one coach and B to C in another.**

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### When is chart prepared for trains leaving before 8 AM or 12 PM?

For early morning trains departing between 00:01 and 14:00 (2:00 PM), the first reservation chart is prepared the previous evening at **21:00 (9:00 PM)**. The second and final chart is prepared 30 to 15 minutes before scheduled departure on the morning of travel.

### What is Vande Bharat chart preparation time for a morning train?

For morning Vande Bharat trains departing before 14:00 (such as 22436 New Delhi to Varanasi at 06:00 AM or 22225 Mumbai to Solapur at 06:05 AM), the first chart is prepared the previous evening between **20:00 and 21:00 IST**. The second chart is finalized 15 to 30 minutes before morning departure.

### When is Shatabdi Express chart prepared?

Shatabdi Express trains departing early morning (such as 12002 Bhopal Shatabdi or 12004 Lucknow Shatabdi leaving around 06:00 AM) have their first chart prepared at **21:00 IST the previous night**. Return afternoon Shatabdis have their first chart prepared approximately 4 hours before departure (around 11:30 AM to 12:30 PM IST).

### Does Vande Bharat have RAC after chart preparation?

No, Vande Bharat Express chair car trains do not have RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) status because all coaches are seated chair cars without sleeper berths. Waiting list tickets on Vande Bharat advance directly from Waiting List (WL) to Confirmed (CNF) status upon cancellations, or are auto-dropped.

### Can you book Vande Bharat or Shatabdi seats after chart preparation?

Yes, unsold seats on Vande Bharat and Shatabdi trains become available as **Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`)** tickets immediately after the first chart is prepared. These are 100% confirmed seats with coach and seat allocations, sold at a statutory 10% discount on base fares until 15 minutes before departure.

### Can you cancel a confirmed ticket after chart preparation?

No refund is granted if a confirmed ticket is cancelled online less than 4 hours before departure or after first chart preparation (whichever is earlier). However, if your train is delayed by more than 3 hours, you can file a TDR online before the train departs to receive a 100% full refund.

### Is a waitlisted online e-ticket valid for boarding after chart preparation?

No, fully waitlisted e-tickets are automatically cancelled upon second charting and are completely invalid for travel. Boarding a reserved coach with a waitlisted e-ticket is treated as ticketless travel under Section 138 of the Railways Act, punishable by a minimum fine of ₹500 plus the class fare.

### What is current availability in IRCTC after charting?

Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL` or `CURR AVL`) refers to unsold berths that go on sale after first chart preparation. Current reservation tickets are 100% confirmed seats with allocated coach and berth numbers, bookable online or at station counters until 15 to 30 minutes before departure.

### How much refund do you get if a waitlisted e-ticket is dropped?

When an e-ticket remains waitlisted after final charting, IRCTC automatically cancels it and credits a **100% full refund** to your original payment account. No cancellation charges or clerkage fees are deducted for auto-dropped waitlisted e-tickets.

### What is the deadline to book current reservation tickets on Vande Bharat?

Current reservation bookings on Vande Bharat Express remain open on the IRCTC website, mobile app, and PRS ticket counters until the second reservation chart is prepared, which is exactly **15 minutes before scheduled train departure**.
