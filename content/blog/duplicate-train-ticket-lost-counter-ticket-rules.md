## TL;DR
If you lose a confirmed or RAC counter ticket, you can get a duplicate train ticket at any railway reservation counter. The charges are flat clerkage fees (₹50–₹100) before chart preparation, or 50% of the fare for confirmed tickets after chart preparation. Waitlisted tickets cannot be replaced. For online e-tickets, no duplicate is needed—simply show your SMS or PDF with a valid ID.

## What Happens If You Lose Your Indian Railways Counter Ticket?

**If you lose a physical Indian Railways counter ticket, you cannot travel using a photocopy or SMS. You must obtain an official duplicate train ticket from a computerized reservation counter before the train departs. Otherwise, a traveling ticket examiner (TTE) will treat you as a passenger traveling without a ticket and impose heavy fines.**

Unlike online bookings, physical counter tickets (also known as I-tickets or window tickets) are considered valuable financial documents. If you lose yours, you cannot simply show a photo of it on your phone. If you board the train without a physical ticket or an officially issued duplicate, the TTE has the authority to charge you the full ticket fare from the train's origin station plus a penalty of ₹250. 

To avoid this, you must apply for a duplicate ticket immediately upon realizing the loss. The process must be completed at a reservation counter before the train's chart is prepared. If the chart is already prepared, getting a duplicate is still possible for confirmed tickets, but it will cost more.

## How to Get a Duplicate Train Ticket from the Counter?

**To get a duplicate train ticket, visit any computerized railway reservation counter, fill out the requisition form, and submit a written application along with a valid ID proof (like Aadhaar card). The booking clerk will verify your details against the reservation database and issue a duplicate ticket after collecting the applicable fee.**

When you go to the reservation counter, you need to provide specific details of your journey to help the clerk locate your booking. These details include:
* The PNR number (if you remember it or have a photo/copy of the ticket).
* The train number, date of journey, origin, and destination.
* Passenger names, ages, and genders as printed on the original ticket.

Having a digital photo of your lost ticket or keeping the PNR number noted down makes this process extremely fast. The clerk will verify your identity using the submitted ID proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, or Passport). Once verified, the clerk will print a duplicate ticket clearly marked with "DUPLICATE" at the top.

## What Are the Duplicate Train Ticket Charges in Indian Railways?

**The duplicate train ticket charges depend on the ticket status and class. Before chart preparation, confirmed and RAC tickets are replaced for a clerkage fee of ₹50 (Sleeper) or ₹100 (AC). After chart preparation, confirmed tickets cost 50% of the fare, while waitlisted tickets are never replaced.**

It is important to understand how waitlists affect these rules. When you book a ticket, your reservation queue moves along a specific track. The **WL full form is Waiting List**. The booking status typically progresses in the following order: **WL (Waiting List) → RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) → Confirmed**. 

If your ticket is an e-ticket that remains waitlisted (for example, moving from `WL/10` to `WL/1` but failing to reach RAC or Confirmed status), the system triggers an automatic refund and cancellation. For counter tickets, you must manually visit the counter for a refund. However, if you lose a waitlisted counter ticket, the railway does not issue a duplicate under any circumstances.

The table below details the exact charges for issuing a duplicate ticket:

| Ticket Type & Class | Lost Before Chart Prep | Lost After Chart Prep |
|---|---|---|
| **Second Class (2S) - Confirmed / RAC** | ₹50 per passenger | 50% of ticket fare |
| **Sleeper Class (SL) - Confirmed / RAC** | ₹50 per passenger | 50% of ticket fare |
| **AC Classes (3A, 2A, 1A, CC) - Confirmed / RAC** | ₹100 per passenger | 50% of ticket fare |
| **Waiting List (WL) - All Classes** | No duplicate ticket issued | No duplicate ticket issued |
| **Torn / Damaged Ticket - Confirmed / RAC** | ₹50 (2S/SL) or ₹100 (AC) | 25% of ticket fare |

## Can You Travel with a Soft Copy of a Lost Counter Ticket?

**No, you cannot travel with a soft copy, photo, or SMS of a lost counter ticket. Indian Railways rules state that physical counter tickets are financial documents; a digital copy is invalid for travel. You must get a duplicate train ticket or book a new ticket to avoid being penalized as ticketless.**

If your train is departing soon and you do not have time to get a duplicate ticket at the counter, you should search for a **current available ticket**. A **current available ticket** is a **fully confirmed seat** with coach/berth numbers, which opens 4 hours before departure (right after chart preparation) and closes 30 minutes before the train leaves. 

If you choose to board the train with only a photo of your counter ticket, the TTE will treat you as ticketless. Even if you show your ID and prove that the ticket was booked under your name, you will be fined. The only way to travel legally is by holding the physical duplicate ticket or booking a fresh ticket (such as a current available ticket).

## What Are the Refund Rules If You Find the Lost Ticket Later?

**If you find your original lost ticket later, you can claim a refund on the duplicate train ticket charges. Present both the original and duplicate tickets at the station reservation counter before train departure. The railway will refund your duplicate charges after deducting a processing fee of ₹20 or 5%.**

This refund rule is designed to protect genuine passengers who misplaced their tickets. However, please note that the original and duplicate tickets must be presented together before the actual departure of the train. If you present them after the train has left, no refund will be processed.

## Lost and Duplicate Train Ticket Questions (FAQ)

### Can I travel with a photocopy of a lost counter ticket?
No. Indian Railways rules do not permit travel with a photocopy of a counter ticket. You must obtain an official duplicate ticket from the reservation counter.

### What should I do if I lose my online e-ticket?
If you lose your online e-ticket, you do not need a duplicate ticket. You can simply show the SMS received from IRCTC or download the PDF ticket on your mobile phone, along with a valid ID proof.

### Can I get a duplicate ticket for a waitlisted counter ticket?
No. Indian Railways does not issue duplicate tickets for waitlisted counter tickets under any circumstances. If you lose a waitlisted counter ticket, you cannot claim a refund or travel.

### How much does it cost to get a duplicate ticket after chart preparation?
If you request a duplicate ticket after chart preparation, you will be charged 50% of the total ticket fare, provided the ticket is confirmed. No duplicate is issued for RAC tickets after chart preparation.

### What is the full form of WL in railway?
WL full form is Waiting List. It indicates that the ticket is not confirmed and the passenger is in a queue waiting for cancellations to secure a seat.
