## What Is Bandhan Express and Which Route Does It Run?

**The Bandhan Express is a premier international passenger train service connecting Kolkata Terminal (KOAA) in West Bengal with Khulna Junction (KLNB) in southwestern Bangladesh. Covering 172 kilometers across the Petrapole-Benapole border, the service operates with fully air-conditioned LHB rake coaches, providing direct cross-border transit between India and Bangladesh with integrated customs and immigration clearance.**

Inaugurated in November 2017, the service revived a historic rail link between West Bengal and the Khulna division that had been closed for over five decades. The train runs via Dum Dum, Bangaon, and the Petrapole border checkpost on the Indian side, enters Bangladesh through Benapole, and continues onward through Jhikargachha and Jessore before terminating at Khulna.

Unlike standard domestic trains, the Bandhan Express functions as an international diplomatic corridor. Passenger manifests are screened by border security forces and customs officials before departure, ensuring smooth transit without road border queues at the Haridaspur land port.

## How Do You Book Bandhan Express Tickets at Fairlie Place PRS Counters?

**Bandhan Express tickets cannot be booked online through IRCTC or mobile ticketing apps. Passengers must purchase physical international tickets in person at designated railway PRS counters, primarily Fairlie Place (Dalhousie) or Kolkata Station in India, and Khulna Station in Bangladesh. Every passenger must present an original valid passport and an approved visa before ticket issuance.**

The bilateral security agreement between India and Bangladesh requires physical inspection of travel credentials before ticket issuance. Each issued ticket is personalized with the passenger's passport number, visa number, and nationality.

To book your ticket in Kolkata when service is active:

1. **Visit Fairlie Place PRS Counter:** Head to Eastern Railway Headquarters, 6 Fairlie Place, BBD Bagh (Dalhousie), Kolkata. International reservation counters operate from **10:00 AM to 05:00 PM** on weekdays and Saturdays.
2. **Alternative Counter at Kolkata Station:** You can also purchase tickets at the International Passenger Reservation counter located on Platform 1 of Kolkata Chitpur Terminal (`KOAA`).
3. **Carry Mandatory Documents:** Bring your original passport along with clear photocopies of the passport information page and valid Bangladesh visa.
4. **Fill the International Requisition Form:** Complete the specialized international PRS booking slip detailing your passport number, visa number, and address in Bangladesh.
5. **Payment Mode:** Counters accept Indian Rupees through cash, UPI, and major debit or credit cards.

In Bangladesh, tickets can be purchased at the international counters of **Khulna Railway Station** or **Kamalapur Railway Station in Dhaka**, payable in Bangladeshi Taka (BDT).

## What Are the Passport, Visa, and Document Requirements for Travel?

**Cross-border rail travel on the Bandhan Express requires an original passport with at least six months remaining validity and two blank pages, alongside an approved destination visa. Digital photos or mobile screenshots are strictly rejected. Indian travelers require a Bangladesh visa, while Bangladeshi visitors must carry a valid Indian visa with authorized rail transit.**

Both Indian citizens traveling to Bangladesh and Bangladeshi citizens traveling to India must comply with these strict cross-border immigration requirements:

- **Valid Passport:** Your passport must have at least two blank pages for immigration entry and exit stamps, with a minimum validity of 6 months from the date of travel.
- **Destination Visa:** Indian travelers must obtain an approved Bangladesh visa (tourist, business, medical, or student visa) from Bangladesh diplomatic missions in Kolkata, New Delhi, or Mumbai before booking.
- **Return Visa Check:** Bangladeshi travelers must possess an active Indian visa with sufficient validity.
- **Advance Reservation Period (ARP):** Tickets open up to **30 days in advance** of the journey date at designated railway counters for regular passengers, and up to **365 days in advance** for foreign tourists under official Ministry of Railways guidelines.
- **Third-Country Foreign Nationals:** Non-Indian and non-Bangladeshi travelers holding valid visas for both nations are permitted to travel on Bandhan Express, provided their visa allows travel via rail and the Petrapole-Benapole international land border.

Ensure you carry original physical passports on travel day. Digital soft copies or mobile screenshots are strictly rejected at immigration checkpoints.

## How Do Customs and Immigration Work at Kolkata Station and Benapole?

**Customs and immigration screening for India departures happens inside the International Passenger Terminal at Kolkata Station (KOAA) prior to train boarding. Passengers board a sealed rake following baggage X-ray scans and passport exit stamping. Bangladesh entry immigration and customs checks take place upon arrival at Benapole station before the train continues directly toward Khulna.**

This streamlined dual-terminal procedure eliminates the cumbersome practice of passengers having to disembark with heavy baggage along isolated railway tracks:

1. **Step 1: Kolkata Station Security (05:15 AM to 06:15 AM):** Arriving passengers enter the dedicated international departure concourse. Luggage is scanned through registered customs X-ray scanners.
2. **Step 2: Indian Immigration Departure Stamp:** Bureau of Immigration officers verify your passport, inspect the Bangladesh visa, capture biometric data if needed, and stamp the official Indian departure exit seal.
3. **Step 3: Boarding the Sealed Rake (06:30 AM):** Once cleared, passengers board the train directly from the sterile platform. The train doors are locked for transit to the border.
4. **Step 4: Petrapole Operational Clearance:** The train halts briefly at Petrapole on the Indian border for mechanical and track clearances, without passengers disembarking.
5. **Step 5: Benapole Border Clearance (Bangladesh):** At Benapole station, Bangladesh Immigration and Customs officers complete passenger entry manifests and entry stamping.
6. **Step 6: Arrival at Khulna:** After customs clearance, the train proceeds non-stop through Jessore into Khulna, where passengers exit freely.

For the return journey on Train 13130, departure immigration is conducted at Benapole and final Indian entry immigration is completed at Kolkata Station.

## What Are the Baggage Rules and Prohibited Items on Bandhan Express?

**Adult passengers traveling on the Bandhan Express receive a free baggage allowance of 35 kilograms across both 1AC and CC classes, while children receive 20 kilograms. Excess luggage is charged at standard railway parcel rates. Commercial consignments, gold bullion, narcotics, weapons, and unprocessed seeds are strictly prohibited under Indian and Bangladeshi border customs laws.**

Because the train traverses an international frontier, customs authorities enforce strict cross-border baggage restrictions:

- **Free Allowance for Children:** Children traveling on child tickets (ages 5 to 11) are entitled to a 20 kg free luggage allowance across all classes.
- **Currency Regulations:** Indian travelers can carry up to ₹25,000 INR in cash or foreign currency notes within Reserve Bank of India (RBI) customs limits. Bangladeshi passengers must adhere to Bangladesh Bank foreign currency declaration thresholds.
- **Prohibited Items:** Narcotics, firearms, ammunition, commercial quantities of gold bullion, unprocessed agricultural seeds, and duty-exceeding tobacco or alcohol are strictly prohibited.
- **Commercial Goods Restriction:** Bandhan Express is exclusively a passenger service. Carrying commercial consignments or wholesale trade goods in passenger luggage is treated as smuggling and results in seizure under the Customs Act.

## What Are the Cancellation and Refund Rules for International Train Tickets?

**Bandhan Express international tickets must be surrendered in person at the issuing railway PRS counter for cancellation and refunds. Ticket cancellation more than 48 hours before scheduled departure incurs a flat deduction of ₹100 or 150 BDT per passenger. Cancellations within 48 to 12 hours lose 50 percent, while no refund applies under 12 hours.**

Because international tickets are issued with physical passport verification, cancellation cannot be processed online through IRCTC:

- **More Than 48 Hours Before Departure:** Flat clerkage deduction of ₹100 INR (or 150 BDT) per passenger; balance refunded immediately in cash or credited back.
- **Between 48 Hours and 12 Hours Before Departure:** 50% deduction of base fare, plus applicable service and reservation charges.
- **Less Than 12 Hours or After Departure:** Zero refund. No cancellation is entertained once the immigration manifest has closed.
- **Full Refund for Service Cancellations:** When train runs are officially cancelled by railway authorities due to bilateral directives, passengers receive a **100% full refund** without any clerkage charge upon surrendering original tickets at the booking counter within 30 days of the journey date.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### Is Bandhan Express currently running between Kolkata and Khulna?

No, commercial passenger operations on the Bandhan Express (13129/13130) remain temporarily suspended following bilateral security reviews between Indian and Bangladeshi railway authorities. Freight train operations continue along the border, while passenger travel is currently conducted by road through the Petrapole-Benapole Integrated Check Post.

### Why were Bandhan Express and Maitree Express passenger services suspended?

Cross-border passenger train services were halted due to political unrest, curfew conditions, and security directives along the international rail link in Bangladesh. Operations remain on hold pending formal intergovernmental clearance and reciprocal protocol finalization.

### When will Bandhan Express train services resume between India and Bangladesh?

A formal resumption date has not been officially released by the Ministry of Railways or Bangladesh Railway. While high-level bilateral coordination meetings have reviewed track and security protocols, ticket counters will only resume bookings after an official joint government notification.

### Can I book Bandhan Express tickets online through the IRCTC website or app?

No, Bandhan Express tickets cannot be purchased through the IRCTC website, Rail Connect app, or any third-party travel portal. Bilateral regulations mandate in-person purchasing at designated railway PRS counters like Fairlie Place in Kolkata or Khulna Station with original physical passports and approved visas.

### Which railway station in Kolkata does the Bandhan Express depart from?

The Bandhan Express operates exclusively from Kolkata Terminal (station code `KOAA`), locally known as Chitpur Station. It does not operate from Howrah Junction (`HWH`) or Sealdah (`SDAH`).

### What are the operating days and departure timings for Bandhan Express?

When active, the train runs twice weekly on Thursdays and Sundays. Train 13129 departs Kolkata Terminal at 07:10 AM IST and arrives in Khulna at 12:30 PM BST (12:00 PM IST). Return Train 13130 leaves Khulna at 01:30 PM BST and reaches Kolkata at 06:10 PM IST.

### How do I travel between Kolkata and Khulna while the train is suspended?

Travelers can easily reach Khulna by road via the Petrapole-Benapole Integrated Check Post (ICP). Take an EMU local train from Sealdah to Bongaon (₹20), an auto to the border (₹30), complete immigration on foot, and board a connecting bus from Benapole to Khulna (150–250 BDT).

### What documents are mandatory to purchase Bandhan Express tickets?

Every passenger must present an original valid passport with at least six months validity, an approved destination visa (Bangladesh visa for Indian citizens or Indian visa for Bangladeshi citizens), and clear photocopies of both documents at the booking counter.

### What is the baggage allowance and customs limit on Bandhan Express?

Adult passengers across both 1AC and CC classes receive a free baggage allowance of 35 kilograms, while children on child tickets receive 20 kilograms. Passengers can carry up to ₹25,000 INR in Indian currency, and commercial merchandise is strictly prohibited.

### Do I need to pay travel tax when leaving Bangladesh on Train 13130?

Yes, all passengers exiting Bangladesh by rail or road must pay the statutory Bangladesh government travel tax of 500 BDT, which can be cleared at Sonali Bank booths at Khulna station or the Benapole border terminal.
