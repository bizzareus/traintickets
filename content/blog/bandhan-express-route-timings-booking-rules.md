## TL;DR

The Bandhan Express operates bi-weekly on **Thursdays and Sundays**, connecting Kolkata Station (KOAA) in India with Khulna (KLNB) in Bangladesh over a **172-kilometer journey lasting 4.5 to 5.5 hours**. Train 13129 departs Kolkata at **07:10 AM IST**, while Train 13130 leaves Khulna at **13:30 BST (01:00 PM IST)**. International tickets **cannot be booked online via IRCTC**; they require in-person booking at designated railway PRS counters such as Fairlie Place in Kolkata or Khulna Station, requiring a **valid passport with 6 months validity and an active visa**. Air-conditioned First Class (1AC) fares average around **₹1,600 to ₹2,000 INR**, while AC Chair Car (CC) costs roughly **₹1,000 to ₹1,300 INR**.

## What Is Bandhan Express and Which Route Does It Run?

**The Bandhan Express is a premier international passenger train service operated jointly by Indian Railways and Bangladesh Railway, connecting Kolkata Terminal (KOAA) in West Bengal with Khulna Junction (KLNB) in southwestern Bangladesh. Covering 172 kilometers across the Petrapole-Benapole border, it provides direct, air-conditioned passenger travel between the two nations.**

Inaugurated in November 2017, the service revived a historic rail link between West Bengal and the Khulna division that had been closed since the 1965 India-Pakistan war. The train runs via Dum Dum, Bangaon, the Petrapole border checkpost on the Indian side, enters Bangladesh through Benapole, and continues onward through Jhikargachha and Jessore before terminating at Khulna.

Unlike standard domestic trains, the Bandhan Express runs as an international diplomatic corridor. Passenger manifests are screened by border security forces and customs officials before departure, ensuring smooth transit without lengthy road border queues at the Haridaspur land port.

## What Are the Timings and Schedule for Trains 13129 and 13130?

**Train 13129 departs Kolkata Terminal at 07:10 AM IST every Thursday and Sunday, arriving at Khulna at 12:30 PM BST (12:00 PM IST) after a 5-hour-20-minute run. Train 13130 departs Khulna at 13:30 BST (01:00 PM IST) on the same days, arriving at Kolkata at 18:10 PM IST.**

Because Bangladesh Standard Time (BST) is **30 minutes ahead of Indian Standard Time (IST)**, travelers must pay close attention to local station clocks when scheduling connecting transport.

## How Do You Book Bandhan Express Tickets at Fairlie Place?

**Bandhan Express tickets cannot be booked online through IRCTC or mobile ticketing apps. Passengers must purchase physical tickets in person at designated international Passenger Reservation System (PRS) counters, primarily at the Eastern Railway Foreign Passenger Booking Office at Fairlie Place (Dalhousie) or Kolkata Station in Kolkata, and Khulna Station in Bangladesh.**

The bilateral security agreement between India and Bangladesh requires physical inspection of travel credentials before ticket issuance. Each issued ticket is personalized with the passenger's passport number, visa number, and nationality.

To book your ticket in Kolkata:

1. **Visit Fairlie Place PRS Counter:** Head to Eastern Railway Headquarters, 6 Fairlie Place, BBD Bagh (Dalhousie), Kolkata. International reservation counters operate from **10:00 AM to 05:00 PM** on weekdays and Saturdays.
2. **Alternative Counter at Kolkata Station:** You can also purchase tickets at the International Passenger Reservation counter located on Platform 1 of Kolkata Chitpur Terminal (`KOAA`).
3. **Carry Mandatory Documents:** Bring your original passport along with clear photocopies of the passport information page and valid Bangladesh visa.
4. **Fill the International Requisition Form:** Complete the specialized international PRS booking slip detailing your passport number, visa number, and address in Bangladesh.
5. **Payment Mode:** Counters accept Indian Rupees (cash, UPI, and major debit or credit cards).

In Bangladesh, tickets can be purchased at the international counters of **Khulna Railway Station** or **Kamalapur Railway Station in Dhaka**, payable in Bangladeshi Taka (BDT).

## What Are the Passport, Visa, and Document Requirements for Travel?

**Travel on the Bandhan Express requires a valid passport with at least six months of remaining validity, an approved entry visa for the destination country, and clear photocopies of both documents for counter registration. Without a physical passport and valid visa, railway booking counters will refuse to issue tickets.**

Both Indian citizens traveling to Bangladesh and Bangladeshi citizens traveling to India must comply with these strict cross-border immigration requirements:

* **Valid Passport:** Your passport must have at least two blank pages for immigration entry and exit stamps, with a minimum validity of 6 months from the date of travel.
* **Destination Visa:** Indian travelers must obtain an approved Bangladesh visa (tourist, business, medical, or student visa) from Bangladesh diplomatic missions in Kolkata, New Delhi, or Mumbai before booking.
* **Return Visa Check:** Bangladeshi travelers must possess an active Indian visa with sufficient validity.
* **Advance Reservation Period (ARP):** Tickets open up to **30 days in advance** of the journey date at designated railway counters for regular passengers, and up to **365 days in advance** for foreign tourists under official Ministry of Railways guidelines.
* **Third-Country Foreign Nationals:** Non-Indian and non-Bangladeshi travelers holding valid visas for both nations are permitted to travel on Bandhan Express, provided their visa allows travel via rail and the Petrapole-Benapole international land border.

Ensure you carry original physical passports on travel day. Digital soft copies or mobile screenshots are strictly rejected at immigration checkpoints.

## What Are the Fares for AC First Class (1A) and AC Chair Car (CC)?

**Bandhan Express fares are structured around international passenger tariffs benchmarked against United States Dollar equivalents. AC First Class (1A) costs approximately ₹1,600 to ₹2,000 INR (or 2,000 to 2,500 BDT) including taxes, while AC Chair Car (CC) costs roughly ₹1,000 to ₹1,300 INR (or 1,400 to 1,600 BDT).**

Fares cover base rail fare, air-conditioned service charges, reservation fees, and statutory travel surcharges. Unlike domestic trains, Dynamic Pricing (Flexi-Fare) does not apply to the Bandhan Express.

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AC First Class (1AC)** | Private 2-berth coupes and 4-berth lockable cabins | ₹1,650 – ₹1,950 | 2,100 – 2,500 BDT | Clean linen, spacious legroom, priority customs |

*Note on Bangladesh Travel Tax:* Passengers departing Bangladesh are required by the National Board of Revenue (NBR) to pay a statutory **Bangladesh Travel Tax of 500 BDT**. This can be paid at Sonali Bank counters at Khulna station, Benapole border, or online prior to departure.

## How Do Customs and Immigration Work at Kolkata Station and Benapole?

**Customs and immigration checks for outbound passengers from India take place entirely inside the dedicated International Passenger Terminal at Kolkata Station (KOAA) prior to boarding. Inbound checks to Bangladesh are finalized upon arrival at Benapole International Passenger Terminal before the train continues to Khulna.**

This streamlined dual-terminal procedure eliminates the cumbersome practice of passengers having to disembark with heavy baggage along isolated railway tracks:

1. **Step 1: Kolkata Station Security (05:15 AM – 06:15 AM):** Arriving passengers enter the dedicated international departure concourse. Luggage is scanned through registered customs X-ray scanners.
2. **Step 2: Indian Immigration Departure Stamp:** Bureau of Immigration officers verify your passport, inspect the Bangladesh visa, capture biometric data if needed, and stamp the official Indian departure exit seal.
3. **Step 3: Boarding the Sealed Rake (06:30 AM):** Once cleared, passengers board the train directly from the sterile platform. The train doors are locked for transit to the border.
4. **Step 4: Petrapole Operational Clearance:** The train halts briefly at Petrapole on the Indian border for mechanical and track clearances, without passengers disembarking.
5. **Step 5: Benapole Border Clearance (Bangladesh):** At Benapole station, Bangladesh Immigration and Customs officers complete passenger entry manifests and entry stamping.
6. **Step 6: Arrival at Khulna:** After customs clearance, the train proceeds non-stop through Jessore into Khulna, where passengers exit freely.

For the return journey on Train 13130, departure immigration is conducted at Benapole and final Indian entry immigration is completed at Kolkata Station.

## What Are the Baggage Rules and Prohibited Items on Bandhan Express?

**Adult passengers traveling on Bandhan Express across all classes (both 1AC and CC) are permitted up to 35 kilograms of free personal luggage, while children traveling on child tickets are entitled to 20 kilograms (in up to two pieces). Excess luggage beyond these thresholds is charged at standard parcel excess baggage rates at the station booking counter.**

Because the train traverses an international frontier, customs authorities enforce strict cross-border baggage restrictions:

* **Free Allowance for Children:** Children traveling on child tickets (ages 5 to 11) are entitled to a 20 kg free luggage allowance across all classes.
* **Currency Regulations:** Indian travelers can carry up to ₹25,000 INR in cash or foreign currency notes within Reserve Bank of India (RBI) customs limits. Bangladeshi passengers must adhere to Bangladesh Bank foreign currency declaration thresholds.
* **Prohibited Items:** Narcotics, firearms, ammunition, commercial quantities of gold bullion, unprocessed agricultural seeds, and duty-exceeding tobacco or alcohol are strictly prohibited.
* **Commercial Goods Restriction:** Bandhan Express is exclusively a passenger service. Carrying commercial consignments or wholesale trade goods in passenger luggage is treated as smuggling and results in seizure under the Customs Act.

## What Are the Alternative Ways to Travel Between Kolkata and Khulna?

**If Bandhan Express is fully booked or not operating on your intended travel day, the fastest alternative is traveling by road via the Haridaspur-Petrapole Integrated Check Post (ICP), or taking the Maitree Express to Dhaka and transferring to Khulna by domestic rail.**

Depending on your travel urgency and budget, consider these three dependable alternatives:

1. **Road Crossing via Haridaspur / Petrapole Border:**
   * Take a local suburban EMU train from Sealdah (`SDAH`) to Bongaon Junction (`BNJ`) for ₹20 (roughly 2 hours).
   * Hire an auto-rickshaw or e-rickshaw from Bongaon to the Petrapole border checkpost (6 km, ₹30–₹50).
   * Complete immigration on foot through the integrated ICP terminal into Benapole.
   * From Benapole bus stand, take a direct air-conditioned or non-AC bus to Jessore or Khulna (roughly 1.5 to 2 hours, 150–250 BDT).
2. **Direct International Bus Services:**
   * State-run and private international bus services (such as Shyamoli NR, Green Line, and Shohagh Paribahan) operate daily luxury Volvo coach services between Karunamoyee (Salt Lake, Kolkata) and Khulna/Dhaka.
3. **Maitree Express via Dhaka (Train 13107/13108):**
   * Operates five days a week between Kolkata Station and Dhaka Cantonment. From Dhaka, frequent domestic intercity trains connect directly to Khulna.

> **Operational Status Advisory:** International cross-border train schedules between India and Bangladesh are periodically adjusted due to bilateral operational reviews, festive timetable shifts, or maintenance track works. Always verify active service dates at Fairlie Place booking office or official Eastern Railway bulletins before finalizing travel.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### Can I book Bandhan Express tickets online through the IRCTC app?
No, Bandhan Express tickets cannot be booked through the IRCTC app, IRCTC website, or private ticketing apps. Tickets must be purchased in person at designated international railway PRS counters like Fairlie Place or Kolkata Chitpur Terminal with original passports and valid visas.

### Which railway station in Kolkata does the Bandhan Express depart from?
The Bandhan Express departs exclusively from Kolkata Terminal (station code `KOAA`), also known locally as Chitpur Station. It does not depart from Howrah Junction (`HWH`) or Sealdah (`SDAH`).

### What days does the Bandhan Express run each week?
The Bandhan Express runs twice a week on Thursdays and Sundays in both directions (13129 from Kolkata to Khulna in the morning, and 13130 from Khulna to Kolkata in the afternoon).

### How far in advance can I book tickets for Bandhan Express?
The Advance Reservation Period (ARP) for Bandhan Express is up to 30 days prior to the journey date for regular passengers at railway counters, and up to 365 days in advance for foreign tourists under Ministry of Railways guidelines.

### Can foreign tourists of other nationalities travel on Bandhan Express?
Yes, foreign nationals holding valid passports and active visas for both India and Bangladesh are permitted to book and travel on Bandhan Express via the Petrapole-Benapole border.

### Is food served onboard the Bandhan Express?
The train does not include mandatory catering charges in the ticket fare. Passengers can purchase bottled Rail Neer drinking water, packaged snacks, tea, and light refreshments from onboard pantry staff.

### What is the travel distance and running time of Bandhan Express?
The train covers 172 kilometers between Kolkata and Khulna in approximately 4 hours 40 minutes to 5 hours 20 minutes, including border operational halts and customs checks.

### Do I need to pay travel tax when leaving Bangladesh on Train 13130?
Yes, all passengers departing Bangladesh by rail must pay the statutory Bangladesh government travel tax of 500 BDT, payable at Sonali Bank booths at Khulna station or Benapole.

### Can I travel on Bandhan Express with a waitlisted or Tatkal ticket?
No, Bandhan Express does not operate Tatkal, Premium Tatkal, or RAC quotas. Only confirmed passengers holding valid international travel manifests are allowed to board.

### What happens if I miss the 2-hour reporting cutoff at Kolkata Station?
If you arrive later than the required 2-hour pre-departure cutoff, customs and immigration desks may be closed, and security officials will deny access to the sterile platform even with a confirmed ticket.
