## TL;DR

Passengers traveling on Indian Railways with reserved tickets must carry at least one of ten officially approved photo identity proofs in original physical form or accessed live inside government platforms like **DigiLocker** or **mAadhaar**. Static screenshots, photocopies, or downloaded gallery images are strictly invalid and result in being marked ticketless under **Section 138 of the Railways Act**, incurring a flat **₹500 fine** plus single journey fares. For group bookings on a single PNR, at least one passenger must produce original ID, while travellers using concession or special quotas must produce matching original eligibility certificates.

## What Are the 10 Official Acceptable ID Proofs for Train Travel?

**To travel on reserved Indian Railways coaches, passengers must carry one of ten officially authorized identity documents in original physical form: Aadhaar card, Voter ID, Passport, Driving License, PAN card, nationalized bank passbook with photo, student ID, government-issued photo card, photo credit card, or municipal local body identity card.**

The Travelling Ticket Examiner (TTE) verifies this identity to prevent commercial ticket black-marketing, impersonation, and unauthorized transfers. The document must be original, legible, and currently valid. Expired passports or illegible, torn paper cards are rejected during physical verification.

The master reference table below details all 10 acceptable documents, their issuing authorities, and verification conditions:

| # | Prescribed Identity Proof | Authorized Issuing Body | Mandatory Verification Standards |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Aadhaar Card** | UIDAI (Unique Identification Authority of India) | Original PVC card, printed e-Aadhaar letter with clear photo, or live in DigiLocker/mAadhaar |
| 2 | **Voter Identity Card (EPIC)** | Election Commission of India (ECI) | Original physical card or digital e-EPIC pulled live through DigiLocker |
| 3 | **Passport** | Ministry of External Affairs (Govt. of India / Foreign Govt.) | Original valid passport (compulsory for foreign tourists traveling on FTQ quota) |
| 4 | **Driving License** | Regional Transport Offices (State RTOs) | Original physical smart card or digital license retrieved live within DigiLocker or mParivahan |
| 5 | **PAN Card** | Income Tax Department | Original laminated card with photo and permanent account number, or digital PAN via DigiLocker |
| 6 | **Bank Passbook with Photo** | Nationalized, Scheduled Commercial, or Regional Rural Banks | Original laminated passbook bearing the account holder's photograph, stamped and cross-signed by the branch manager |
| 7 | **Student Photo Identity Card** | Recognized Schools, Colleges, or Universities | Original current photo ID card (mandatory for student concession travel, valid for general travel) |
| 8 | **Photo Credit Card** | Scheduled Commercial Banks | Laminated credit card featuring the cardholder's engraved photo and name |
| 9 | **Local Authority Photo ID** | Municipal Corporations, District Administrations, Zila Parishads | Official identity card bearing serial number, stamp, and authorized signature |
| 10 | **Government Service Photo ID** | Central Government, State Governments, or Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) | Official employee badge or service photo identity card |

Laminated photocopies, digital scans saved in messaging apps, and phone gallery screenshots are inadmissible. If a passenger presents a scanned copy, the TTE has statutory instructions to disallow travel on that reservation.

## Why Is Central Railway Conducting a Special Ticket-Checking Drive in October 2026?

**Central Railway is executing an intensive, month-long ticket checking drive from October 1 to October 31, 2026 across stations and express trains to curb revenue loss, verify passenger credentials, eliminate tout-booked tickets, and strictly validate concession eligibility documents and railway travel passes.**

During peak autumn festival rush (Navratri, Durga Puja, Dussehra, and Diwali preparations), touts and unauthorized agents frequently exploit special quotas and concession categories. Central Railway flying squads and ticket-checking personnel equipped with Handheld Terminals (HHT) are conducting coordinated on-train and platform inspections.

The October 2026 enforcement drive focuses on four critical areas:
- **Concession and Quota Authenticity:** Scrutinizing Divyangjan, Senior Citizen, and medical concession bookings to verify that the passenger traveling is the registered beneficiary.
- **Railway Travel Authority:** Inspecting Duty Passes, Privilege Ticket Orders (PTO), and employee E-Passes to confirm they were formally exchanged for reserved tickets before boarding.
- **Single PNR Group Validation:** Confirming that the passenger carrying the original photo ID is physically present in the coach.
- **Unreserved Incursions:** Deboarding unreserved and platform ticket holders attempting to crowd Sleeper (SL) and AC 3-Tier (3A) compartments.

Passengers found traveling on tickets where the quota beneficiary or primary ID holder is absent are immediately treated as ticketless under Section 138, losing all reservation claims.

## Is DigiLocker or mAadhaar Valid for Train Travel During TTE Inspection?

**Yes, digital identity documents accessed live inside the official DigiLocker app, mAadhaar app, or mParivahan app are 100% legally valid for Indian Railways travel, provided they are fetched live from the government repository and presented directly to the TTE.**

Under Rule 9A of the Information Technology (Preservation and Retention of Information by Intermediaries Providing Digital Locker Facilities) Rules, documents fetched live inside DigiLocker are treated at par with original physical certificates. Ticket Examiners are bound by Railway Board commercial circulars to accept them.

To present digital proof safely without service disruption:
1. **Open the Official App:** Launch DigiLocker, mAadhaar, or NextGen mParivahan on your smartphone.
2. **Navigate to Issued Documents:** Tap on the verified document (such as Aadhaar, Driving License, or PAN Verification Record).
3. **Display Live Screen with QR Code:** Allow the TTE to inspect the dynamic timestamp and digital signature QR code.

Downloaded PDF files stored in your phone's file manager, exported screenshots, WhatsApp forwards, or screen recordings are strictly prohibited. Because remote railway corridors frequently suffer from cellular network dead zones, download your documents into the app's secure offline cache before boarding the train.

## What Are the Mandatory ID Rules for Concessions, Senior Citizens and Quota Bookings?

**Passengers traveling under special quotas or concessional fares must carry the specific original proof of eligibility alongside a standard government photo ID: Senior Citizens must show date-of-birth proof, Divyangjan passengers must present their original UDID card or Railway Concession Certificate, and employees must carry their original Railway Pass.**

Failing to present the specialized qualifying certificate invalidates the concession. Even if the passenger carries a valid Aadhaar card, lacking the underlying concession document results in the TTE canceling the concession, recovering the fare difference, and imposing statutory Section 138 penalties.

The table below outlines the mandatory documents required for each reservation quota:

| Travel Quota / Concession Category | Prescribed Eligibility Document | Backup Photo ID Required | Failure to Produce Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Senior Citizen Quota / Lower Berth** | Original Birth Certificate, PAN, Passport, Voter ID, or Aadhaar verifying age (Men 60+, Women 58+) | Mandatory (Photo ID with DOB) | Charged fare difference to normal fare + ₹500 penalty; may forfeit lower berth to senior waiting passenger |
| **Divyangjan (Disability) Concession** | Original Unique Disability ID (UDID) Card or Railway Concession Certificate issued by CMO/DMO | Mandatory original Govt ID | Concession revoked; charged full ticket fare + ₹500 penalty; escort ticket invalidated if traveling |
| **Ladies Quota (LD)** | Valid government-issued photo ID establishing female identity | Mandatory original Govt ID | Male passenger seated in Ladies Quota coach/berth is deboarded and prosecuted under Section 162 |
| **Defence / Military Quota** | Original Armed Forces Identity Card and Movement Order / Warrant | Military Service Badge / ID | Ticket treated as unreserved; referred to Military Liaison Officer / TTE penalty |
| **Railway Pass / PTO (E-Pass)** | Original Railway Employee Identity Card + Valid exchanged reservation journey ticket | Employee Identity Card | Passenger treated as traveling without ticket; penalty levied under Railway Servants Discipline Rules |

For Divyangjan travellers, the digitized Unique Disability Identity Card (UDID) issued by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment is universally accepted without requiring physical railway concession photo cards.

## Do All Passengers on a Group or Family Ticket Need to Show ID?

**No, every passenger on a group or family booking does not need to show individual identification; Indian Railways rules state that at least one passenger listed on the reserved PNR ticket must produce a valid original photo identity proof during train verification.**

When booking family vacations or corporate travel, up to 6 passengers can be reserved on a standard General reservation ticket (and up to 4 passengers on a Tatkal booking). As long as one passenger named on that e-ticket or counter reservation slip carries an approved original document, the entire travel party is verified.

However, three critical caveats apply:
- **The ID Holder Must Be Traveling:** If Passenger 1 is the only person carrying an ID, but Passenger 1 misses the train or cancels their ticket, the remaining 5 passengers cannot validate their journey. The entire party will be treated as traveling without a valid ticket.
- **Children Below 5 Years:** Children traveling free without an assigned berth do not require photo identification, though carrying age verification is advised if age is disputed.
- **Children Aged 5 to 11:** Children booked on child tickets (half fare without berth or full fare with berth) are covered by the accompanying adult's photo identity proof.

If your travel party is boarding from different stations along the route, ensure that the very first group boarding at the originating station includes at least one passenger carrying an original ID matching the reservation slip.

## What Happens If You Travel in a Reserved Train Coach Without a Valid ID?

**If you fail to produce a valid original photo ID during ticket checking, you will be treated as traveling without a ticket under Section 138 of the Railways Act, charged the full journey fare from the originating station, and assessed a flat ₹500 fine under the Jan Vishwas Act 2026.**

Lacking an original ID legally voids your reservation. The TTE cannot verify that you are the legitimate ticket holder. Consequently, the TTE generates an electronic Excess Fare Ticket (EFT) directly through their digital Handheld Terminal (HHT).

The penalty structure operates under strict statutory parameters:
1. **Section 138 Excess Fare Penalty:** A flat penalty of **₹500** is levied per passenger under the Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Act 2026.
2. **Single Journey Fare Recovery:** The passenger must pay the full single journey fare for the class of travel from the train's starting point to the destination station.
3. **Forfeiture of Reserved Berth:** The TTE reallocates the berth to passengers on the RAC queue or waiting list via the Handheld Terminal algorithm. The passenger without ID may be directed to deboard at the next scheduled halt.
4. **Impersonation Penalties (Section 142):** Transferring or selling a reserved railway ticket to someone else is a cognizable offense punishable under Section 142 by imprisonment up to 3 months, a fine up to ₹500, or both.

Payment must be made immediately via cash or UPI to the TTE, who is required by law to issue an automated digital receipt generated by the Handheld Terminal. Never accept handwritten receipts without official serial numbers.

## What Are TTE Nighttime Inspection Hours and Passenger Protections?

**Travelling Ticket Examiners (TTEs) are legally prohibited from checking tickets, disturbing passengers, or demanding identity cards between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM, except for passengers boarding the train after 10:00 PM at intermediate stations.**

Indian Railways passenger charter rules establish sleeping hours between 22:00 and 06:00 hours to guarantee peaceful rest. During this 8-hour nighttime window:
- TTEs must complete all routine verification before 10:00 PM.
- Middle berths in Sleeper and AC 3-Tier coaches may remain unfolded from 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM.
- Passengers who boarded prior to 10:00 PM and were verified cannot be awakened for re-checking by changing ticket crews.

Essential legal protections for passengers during ticket inspections:
- **Mandatory HHT Digital Receipt:** TTEs cannot collect fines without generating an instant electronic receipt on their Handheld Terminal.
- **Two-Stop Rule for Absent Passengers:** If a passenger is not at their berth when the TTE visits, the TTE cannot mark them absent or reallocate their seat until the train passes **two scheduled halts or one hour**, whichever is longer.
- **Protection for Women Passengers:** Under Section 139 of the Railways Act, railway staff cannot deboard an unaccompanied woman traveling without a valid ticket or ID at night. She can only be handed over to female RPF personnel at a major divisional railway station during daylight hours.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### What are the 10 acceptable ID proofs in Indian Railways?
The ten acceptable ID proofs are Aadhaar card, Voter ID, Passport, Driving License, PAN card, nationalized bank passbook with photo, student photo ID, government-issued photo card, photo credit card, and municipal authority identity cards.

### Is a photo of an ID card on a mobile phone accepted by the TTE?
No, photos, photocopies, or downloaded gallery screenshots of identity cards are strictly rejected by Ticket Examiners. Passengers must present either the original physical card or live documents fetched inside DigiLocker, mAadhaar, or mParivahan.

### Can I show my Driving License in DigiLocker during ticket checking?
Yes, displaying your Driving License, Aadhaar, or PAN card live within the official DigiLocker app is legally recognized by Indian Railways and carries the same statutory validity as the physical document.

### Do all passengers on a family ticket need to carry an ID card?
No, Indian Railways rules require only one passenger listed on a multi-passenger PNR ticket to carry an original valid photo ID. However, that specific passenger must be physically present and traveling with the party.

### What is the penalty for traveling without an original ID card?
Traveling without an original ID card treats the passenger as ticketless under Section 138 of the Railways Act, requiring payment of the full single journey ticket fare plus a flat ₹500 penalty under the Jan Vishwas Act 2026.

### Why is Central Railway running a ticket-checking drive in October 2026?
Central Railway is running an intensive month-long ticket-checking drive from October 1 to October 31, 2026 to detect tout-booked tickets, verify concession and quota eligibility documents, inspect employee travel passes, and curb festive travel fraud.

### What documents are required for Senior Citizen and Lower Berth quotas?
Passengers traveling under Senior Citizen or Lower Berth (SS) quotas must carry an original government-issued photo ID verifying their date of birth (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, or Passport). Failing to prove age incurs full fare adjustment plus a ₹500 penalty.

### Can a TTE check tickets after 10:00 PM at night?
No, TTEs are prohibited from checking tickets between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM, except for passengers who boarded the train after 10:00 PM at intermediate stations.

### What happens if the passenger carrying the ID card cancels their ticket?
If the sole passenger carrying an original ID cancels their ticket or misses the train, the remaining passengers on the PNR cannot validate their booking. Unless another traveling passenger possesses an original ID and is named on the ticket, the group is treated as ticketless.

### What is the difference between GNWL and TQWL confirmation chances?
GNWL (General Waiting List) has the highest confirmation priority because it clears against cancellations across the entire general coach quota, whereas TQWL (Tatkal Quota Waiting List) clears only when another Tatkal passenger cancels, making confirmation extremely rare.
