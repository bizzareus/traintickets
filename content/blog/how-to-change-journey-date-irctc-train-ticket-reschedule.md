## Can You Reschedule or Change the Journey Date on an Online IRCTC e-Ticket?

**You cannot reschedule or change the journey date directly on the IRCTC website or mobile app for online e-tickets.** Railway IT systems do not offer an online date modification button. To travel on another date, you must either cancel your e-ticket online and book a fresh ticket, or present a printed copy at a railway PRS counter at least 48 hours before train departure.

The Centre for Railway Information Systems (CRIS) intentionally blocks online date changes to protect inventory integrity and prevent automated ticket scalping. When you book an e-ticket, your reservation links directly to banking settlement gateways, Passenger Reservation System (PRS) passenger quotas, and commercial audit records. 

If you hold an online e-ticket and your travel plans change, you have two legitimate courses of action:

1. **Cancel Online and Rebook Instantly:** If vacant berths exist on your preferred new date, cancel your existing e-ticket through your IRCTC account. Refunds credit directly to your original payment method (UPI, net banking, or debit card) within 2 to 4 business days, deducting standard cancellation charges based on how close you are to chart preparation.
2. **Offline Modification at PRS Counter:** Take a clear printout of your Electronic Reservation Slip (ERS), along with an original government-approved photo ID card (such as Aadhaar, Passport, or Voter ID), to the Chief Reservation Supervisor (CRS) counter at a major railway station at least 48 hours before the scheduled departure of your booked train. The supervisor can cancel the online seat and issue a modified counter journey slip for the new date, subject to berth availability.

Remember that once an e-ticket is modified at a railway reservation counter, it enters manual counter handling rules. Any subsequent cancellation or refund claim must be filed in person at a station counter rather than online.

## How Do Online e-Ticket and Counter Rescheduling Rules Compare?

Indian Railways maintains separate administrative workflows for tickets booked through digital channels versus physical station counters. The following matrix contrasts how rescheduling, cancellations, and passenger rights function across both ticket types:

| **Rescheduling Cutoff Deadline** | At least 48 hours before scheduled train departure | At least 48 hours before scheduled train departure |

Understanding this distinction ensures you do not waste valuable time searching for a non-existent reschedule button inside the IRCTC mobile app when departure deadlines are ticking down.

## How to Prepone or Postpone a Train Ticket at the Station Counter Step by Step

**To prepone or postpone a train ticket, visit any computerized railway Passenger Reservation System (PRS) counter at least 48 hours before scheduled train departure.** Submit your original ticket, a written application detailing your requested new date and train, and an official government photo identity card to the Chief Reservation Supervisor. If berths exist in your requested class, the supervisor issues an updated reservation slip.

Indian Railways permits passengers to advance (prepone) or defer (postpone) their journey date across all major reserved classes, including First AC (1A), Executive Class (EC), AC 2-Tier (2A), AC 3-Tier (3A), AC 3-Economy (3E), AC Chair Car (CC), Sleeper (SL), and Second Sitting (2S).

Follow these five practical steps to complete your counter rescheduling successfully:

1. **Locate the CRS Office:** Head to any major computerized railway reservation centre. Look for the Chief Reservation Supervisor (CRS) room or the Station Manager commercial window during regular working hours (typically 08:00 AM to 08:00 PM).
2. **Draft a Simple Application Letter:** On a clean sheet of paper, address a short letter to the Chief Reservation Supervisor. State your current PNR number, booked train number, original journey date, passenger names, and the requested new travel date, preferred train number, and class.
3. **Submit Physical Documents:** Hand over your original paper counter ticket (or printed e-ticket confirmation), your written application, and the original government photo ID of the primary passenger.
4. **Pay Clerkage and Fare Adjustments:** The booking clerk verifies seat availability on your requested train. If berths are available, you pay the nominal clerkage fee (~₹30 to ₹60 plus GST) and any positive fare difference if moving to a higher travel class or a premium train category.
5. **Collect Your Endorsed Reservation Slip:** The supervisor cancels your previous seat allocation in the central PRS database and hands you a freshly printed computer journey slip displaying your new journey date, train number, coach, and berth details.

Keep in mind that Indian Railways allows only one journey date modification per ticket. Once a ticket has been rescheduled at a counter, it cannot be modified a second time under any circumstance.

## What Are the Cancellation and Rescheduling Fees Under 2026 Railway Rules?

**Indian Railways charges no cancellation penalty when you reschedule a train ticket, deducting only nominal clerkage fees ranging from ₹30 to ₹60 plus GST.** You retain your base fare investment and pay only the difference if upgrading travel class or moving to a higher-fare train. However, Indian Railways does not refund the difference if the new train has a lower fare.

When your travel schedule shifts unexpectedly, rescheduling your counter ticket is vastly cheaper than cancelling and booking anew. Normal cancellation charges scale aggressively as the departure date approaches, deducting up to 50% of the entire ticket fare within 12 hours of departure. In contrast, counter date modification preserves your principal ticket payment.

The following table breaks down official fees and fare adjustment policies across all railway travel classes:

| **AC First Class (1A / Executive)** | ₹60 + 5% GST | Flat ₹240 + 5% GST | 25% of fare (min ₹240 + GST) | 50% of fare (min ₹240 + GST) |
| **AC 2-Tier (2A)** | ₹60 + 5% GST | Flat ₹200 + 5% GST | 25% of fare (min ₹200 + GST) | 50% of fare (min ₹200 + GST) |
| **AC 3-Tier / 3-Economy (3A / 3E / CC)** | ₹60 + 5% GST | Flat ₹180 + 5% GST | 25% of fare (min ₹180 + GST) | 50% of fare (min ₹180 + GST) |

Under commercial regulations, if you subsequently decide to cancel a rescheduled ticket, Indian Railways calculates cancellation deductions against the original booking baseline rather than treating it as a brand-new booking.

## Can You Change the Boarding Station Instead of Rescheduling the Entire Ticket?

**Yes, you can change your boarding station online on the IRCTC website or Rail Connect app up to second reservation chart preparation (~30 to 45 minutes before departure) without paying any fee.** Modifying your boarding point allows you to board from another scheduled halt on the booked route without losing your confirmed berth or incurring unauthorized travel penalties.

Many passengers mistakenly believe they need to cancel or reschedule their entire ticket when they only need to board at a different stop along the train route. For example, if you booked from New Delhi to Patna but need to board at Ghaziabad or Kanpur instead, you do not need to cancel your booking.

Follow these steps to change your boarding point online:

1. **Log into IRCTC:** Open the official IRCTC portal (`irctc.co.in/nget`) or the IRCTC Rail Connect mobile application.
2. **Navigate to Booked Ticket History:** Go to **My Account ➔ My Transactions ➔ Booked Ticket History** and select the active PNR you wish to modify.
3. **Select Change Boarding Point:** Click on the three vertical dots or menu options and select **Change Boarding Point**.
4. **Choose Your New Station:** The system displays a dropdown list of all scheduled intermediate halts between your origin and destination. Select your new boarding station and click **Submit**.
5. **Receive Confirmation SMS:** An instant confirmation message arrives on your registered mobile number confirming the updated boarding point.

Important conditions governing boarding point changes:

- **Free of Charge:** Indian Railways levies zero administrative fees for modifying your boarding point.
- **Single Modification:** You can change your boarding station only once per PNR.
- **Irreversible Action:** Once your boarding point changes, you lose all rights to occupy the berth from the original boarding station. If you board at the original station after changing your boarding point, the Travelling Ticket Examiner (TTE) can treat you as traveling without a valid ticket and re-allocate your berth.
- **Timing Deadline:** You must complete the boarding station change before the second reservation chart prepares (~30 to 45 minutes prior to train departure).

## How to Transfer a Confirmed Train Ticket to a Family Member Without Rescheduling

**You can transfer a confirmed train ticket to an immediate family member by submitting a written request to the Chief Reservation Supervisor at least 24 hours before train departure.** Under Railway Board commercial circulars, transfers are strictly permitted to a father, mother, brother, sister, spouse, son, or daughter upon presenting proof of blood relationship such as a ration card or Aadhaar card.

Ticket transfer provisions exist under Section 53 of the Railways Act to help families avoid cancellation penalties when the original passenger falls ill or faces an unexpected emergency.

Key rules for transferring a confirmed train ticket:

- **Eligible Family Members:** Transfers are permitted strictly within immediate blood relations (father, mother, brother, sister, spouse, son, daughter). Tickets cannot be transferred to cousins, friends, colleagues, or in-laws.
- **Government Employee Exception:** Government servants on official duty can request a ticket transfer to another government employee up to 24 hours prior to departure by producing a requisition letter from their department head.
- **Marriage and Group Exceptions:** Passengers traveling as part of a recognized marriage party or students traveling in an educational group can transfer reservations to other group members up to 48 hours before train departure with written certification from the party organizer or school principal.
- **One-Time Privilege:** A ticket can be transferred only once. The new passenger cannot transfer the ticket further or request subsequent date rescheduling.
- **Required Documentation:** Bring the original confirmed ticket (or printed e-ticket), a written transfer application, government photo identity proofs for both passengers, and valid proof of relationship (family ration card, birth certificate, marriage certificate, or matching Aadhaar cards showing shared parentage or address).

## Can You Reschedule a Tatkal or Premium Tatkal Train Ticket?

**No, you cannot reschedule, prepone, or postpone a Tatkal or Premium Tatkal train ticket under any circumstances.** Indian Railways commercial regulations treat Tatkal quotas as non-transferable, non-modifiable emergency allocations. If you cannot travel on the scheduled date, you must cancel the booking, which results in zero refund on confirmed Tatkal reservations.

The Tatkal scheme is engineered strictly for short-notice, point-to-point passenger movement. Because tickets release just 24 hours prior to origin station departure under tight quota caps, Indian Railways prohibits date alteration, class upgrades, and general date rescheduling on Tatkal tickets.

Key rules to keep in mind regarding Tatkal bookings:

- **Confirmed Tatkal:** 0% refund on voluntary cancellation, 0% date rescheduling eligibility.
- **Waitlisted Tatkal (TQWL):** If a Tatkal ticket remains waitlisted and you voluntarily cancel it before chart preparation, standard clerkage (~₹60 + GST) applies. If it remains waitlisted after final chart preparation, online Tatkal e-tickets auto-cancel with an automatic 100% refund.
- **Premium Tatkal (PT):** Premium Tatkal operates on dynamic pricing algorithms where fares surge up to 3 times the base fare. Like regular Tatkal, Premium Tatkal reservations allow strictly zero date rescheduling and zero refund upon voluntary cancellation.
- **Section 138 Penalties:** Under the Jan Vishwas Act 2026, boarding a reserved train coach with an auto-cancelled waitlisted e-ticket is treated as unauthorized travel, punishable by a flat ₹500 penalty plus the full single fare difference to the next stopping station.

If your travel date changed and you hold a confirmed Tatkal ticket, your best financial strategy is to keep the ticket if there is any chance you might still travel, or file a Ticket Deposit Receipt (TDR) if the train is delayed by more than 3 hours or annulled by the railway administration.

## What Are the Rules for Rescheduling Waitlisted (WL) and RAC Tickets?

**You can reschedule waitlisted (WL) and RAC tickets at any computerized station counter up to 48 hours before scheduled train departure.** Rescheduling transfers your booking into the new train or date queue subject to open quota availability. If the new date has vacant berths, your waitlisted ticket can confirm immediately without paying normal cancellation penalties.

Understanding waitlist hierarchy helps you make calculated rescheduling decisions:

1. **Waiting List (WL):** You hold a booked reservation queue position but have no assigned coach or berth number. When you reschedule a waitlisted counter ticket to a new date, your position enters the respective quota queue of the new train (e.g., GNWL, RLWL, or PQWL).
2. **Reservation Against Cancellation (RAC):** You hold guaranteed boarding clearance and are assigned half of a side lower berth. Rescheduling an RAC ticket to an earlier or later date transfers your booking into the new train's availability pool.
3. **Confirmed (CNF):** You hold a dedicated coach and individual berth allocation.

If you hold an online e-ticket in waitlisted status and want to travel on a different date, you do not need to visit a counter. Simply cancel the waitlisted e-ticket online on the IRCTC portal. You will only be deducted standard clerkage (~₹60 + GST) rather than heavy cancellation fees, freeing your funds to book fresh confirmed seats immediately.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### Can I change my IRCTC train journey date online without visiting a railway station?

No, the IRCTC website and mobile app do not support an online date change feature for e-tickets. To change your journey date online, you must cancel your existing e-ticket and book a new ticket for your preferred travel date. Alternatively, you can visit a physical computerized PRS railway counter at least 48 hours prior to train departure with a printed e-ticket copy and valid government photo identity card to request a manual modification.

### What is the exact time cutoff to reschedule a train ticket at the railway counter?

You must submit your written rescheduling application at a computerized railway PRS counter at least 48 hours prior to the scheduled departure time of your booked train. Railway commercial rules strictly prohibit date changes within 48 hours of departure, leaving cancellation and rebooking as your only alternative.

### How much clerkage fee does Indian Railways charge for preponing a train ticket?

Indian Railways charges a nominal clerkage fee ranging from ₹30 for Second Sitting (2S) to ₹60 plus 5% GST for AC classes (1A, 2A, 3A, 3E, CC) when you prepone or postpone a ticket at a station counter. In addition to clerkage, you must pay any fare difference if your new train has higher fares or if you upgrade to a higher travel class.

### Does Indian Railways refund the fare difference if the new train ticket is cheaper?

No, Indian Railways commercial rules explicitly state that if a rescheduled ticket has a lower fare than your original booking, the railway administration does not refund the fare difference. Passengers forfeit the balance amount upon completing the date change endorsement.

### Can I change my boarding station online if I cannot reschedule my train ticket date?

Yes, you can change your boarding station online on the IRCTC website or Rail Connect app free of charge up to second reservation chart preparation (~30 to 45 minutes before departure). Modifying your boarding point allows you to board from any other scheduled commercial halt on your route without losing your confirmed seat.

### Can I transfer my confirmed train ticket to a friend or colleague?

No, Indian Railways does not permit ticket transfers to friends, colleagues, or extended relatives. Under Section 53 of the Railways Act, confirmed reservations can only be transferred to immediate family members (father, mother, brother, sister, spouse, son, or daughter) upon submitting proof of relationship to the Chief Reservation Supervisor at least 24 hours before departure.

### Can someone else visit the railway station counter to reschedule my ticket for me?

Yes, a family member or representative can submit the rescheduling application at the PRS counter on your behalf. They must carry the original ticket, a signed authorization letter from the passenger named on the ticket, and copies of government photo IDs for both the passenger and the visiting representative.

### What happens to a waitlisted online e-ticket if I do not reschedule or cancel it?

If an online e-ticket remains fully waitlisted after the final reservation chart prepares, CRIS automatically cancels the PNR and refunds the full ticket amount to your bank account with zero cancellation charges. You are legally prohibited from boarding reserved coaches on a dropped waitlisted e-ticket.

### Can I postpone a train ticket to a date beyond the 60-day advance booking window?

No, you can only reschedule your journey to a date where ticket reservations are actively open. Because Indian Railways enforces a 60-day Advance Reservation Period (ARP), your new desired journey date must fall within the open 60-day calendar window on the day you submit your counter request.

### Can I reschedule a confirmed Tatkal ticket to a different train or class?

No, Tatkal and Premium Tatkal tickets are strictly non-reschedulable and non-transferable under all circumstances. If you cannot travel on your booked Tatkal date, you must cancel the reservation, which results in zero refund on confirmed Tatkal tickets.
