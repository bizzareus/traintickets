## TL;DR

You can prepone or postpone a confirmed, RAC, or waitlisted counter ticket at any computerized PRS railway station counter at least 48 hours before train departure without paying cancellation penalties. Online IRCTC e-tickets cannot be rescheduled directly through the website or app, meaning you must cancel and rebook or visit a counter with a physical printout. Tatkal tickets cannot be rescheduled under any circumstance.

---

## Can You Change the Journey Date on a Confirmed Train Ticket?

**Yes, you can change the journey date on a confirmed train ticket** by preponing or postponing your travel to another date for the same destination. Under Indian Railways commercial rules, you must submit a written request at a computerized **PRS reservation counter at least 48 hours before scheduled departure**, subject to berth availability.

Indian Railways permits date modification on confirmed, RAC, and waitlisted tickets across all regular reserved classes (1A, 2A, 3A, 3E, CC, EC, SL, 2S). This flexibility applies to passenger convenience when plans shift unexpectedly.

However, several strict conditions apply under Railway Board guidelines:

- **Single modification only**: You can reschedule your journey date only once per ticket.
- **Station origin and destination**: The originating and terminating stations must remain the same or fall along the identical route within the same fare zone.
- **Advance Reservation Period (ARP)**: The new travel date must fall within the official 60-day ARP booking window.
- **Availability dependent**: Confirmation on the new date is strictly subject to available vacant berths in the respective quota.

## How to Prepone or Postpone Your IRCTC Train Ticket Date Step by Step

To prepone or postpone your train journey, visit the nearest **Chief Reservation Supervisor (CRS)** counter during working hours with your physical ticket and original ID. Submit a written application specifying your **new travel date, train number, and class** at least **48 hours prior to scheduled departure** to receive a modified reservation slip.

The complete offline rescheduling workflow follows these precise steps:

1. **Locate the CRS counter**: Head to any major computerized Passenger Reservation System (PRS) centre at a nearby railway station. Look for the Chief Reservation Supervisor / Station Manager window.
2. **Draft a simple application**: Write a brief letter detailing your current PNR number, train number, date of travel, passenger names, and the requested new train number, class, and journey date.
3. **Submit physical documentation**: Hand over the original counter ticket (or printed e-ticket copy), the application letter, and government-approved photo identity proof (such as Aadhaar, Voter ID, or Passport).
4. **Pay fare differences or clerkage**: The PRS clerk checks seat availability on your requested train. If seats are available, you pay nominal clerkage fees plus any fare difference if upgrading class.
5. **Collect updated journey slip**: The reservation clerk cancels the old booking record and issues a fresh computer-generated endorsement slip showing your new coach, berth, and journey date.

## What Are the Fees and Fare Differences for Rescheduling Train Tickets?

Indian Railways charges **no cancellation penalty for date changes**, but passengers must pay applicable **clerkage charges and fare differences** at the counter. If the new train or upgraded class costs more, you pay the extra balance. However, if the new ticket fare is lower, Indian Railways **does not refund the fare difference**.

When you reschedule a ticket rather than cancelling it, you avoid the heavy cancellation deductions that apply close to chart preparation. Standard cancellation charges range from ₹60 to ₹240 plus GST per passenger, whereas a date change retains your base ticket value.

| Ticket Class | Normal Cancellation Fee (Within 48h to 12h) | Counter Rescheduling Fee (Clerkage) | Fare Adjustment Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AC First Class (1A / Executive)** | 25% of fare (min ₹240 + GST) | Nominal clerkage (~₹60 + GST) | Pay difference if new train fare is higher |
| **AC 2 Tier (2A)** | 25% of fare (min ₹200 + GST) | Nominal clerkage (~₹60 + GST) | Pay difference if new train fare is higher |
| **AC 3 Tier / 3 Economy (3A / 3E / CC)** | 25% of fare (min ₹180 + GST) | Nominal clerkage (~₹60 + GST) | Pay difference if new train fare is higher |
| **Sleeper Class (SL)** | 25% of fare (min ₹120) | Nominal clerkage (~₹30–₹60) | Pay difference if new train fare is higher |
| **Second Sitting (2S)** | 25% of fare (min ₹60) | Nominal clerkage (~₹30) | Pay difference if new train fare is higher |

If your rescheduled ticket is subsequently cancelled later, Indian Railways calculates cancellation deductions using the original booking date and fare baseline.

## Can You Change the Date of an IRCTC Online E-Ticket?

**You cannot change the journey date directly on the IRCTC website or app** for online e-tickets. Instead, passengers must either **cancel the existing e-ticket online and book a fresh ticket** for the new date, or take an e-ticket printout to a computerized **PRS counter 48 hours before departure** for manual offline modification.

Most digital passengers find it faster to cancel online and make a fresh booking, provided seats exist on the target train. Online cancellation is instantaneous, and refunds process back to the source payment method within 2 to 4 business days.

| Feature | Online IRCTC E-Ticket | Physical PRS Counter Ticket |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Online date change button** | Not available on IRCTC website/app | Not applicable |
| **Counter date change permitted** | Yes (with printed ticket + original ID, 48h prior) | Yes (with original ticket, 48h prior) |
| **Cancellation mechanism** | 100% online via IRCTC portal / app | Physical surrender at PRS ticket counter |
| **Refund speed** | Direct back to bank / card / UPI in 2–4 days | Instant cash or digital refund at counter |
| **Chart waitlist status** | Auto-cancelled if WL remains after charting | Permitted to travel in general/unreserved |

If you decide to modify an e-ticket at a physical railway counter, the CRS issues a counter journey slip. Remember that once an e-ticket is modified at a counter, it converts to counter handling rules for any future cancellation.

## Can You Reschedule a Tatkal or Premium Tatkal Ticket?

**No, you cannot reschedule or change the journey date on Tatkal or Premium Tatkal tickets** under Indian Railways commercial regulations. Tatkal quotas are strictly non-transferable and non-reschedulable. If you cannot travel on the booked date, you must cancel the booking, which yields **zero refund on confirmed Tatkal tickets**.

Tatkal tickets are intended exclusively for urgent, point-to-point travel. Because dynamic demand and special quotas govern Tatkal allocations, Indian Railways does not permit date alteration, boarding point changes to unreserved zones, or class transfers on these reservations.

Key rules to keep in mind regarding Tatkal bookings:

- **Confirmed Tatkal**: 0% refund on cancellation, 0% date rescheduling eligibility.
- **Waitlisted Tatkal**: Standard clerkage deduction applies if cancelled before chart preparation. If the waitlist remains unconfirmed after chart preparation, online Tatkal e-tickets are auto-cancelled with automatic refund.
- **Premium Tatkal (PT)**: Governed by dynamic pricing; strictly zero date modification and zero cancellation refund.

## What Are the Rules for Changing Travel Date on Waitlisted or RAC Tickets?

**You can reschedule waitlisted (WL) and RAC tickets at any PRS counter** up to 48 hours before scheduled train departure. Rescheduling transfers your booking to the new train or date subject to existing availability in the progression queue, moving through **WL (Waiting List) → RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) → Confirmed** status.

WL full form is Waiting List. When you reschedule a waitlisted ticket to a new date, you enter the general quota waitlist of the new train at the current position.

Understanding the status hierarchy helps you make smart choices:

1. **Waiting List (WL)**: You hold a booking number but no allocated coach or berth. An unconfirmed online e-ticket cannot be used to board reserved coaches.
2. **Reservation Against Cancellation (RAC)**: You are guaranteed boarding on the train and receive half of a shared lower side berth (Side Lower).
3. **Confirmed (CNF)**: A dedicated coach and individual berth number are assigned to you.

If your rescheduled counter ticket remains in RAC or waitlist status at the time of chart preparation, you can still board unreserved compartments or approach the Travelling Ticket Examiner (TTE) for vacant seats.

When your desired new date shows heavy waitlists, avoid taking high-risk waiting tickets that face auto-cancellation. Instead, search for split-ticket options using **[Smart Seats](/)** to secure confirmed journey legs, check seat vacancies via **[Coach Journey Lookup](/seat-status)**, or book confirmed **Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`)** berths ~8 hours before departure after initial chart preparation.

When direct booking says `REGRET` or high `WL/45`, standard booking engines leave you stranded. Use these reliable recovery strategies:

- **Split-journey confirmation**: Use **[Smart Seats](/)** to identify contiguous seat vacancies on the exact same train. For example, sit in coach B2 from New Delhi to Kanpur, and shift to coach B5 from Kanpur to Prayagraj without missing a beat.
- **Vacant berth lookup**: Use **[Coach Journey Lookup](/seat-status)** to see station-to-station berth occupancy maps and pinpoint which berths empty out mid-route.
- **Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`)**: A "current available ticket" (status `CURR_AVBL` or `CURR AVL`) is a **100% fully confirmed seat** with an assigned coach and berth. It opens on IRCTC and PRS counters after the first reservation chart prepares (~8 hours prior to departure) and remains bookable until ~30 minutes before the train leaves.
- **Vikalp scheme**: When booking fresh tickets online, select the IRCTC Vikalp (Alternate Train Accommodation Scheme) option to be considered for vacant berths on alternate trains running along the same route.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### Can I change my IRCTC train journey date online without visiting a counter?
No, the IRCTC website and mobile app currently do not offer an online date modification feature for e-tickets. To change your travel date online, you must cancel your current ticket and purchase a fresh ticket for your intended date. Alternatively, you can visit a physical PRS railway counter at least 48 hours prior to departure with a printed e-ticket and valid ID.

### What is the time limit for changing the journey date on a confirmed train ticket?
You must submit your date change request at a computerized railway PRS counter at least 48 hours prior to the scheduled departure time of the train in which your ticket is originally booked. Requests submitted within 48 hours of departure are rejected under standard Indian Railways commercial rules.

### How many times can you change the journey date on an Indian Railways ticket?
Indian Railways permits only one journey date change per ticket. Once a ticket has been preponed or postponed at a PRS counter, no further date or train adjustments are allowed on that reservation record.

### Can I change my train ticket date if the new train has a higher fare?
Yes, you can reschedule your ticket to a train with higher fares (such as switching from an Express train to a Rajdhani, Shatabdi, or Vande Bharat, or upgrading class). You must pay the difference in base fare, reservation fees, and superfast surcharges at the counter during the modification process.

### Can someone else submit the date change application at the railway counter on my behalf?
Yes, a family member or representative can submit the request on your behalf. They must carry the original ticket, a signed written authorization letter from the passenger, and copies of government-issued photo ID proofs for both the passenger and the representative.

### What happens if I want to advance my journey date to a Tatkal ticket?
You cannot directly convert or reschedule a general quota ticket into a Tatkal quota ticket. If you need urgent travel under Tatkal, you must purchase a separate Tatkal ticket when the booking window opens (10:00 AM for AC classes, 11:00 AM for Sleeper) and separately cancel your original general ticket.

### Can I change both the journey date and the boarding station at the same time?
Yes, you can request a change of boarding point along with your journey date change when submitting your written application to the Chief Reservation Supervisor at the PRS counter. The new boarding station must be an official scheduled halt for the new train.

### Does Indian Railways refund the difference if the new train ticket is cheaper?
No, if you reschedule your journey to a train or class with a lower fare, Indian Railways retains the difference and does not issue a refund for the excess amount paid.

### What happens to a waitlisted e-ticket if I do not reschedule or cancel it?
If you booked an online e-ticket and it remains fully waitlisted (all passengers on the PNR are in WL status) after the final chart is prepared, IRCTC automatically cancels the ticket and processes a full refund (with no cancellation or clerkage fee deducted) directly to your bank account. You are not permitted to board reserved coaches with a fully waitlisted e-ticket.

### Can I postpone a confirmed train ticket to a date beyond the 60-day Advance Reservation Period?
No, you can only postpone your ticket to a date where reservations are actively open. Indian Railways operates a 60-day Advance Reservation Period (ARP), so your requested new journey date must fall within this open window at the time of your application.
