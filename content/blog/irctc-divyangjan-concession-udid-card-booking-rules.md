## TL;DR

**Indian Railways provides a 25% to 75% fare concession for Divyangjan (differently-abled) passengers and their eligible escorts across Sleeper, 3AC, 3E, 2AC, First Class, and Chair Car.** Online e-ticketing on IRCTC requires a one-time verified Railway Divyangjan Photo Identity Card issued by the Divisional Railway Manager (DRM). Under 2026 guidelines, Unique Disability ID (UDID) cardholders can directly board unreserved Divyangjan (SLRD) coaches and book counter tickets at PRS windows without hassle.

## What Is the IRCTC Divyangjan Concession in Indian Railways?

**The IRCTC Divyangjan concession is a subsidized railway fare facility offering 25% to 75% discounts on base fares for passengers with eligible disabilities and their accompanying escorts.** Four broad categories qualify: orthopaedically handicapped or paraplegic individuals, visually impaired persons with total absence of sight, hearing and speech impaired individuals, and persons with intellectual disabilities.

The concession applies across express, superfast, mail, and premium trains like Rajdhani and Shatabdi, though specific discount percentages vary by travel class and train category:

- **Orthopaedic, Visual, and Intellectual Disabilities:** Eligible for the highest concession slabs (up to 75% in Sleeper and 3AC) and qualify for an accompanying escort concession on the same PNR.
- **Hearing and Speech Impairment:** Must have both conditions together in the same person to qualify for a 50% concession for solo travel.
- **Dedicated Quota (HP):** Indian Railways reserves dedicated lower and middle berths in Sleeper, 3AC, and 3E classes specifically for Divyangjan travelers under the Physically Handicapped (HP) booking quota.

## What Percentage of Concession Is Given to Divyangjan Passengers?

**Divyangjan passengers receive a 75% concession on base fare in Sleeper, 3AC, 3E, and AC Chair Car, a 50% concession in 2AC and First Class, and a 25% concession in 3AC and Chair Car on Rajdhani and Shatabdi trains.** Escorts receive identical discounts when accompanying eligible passengers.

Here is the complete concession breakdown across railway travel classes and train categories:

| Disability Category | Sleeper (SL) & 3E | AC 3-Tier (3AC) & CC | AC 2-Tier (2AC) & 1AC | Rajdhani / Shatabdi (3AC/CC) | Escort Concession Allowed? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Orthopaedically Impaired / Paraplegic** | **75% discount** | **75% discount** | **50% discount** | **25% discount** | Yes (Same concession rate) |
| **Visually Impaired (Total Blindness)** | **75% discount** | **75% discount** | **50% discount** | **25% discount** | Yes (Same concession rate) |
| **Intellectual Disability / Mentally Retarded** | **75% discount** | **75% discount** | **50% discount** | **25% discount** | Yes (Same concession rate) |
| **Hearing & Speech Impaired (Both Together)** | **50% discount** | **50% discount** | Nil (Full fare) | Nil (Full fare) | No (Solo travel concession only) |

*Note: Concession is calculated purely on the base fare. Superfast surcharges, GST on AC classes, and reservation charges are payable in full.*

## How to Book Divyangjan Concession Train Tickets Online on IRCTC?

**To book Divyangjan concession tickets online, you must register your 19-digit Railway Divyangjan Photo Identity Card on your IRCTC profile under the Divyangjan Authentication tab.** Once verified, select the Divyangjan / Handicapped Quota on the train search page, enter your card details, and book confirmed concessional tickets with an escort.

Follow these step-by-step instructions to set up online booking:

1. **Obtain Your Railway Photo ID Card:** Visit your local Divisional Railway Manager (DRM) Commercial Branch with your Government Disability Certificate or UDID card, age proof, address proof, and 2 passport photos to receive your laminated railway card.
2. **Authenticate on IRCTC:** Log in to your IRCTC account, go to **My Profile ➔ Divyangjan Authentication**, enter your 19-character card number, date of birth, and card expiry date, then click verify.
3. **Search Trains:** On the IRCTC search bar, check the **Divyangjan** quota box. The system automatically searches for reserved disability quota berths (HP).
4. **Add Passenger Details:** Select your authenticated profile name. The concession is automatically applied to your fare. If your card allows an escort, add the escort's name on the same booking screen.
5. **Complete Payment:** Pay the discounted fare via UPI, net banking, or debit card to receive your confirmed e-ticket.

## Can You Book Train Tickets with a UDID Card in 2026?

**Yes, you can use your Unique Disability ID (UDID) card directly for unreserved Divyangjan coach travel and counter bookings at PRS reservation windows.** For online IRCTC e-ticketing, the UDID card is used to apply for the standardized Railway Photo Identity Card at your regional DRM office.

Under 2026 railway travel regulations, the access rules between unreserved and reserved travel are clearly separated:

- **Unreserved Train Travel (Local & Mail/Express):** Passengers holding an authentic UDID card (Swavlamban card) can directly purchase concessional unreserved tickets at UTS railway counters or travel in specially designated unreserved Divyangjan (SLRD) coaches without needing a separate railway ID card.
- **Reserved PRS Counter Booking:** You can present your original UDID card or prescribed Railway Doctor's Disability Certificate at any computerized PRS counter to book reserved concessional tickets across India.
- **Online E-Ticketing on IRCTC:** Because IRCTC's automated reservation system validates bookings against the centralized CRIS railway database, e-ticketing requires the pre-issued 19-digit DRM Photo ID card number.

## What Are the Rules for Escorts Traveling with Divyangjan Passengers?

**An escort traveling with an eligible Divyangjan passenger receives the exact same fare concession percentage (up to 75%) and must be booked on the same PNR.** The escort must be an adult aged 18 or above, capable of assisting the passenger throughout the entire journey.

Keep these essential companion rules in mind:

- **Single PNR Requirement:** Both the Divyangjan passenger and the escort must travel on the same ticket and in the same coach. You cannot book an escort in a different class.
- **Escort Eligibility Check:** Escort concessions are permitted only for orthopaedically disabled, visually impaired, and intellectually disabled passengers where the medical certificate explicitly states that the person cannot travel without assistance.
- **Cancellation Rule:** If the Divyangjan passenger cancels their ticket, the escort's ticket automatically loses its concession and validity. The escort cannot travel alone on a concessional ticket.

## How Does the Divyangjan Quota (HP) and Lower Berth Allotment Work?

**Indian Railways earmarks two lower berths and two middle berths in Sleeper class, and two lower berths in 3AC and 3E coaches specifically for Divyangjan passengers under the HP quota.** Berths are allocated automatically near the coach doors and disabled-friendly lavatories for convenient wheelchair boarding.

Key berth allotment details include:

- **Coach Proximity:** In Sleeper and 3AC coaches, berths 1, 2, 3, and 4 (or side lower berths) are commonly designated as the disability quota block to ensure easy exit during station halts.
- **Escort Placement:** The accompanying escort is assigned the adjacent berth (e.g., middle or upper berth right next to the passenger's lower berth) to ensure continuous assistance.
- **SLRD Coaches on Trains:** Most LHB and ICF train rakes include dedicated **SLRD (Second Class, Luggage, Rake & Divyangjan)** coaches at the front and rear of the train, fitted with wide sliding doors, wheelchair ramps, wider gangways, and specialized accessible toilets.

## What Documents Must Be Carried During Train Travel?

**Divyangjan passengers must carry the original Railway Photo Identity Card or valid UDID card, along with a government photo ID proof like an Aadhaar card or voter ID.** Escorts must carry their own original government-issued photo ID during the journey for TTE verification.

The Travelling Ticket Examiner (TTE) will inspect the following credentials on board:

1. **For E-Ticket Passengers:** The original laminated Railway Divyangjan Photo ID card whose 19-digit number was entered during IRCTC booking.
2. **For Counter Ticket Passengers:** The original Government Concession Certificate / UDID card and the physical PRS ticket.
3. **Identity Proof for Escort:** Valid original ID (Aadhaar card, PAN card, driving licence, or passport) matching the escort details printed on the chart.

> [!WARNING]  
> Traveling on a concessional Divyangjan ticket without carrying the original valid disability certificate or DRM photo ID is treated as travel without a ticket under Section 138 of the Railways Act. The TTE will forfeit the concession, collect the full standard fare plus a ₹500 penalty, and may initiate legal proceedings.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### Can a Divyangjan passenger travel alone without an escort?
Yes, visually impaired and orthopaedically handicapped passengers whose medical certificate states they can travel independently can book solo tickets and enjoy the full 75% or 50% concession.

### Is the senior citizen concession available alongside the Divyangjan concession?
No, Indian Railways does not allow combining multiple concessions on a single ticket. However, senior citizen Divyangjan passengers can choose the Divyangjan concession, which offers up to 75% off compared to zero senior citizen discounts.

### How many lower berths are reserved for disabled passengers per train?
Indian Railways earmarks a minimum of 4 berths (2 lower and 2 middle/upper) in Sleeper class, and 2 lower berths in each 3AC and 3E coach under the Divyangjan (HP) quota.

### How long is the Railway Divyangjan Photo ID Card valid?
For permanent disabilities, the card is valid for 5 years (or lifetime for senior citizens above 60). For temporary disabilities, the card validity matches the duration specified on the medical certificate.

### Can Divyangjan concession be claimed on Tatkal tickets?
No, Indian Railways does not offer any fare concessions on Tatkal or Premium Tatkal ticket bookings. Concessions apply exclusively to General and Divyangjan (HP) quota bookings.

### What happens if the Divyangjan quota is on the waiting list?
If all earmarked HP quota berths are booked, the ticket is placed on the Divyangjan Waiting List. Unused Emergency Quota (EQ) or General berths are often used at first chart preparation (~8 hours before departure) to confirm these tickets.

### Does Vande Bharat Express offer concessions for Divyangjan passengers?
No fare concessions are currently available on Vande Bharat Express trains for any category except accredited journalists, though Divyangjan passengers can still utilize onboard wheelchair assistance.

### Can an escort be a minor child?
No, the accompanying escort must be an adult aged 18 years or older who is physically capable of rendering complete care and assistance to the passenger throughout the journey.
