### Terminal Station Gotcha (`BSB` vs `BSBS` vs `DDU`, and `NDLS` vs `ANVT`)

Metro and Varanasi travelers often mix up nearby terminals when searching IRCTC:

2. **In Delhi:** The sleeper rake uses **New Delhi (`NDLS`)** with a halt at **Ghaziabad (`GZB`)**, not Anand Vihar Terminal (`ANVT`) or Old Delhi (`DLI`). East Delhi and Noida passengers can board conveniently at `GZB` instead of crossing central Delhi traffic.

## What Are the Booking, Cancellation, Tatkal and Charting Rules?

**Bookings open 60 days ahead under the Advance Reservation Period (ARP), and Railway Board Vande Bharat Sleeper rules exclude RAC, Tatkal, and Premium Tatkal quotas while limiting boarding strictly to confirmed tickets. First reservation charts finalize 8 to 10 hours before departure, followed by a second chart 30 to 45 minutes prior.**

Unlike conventional mail/express trains that rely heavily on 10:00 AM Tatkal quotas and RAC side-lower sharing, Vande Bharat Sleeper operations follow stricter confirmed-seat rules:

1. **60-Day ARP & Quota Rules:** Tickets open 60 days in advance (excluding journey date) at 08:00 AM IST, with Aadhaar-authenticated IRCTC accounts prioritized on opening day. Only General, Ladies, Senior Citizen, Divyangjan, and Duty Pass quotas apply; **no Tatkal, Premium Tatkal, or RAC** is offered under Railway Board guidelines.
2. **Confirmed-Only Boarding & Cancellation Slabs:** Unconfirmed passengers cannot board. Reported voluntary cancellation deductions for confirmed Vande Bharat Sleeper tickets are **25% more than 72 hours before departure**, **50% between 72 hours and 8 hours**, and **zero refund within 8 hours** of scheduled departure (verify exact deduction slabs on IRCTC before cancelling).
3. **Chart Preparation & `CURR_AVBL`:** Because both 27421 (20:50 IST) and 27422 (21:40 IST) depart after 14:00, their first charts are prepared **~8 to 10 hours prior** (roughly **10:50 to 13:40 IST** on departure day). Any unallocated quota berths release under Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`) until the final chart closes 30 to 45 minutes prior (with live current booking cutoffs extending up to 15 minutes before departure on select Vande Bharat rakes).
4. **Nighttime Server Blackout:** IRCTC conducts daily maintenance from **11:45 PM to 12:20 AM IST (23:45 to 00:20)**. Avoid attempting bookings or cancellations during this 35-minute window.

> **Pro-Tip on Early Evening Boarding:** Since Train 27421 departs Varanasi at 20:50 IST, its first chart normally drops around midday (10:50 to 12:50 IST). Set a departure-day reminder on [Chart Times](/chart-times) to catch newly freed `CURR_AVBL` berths right when midday quotas unlock.

## What Happens If You Board Without a Confirmed Ticket on Train 27421 / 27422?

**Passengers without a confirmed berth are strictly barred from boarding Train 27421 or 27422. Under Section 138 of the Railways Act and the Jan Vishwas Act 2026, entering any AC sleeper coach without a confirmed reservation triggers a flat ₹500 fine plus the full class fare difference and deboarding at the next halt.**

Because Vande Bharat Sleeper rakes carry zero unreserved (`GS`) and zero `RAC` accommodations, there is no fallback general coach to move into if a Travelling Ticket Examiner (TTE) finds you without a confirmed berth on their Handheld Terminal (HHT).

Baggage allowances match standard AC class rules: **70 kg free in 1A**, **50 kg free in 2A**, and **40 kg free in 3A**. Oversized trunks exceeding coach under-berth clearances may be refused in passenger bays and booked via the parcel van where applicable.
