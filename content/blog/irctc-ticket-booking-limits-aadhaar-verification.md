You sit at your screen ready to book, and a warning pops up: "Monthly booking limit reached." Indian Railways restricts standard accounts to prevent hoarding, but offers a simple way to double your slots. Here is a complete breakdown of monthly, daily, and session-specific ticket limits, along with steps to link your Aadhaar card.

## How Many Tickets Can Be Booked in IRCTC in a Month?

**You can book up to 12 tickets in a calendar month from a standard, unverified IRCTC account. By linking your Aadhaar card and verifying at least one passenger, you can double your monthly booking limit to 24 tickets per IRCTC user ID.** This count resets on the first day of each month.

| Booking Type / Limit Category | Limit Value | Key Rule & Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monthly booking (Unverified)** | 12 tickets | Standard account with no identity verification. |
| **Monthly booking (Aadhaar-verified)** | 24 tickets | Account linked to Aadhaar + at least one verified passenger on booking. |
| **Daily Tatkal booking limit** | 2 tickets | Maximum of two Tatkal PNRs booked per day per user ID. |
| **Peak-hour session booking limit** | 2 tickets | Applies during 8:00 AM – 12:00 noon (General & Tatkal openings). |
| **Max passengers (General)** | 6 passengers | Per single booking / PNR. |
| **Max passengers (Tatkal)** | 4 passengers | Per single booking / PNR. |

Indian Railways introduced this limit to curb the black market, where unauthorized agents buy tickets in bulk and resell them. For a regular traveller, 12 a month is usually plenty, but a large family or a frequent traveller can burn through it fast. Once you hit the wall, you cannot book again from that ID until the counter resets on the first day of the next month, so it pays to set up your Aadhaar link before you actually need it.

## Is the limit per account, per user, or per ID?

**The monthly ticket limit is counted per IRCTC user ID (one account), not per person, payment card, or device.** If you share a login, you share the monthly quota. However, if family members register separate IRCTC accounts, each account gets its own individual 12 or 24 ticket limit.

## How many tickets can you book per day on IRCTC?

There is **no separate daily cap on normal bookings, but you can only book up to 2 Tatkal tickets per day per user ID.** Additionally, during peak hours (8:00 AM to 12:00 noon), IRCTC restricts individual users to a maximum of 2 tickets per session across all booking types to prevent hoarding.

This daily Tatkal restriction ensures that users cannot monopolize last-minute seats. Outside these peak windows and Tatkal limitations, only your monthly limit applies.

## How many passengers can you book on one ticket?

A single ticket (one PNR) can hold **up to 6 passengers in the general quota** and **up to 4 passengers in Tatkal**. Each booked ticket counts as one unit against your monthly limit, regardless of passenger count. Splitting groups across multiple bookings consumes your monthly limit faster, making Aadhaar verification highly beneficial.

## How to link Aadhaar with IRCTC to increase the booking limit

**Log into the IRCTC website or Rail Connect app, go to My Account, select Link Your Aadhaar, and enter your personal details. You will receive a verification OTP on the mobile number registered with Aadhaar to complete the linking process securely.**

Make sure your mobile number is linked to your Aadhaar first, since you will need the OTP.

1. Log into your official IRCTC account.
2. Go to the **My Account** tab and click **Link Your Aadhaar** under profile settings.
3. Type your name and 12-digit Aadhaar number, exactly as printed on the card.
4. Tick the consent box and click **Send OTP**.
5. Enter the code sent to your phone and click **Verify**.

Log out and back in, and your profile should show as Aadhaar-verified.

## Master List: verify passengers (and how many you can save)

**To book beyond 12 tickets, add passengers to your IRCTC Master List, enter their Aadhaar numbers, and click Verify. A green tick appears once the details check out, and you pick that passenger during booking.** You can save **up to 20 passengers** in the Master List, so set up your regular travellers once and reuse them.

A common mistake is thinking that verifying your own account is enough. It isn't. On your 13th ticket of the month, at least one passenger on that specific booking must be Aadhaar-verified, whether that is you or someone else on the list.

1. Go to **My Account** and select **My Master List**.
2. Click **Add Passenger** and fill in name, date of birth, gender and Aadhaar number.
3. Submit. The status will say "Pending" at first.
4. Refresh after a couple of minutes for a green "Verified" status.

When booking, choose the verified passenger from the list. If you type their details manually at checkout, the system won't recognize the verification and the booking fails.

## What are the peak-hour priority Aadhaar validation rules on IRCTC?

**During the high-traffic peak hours of 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM, IRCTC enforces priority validation rules that prioritize bookings from Aadhaar-verified accounts.** Verified users benefit from faster checkout processing and bypass strict bot-detection screens, while unverified accounts face extra security checks, CAPTCHAs, and higher checkout failure rates.

This prioritization helps genuine travellers book tickets successfully before seats run out, particularly during the opening minutes of general reservations (8:00 AM) and Tatkal bookings (10:00 AM for AC, 11:00 AM for non-AC). Because the system checks linked credentials pre-session, verified profiles experience reduced latency and a much higher chance of securing a confirmed berth.

## Why is Aadhaar OTP Verification Mandatory for Tatkal Bookings?

**Starting July 15, 2026, IRCTC has implemented mandatory Aadhaar-based OTP verification for online Tatkal ticket bookings. This security measure requires passengers to authenticate their identity using an OTP sent to their Aadhaar-linked mobile number to check bots and ensure fair ticket access.**

### How Aadhaar OTP Verification Works
During the peak Tatkal booking window (10:00 AM for AC and 11:00 AM for non-AC), the IRCTC system prompts users for Aadhaar OTP verification. This happens either at the time of login or during the passenger detail entry phase of the Tatkal flow. Once the passenger's details are retrieved from the Master List or entered, an OTP is triggered to the Aadhaar-registered mobile number, which must be entered within a limited window to complete the transaction.

### Common Issues Faced by Passengers
* **OTP Delivery Delay:** Network congestion during peak booking hours can delay the arrival of the OTP, leading to session timeouts and missed tickets.
* **Mismatched Mobile Numbers:** Passengers often discover that the phone number currently in use does not match the one registered with UIDAI.
* **Failure to Link Profile in Advance:** Trying to link Aadhaar or verify passenger profiles during the peak Tatkal booking window causes high transaction failure rates.

### How to Prepare
* **Pre-link in My Account:** Complete your own Aadhaar linking under the "Link Your Aadhaar" option in your IRCTC profile settings well ahead of the booking window.
* **Verify Master List:** Add all frequent travellers to your Master List and ensure they have a "Verified" status with a green tick before Tatkal booking opens.
* **Keep Registered Phone Active:** Ensure the mobile phone linked to your Aadhaar card is close by, active, and has sufficient network coverage to receive the OTP instantly.

## What happens when you reach the monthly limit?

**When you reach the monthly IRCTC limit, you cannot book any more tickets until the quota resets at midnight on the first of the next calendar month.** Cancelling tickets does not restore your booking slots. To proceed, you must link your Aadhaar or use a family member's account.

## IRCTC ticket limit FAQ

Here are the most common questions about how many tickets you can book on IRCTC.

### Is Aadhaar OTP verification mandatory for Tatkal bookings?
Yes. Starting July 15, 2026, IRCTC requires Aadhaar OTP verification for all online Tatkal and Premium Tatkal bookings. Unverified profiles or users who fail to complete OTP authentication cannot book Tatkal tickets during peak hours.

### How many tickets can be booked in IRCTC in a month?
Up to 12 tickets per calendar month on a normal IRCTC account, or 24 tickets if your account is Aadhaar-linked and at least one passenger on each ticket beyond the first 12 is Aadhaar-verified.

### How many tickets can I book on IRCTC in a day?
There is no separate daily limit for normal bookings, you can use your whole monthly quota in one day. However, you can only book up to 2 Tatkal tickets per day per user ID. During the peak windows (8:00 AM to 12:00 noon), individual users are capped at 2 tickets per session.

### What are the peak-hour priority Aadhaar validation rules?
During high-traffic peak hours (8:00 AM to 12:00 PM), IRCTC fast-tracks transactions from Aadhaar-verified profiles. Linking Aadhaar helps bypass strict bot-detection queues and extra verification steps, yielding higher checkout success rates than unverified accounts.

### What is the IRCTC ticket limit per user or per account?
The limit is 12 (or 24 with Aadhaar) per IRCTC user ID. It is counted per account, not per person, card or device. Separate accounts each get their own monthly limit.

### How many tickets can be booked in IRCTC in a year?
There is no annual limit. The cap is monthly (12 or 24 per calendar month) and it resets on the 1st of every month.

### How many passengers can I book on one IRCTC ticket?
Up to 6 passengers per ticket in the general quota, and up to 4 per ticket in Tatkal. Each ticket is one PNR and counts as one against your monthly limit regardless of the number of passengers.

### How many passengers can I save in the IRCTC Master List?
Up to 20 passengers. Adding and Aadhaar-verifying them in advance saves time during the booking rush.

### Does the monthly limit include Tatkal tickets?
Yes. The limit counts all tickets from one user ID in a calendar month, including general, Tatkal and Premium Tatkal.

### Does cancelling a ticket restore my monthly limit?
No. A ticket you have booked still counts toward your monthly limit even after you cancel it. The count only resets on the 1st of the next month.

### How many PNRs can be booked on IRCTC in a month?
Each ticket is one PNR, so the PNR count matches the ticket limit: 12 per month normally, or 24 with an Aadhaar-verified account.
