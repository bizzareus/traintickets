Have you ever tried booking train tickets for a family reunion or a big group of friends on IRCTC? If so, you know how fast it can turn into a circus. You sit at your laptop, tea in hand, ready to grab those seats, and suddenly a red warning pops up: "Monthly booking limit reached." Most people don't even realize there is a limit until they run face-first into it. The good news is Indian Railways gives you a built-in way to raise it. Below is exactly how many tickets you can book on IRCTC in a month, how the limit works per account, per day and per booking, and how to double it from 12 to 24.

## How many tickets can be booked from one IRCTC account per month?

**You can book up to 12 train tickets in a calendar month from a standard, unverified IRCTC account. If you link your Aadhaar card to your IRCTC profile and verify at least one passenger on each booking, the monthly limit doubles to 24 tickets per user ID.** This count resets automatically on the 1st day of every month and includes all types of ticket bookings, such as general quota, Tatkal, and Premium Tatkal.

| Account type | Monthly ticket limit | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Unverified IRCTC account | 12 tickets | None (standard booking) |
| Aadhaar-verified account | 24 tickets | At least 1 passenger on the ticket is Aadhaar-verified in your Master List |

Indian Railways introduced this limit to curb the black market, where unauthorized agents buy tickets in bulk and resell them. For a regular traveller, 12 a month is usually plenty, but a large family or a frequent traveller can burn through it fast. Once you hit the wall, you cannot book again from that ID until the counter resets on the first day of the next month, so it pays to set up your Aadhaar link before you actually need it.

## Is the limit per account, per user, or per ID?

These all mean the same thing. **The limit is counted per IRCTC user ID (one account).** It is not per card, per device, or per family. If two people share one login, they share the same 12 or 24 ticket slots. If each person makes their own IRCTC account, each account gets its own monthly limit. There is no separate limit "per Aadhaar" card beyond the account to which it is linked.

## How many tickets can you book per day on IRCTC?

There is **no separate daily cap** on normal bookings, you can use your full monthly allowance in a single day if you want. The one exception is the busy morning windows, where IRCTC restricts individual users to **2 tickets per user ID per session**:

- 8:00 AM to 10:00 AM (general advance-reservation opening)
- 10:00 AM to 11:00 AM (AC Tatkal)
- 11:00 AM to 12:00 noon (non-AC Tatkal)

This rule exists to stop agents from hogging seats the moment booking opens. Outside these windows, only your monthly limit applies.

## How many passengers can you book on one ticket?

A single ticket (one PNR) can hold **up to 6 passengers in the general quota** and **up to 4 passengers in Tatkal**. So each ticket counts as one against your monthly limit no matter how many passengers are on it. If you need to move a large group, splitting them across multiple tickets uses up your monthly count faster, which is another reason the 24 limit is worth unlocking.

## How to link Aadhaar with IRCTC to increase the booking limit

**Log into the IRCTC website or Rail Connect app, go to My Account, select Link Your Aadhaar, and enter your details. You will get a verification code on the mobile number registered with your Aadhaar to complete the link.**

Make sure your mobile number is linked to your Aadhaar first, since you will need the OTP.

1. Log into your official IRCTC account.
2. Go to the **My Account** tab and click **Link Your Aadhaar** under profile settings.
3. Type your name and 12-digit Aadhaar number, exactly as printed on the card.
4. Tick the consent box and click **Send OTP**.
5. Enter the code sent to your phone and click **Verify**.

Log out and back in, and your profile should show as Aadhaar-verified.

## Master List: verify passengers (and how many you can save)

**To book beyond 12 tickets, add passengers to your IRCTC Master List, enter their Aadhaar numbers, and click Verify. A green tick appears once the details check out, and you pick that passenger during booking.** You can save **up to 20 passengers** in the Master List, so set up your regular travellers once and reuse them.

A common mistake is thinking that verifying your own account is enough. It isn't. On your 13th ticket of the month, at least one passenger on that specific booking must be Aadhaar-verified, whether that is you or someone else on the list.

1. Go to **My Account** and select **My Master List**.
2. Click **Add Passenger** and fill in name, date of birth, gender and Aadhaar number.
3. Submit. The status will say "Pending" at first.
4. Refresh after a couple of minutes for a green "Verified" status.

When booking, choose the verified passenger from the list. If you type their details manually at checkout, the system won't recognize the verification and the booking fails.

## What happens when you reach the monthly limit?

You simply cannot book any more tickets from that user ID until the limit resets at midnight on the 1st of the next calendar month. Cancelling a ticket does **not** give the slot back, a booked ticket still counts even after cancellation. If you are stuck mid-month, your options are to get your Aadhaar verified to unlock 24, or book from a different family member's IRCTC account.

If your tickets are waitlisted, the **PNR Status Search** tool shows confirmation chances, and the **Seat Status Coach Journey Lookup** finds vacant berths on the train.

## IRCTC ticket limit FAQ

Here are the most common questions about how many tickets you can book on IRCTC.

### How many tickets can be booked in IRCTC in a month?
Up to 12 tickets per calendar month on a normal IRCTC account, or 24 tickets if your account is Aadhaar-linked and at least one passenger on each ticket beyond the first 12 is Aadhaar-verified.

### How many tickets can I book on IRCTC in a day?
There is no separate daily limit for normal bookings, you can use your whole monthly quota in one day. During the peak windows (8 to 10 AM, and the 10 to 11 AM and 11 to 12 noon Tatkal slots), individual users are capped at 2 tickets per session.

### What is the IRCTC ticket limit per user or per account?
The limit is 12 (or 24 with Aadhaar) per IRCTC user ID. It is counted per account, not per person, card or device. Separate accounts each get their own monthly limit.

### How many tickets can be booked in IRCTC in a year?
There is no annual limit. The cap is monthly (12 or 24 per calendar month) and it resets on the 1st of every month.

### How many passengers can I book on one IRCTC ticket?
Up to 6 passengers per ticket in the general quota, and up to 4 per ticket in Tatkal. Each ticket is one PNR and counts as one against your monthly limit regardless of the number of passengers.

### How many passengers can I save in the IRCTC Master List?
Up to 20 passengers. Adding and Aadhaar-verifying them in advance saves time during the booking rush.

### Does the monthly limit include Tatkal tickets?
Yes. The limit counts all tickets from one user ID in a calendar month, including general, Tatkal and Premium Tatkal.

### Does cancelling a ticket restore my monthly limit?
No. A ticket you have booked still counts toward your monthly limit even after you cancel it. The count only resets on the 1st of the next month.

### How many PNRs can be booked on IRCTC in a month?
Each ticket is one PNR, so the PNR count matches the ticket limit: 12 per month normally, or 24 with an Aadhaar-verified account.
