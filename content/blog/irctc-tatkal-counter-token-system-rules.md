## What is the New IRCTC Tatkal Counter Token System?

**The new IRCTC Tatkal counter token system is a queue management policy implemented by Indian Railways at Passenger Reservation System (PRS) counters to eliminate physical crowding and line-jumping. Passengers receive a unique numbered token prior to Tatkal window opening, which dictates their exact turn at the booking window based on arrival order and priority category.**

Before this update, Tatkal booking at physical railway counters was chaotic. Passengers stood in line for hours overnight, often resulting in heated arguments or unauthorized agents jumping the line. 

Under the updated system, PRS counters issue numbered tokens during fixed morning windows. Once you receive your token, your position in line is secured. When the Tatkal opening time arrives (10:00 AM for AC, 11:00 AM for Sleeper), railway clerks call token numbers sequentially. This ensures a transparent, orderly process for every traveller at the counter.

## What Are the Staggered Token Timings for AC and Non-AC Classes?

**Staggered token timings require passengers booking AC Tatkal tickets to collect tokens between 8:30 AM and 9:30 AM, while Non-AC Sleeper class tokens are distributed between 9:00 AM and 9:30 AM. Token distribution stops at 9:30 AM to allow counter staff to register profiles and organize queues before booking windows open.**

The table below outlines the exact schedule for collecting tokens and booking Tatkal tickets at PRS counters:

| Travel Class | Token Issuance Window | Tatkal Window Opens | Key Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AC Classes (1A, 2A, 3A, 3E, CC)** | **8:30 AM – 9:30 AM** | **10:00 AM** | Token collected before 9:30 AM; presence required at 10:00 AM |
| **Non-AC Classes (SL, 2S)** | **9:00 AM – 9:30 AM** | **11:00 AM** | Token collected before 9:30 AM; presence required at 11:00 AM |

Arriving at the PRS counter after 9:30 AM means you will not be issued a numbered token for that day's Tatkal opening. In such cases, counter staff will only serve tokenless passengers after all token holders have been processed, by which time Tatkal quotas are typically exhausted.

## What is Category A vs Category B in Tatkal Counter Bookings?

**Category A consists of genuine travellers booking tickets for themselves or immediate family members, receiving top priority at counter windows. Category B includes representative bookers or authorized travel agents, who are issued tokens only after Category A queue demands are met and must verify identity via Aadhaar.**

Indian Railways created this two-tier classification to prioritize everyday passengers over commercial agents:

1. **Category A (Self & Family - First Priority):**
   - Applies when the person standing in line is travelling themselves or booking for immediate family members sharing the same surname or address.
   - Served first when booking counters open at 10:00 AM (AC) and 11:00 AM (Sleeper).
   - Requires filling the standard requisition slip with complete passenger names and contact numbers.

2. **Category B (Representatives & Agents - Second Priority):**
   - Applies to representatives, friends, or travel agents booking on behalf of someone else.
   - Category B token holders are processed **only after** all Category A passengers at that counter have completed their transactions.
   - Must submit a mandatory physical Aadhaar card or government-issued photo ID of the booker at the token window.

## What Photo ID is Required for Tatkal Counter Booking?

**A physical Aadhaar card, Voter ID, Passport, Driving License, or official government photo ID is required for all Category B counter bookers and recommended for Category A travellers. The name on the requisition form must match the photo ID presented to the counter supervisor.**

When securing a Tatkal token at the PRS counter, keep these ID rules in mind:

- **Aadhaar Verification:** Category B bookers must present a physical Aadhaar card. Digital screenshots or unverified photocopies may be rejected by station supervisors.
- **Form Matching:** The passenger details written on the Tatkal requisition form (name, age, gender, address) must match valid identity documents.
- **Original Photo ID During Journey:** Remember that during train travel, at least one passenger on the confirmed Tatkal PNR must carry an original valid photo ID to show the Traveling Ticket Examiner (TTE).

## Counter Booking vs Online IRCTC Tatkal: Which is Faster?

**Online IRCTC Tatkal booking via app or website is generally faster due to direct system API connectivity, provided your Aadhaar OTP verification is completed in advance. However, PRS counter booking provides a reliable fallback if internet connectivity fails or online payment gateways stall.**

Here is how counter Tatkal compares with online Tatkal booking:

| Feature | PRS Counter Tatkal | Online IRCTC Website / App |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Token / Queue Requirement** | Token required (8:30 AM / 9:00 AM) | Aadhaar OTP verification required |
| **Opening Time (AC)** | 10:00 AM | 10:00 AM |
| **Opening Time (Sleeper)** | 11:00 AM | 11:00 AM |
| **Payment Options** | Cash, UPI, Debit/Credit Card | UPI, Netbanking, e-Wallet |
| **Max Passengers per PNR** | 4 passengers | 4 passengers |
| **Failure Risk** | Counter queue position / quota exhaust | Bank OTP delay / session timeout |

If you book online, ensure your IRCTC profile is Aadhaar-linked. For counter bookings, arrive early to secure a low Category A token number.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### What time are Tatkal tokens issued at railway counters?
Tatkal tokens are issued between 8:30 AM and 9:30 AM for AC classes, and between 9:00 AM and 9:30 AM for Non-AC Sleeper classes. Token distribution closes strictly at 9:30 AM across all PRS counters.

### Can I get a Tatkal token after 9:30 AM at the counter?
No, token distribution ends at 9:30 AM. Passengers arriving after 9:30 AM must wait in the non-token line and will only be served after all token holders have finished their bookings.

### What is the difference between Category A and Category B Tatkal tokens?
Category A is for passengers booking for themselves or immediate family members and receives first priority. Category B is for agents or representatives and is served only after all Category A passengers finish.

### Is Aadhaar mandatory for counter Tatkal ticket booking?
Aadhaar or valid government photo ID is mandatory for Category B bookers receiving tokens. For Category A travellers, keeping Aadhaar details handy ensures quick requisition verification.

### What happens if my Tatkal ticket remains waitlisted (TQWL)?
If a Tatkal ticket booked online remains in TQWL status after final chart preparation, it is automatically cancelled and refunded. Counter TQWL tickets must be cancelled manually at the station window.

### Can I book Current Availability if Tatkal is full?
Yes. Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`) opens ~8 hours before train departure right after chart preparation. It offers 100% confirmed seats with no Tatkal premium charges.
