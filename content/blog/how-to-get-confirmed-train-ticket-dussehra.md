## Facts first — Puja 2026 dates & booking days

No story, straight facts. Puja travel rush: **Oct 15 (Panchami) to Oct 21 (Vijayadashami/Dussehra)**. Kolkata, Howrah, Patna, Varanasi, Ahmedabad routes fullest. General quota on top trains goes in under a minute at 8 AM — I've watched Howrah Rajdhani vanish by 8:01.

2026 break-up you need:

- Panchami/Sasthi: Oct 15–16. Saptami/Ashtami/Navami: Oct 17–19 (peak pandal days). Dashami/Dussehra: Oct 20–21.
- IRCTC opens 60 days before, **8:00 AM IST sharp**. Tatkal: **10 AM AC, 11 AM sleeper**, one day before. Chart: **4–8 hrs before** the train leaves its origin (morning trains usually chart previous evening ~8 PM).
- REGRET on IRCTC = they won't even sell waitlist now. And an online waitlist left over after chart = auto-cancelled, refund in a few days, but no travel. Don't board on it — TTE fines under Section 138.

## If direct is REGRET, do these 3 things (in order)

**1. Look for a split on the same train.** Railways splits seats by station quota — Delhi–Howrah full, but Delhi–DDU + DDU–Howrah empty, same train, same day. [Smart Seats](/) finds these combos. You book two PNRs on IRCTC, shift berths at the middle station. Legal, TTEs see it daily during Puja. Extra cost? Just double reservation charge, ~₹40–80. Worth it, no?

**2. Try Tatkal correctly, then stop chasing it.** 10 AM AC / 11 AM sleeper, day before origin departure. Puja Tatkal is a bloodbath though — gone in seconds. If you miss it once, jump to step 1 and 3 instead of retrying agents. My brother-in-law got Howrah-bound last year this way after Tatkal failed.

**3. Grab post-chart leftovers.** After first chart, unused VIP/defence/middle-station seats release as CURR_AVBL — fully confirmed, bookable on IRCTC till ~30 mins before departure. Check [Get Confirmed Tickets](/) right after charting; confirm timing on [Chart Times](/chart-times). I once picked 3AC at 11 PM for a next-morning train. Keep IRCTC logged in, UPI ready.

## Exact splits that work — train numbers included

Vague advice doesn't help at 8 AM, so here are the three I get asked about most. Always re-check on your date — quotas shift daily.

### Delhi → Kolkata (Durga Puja peak)

- Train **12302 Howrah Rajdhani**, NDLS → HWH, 3AC REGRET? Typical.
- Break that works: **NDLS → DDU** (Pt. Deen Dayal Upadhyaya, old Mughalsarai) confirmed + **DDU → HWH** confirmed. Same train.
- Book both legs on IRCTC, carry both SMS/PDFs. Tell the attendant at DDU — hundred passengers do this during Puja week.

### Delhi → Patna (Dussehra rush)

- Train **12392 Shramjeevi**, Sleeper + 3AC REGRET? Happens every year.
- Break: **NDLS → Lucknow** confirmed + **Lucknow → PNBE** confirmed. Same rake.
- Lucknow halt gives you time to refill water, shift seats. I reached Patna fresh morning for the mela doing exactly this.

## FAQ — questions I get every single Puja

### Tatkal failed at 10 AM, now what?

Fact: try general-quota splits on [Smart Seats](/) first — no Tatkal needed. Then watch [Get Confirmed Tickets](/) after first chart for CURR_AVBL. Tatkal re-attempts rarely work Puja week; splits + chart leftovers do. That's how my brother-in-law reached Howrah last year.

### Are those 0-number Puja specials cheaper?

No — costlier. Fact: 0-series = special fare, **+10% in 2S, +30% in sleeper/AC**, no senior-citizen concession. Still, if it's confirmed vs. missing Ashtami anjali at home? My family paid the extra ₹300 happily. Just verify the train number on IRCTC before paying — numbers shuffle yearly.
