## Why Dussehra & Durga Puja Train Tickets Sell Out Fast

Durga Puja and Dussehra are among the biggest festive periods in Eastern and Northern India. In 2026, the key festival dates span:
- **Maha Panchami / Sasthi:** October 15–16, 2026
- **Maha Saptami / Ashtami / Navami:** October 17–19, 2026
- **Maha Dashami / Dussehra (Vijayadashami):** October 20–21, 2026

Major flagship trains like the Howrah Rajdhani, Sealdah Duronto, Poorva Express, Vikramshila Express, and Western Railway expresses connecting **Mumbai to Ahmedabad** and **Delhi to Patna/Howrah** see general quota seats vanish within 60 seconds of the 8:00 AM IRCTC booking opening.

When direct booking displays `REGRET`, buying a waiting list ticket is extremely risky because waitlisted online e-tickets are automatically cancelled at chart preparation.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What happens if I can't get a direct Tatkal ticket for Dussehra?
If Tatkal tickets sell out instantly at 10:00 AM / 11:00 AM, use [LastBerth Smart Seats](/) to find available general quota split-segments, or monitor [Chart Vacancy](/chart-vacancy) after 1st chart preparation for Current Reservation berths.

### Are 0-series Durga Puja special trains cheaper than regular trains?
No. Festival special trains run under 0-series numbers with **Train on Special Fare (TOSF)** surcharges (+10% on 2S, +30% on SL/AC) over regular base fares.
