## The trick that saved my Puja trip: split tickets, same train

Okay so here's the bit nobody tells you clearly.

Railways doesn't keep all seats for Delhi-to-Howrah people. They slice up the coach — some for Delhi to DDU, some for DDU to Howrah, some for Patna quota, etc. So your end-to-end search can scream REGRET while the pieces are just sitting there empty. Annoying, right?

That's what [LastBerth Smart Seats](/) looks for. You type your full trip, it quietly checks every middle station combo on that same train.

My neighbour did Delhi to Howrah last Puja exactly like this. Same train, same day. He boarded at New Delhi, sat till DDU, then walked two bays over to his second seat till Howrah. Had chai in between. No running, no new train.

## How I actually book it (step by step, no jargon)

1. I open [LastBerth home](/). On phone is fine, site is light.

2. I punch origin, destination, date, class. Like NDLS, HWH, Oct 17, 3A. Keep 2-3 dates flexible if you can — one day shift can be the difference between REGRET and confirmed. Trust me.

3. If direct is dead, I scroll to the Smart Seats bit. It'll show you something like Leg 1 + Leg 2, same train number. Check the train number matches! That's important. Same train, same class ideally.

4. Then I jump to IRCTC app and book those two legs myself. Yes, two tickets, two PNRs. Costs a little extra (reservation charge twice), maybe 40-80 bucks. Worth it.

Small tip: book Leg 2 first if it's the longer one? I do, because that's the one that usually fills faster. Then Leg 1 immediately.

## FAQ — questions I get every single Puja

### Tatkal failed at 10 AM, now what?

Happens to most of us. Tatkal for Puja is a bloodbath — 10 AM AC, 11 AM sleeper, gone in seconds. Don't panic. Check [Smart Seats](/) for general quota splits first, they don't need Tatkal. Then keep [Chart Vacancy](/chart-vacancy) open for after-chart leftovers. That's how my brother-in-law got Howrah-bound last year after missing Tatkal.

### Are those 0-number Puja specials cheaper?

Nope, opposite. Anything starting with 0 is a special fare train. Railways adds about 10% extra in 2S, 30% extra in sleeper/AC. No senior citizen discount either. Still, if that's all that's confirmed, take it. Reaching home for Ashtami anjali is worth the extra 300 rupees, no?
