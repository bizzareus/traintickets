## TL;DR

If you can't travel on your confirmed train ticket, don't cancel it just yet. You can legally transfer it to a family member like your spouse, parents, siblings, or children. However, you cannot transfer a ticket to a friend. Since this process is completely offline, you must submit a written application at a railway reservation counter along with proof of relationship at least 24 hours before the train's departure.

---

## Can we transfer a confirmed train ticket to another person?

**Yes, you can transfer your confirmed ticket to another person, but only if they are a close family member. Indian Railways allows name changes for your spouse, parents, children, or siblings, but you cannot transfer a ticket to a friend or colleague.**

If your travel plans suddenly change, cancelling a confirmed ticket means losing money to cancellation fees. Instead, you can transfer the ticket to a relative. The railways allow this to help families save money, but they keep the rules tight. You can't transfer your seat to a friend or neighbor. The change must be done by a railway official at the station. Once they verify your relationship and update the chart, your family member can travel legally.

## Who is eligible for an IRCTC train ticket transfer?

**Only immediate family members—meaning parents, spouse, siblings, and children—are eligible for an individual ticket transfer. Indian Railways also allows ticket transfers for government employees on duty, students of recognized schools, marriage groups, and NCC cadets, subject to different deadlines.**

Here is how the eligibility and timelines look for different categories:

- **Family Members**: You can transfer your ticket to your father, mother, spouse, brother, sister, son, or daughter. You need to apply at least 24 hours before departure.
- **Government Employees**: If you are a government worker on duty and get held up, you can transfer your ticket to a colleague. Apply 24 hours before the train leaves.
- **Students**: Recognized school or college students can transfer their tickets to another student. The school principal must sign the request, which you must submit 48 hours before the journey.
- **Marriage Parties**: If you booked under a group reservation for a wedding, the group leader can request transfers to other guests. This needs to be done 48 hours before departure.
- **NCC Cadets**: Cadets traveling in a group can transfer their tickets to other cadets. You must submit the request 24 hours before the train departs.

## What is the step-by-step process to transfer a train ticket?

**To transfer a train ticket, you must print the ticket, gather proof of relationship, and submit a written application to the Station Manager at a reservation counter at least 24 hours before departure. The railway staff will verify the details and update the passenger name in their database.**

Since you cannot do this online, here is the exact offline process you need to follow:

1. **Print Your Ticket**: Get a physical printout of your confirmed ticket, whether it is an online e-ticket or a counter ticket.
2. **Collect Proof**: Bring a document showing how you are related to the new traveler. You can use an Aadhaar Card, Ration Card, Marriage Certificate, or Birth Certificate.
3. **Copy ID Cards**: Take photocopies of photo IDs for both yourself and the relative who will be traveling.
4. **Write an Application**: Draft a simple letter to the Station Manager or Chief Reservation Supervisor asking for the name change. Explain why you cannot travel.
5. **Visit the Counter**: Go to the nearest computerized reservation counter (PRS counter) at any major railway station.
6. **Get Approval**: Submit your papers. The officer will verify the relation and sign off on it.
7. **Name Change**: The railway staff will update the passenger name in the reservation system. The new name will print on the final chart.

## Can you transfer a train ticket to a friend?

**No, you cannot transfer a confirmed train ticket to a friend under any circumstances. Indian Railways strictly bans transferring tickets to friends or unrelated people to stop travel agents and brokers from buying seats in bulk and reselling them at higher rates.**

If your friend wants to travel, they must buy their own ticket. Do not let your friend travel under your name. The TTE checks photo IDs on the train. If they catch someone else traveling on your ticket, the TTE will treat them as a ticketless passenger. They will have to pay the full fare, a ₹250 fine, and could face legal action. The only legal route is to cancel your ticket and let your friend book a new one.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many times can a train ticket be transferred?

You can only transfer a train ticket once. Once the railway supervisor approves the change and updates the database, the ticket details are locked. You cannot request a second transfer for the same ticket.

### Can I transfer an RAC or waiting list ticket?

No. The transfer option is only for confirmed tickets. If your ticket is in RAC or waiting list status, you cannot transfer it. You will have to cancel the ticket and let the other person book a fresh one.

### Can you transfer a train ticket online?

No. There is no option to transfer a ticket online on the IRCTC website or app. You have to physically visit a computerized railway reservation counter to submit your request and documents.

### What documents are required for ticket transfer?

You need the confirmed ticket printout, a written application letter, copies of photo IDs for both passengers, and official relationship proof like a ration card or Aadhaar card.
