## What is the Shatabdi Express and Its Key Features?

**Shatabdi Express trains are a series of premium, fast daytime intercity passenger trains operated by Indian Railways. Designed for rapid transit, they connect metropolitan capitals with commercial hubs within a 300 to 700-kilometer radius. These fully air-conditioned trains feature modern chair cars, onboard hospitality, high track priority, and same-day turnaround schedules.**

Shatabdi trains are among the most reliable options for daytime train travel in India. Unlike long-distance sleeper trains, Shatabdis operate entirely during the day, returning to their originating station on the same day.

### Modern Passenger Amenities

- **Seating Classes:** Shatabdis feature two main classes: AC Chair Car (CC) with 3×2 seating and Executive Chair Car (EC) with plush 2×2 seating, extra legroom, and enhanced catering menus.
- **Priority on Tracks:** Shatabdis enjoy top priority across the Indian Railways network, trailing only Rajdhani and Vande Bharat trains, resulting in minimal signal halts and high punctuality.
- **Onboard Hospitality:** Every passenger receives complimentary newspapers, 1-liter Rail Neer packaged water bottles, and hand sanitizers upon boarding, alongside full meal service.

## What Time Is Shatabdi Express Chart Prepared: Morning and Evening Schedules

**Shatabdi Express chart preparation occurs in two stages: the first chart is prepared at 21:00 IST the previous evening for morning departures leaving before 14:00, or 4 hours prior for afternoon return services. The second chart is finalized 30 minutes before departure, permanently locking seat allocations and closing online Current Availability booking.**

Because virtually all Shatabdi services operate on daytime intercity corridors, understanding the exact charting schedule is critical for commuters tracking waitlists or seeking last-minute seats:

### 1. Morning Shatabdi Departures (The 21:00 Night Rule)

Because the majority of Shatabdi trains depart their origin stations early in the morning (between 05:00 AM and 07:45 AM):

- **First Chart Preparation:** Indian Railways finalizes the first reservation chart the **previous evening between 20:00 and 21:00 IST**.
- **Quota Release:** Unused VIP, administrative emergency, and foreign tourist quotas are released overnight to clear general waiting list passengers.
- **Notification:** Waitlisted passengers receive confirmation SMS alerts with assigned coach and seat numbers (e.g., `C4-32` or `E1-18`) before bedtime.

### 2. Afternoon and Return Shatabdi Departures

For return services departing their turnaround stations in the late afternoon or evening:

- **First Chart Preparation:** Finalized **4 hours before departure**, typically between 11:30 AM and 13:00 IST on the day of travel.
- **Current Reservation Opening:** Any unsold seats or unallocated quota quotas open on IRCTC and [LastBerth Chart Vacancy](/chart-vacancy) under **Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`)** at a statutory 10% base fare discount.

### 3. Second and Final Chart Preparation

The second chart is finalized **30 minutes to 15 minutes before scheduled train departure**:

- Last-minute passenger cancellations are recorded.
- Online booking on IRCTC and ticket counter booking at PRS windows officially close.
- All waitlisted e-tickets that remain unconfirmed are automatically cancelled by CRIS, triggering a 100% refund.

## What are the Booking Rules and Tatkal Timings for Shatabdi Express?

**Booking rules for Shatabdi Express follow the standard 60-day advance reservation period on IRCTC. Shatabdi Express Tatkal timings open one day before departure at 10:00 AM strictly for AC classes (CC and EC). No senior citizen concessions apply on Shatabdi trains, with discounts restricted to eligible Divyangjan passenger categories.**

Planning a Shatabdi journey requires understanding these essential booking parameters:

- **Advance Reservation Period (ARP):** You can book seats up to **60 days** before the journey date starting at 08:00 AM IST daily on the IRCTC portal and PRS counters.
- **Shatabdi Express Tatkal Timings:** Tatkal bookings open **1 day prior** to the train's scheduled departure from its originating station. Since all Shatabdi coaches are AC (Chair Car and Executive Chair Car), booking opens strictly at **10:00 AM IST**. There is no 11:00 AM non-AC booking window for Shatabdi trains.
- **Dynamic Aadhaar OTP:** During the initial 10:00 AM Tatkal booking rush, IRCTC enforces mandatory Aadhaar OTP authentication. Save your co-passengers to your IRCTC Master List 24 hours in advance to autofill names within seconds.
- **Concession Restrictions:** Indian Railways does not offer senior citizen fare concessions on Shatabdi Express trains. Concessions are strictly limited to authorized railway duty passes and certified Divyangjan disability categories.

## Are Onboard Catering and Food Options Included in Shatabdi Express Fares?

**Yes, onboard catering is included in Shatabdi Express ticket fares, featuring breakfast, lunch, high tea, or dinner depending on journey timings. However, passengers can opt out of meals during booking to reduce ticket costs. Opting in onboard incurs an additional service fee of ₹50 per meal.**

Catering remains a signature aspect of the Shatabdi travel experience. Here is how the catering policy works:

1. **Catering Opt-Out Option:** During ticket booking on IRCTC, you can uncheck the food option to reduce your total fare by removing the bundled catering tariff (saving between ₹185 and ₹275 depending on class and journey length).
2. **Onboard Food Requests:** If you opt out during booking but later request meals onboard from the pantry car staff, you must pay the regular meal charge plus a mandatory **service charge of ₹50 per meal**.
3. **Menu Selection:** Passengers choose between vegetarian and non-vegetarian menus during booking. Executive Class passengers receive expanded multi-course spreads with premium branded beverages and confectionery.

Because of their immense popularity, Shatabdi trains regularly show long waiting lists on weekends.

### Understanding Waitlist Mechanics: No RAC on Shatabdi

The abbreviation WL stands for Waiting List. On standard sleeper trains, tickets progress through three stages:

$$\text{WL (Waiting List)} \longrightarrow \text{RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation)} \longrightarrow \text{Confirmed (CNF)}$$

However, because Shatabdi Express trains consist exclusively of seated chair cars (CC and EC) without berths, **no RAC category exists on Shatabdi trains**. Tickets advance directly from the waiting list to a fully confirmed individual chair:

$$\text{WL (Waiting List)} \longrightarrow \text{CNF (Confirmed Seat)}$$

If your online e-ticket does not confirm after final chart preparation, IRCTC automatically cancels it and refunds 100% of the fare. Traveling with an unconfirmed e-ticket is strictly prohibited; under Section 138 of the Railways Act, passengers face a minimum **₹500 penalty plus single fare**.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### When is Shatabdi Express chart prepared?

For early morning Shatabdi departures leaving before 14:00 (such as 12002 Bhopal Shatabdi or 12004 Lucknow Shatabdi), the first reservation chart is prepared the previous evening at **21:00 IST**. For afternoon return services, the first chart is prepared 4 hours before departure (around 11:30 AM to 13:00 IST). The second chart is finalized 30 minutes before departure.

### When does Tatkal booking open for Shatabdi Express?

Because Shatabdi Express is a fully air-conditioned train consisting of CC and EC classes, Tatkal bookings open at **10:00 AM IST** daily, exactly 1 day before the train departs from its originating station.

### Does Shatabdi Express have RAC after chart preparation?

No, Shatabdi Express trains do not have RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) status because all coaches are seated chair cars without sleeper berths. Waiting list tickets move directly from Waiting List (WL) to Confirmed (CNF) status upon passenger cancellations.

### What is a current available ticket in Shatabdi Express?

A Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`) ticket is a 100% confirmed seat that goes on sale online and at PRS counters after the first reservation chart is prepared. These seats carry a statutory 10% discount on base fares and remain bookable until 15 to 30 minutes before train departure.

### Is food free on the Shatabdi Express?

Food is not free; catering charges are included in the ticket fare by default. However, passengers can opt out of meals during booking to reduce their overall ticket cost. Requesting food onboard after opting out incurs an additional ₹50 service fee per meal.

### Can we travel on a waitlisted ticket on Shatabdi?

No, passengers holding waitlisted e-tickets cannot board Shatabdi Express trains. Waitlisted e-tickets are automatically cancelled and refunded by IRCTC after chart preparation. Boarding with an unconfirmed ticket carries a flat ₹500 fine under Section 138 of the Railways Act.

### Which train is number 12002?

Train 12002 is the New Delhi to Rani Kamlapati (Bhopal) Shatabdi Express, departing New Delhi at 06:00 AM IST. Its first reservation chart is prepared at 21:00 IST the previous evening. Its return service runs as Train 12001.

### What is the New Delhi to Amritsar Shatabdi train number?

The New Delhi to Amritsar Swarna Shatabdi runs as **12029** (New Delhi to Amritsar, departing 07:20 AM) and **12030** (Amritsar to New Delhi, departing 16:50 PM). Train 12029 charts at 21:00 IST the night before, while Train 12030 charts at 12:50 PM on departure day.

### Which is the New Delhi to Chandigarh Shatabdi?

The New Delhi to Chandigarh Shatabdi runs as **12045** (New Delhi to Chandigarh, departing 19:15 PM) and **12046** (Chandigarh to New Delhi, departing 12:05 PM). Train 12045 charts at 15:15 IST, while Train 12046 charts at 08:05 IST on departure morning.

### What happens if my Shatabdi ticket is still waitlisted after charting?

If your e-ticket remains on the waiting list after final chart preparation, IRCTC automatically cancels the ticket and refunds 100% of the fare back to your bank account within 3 to 5 business days with zero clerkage deductions.
