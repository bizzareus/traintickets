## What is the Saryu-Kamala Express (15595/15596) and When Does It Start?

**The Saryu-Kamala Express (Trains 15595 and 15596) is a weekly cross-border passenger service connecting Ayodhya Cantt in Uttar Pradesh with Janakpur Dham in Nepal. Jointly flagged off on November 23, 2026, regular commercial departures begin on November 28, 2026 from Janakpur and November 30, 2026 from Ayodhya.**

Named after the sacred Saryu river flowing through Ayodhya and the Kamala river basin in Mithila, the service directly links the birthplace of Lord Ram with the birthplace of Goddess Sita. Spanning approximately 604 kilometers, the corridor eliminates the multi-hop bus and rail transfers that pilgrims previously endured across the Uttar Pradesh and Bihar border.

Regular commercial operations run once a week in each direction using a modern 17-coach LHB (Linke Hofmann Busch) rake capable of carrying around 1,158 passengers. Because Indian Railways operates the service under a bilateral rail agreement with Nepal, cross-border scheduling accounts for dedicated security and customs halts at Jaynagar.

## What Are the Exact Timings and Weekly Schedule of Trains 15596 and 15595?

**Train 15596 departs Ayodhya Cantt every Monday at 5:50 PM IST and reaches Janakpur Dham on Tuesday at 12:40 PM Nepal Standard Time (NST). In the return direction, Train 15595 leaves Janakpur Dham every Saturday at 1:10 PM NST and arrives at Ayodhya Cantt on Sunday at 8:10 AM IST.**

## Which Stations Does the Saryu-Kamala Express Stop At?

Major junctions along the 604 km route include Ayodhya Dham, Mankapur, Basti, Gorakhpur, Narkatiaganj, Raxaul, Sitamarhi, Shisho, Madhubani, and the Jaynagar border checkpoint.**

Serving both spiritual pilgrims and regional Mithilanchal commuters, the route connects eastern Uttar Pradesh with northern Bihar's major railheads:

## Who Can Travel and What Passport or ID Rules Apply?

**Travel on the Saryu-Kamala Express is initially restricted exclusively to citizens of India and Nepal due to border checkpoint infrastructure at Jaynagar. While a formal passport or visa is not strictly mandatory under the open-border treaty, carrying a valid government photo ID like a Passport or Voter ID Card is compulsory.**

Unlike the Bandhan Express to Bangladesh, which requires a stamped passport and visa, Indo-Nepal rail travel follows the bilateral open-border framework. However, strict identity checks are enforced on every trip:

- **Eligible Nationalities:** Only Indian and Nepalese citizens can currently book tickets and cross the border on Trains 15595 and 15596. Third-country foreign nationals cannot use this service until full international immigration counters are expanded across designated halts.
- **Accepted Identification:** You must carry an original, physical **Passport** or **Voter ID Card** (EPIC). Security personnel from the Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB) and Nepal border officials verify documents physically during the Jaynagar halt. Digital screenshots without an original government ID risk denial of border transit.
- **Luggage & Customs:** Keep baggage within standard Indian Railways class limits (40 kg for SL, 40 kg for 3A/3E, 50 kg for 2A) and avoid carrying restricted commercial quantities of goods across the international boundary.

## How Do You Book Saryu-Kamala Express Tickets and What Quotas Are Missing?

**Tickets for the Saryu-Kamala Express are bookable online via the official IRCTC website and Rail Connect app, or at computerized PRS railway counters, within the standard 60-day Advance Reservation Period. Crucially, Indian Railways issues only confirmed tickets on this train, with zero RAC, Tatkal, or Premium Tatkal quotas.**

Understanding these booking restrictions prevents last-minute surprises during peak festival and pilgrimage seasons:

1. **No Tatkal or Premium Tatkal:** You cannot rely on 10:00 AM AC or 11:00 AM Sleeper Tatkal windows for Trains 15595/15596. All bookings happen strictly under the general Advance Reservation Period (up to 60 days ahead).
2. **No RAC Buffer:** Because international manifests require fixed passenger counts for border verification, **RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation)** is disabled. Tickets are either issued as Confirmed (CNF) or placed directly on Waiting List.
3. **Fare Structure:** Official class-wise rupee tariffs for SL, 3E, 3A, and 2A are finalized by Railway Board commercial circulars ahead of commercial launch (November 28 and 30, 2026). Check live class fares directly on the IRCTC portal when selecting your journey date, and remember that 5% GST applies to AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E) while Sleeper (SL) remains GST-exempt.
4. **Night Maintenance Blackout:** Remember that IRCTC servers shut down daily from **11:45 PM to 12:20 AM IST** for database reconciliation, during which no bookings or cancellations work.

## What Are the Chart Preparation Times and Penalty Rules?

**The first reservation chart for the Saryu-Kamala Express is prepared roughly 8 to 10 hours before scheduled departure, with the second and final chart locked 30 to 45 minutes prior to departure. Under the Jan Vishwas Act 2026, boarding reserved coaches with an unconfirmed e-ticket incurs a flat ₹500 Section 138 penalty.**

For Monday evening departures from Ayodhya Cantt (5:50 PM IST), expect the first reservation chart to finalize around 8:00 AM to 10:00 AM on Monday morning. For Saturday afternoon departures from Janakpur (1:10 PM NST), the first chart prepares the previous evening between 8:00 PM and 9:00 PM.

Follow these compliance rules on travel day:

- **Auto-Cancellation of Waitlisted E-Tickets:** Any online IRCTC ticket remaining unconfirmed after final chart preparation cancels automatically, with the fare refunded to your bank within 3 to 5 working days. You cannot board any coach on an auto-cancelled e-ticket.
- **Section 138 Fine:** Boarding a reserved Sleeper or AC coach without a valid confirmed ticket triggers a flat **₹500 fine** plus the single fare difference from the originating station under Section 138 of the Railways Act.
- **Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`):** If unallocated berths remain after the first chart is prepared, IRCTC releases them as 100% confirmed `CURR_AVBL` seats at a **10% discount on the basic fare** up to 30 minutes before departure.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### What are the train numbers for the Saryu-Kamala Express?

The Saryu-Kamala Express operates as Train **15596** from Ayodhya Cantt (India) to Janakpur Dham (Nepal), and as Train **15595** in the return direction from Janakpur Dham to Ayodhya Cantt.

### On which days does the Ayodhya to Janakpur train run?

Train 15596 departs Ayodhya Cantt every **Monday at 5:50 PM IST**, reaching Janakpur Dham on Tuesday at 12:40 PM NST. Return Train 15595 departs Janakpur Dham every **Saturday at 1:10 PM NST**, arriving at Ayodhya Cantt on Sunday at 8:10 AM IST.

### Do Indian citizens need a passport or visa for the Saryu-Kamala Express?

Indian and Nepalese citizens do not need a visa under the bilateral open-border treaty, and a passport is not strictly mandatory if you carry another accepted national ID. However, carrying an original **Passport or Voter ID Card** is compulsory for physical verification by border security at Jaynagar.

### Can foreign tourists book tickets on the Saryu-Kamala Express?

No. During its initial launch phase, the Saryu-Kamala Express is open exclusively to citizens of India and Nepal because full international immigration clearance facilities are currently limited to select border checkpoints like Jaynagar.

### Is Tatkal or RAC available on the Saryu-Kamala Express?

No. Neither Tatkal, Premium Tatkal, nor RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) quotas are available on Trains 15595 and 15596. IRCTC issues only confirmed tickets (or waitlist) within the standard 60-day Advance Reservation Period.

### How long does the Saryu-Kamala Express take to reach Janakpur from Ayodhya?

The 604 km journey takes roughly **18.5 to 19 hours**, including a scheduled 1-to-2-hour border verification halt at Jaynagar Station and accounting for the 15-minute time difference between IST and NST.

### Which coach classes are available on the Saryu-Kamala Express?

The 17-coach modern LHB rake features **AC 2-Tier (2A), AC 3-Tier (3A), AC 3-Tier Economy (3E), and non-AC Sleeper (SL)** coaches, totaling roughly 1,158 passenger berths.

### When do regular commercial bookings start for the Saryu-Kamala Express?

Following the November 23, 2026 inauguration, regular commercial service starts on **November 28, 2026** from Janakpur Dham (15595) and **November 30, 2026** from Ayodhya Cantt (15596).
