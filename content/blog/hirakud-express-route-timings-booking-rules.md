## What Is Hirakud Express and Which Route Does It Cover?

**Hirakud Express is an essential interstate superfast express train running between Visakhapatnam Junction in Andhra Pradesh and Amritsar Junction in Punjab.** Spanning **2,570 kilometers across 8 Indian states**, the service bridges coastal Odisha, the mineral heartlands of Chhattisgarh and Madhya Pradesh, and major northern transit hubs including Jhansi, Agra, New Delhi, Ambala, and Ludhiana.

Named after the historic Hirakud Dam on the Mahanadi River near Sambalpur, this long-distance service plays a vital cultural and economic role. It links the eastern seaboard directly to the Golden Temple city of Amritsar.

The train operates tri-weekly in each direction:

- **Train 20807:** Departs Visakhapatnam on Tuesdays, Fridays, and Saturdays at 00:25 IST, arriving in Amritsar at 23:25 IST the following day (total travel time: 47 hours).
- **Train 20808:** Departs Amritsar on Wednesdays, Saturdays, and Sundays at 23:55 IST, reaching Visakhapatnam at 21:50 IST on Day 3 (total travel time: 45 hours 55 minutes).

Because the train touches prominent pilgrimage sites, industrial belts, and state capitals, passenger demand remains heavy throughout the year.

## When Is the Reservation Chart Prepared for Hirakud Express?

**For Train 20807 departing Visakhapatnam at 00:25 midnight, the First Reservation Chart is prepared the previous afternoon between 14:20 and 20:00 IST.** For Train 20808 departing Amritsar at 23:55, the First Chart is finalized around 15:55 IST on the departure day. The Second Chart finalizes 30 minutes prior to departure at both origins.

Understanding the difference between early-morning and night charting is critical for waitlisted passengers. Under Indian Railways charting guidelines:

1. **Early Morning Departures (00:01 to 14:00 IST):** Because Train 20807 departs at 00:25, CRIS charting runs on the preceding day. Waiting list confirmations, quota transfers, and seat allocations are completed hours before passengers arrive at the station.
2. **Late Night Departures (after 14:00 IST):** For Train 20808 departing Amritsar at 23:55, charting takes place approximately 8 hours prior on the same calendar day.
3. **Remote Charting at Intermediate Junctions:** Because the journey takes nearly two days, major intermediate stations (such as Sambalpur, Bilaspur, Katni Murwara, Jhansi, and New Delhi) operate under pooled quotas (RLWL or PQWL). Remote charts for these stations are generated 4 to 8 hours before the train's scheduled arrival at that specific station.

## How Does Tatkal Booking Work for Hirakud Express?

**Tatkal booking for Hirakud Express opens at 10:00 AM IST for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E) and 11:00 AM IST for Sleeper class (SL) one day prior to departure from the originating station.** Because the train operates over two days, intermediate passengers must calculate opening dates from the train's origin date, not their boarding date.

This rule catches many passengers off guard:

- **The Origin Departure Rule:** If you want to board Train 20807 from New Delhi to Amritsar on Day 2 (Thursday), you cannot book Tatkal on Wednesday morning. The train started from Visakhapatnam on Wednesday at 00:25 midnight. Its Tatkal window opened on Tuesday morning at 10:00 AM. Waiting until Wednesday guarantees that Tatkal quotas are completely sold out.
- **Tatkal Quota Quirk:** Tatkal quotas on long-distance trains like Hirakud Express are heavily allocated to the full origin-to-destination stretch. Intermediate legs share smaller pooled quotas (TQWL).
- **Payment Readiness:** Keep your IRCTC Master List pre-saved and verified with Aadhaar. Tatkal seats on this route frequently sell out within 90 seconds.

## What Food and Pantry Facilities Are Available on Hirakud Express?

**Hirakud Express features an attached onboard pantry car that prepares standard hot vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals, snacks, and beverages throughout its 47-hour run.** Catering charges are not bundled into the ticket fare, allowing passengers to purchase meals directly from IRCTC pantry staff or order via the IRCTC e-Catering portal.

Because the journey spans two full days and nights, meals follow a predictable onboard cycle:

- **Morning (06:30 to 08:30 IST):** Tea (₹10), Coffee (₹15), and Breakfast choices including Veg Cutlet with bread (₹40) or Omelette with bread (₹50).
- **Lunch (12:00 to 14:00 IST):** Standard Veg Thali (₹80) containing rice, dal, seasonal sabzi, two parathas or four puris, and pickle. Egg Thali is ₹90 and Chicken Thali is ₹130.
- **Evening (16:30 to 18:00 IST):** Samosas, tea, packaged biscuits, and snacks.
- **Dinner (19:30 to 21:30 IST):** Thali options identical to lunch.
- **Rail Neer Packaged Drinking Water:** Strictly ₹15 for a 1-liter bottle. Do not pay over the printed MRP.

In addition to the pantry car, passengers can order hot food from verified restaurant brands via the IRCTC e-Catering app (or dial 1323) delivered directly to their seat at major halts:

- **Bhubaneswar (BBS) and Sambalpur (SBP):** Ideal for regional Odia meals and breakfast.
- **Bilaspur (BSP):** Excellent for evening dinner on Day 1.
- **Katni Murwara (KMZ) and Jhansi (VGLJ):** Good delivery hubs for early breakfast and morning snacks on Day 2.
- **New Delhi (NDLS) and Ambala Cantt (UMB):** Wide selection of North Indian thalis and branded dining options.

If onboard pantry vendors attempt to overcharge for meals or water bottles, lodge an immediate complaint on the **RailMadad portal (dial 139)**.

## What Are the Best Alternatives If Hirakud Express Is Waitlisted?

**When direct tickets on Hirakud Express enter REGRET or high waitlist queues, you can secure confirmed travel using contiguous split ticketing on the same train or booking via origin station quotas.** Contiguous split ticketing through LastBerth finds two back-to-back confirmed seats on the exact same rake without requiring you to switch trains.

Here are the four most effective confirmed seat rescue strategies:

### 1. Smart Split Ticketing on the Same Train

Because Hirakud Express traverses 2,570 km, different portions of the train empty and fill along the journey. A berth might be occupied from Visakhapatnam to Bilaspur, but sit completely vacant from Bilaspur to New Delhi.

Instead of waiting for a direct end-to-end waitlist ticket to confirm:

- Leg 1: Book Seat A from Visakhapatnam to Bilaspur (Confirmed).
- Leg 2: Book Seat B from Bilaspur to New Delhi on the same train (Confirmed).

You remain on the exact same train. At Bilaspur, you simply move your luggage to your second confirmed berth. Use the [LastBerth Smart Seats Finder](/) to automatically uncover these split combinations.

### 2. Origin Station Quota Booking with Boarding Point Modification

IRCTC allocates 70% to 80% of all berths to the General Quota (GNWL), which is strictly reserved for passengers booking from the originating station (Visakhapatnam or Amritsar). Intermediate stations receive smaller Remote Location Quotas (RLWL) that enter waitlist status rapidly.

If your booking from Sambalpur to New Delhi shows `RLWL 45` (unlikely to confirm):

1. Check availability from **Visakhapatnam (VSKP)** to New Delhi. If seats are available in the general quota, book from Visakhapatnam.
2. During booking, set your **Boarding Station** to **Sambalpur (SBP)**.
3. You pay the fare difference from Visakhapatnam, but you lock in a 100% confirmed berth. The reservation system protects your seat until you board at Sambalpur.

### 3. Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`) Post-Charting Berths

After the First Reservation Chart is prepared (around 14:20 IST for 20807, or 15:55 IST for 20808), any unallocated emergency quotas, vacant berths, or cancelled tickets are released as **Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`)**.

These berths carry a **10% discount on the basic fare** and can be booked directly on the IRCTC app or website until 15 minutes before departure (or final second chart preparation). Check [Chart Vacancy](/chart-vacancy) right after chart preparation to spot available seats.

### 4. Parallel Train Options on Key Corridors

If Hirakud Express is completely sold out, parallel services offer excellent alternative connections:

- **Visakhapatnam to New Delhi:** AP Express (20805/20806) or Samta Express (12807/12808).
- **Bhubaneswar to New Delhi:** Purushottam Express (12801), Rajdhani Express (22823), or Nandankanan Express (12815).
- **New Delhi to Amritsar:** Vande Bharat Express (22487), Shatabdi Express (12013/12029), or Shan-e-Punjab Express (12497).

## Can You Travel on Waiting List Tickets in Hirakud Express?

**No, passengers holding an unconfirmed online e-ticket cannot board reserved coaches on Hirakud Express under any circumstances.** If your e-ticket remains on the waiting list after final chart preparation, IRCTC automatically cancels it and refunds the fare directly to your original payment method.

Under the **Railways Act (Section 138)** as updated by the **Jan Vishwas Act 2026**:

1. **Auto-Cancellation of E-Tickets:** Once the final chart is released, waitlisted e-tickets become invalid travel documents. Your name does not appear on the onboard Ticket Examiner's chart.
2. **Section 138 Penalties:** If you board a Sleeper or AC coach on an auto-cancelled e-ticket or a general unreserved ticket, the Travelling Ticket Examiner (TTE) will treat you as traveling without a valid ticket. You will face a **flat ₹500 penalty plus the full single fare difference from the train's originating station**, and you will be deboarded at the next scheduled halt.
3. **Physical Counter PRS Tickets:** Passengers holding a physical paper waitlist ticket bought directly from a railway station PRS counter are permitted to board unreserved General coaches (GS) only. They cannot occupy reserved berths in Sleeper or AC classes unless the TTE officially re-allots a vacant berth onboard.

Always verify your PNR status before heading to the station. If your ticket remains unconfirmed, consider alternative trains or search for post-chart vacant seats.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### What days does Hirakud Express operate in each direction?

Train 20807 departs Visakhapatnam on Tuesdays, Fridays, and Saturdays at 00:25 IST. In the return direction, Train 20808 departs Amritsar on Wednesdays, Saturdays, and Sundays at 23:55 IST.

### When does the chart get prepared for 20807 departing at midnight?

Because Train 20807 departs Visakhapatnam at 00:25 midnight, its first reservation chart is prepared the previous afternoon between 14:20 and 20:00 IST. The final second chart is prepared around 23:55 IST, approximately 30 minutes before departure.

### How much does a 3A ticket cost on Hirakud Express from Visakhapatnam to Amritsar?

A standard AC 3-Tier (3A) ticket from Visakhapatnam to Amritsar costs approximately ₹2,440. This includes the basic distance fare of ₹2,263, a ₹45 superfast charge, a ₹40 reservation fee, and 5% GST.

### Is food free on Hirakud Express?

No, meals and snacks are not free on Hirakud Express. Because it is a Superfast express rather than a Rajdhani, catering charges are not included in the ticket fare. Hot meals, breakfast, and snacks can be purchased directly from the attached pantry car or ordered via IRCTC e-Catering.

### What is the difference between 3A and 3E coaches on Hirakud Express?

Standard 3A coaches feature 72 berths with two tiers on the side bay (Side Lower and Side Upper). The 3E (AC 3 Economy) coaches carry 83 berths by adding a Side Middle berth, resulting in slightly less vertical headroom and narrower aisle space, but at an approximately 6% cheaper fare.

### Can I book Tatkal for Hirakud Express from an intermediate station like New Delhi?

Yes, but you must remember that Tatkal opens one day prior to the train's origin departure date from Visakhapatnam (for 20807) or Amritsar (for 20808). If the train arrives in New Delhi on Day 2, Tatkal booking will have opened on Day 0 from the origin date, not Day 1.

### Does Hirakud Express stop at New Delhi or Hazrat Nizamuddin?

Hirakud Express halts at both major capital terminals. In both directions, the train stops at Hazrat Nizamuddin (NZM) for 2 minutes and at New Delhi Railway Station (NDLS) for 15 minutes.

### What happens if my Hirakud Express waitlist e-ticket does not confirm?

If your waitlisted e-ticket does not confirm after final chart preparation, IRCTC automatically cancels it and issues a 100% full refund (with zero cancellation or clerkage fee deducted) to your original payment source within 3 to 5 business days. You cannot board reserved coaches with this ticket.

### Can I get confirmed seats on Hirakud Express after chart preparation?

Yes. Any unallocated berths or last-minute cancellations are released as Current Availability (`CURR_AVBL`) after the first chart is prepared. These berths carry a 10% discount on the base fare and can be booked online via IRCTC or at PRS counters until 15 minutes before departure.

Hirakud Express (20807/20808) is one of Indian Railways' longest and most vital interstate connections, linking 8 states across 2,570 km. Because the service runs tri-weekly and passes through major industrial and tourist junctions, direct tickets often sell out weeks in advance.
