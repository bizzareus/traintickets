## Which Festival Special Trains Has North Central Railway Announced for 2026?

**North Central Railway has notified over 1,000 Trains on Demand and festival special trips across Kanpur, Prayagraj, Agra, and Jhansi divisions for the 2026 autumn festive rush. Operating from October 1 to November 30, these services link Uttar Pradesh with major industrial and employment hubs in Gujarat, Maharashtra, West Bengal, and Karnataka.**

Every autumn, millions of passengers travel through the North Central Railway network to celebrate Durga Puja, Diwali, and Chhath Puja. Because the Northern trunk corridor connecting Delhi, Punjab, and Haryana to Bihar and West Bengal passes directly through Kanpur and Prayagraj, regular services like the Prayagraj Express, Shram Shakti Express, and Purushottam Express sell out within seconds of reservation opening.

To absorb this overflow, North Central Railway (headquartered at Prayagraj) operates an extensive fleet of supplementary 0-series trains. These trains carry special five-digit numbers beginning with zero (such as 04105 or 04157) and operate across the zone's three divisions: Prayagraj, Agra, and Virangana Lakshmibai Jhansi.

To manage severe platform congestion, North Central Railway routes special departures through strategic satellite terminals:
- **Subedarganj (`SFG`):** Located 4 km west of Prayagraj Junction, handling Western Railway specials bound for Surat, Valsad, and Mumbai.
- **Prayagraj Junction (`PRYJ`):** Primary transit terminal for high-capacity long-distance festival services to Gujarat and Maharashtra.
- **Prayagraj Chheoki (`PCOI`):** Situated 10 km south across the Yamuna River, managing bypass routes connecting Mumbai and Jabalpur to Patna and Howrah.
- **Kanpur Central (`CNB`):** Major railway hub serving East-bound festival expresses to Kolkata and South-bound trains to Bengaluru.
- **Govindpuri (`GOY`):** Satellite junction 3 km west of Kanpur Central, handling specials running between Jhansi and Lucknow without loco reversals.
- **Agra Cantt (`AGC`) & Virangana Lakshmibai Jhansi (`VGLJ`):** Key feeder junctions connecting central Uttar Pradesh and Bundelkhand to Bengal and Gujarat.

## What Is the Satellite Station Difference: Subedarganj vs Prayagraj and Govindpuri vs Kanpur?

**Subedarganj (code: `SFG`) is located 4 km west of Prayagraj Junction, while Govindpuri (code: `GOY`) sits 3 km west of Kanpur Central. Indian Railways originates special trains from these secondary satellite yards to avoid heavy yard gridlock, platform shortages, and locomotive turn-around delays at the main heritage junctions.**

Missing a festival train due to terminal confusion is one of the most common travel disasters in Uttar Pradesh. When booking NCR festival specials, double-check the exact boarding station code printed on your e-ticket:

1. **Subedarganj (`SFG`) vs Prayagraj Junction (`PRYJ`):** Subedarganj has its own dedicated platform complex, reservation counters, and direct road access from the Grand Trunk Road. If your ticket specifies `SFG`, do not go to Prayagraj Junction. Navigating city traffic between the two stations during festive evenings can take over 45 minutes.
2. **Prayagraj Chheoki (`PCOI`) vs Prayagraj Junction (`PRYJ`):** Chheoki is situated on the southern bank of the Yamuna River, roughly 10 km from the main city centre. It serves trains running on the Jabalpur-Manikpur-Pt Deen Dayal Upadhyaya bypass route that skip Prayagraj Junction entirely.
3. **Govindpuri (`GOY`) vs Kanpur Central (`CNB`):** Govindpuri handles trains operating along the Jhansi-Banda-Lucknow corridor. Stopping trains at Govindpuri eliminates the 40-minute engine reversal process required inside Kanpur Central yard, saving vital operational time on congested routes.

Always verify your station code on your SMS or electronic reservation slip at least three hours before departure.

## When Does Booking Open for NCR Festival Specials, and How Do Tatkal Windows Work?

**Online ticket booking for NCR festival special trains opens at 8:00 AM IST on the IRCTC website and Rail Connect app on a rolling 10 to 30-day schedule prior to each train's departure date. Tatkal reservations open one day before travel from the originating station at 10:00 AM for AC classes and 11:00 AM for Sleeper.**

Because festival trains are announced in phases as passenger demand rises, their reservation schedules do not always follow the standard 60-day Advance Reservation Period (ARP). Keep these critical booking rules in mind:

1. **Daily Opening Timing:** New quotas go live at exactly **8:00 AM IST**. To secure confirmed berths on high-demand routes like Kanpur to Kolkata or Subedarganj to Surat, log in by 7:55 AM with pre-saved passenger profiles in your IRCTC Master List.
2. **Aadhaar Authentication Rule:** Under current anti-bot regulations, online bookings during opening peak hours require Aadhaar-authenticated user accounts. Ensure your mobile number is linked to your IRCTC profile before the morning rush.
3. **Tatkal Quota Windows:** AC Tatkal (2A, 3A, 3E) opens at **10:00 AM IST**, while Sleeper Tatkal opens at **11:00 AM IST** one day prior to departure from the originating station. Bookings are capped at 2 Tatkal PNRs per user per day.
4. **Night Maintenance Blackout:** Remember that IRCTC servers shut down daily for scheduled maintenance from **11:45 PM to 12:20 AM IST (23:45 to 00:20)**. All bookings, cancellations, and PNR inquiries are offline during these 35 minutes.

## What Are the Cancellation and Refund Rules for NCR Festival Special Trains?

**Cancellation refunds on NCR festival special trains depend on when the cancellation is requested before scheduled departure. A flat clerkage fee applies up to 48 hours before journey time, 25% fare deduction between 48 and 12 hours, and 50% deduction between 12 and 4 hours. Unconfirmed waitlisted e-tickets are auto-cancelled with a full refund.**

The cancellation slabs and refund mechanisms for 0-series festival special trains operate as follows:

| Cancellation Timing | Refund Deduction Slab | Applicable Deductions / Conditions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **More than 48 hours prior** | Flat clerkage fee per passenger | ₹240 for 2A, ₹180 for 3A/3E, ₹120 for SL, ₹60 for 2S (plus GST on AC) |
| **48 hours to 12 hours prior** | 25% of total fare deducted | Subject to minimum flat clerkage fee for the booked travel class |
| **12 hours to 4 hours prior** | 50% of total fare deducted | Permitted online up to first reservation chart preparation |
| **Less than 4 hours (Post charting)** | Zero refund on confirmed tickets | No refund permitted once the reservation chart is finalized |
| **Fully Waitlisted Online E-Ticket** | 100% automated refund | Automatically processed to original bank account; passenger cannot board |

Waitlisted e-ticket passengers must note that if their status remains in Waiting List (WL) after chart finalization, the ticket is automatically cancelled by the PRS system. Boarding a reserved Sleeper or AC coach on a cancelled waitlist ticket or general ticket attracts a flat **₹500 penalty under Section 138 of the Railways Act (Jan Vishwas Act 2026)** plus the single journey fare.

## Common Booking Questions (FAQ)

### What is the full form of NCR in Indian Railways?
NCR stands for North Central Railway, one of the 17 operational zones of Indian Railways, headquartered at Subedarganj in Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh.

### Why do festival special train numbers start with a zero?
Train numbers starting with zero designate temporary special services operating outside the standard railway timetable to manage holiday rushes, carrying Trains on Special Fare pricing.

### Are Subedarganj and Prayagraj Junction the same railway station?
No, Subedarganj (station code SFG) is an independent satellite station situated 4 km west of Prayagraj Junction (station code PRYJ) handling western and southern festival trains.

### Can I travel in a Sleeper coach with a general unreserved ticket during Diwali?
No, boarding a reserved Sleeper coach with a general or platform ticket is illegal and attracts a flat ₹500 fine under Section 138 of the Railways Act plus fare differences.

### Are concessions for senior citizens valid on NCR festival special trains?
No, Indian Railways does not offer senior citizen or student fare concessions on any 0-series festival special trains operating on special fares.

### What is the difference between Kanpur Central and Govindpuri?
Kanpur Central (CNB) is the primary junction terminal, whereas Govindpuri (GOY) is a satellite station 3 km west handling bypass routes toward Jhansi and Lucknow.

### What happens if my festival special e-ticket remains waitlisted after charting?
If an online e-ticket remains waitlisted after final chart preparation, the system cancels it automatically and refunds the full fare to your source bank account within 3 to 5 business days.

### When does Tatkal booking open for North Central Railway special trains?
Tatkal reservation opens at 10:00 AM IST for AC classes and 11:00 AM IST for Sleeper class one day prior to the train's departure from its originating station.

### How much extra fare is charged on Trains on Special Fare (TOSF)?
Indian Railways levies a 10% surcharge on Second Seating (2S) and a 30% surcharge on Sleeper and all AC classes over normal mail and express base fares.

### Can I book Current Availability tickets on NCR special trains?
Yes, vacant berths remaining after chart preparation are released under Current Availability (CURR_AVBL) at a 10% base fare discount until 30 minutes before departure.
