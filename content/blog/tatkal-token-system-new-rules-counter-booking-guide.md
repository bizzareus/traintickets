To eliminate overcrowding, queue pushing, and unauthorized booking agents at station reservation windows, Indian Railways has introduced a mandatory **Tatkal Token System** across major PRS booking counters in 2026. This systematic queue management policy ensures fair access for genuine passengers.

## TL;DR

The PRS counter Tatkal token system prevents queue jumping and ticket touting by issuing serialized, time-stamped biometric or physical tokens to passengers starting at 7:00 AM to 8:00 AM. Token holders are called sequentially when AC Tatkal (10:00 AM) and Non-AC Tatkal (11:00 AM) booking windows open.

---

## What Is the Tatkal Token System and How Does It Work at Railway Counters?

**The Tatkal Token System is a queue-control mechanism at railway PRS counters where passengers receive numbered physical tokens before Tatkal booking windows open. Tickets are issued strictly in order of token numbers, preventing unauthorized line jumping.**

Key operational highlights:
- **Biometric / QR Verification:** Passengers present a valid photo ID to receive a stamped token.
- **One Token Per Person:** One token allows ticket booking for a maximum of 4 passengers on a single requisition slip.
- **Anti-Agent Security:** Railway Protection Force (RPF) personnel monitor token distribution to stop touts.

---

## What Are the Official Token Distribution Timings for AC and Non-AC Tatkal?

**PRS counters distribute Tatkal tokens in two designated morning shifts: 8:30 AM to 9:00 AM for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, EC, CC), and 9:00 AM to 9:30 AM for Non-AC Sleeper (SL) class.**

Official schedule timeline:
- **8:30 AM – 9:00 AM:** AC class token issuance at counter.
- **10:00 AM IST:** AC Tatkal ticket booking window opens at counter.
- **9:00 AM – 9:30 AM:** Non-AC Sleeper class token issuance at counter.
- **11:00 AM IST:** Non-AC Tatkal ticket booking window opens at counter.

## What Documents Are Required to Get a Tatkal Token at Railway PRS Counters?

**To claim a Tatkal token, you must submit a fully filled Reservation Requisition Slip along with an original government-issued photo ID card matching the name on the ticket application.**

Accepted identification documents:
- **Aadhaar Card** (Physical or digital m-Aadhaar)
- **Voter ID Card**
- **Passport or Driving License**
- **PAN Card**

## Is Counter Tatkal Ticket Booking Faster Than Online IRCTC Master List Booking?

**While counter token booking offers physical access, online booking via the IRCTC app using a pre-saved Master List and UPI payment is faster and eliminates the need to stand in early morning counter queues.**

| Parameter | Counter Tatkal (Token System) | Online IRCTC Booking |
|---|---|---|
| **Wait Time** | 2 to 3 hours standing in queue | 2 to 5 minutes at computer/phone |
| **Verification** | Physical ID verification by RPF | Aadhaar KYC online verification |
| **Speed** | Dependent on counter clerk typing speed | One-tap Master List auto-fill |
| **Success Rate** | High for low token numbers (1–5) | High with fast network & pre-filled list |

## What Are the Cancellation and Refund Rules for Counter Tatkal Tokens?

**No refund is granted on cancellation of confirmed Tatkal tickets. For waitlisted (WL) or RAC Tatkal tickets, cancellation is allowed up to 30 minutes before train departure with flat clerkage fee deductions.**

Important cancellation rules:
- **Confirmed Tatkal:** Zero refund upon cancellation.
- **Waitlisted Tatkal (WL full form is Waiting List):** Automatic full refund minus ₹60 clerkage fee if ticket remains waitlisted after final chart preparation.
- **WL Progression Queue:** WL (Waiting List) → RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) → Confirmed.

## Tatkal Token System FAQ

### Can one person take multiple Tatkal tokens for different passengers?
No, only one token is issued per person present in the queue. You cannot claim tokens for absent family members or friends.

### What happens if you miss your token number call at the Tatkal counter?
If your token number is called and you are not present at the window, your token is invalidated, and the counter clerk proceeds to the next token holder.

### Does having a Tatkal token guarantee a confirmed train ticket?
No, a token only guarantees your position in the booking queue. If Tatkal seats sell out before your turn arrives, you will receive a waitlisted ticket or regret status.

### How many Tatkal forms can one token holder submit at the counter?
One token holder is permitted to submit only one Tatkal reservation requisition form for a maximum of 4 passengers per PNR.

### Is Aadhaar mandatory for counter Tatkal token booking?
Passengers or their representatives must produce a self-attested photocopy of the passenger valid government photo ID (such as Aadhaar) to receive a serialized Tatkal token.

### What time do PRS counter Tatkal booking counters open?
AC Tatkal counter bookings begin strictly at 10:00 AM, while Non-AC Sleeper Tatkal counter bookings start at 11:00 AM one day prior to train departure.

### Can you get a refund on a confirmed Tatkal counter ticket?
No, Indian Railways grants zero refund on cancellation of confirmed Tatkal tickets, except under specific railway-fault conditions like train cancellation or major route diversion.

### What is the advantage of PRS counter Tatkal over IRCTC online booking?
At dedicated computerized PRS windows with the token system, ticket requests are routed directly to the CRIS reservation server without internet gateway payment delays or website captcha timeouts.
