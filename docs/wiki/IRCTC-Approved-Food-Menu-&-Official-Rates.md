# 🍱 Official IRCTC Food Menu & Approved Rail Rates

To prevent food vendor overcharging on trains and platforms, Indian Railways and IRCTC publish **standard menu rates** for all Mail, Express, and Superfast trains.

---

## 📋 Standard Meal Price List (Mail / Express Trains)

The following prices are fixed by Indian Railways. Vendors in pantry cars or on platforms cannot charge above these MRPs:

| Food Item | Details / Portions | Approved Price |
|---|---|---|
| ☕ **Standard Tea** | 150ml (with dip tea bag) | **₹10** |
| ☕ **Standard Coffee** | 150ml | **₹10** |
| 💧 **Packaged Drinking Water (Rail Neer)** | 1 Litre bottle | **₹15** |
| 🥪 **Standard Breakfast (Veg)** | 2 Cutlets + 2 Bread Slices + Butter + Sauce | **₹40** |
| 🍳 **Standard Breakfast (Non-Veg)** | 2 Omelettes / Boiled Eggs + 2 Bread Slices | **₹50** |
| 🍱 **Standard Veg Meal (Thali)** | Rice, Dal, 2 Parathas/Rotis, Mix Veg / Paneer, Curd | **₹80** |
| 🍗 **Standard Non-Veg Meal (Thali)** | Rice, Dal, 2 Parathas/Rotis, Egg Curry / Chicken Curry | **₹90** |
| 🍛 **Economy Meal (Jan Ahaar)** | 7 Pooris + Aloo Bhaji + Pickle | **₹20** |

---

## ⚖️ Passenger Rights Against Overcharging

1. **"No Bill, No Payment" Policy**: Indian Railways mandate that if a vendor or pantry car staff fails to provide an official printed invoice/bill, **the meal is free for the passenger**.
2. **Pre-Paid Trains (Rajdhani / Shatabdi / Duronto)**: Catering charges are included in ticket cost unless explicitly opted out during booking.
3. **RailMadad Complaint**: If a vendor charges above MRP, passengers can log an instant complaint via RailMadad App or SMS to **139**.
