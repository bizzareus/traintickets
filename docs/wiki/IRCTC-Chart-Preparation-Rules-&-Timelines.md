# ⏱️ IRCTC Chart Preparation Rules & Timelines

Understanding when Indian Railways prepares reservation charts is key to securing confirmed berths, booking Current Reservation tickets, and tracking vacant seats after finalization.

---

## 📌 The Two-Stage Charting System

Indian Railways operates a **two-stage reservation charting policy** across all Reserved Mail, Express, Rajdhani, Shatabdi, and Vande Bharat trains:

```
[ Departure - 4 Hours / 8 PM Previous Day ] ──► 1st Chart Prepared ──► Current Booking Opens
                                                                               │
[ Departure - 30 Minutes ] ──────────────────► 2nd Chart Prepared ──► Internet Booking Closes
```

---

## 1️⃣ First Reservation Chart Rules

The **First Reservation Chart** finalizes the initial passenger seating plan, processes cancellations, and releases unallotted quota seats.

### A. Daytime / Afternoon Departures (Departing after 14:00)
* **Timing**: Prepared at least **4 hours prior** to the scheduled departure of the train from its originating station.
* *Example*: For a train departing at 17:00 (5:00 PM), the 1st chart is prepared at or before 13:00 (1:00 PM).

### B. Morning & Early Afternoon Departures (Departing between 00:00 and 14:00)
* **Timing**: Prepared by **20:00 (8:00 PM) on the previous evening**.
* *Example*: For a train departing at 06:00 AM on Tuesday, the 1st chart is prepared at 08:00 PM on Monday evening.

---

## 🎟️ What Happens After 1st Chart Preparation?

1. **Current Reservation Opens**: Unallotted berths from VIP, Foreign Tourist, Defense, or Emergency Quotas—along with unbooked Tatkal seats—are thrown open to the general public under **Current Booking**.
2. **Discounted Fares**: Current reservation tickets are available on IRCTC or via [LastBerth](https://lastberth.com) at standard or reduced rates until the 2nd chart is prepared.
3. **Waitlist Movement**: Waitlisted passengers see their final status update (Confirmed, RAC, or WL).

---

## 2️⃣ Second & Final Reservation Chart Rules

The **Second Reservation Chart** is the final seating layout handed over to Ticket Examiners (TTEs).

* **Timing**: Prepared **30 minutes prior** to the scheduled departure from the originating station.
* **Intermediate Stations**: For intermediate passing stations, chart adjustments are updated as the train progresses along its route.
* **Booking Closure**: Internet booking on IRCTC and authorized travel apps closes immediately upon 2nd chart generation.
