# 🎫 Waitlist Confirmation Guide: GNWL vs PQWL vs RLWL

Understanding waitlist types and confirmation priorities helps passengers decide whether to hold a ticket or look for alternative berths post chart preparation.

---

## 📊 Waitlist Types Comparison Matrix

| Quota Code | Full Form | Confirmation Priority | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **GNWL** | General Waitlist | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Highest** | Generated when a passenger books from the train's originating station to the destination station. Clears fastest on cancellations. |
| **PQWL** | Pooled Quota Waitlist | ⭐⭐⭐ **Medium** | Shared between small intermediate stations along a route. Has a smaller quota allocation. |
| **RLWL** | Remote Location Waitlist | ⭐⭐ **Lower** | Generated for major intermediate stations between origin and destination. Clears only if someone from that specific route cancels. |
| **TQWL** | Tatkal Waitlist | ⭐ **Lowest** | Waitlist for Tatkal quota bookings. Directly gets cancelled if not confirmed at 1st chart finalization. |

---

## 🔄 How Berths Are Allotted During Chart Finalization

When the 1st reservation chart is prepared 4 hours prior to departure:

1. **RAC Allotment**: Passengers holding RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) tickets get allotted full berths first.
2. **GNWL Priority**: GNWL passengers are processed next in sequential order.
3. **Quota Merging**: Unutilized VIP, Defense, Foreign Tourist, or Senior Citizen quotas are merged into the general pool to confirm waitlisted passengers.
4. **Current Reservation Clearance**: Any remaining unallotted berths move into the **Current Reservation** pool for last-minute booking on [LastBerth](https://lastberth.com).
