Understanding IRCTC Chart Preparation Rules

Official guidelines for Indian Railways 1st Chart and 2nd Chart timelines.

📌 1st Chart Preparation Rule

For trains departing between 00:00 and 14:00, the first reservation chart is usually prepared at 08:00 PM (20:00) on the previous evening. For trains departing after 14:00, the first chart is prepared at least 4 hours prior to departure from the origin station.

⏱️ 2nd Chart Preparation Rule

The second and final reservation chart is prepared 30 minutes before scheduled departure from the train's originating station. Once the 2nd chart is finalized, internet booking closes and final lists are handed to TTEs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to book vacant berths after 1st chart preparation?

After the 1st chart is prepared, all unallotted quota seats and cancelled berths become available under Current Reservation. You can book these online on IRCTC or via the LastBerth App at a discounted rate until the 2nd chart is prepared.

Can waitlisted tickets get confirmed in 2nd chart?

Yes! If passengers cancel their confirmed tickets between the 1st chart and 2nd chart, waitlisted passengers or current reservation buyers get allotted those berths during the 2nd chart finalization.
